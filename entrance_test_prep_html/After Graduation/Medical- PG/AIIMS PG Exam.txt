<h1 id="aiims-pg-exam-all-india-institute-of-medical-sciences-postgraduate-entrance-exam">AIIMS PG Exam (All India Institute of Medical Sciences Postgraduate Entrance Exam)</h1>

<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The AIIMS PG Exam is a highly competitive and prestigious entrance examination conducted by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, for admission to various postgraduate medical courses such as Doctor of Medicine (MD), Master of Surgery (MS), Master of Dental Surgery (MDS), and other specialized postgraduate programs. Primarily designed for admission to AIIMS institutions and other affiliated government medical colleges, the exam rigorously assesses the in-depth clinical knowledge, critical thinking, diagnostic acumen, and problem-solving skills of MBBS graduates. A stellar performance in the AIIMS PG Exam not only paves the way for advanced medical specialization but also offers the unparalleled opportunity to study at one of India&#8217;s most renowned and esteemed medical institutions, known for its world-class education, research, and healthcare services.</p>

<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Purpose</strong>: To evaluate the clinical knowledge, theoretical proficiency, and aptitude of MBBS graduates for admission to postgraduate medical courses (MD, MS, MDS, and other PG programs) at AIIMS campuses and participating government institutions across India.</li>
<li><strong>Conducting Body</strong>: All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, a premier autonomous medical institution under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, recognized globally for excellence in medical education and research.</li>
<li><strong>Mode of Examination</strong>: Computer-based test (CBT) conducted at designated centers across India, ensuring a standardized and secure testing environment.</li>
<li><strong>Eligibility</strong>: Candidates must hold an MBBS degree (or equivalent) from a recognized institution, duly approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) or Dental Council of India (DCI) for MDS aspirants. They must have completed or be in the process of completing their mandatory one-year rotating internship by the stipulated deadline for admission. Final-year MBBS students are eligible, provided they fulfill internship requirements before the counselling or joining date. Additionally, candidates must possess a permanent or provisional registration certificate from NMC, DCI, or respective State Medical/Dental Councils.</li>
<li><strong>Key Dates</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Application Window: Typically opens in March or April for the July session and September or October for the January session</li>
<li>Exam Date: Usually conducted in May or June (July session) and November or December (January session)</li>
<li>Result Declaration: Generally announced a few weeks after the exam</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h3 id="preparation-tips">Preparation Tips</h3>
<ul>
<li>Revise core MBBS/BDS subjects thoroughly using standard textbooks (e.g., Harrison&#8217;s for Medicine, Bailey &amp;amp; Love for Surgery, Cohen for Dental subjects) and concise review notes tailored for AIIMS PG to cover the extensive syllabus efficiently.</li>
<li>Practice previous years&#8217; AIIMS PG papers and take full-length mock tests regularly to build exam endurance, improve time management, and familiarize yourself with the pattern of clinical and image-based questions under timed conditions.</li>
<li>Focus on clinical case studies, diagnostic scenarios, and application-based questions to enhance problem-solving skills, as AIIMS PG emphasizes practical knowledge and critical thinking over rote learning.</li>
<li>Prioritize high-weightage clinical subjects like Medicine, Surgery, and Obstetrics &amp;amp; Gynecology for MD/MS, while ensuring a strong grasp of foundational Pre-Clinical and Para-Clinical topics for integrated questions; for MDS, focus on clinical dental specialties.</li>
<li>Stay updated with recent medical guidelines, clinical protocols, and advancements, as AIIMS often includes questions testing awareness of current practices and emerging trends in healthcare.</li>
<li>Utilize online platforms, AIIMS-specific question banks, mobile apps for MCQs, and coaching programs to access high-yield topics, expert guidance on tricky assertion-reasoning questions, and stress management strategies during preparation.</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="additional-considerations">Additional Considerations</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Test </strong><strong>Centers</strong>: The AIIMS PG Exam is conducted at numerous designated centers across India (covering major cities and regions) to ensure accessibility for candidates nationwide. The updated list of test centers is available on the official AIIMS website during the registration process, allowing candidates to select their preferred location.</li>
<li><strong>Score Validity</strong>: AIIMS PG Exam scores are valid only for the current admission cycle or session in which the candidate appears (i.e., for the specific January or July intake). Candidates must reappear in subsequent sessions to apply again or improve their rank for desired specialties or institutions.</li>
<li><strong>Updates &amp;amp; Notifications</strong>: Exam details, including application timelines, fee structure, pattern changes (e.g., number of questions or duration), eligibility criteria, and counselling processes, may vary per session or year. Candidates must refer to the latest official AIIMS PG Exam notification on the AIIMS website for the most accurate and up-to-date information.</li>
<li><strong>Reservation Policies</strong>: AIIMS follows the Government of India&#8217;s reservation policies for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and PwD categories as per admission and counselling criteria, offering relaxation in qualifying cutoffs and seat allocation (e.g., 27% for OBC-NCL, 10% for EWS); specific details are provided in the official guidelines. Additionally, institutional preference may be given to AIIMS graduates for a certain percentage of seats.</li>
<li><strong>Retake Policy</strong>: There is no limit on the number of attempts for the AIIMS PG Exam; candidates can reappear in subsequent sessions (January or July cycles) to improve their scores or secure admission to preferred specialties or campuses, subject to eligibility criteria.</li>
<li><strong>Special Accommodations</strong>: AIIMS provides accommodations for candidates with disabilities or special needs (e.g., extra time, scribe assistance, accessible test centers) as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, upon submission of appropriate documentation during registration.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Resources</strong>: In addition to official AIIMS PG sample papers and guidelines available on the AIIMS website, candidates can access third-party resources, including standard medical/dental textbooks, online platforms, question banks, mobile apps for MCQs, and coaching programs tailored for AIIMS PG to support comprehensive preparation and revision, focusing on its unique question style and difficulty level.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2>
<p>The AIIMS PG Exam, conducted by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, stands as a hallmark of excellence and one of the most competitive entrance examinations in India for admission to postgraduate medical and dental courses like MD, MS, and MDS across AIIMS campuses and affiliated government institutions. By rigorously evaluating candidates&#8217; clinical knowledge, theoretical depth, and critical thinking through a standardized computer-based format, the AIIMS PG Exam identifies medical and dental graduates with the potential to excel in specialized healthcare and research domains. Whether your aspiration is to become a leading clinician in a coveted specialty, pursue pioneering medical research, teach future healthcare professionals, or practice globally, a strong performance in the AIIMS PG Exam can profoundly shape your professional journey. Aspiring candidates are encouraged to prepare meticulously with a focus on clinical application, leverage high-quality resources, practice extensively through mock tests, and stay updated with official notifications to approach this esteemed examination with confidence and achieve their aspirations at one of the pinnacle institutions of medical education in India.</p>
