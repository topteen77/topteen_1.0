<h1 id="indraprastha-university-common-entrance-test-ipu-cet-for-b-com-hons">Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test (IPU CET) for B.Com (Hons.)</h1>
<h2 id="overview-and-details"> Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test (IPU CET) for B.Com is a university-level entrance exam conducted by Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), Delhi. This test serves as the primary admission gateway for students seeking enrollment in the B.Com (Hons.) program at various colleges affiliated with IP University.</p>
<p>The exam evaluates candidates on their mathematical, logical, and general awareness skills, ensuring they possess the aptitude required for commerce and business-related studies. It is designed to identify students with a strong foundation in relevant subjects and the potential to excel in the field of commerce.</p>

<h2 id="ipu-cet-b-com-eligibility-criteria">IPU CET (B.Com) - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for the B.Com (Hons.) program through IPU CET, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Class 12) or equivalent from a recognized board.</li>
<li>They must have a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in their best four subjects, which must include:</li>
<ul>
<li>English (compulsory)</li>
<li>Mathematics or Business Mathematics (mandatory)</li>
</ul>
<li>Students appearing for their Class 12 exams in the current year can also apply, provided they meet the eligibility criteria at the time of admission.</li>
<li>Age Limit: Candidates should generally not be more than 21 years old as of the admission cutoff month (typically August of the admission year, subject to university guidelines).</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="ipu-cet-b-com-selection-process">IPU CET (B.Com) - Selection Process</h2>
<p>The admission process for the B.Com (Hons.) program through IPU CET consists of the following main stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>IPU CET Entrance Exam</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must appear for the online entrance test at their designated exam center.</li>
<li>The test evaluates candidates on mathematical, logical, and business-related concepts.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Merit List &amp;amp; Cut-off</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>The university releases a rank-wise merit list based on IPU CET scores.</li>
<li>Candidates meeting the cut-off criteria qualify for the counselling process.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Counselling &amp;amp; Seat Allotment</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Qualified candidates must register for online counselling on the GGSIPU website.</li>
<li>Seats are allotted based on rank, preferences, and availability.</li>
<li>Candidates must verify documents and pay the admission fee to confirm their seat.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="ipu-cet-b-com-application-process">IPU CET (B.Com) - Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for IPU CET (B.Com) is conducted online through the official GGSIPU website. Below are the detailed steps to apply:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official IPU website:&#160;.</li>
<li>Click on the relevant IPU CET registration link and create an account.</li>
<li>Enter personal, academic, and contact details in the application form.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Upload scanned copies of a recent photograph, signature, and Class 10 &amp;amp; 12 certificates as per the specified format and size.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Payment of Application Fee</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>The application fee must be paid online through the available payment modes.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Amount</strong>: Approximately&#160;&#8377;1200&#8377;1200&#160;for all categories (subject to change based on university guidelines).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Once the application is submitted and payment is confirmed, candidates can download the IPU CET admit card from the official website.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Appear for the Exam</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must report to the allotted exam center with the admit card and a valid ID proof on the scheduled exam day.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Counselling &amp;amp; Seat Allotment</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Ranked candidates must participate in the online counselling process.</li>
<li>Seat allotment is based on rank, preferences, and availability of seats in affiliated colleges.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="why-choose-ipu-for-b-com-hons">Why Choose IPU for B.Com (Hons.)?</h2>
<p>Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) is a preferred choice for pursuing a B.Com (Hons.) degree due to several advantages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Top-Notch Faculty</strong>: The university and its affiliated colleges boast highly experienced professors with real-world business expertise.</li>
<li><strong>Industry-Relevant Curriculum</strong>: The B.Com (Hons.) program is designed to align with current market trends and corporate needs, ensuring students are job-ready.</li>
<li><strong>Excellent Placements</strong>: Reputed companies such as Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG, and ICICI Bank regularly recruit from IPU, offering strong career opportunities.</li>
<li><strong>Affordable Fees</strong>: Compared to other private universities, IPU offers quality education at relatively lower costs, making it accessible to a wider range of students.</li>
<li><strong>Strategic Location</strong>: Located in Delhi, IPU provides students with access to numerous internships, networking events, and exposure to the corporate hub of India.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="additional-information">Additional Information</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Affiliated Colleges</strong>: IPU CET scores are accepted by several reputed colleges under GGSIPU, such as Maharaja Surajmal Institute, Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies, and Jagan Institute of Management Studies, among others. Candidates can choose their preferred colleges during the counselling process.</li>
<li><strong>Reservation Policy</strong>: GGSIPU follows a reservation policy for various categories as per government norms, including SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and PWD candidates. Additionally, there are provisions for Delhi and outside-Delhi quotas, with a significant percentage of seats reserved for Delhi region candidates.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Resources</strong>: Candidates are encouraged to refer to standard textbooks for Class 11 and 12 commerce subjects, practice previous years&#8217; question papers, and take mock tests to familiarize themselves with the exam pattern and time management.</li>
<li><strong>Campus Facilities</strong>: IPU and its affiliated colleges offer modern infrastructure, including well-equipped libraries, computer labs, auditoriums, and sports facilities, fostering an environment conducive to learning and overall development.</li>
</ul>
