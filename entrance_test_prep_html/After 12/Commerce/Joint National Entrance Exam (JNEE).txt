<h1 id="joint-national-entrance-exam-jnee">Joint National Entrance Exam (JNEE) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Joint National Entrance Exam (JNEE) is a national-level entrance test conducted by Jayoti Vidyapeeth Women's University (JVWU) for admission into various undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines, including commerce, business, arts, law, science, education, and engineering. Established in 2008, JVWU is India's first state-private women&#8217;s university, offering quality education exclusively to female candidates.</p>
<p>The JNEE exam is conducted once a year in an online mode (computer-based test &#8211; CBT). It serves as a crucial gateway for female students seeking admission to commerce programs such as B.Com, BBA, M.Com, MBA, and PhD in commerce-related fields at JVWU, providing them with opportunities to excel in a supportive and empowering environment.</p>

<h2 id="jnee-eligibility-criteria">JNEE - Eligibility Criteria</h2>

<h3 id="for-undergraduate-ug-programs">For Undergraduate (UG) Programs:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>: Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Senior Secondary) from a recognized board.</li>
<li><strong>Minimum Marks Required</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>General Category: 55% aggregate.</li>
<li>OBC/SC/ST Category: 50% aggregate.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Stream Requirement</strong>: Candidates applying for B.Com and BBA must have studied commerce or related subjects in their 12th grade.</li>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>: No upper age limit.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="for-postgraduate-pg-programs">For Postgraduate (PG) Programs:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>: A Bachelor&#8217;s degree in a relevant commerce-related discipline from a recognized university.</li>
<li><strong>Minimum Marks Required</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>General Category: 55% aggregate.</li>
<li>OBC/SC/ST Category: 50% aggregate.</li>

</ul>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-doctoral-phd-programs">For Doctoral (PhD) Programs:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>: A Postgraduate degree (Master&#8217;s) in Commerce or Business Administration from a recognized university.</li>
<li><strong>Research Proposal</strong>: Candidates must submit a research proposal in their chosen area of specialization.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="day-boarder-reservation-criteria">Day Boarder Reservation Criteria:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates from Jaipur, Ajmer, and Sikar districts are eligible for day boarder reservations.</li>
<li>10-30% of seats in each program are reserved for day boarders.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-jnee">How to Apply for JNEE?</h2>

<p><strong>Step-by-Step Registration Process</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the Official Website</strong>: Navigate to&#160;.</li>
<li><strong>Click on "Apply for JNEE"</strong>: Under the admissions section.</li>
<li><strong>Register Online</strong>: Use a valid email ID and mobile number to create an account.</li>
<li><strong>Fill Out the Application Form</strong>: Provide personal, academic, and course-related details.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Necessary Documents</strong>: Including:</li>
<ul>
<li>Recent passport-size photograph.</li>
<li>Scanned signature.</li>
<li>10th &amp;amp; 12th mark sheets (for UG applicants).</li>
<li>Bachelor&#8217;s degree certificate (for PG applicants).</li>
<li>Master&#8217;s degree &amp;amp; research proposal (for PhD applicants).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Pay the Application Fee</strong>: Through online payment methods.</li>
<li><strong>Submit the Application</strong>: Download the confirmation receipt for future reference.</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="jnee-result-and-selection-criteria">JNEE - Result and Selection Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li>Results will be declared online on the official website&#160;.</li>
<li>Candidates must meet the minimum qualifying marks criteria to be considered for admission.</li>
<li>A merit list will be prepared based on JNEE scores.</li>
<li>Shortlisted candidates will be invited for counselling and seat allotment.</li>
<li>PhD candidates may have to appear for an additional interview based on the evaluation of their research proposal.</li>
</ul>
