<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>St. Xavier&#8217;s College, Mumbai, one of India's most prestigious institutions, conducts an entrance test for undergraduate commerce programs, particularly the Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) and Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA - Finance and Entrepreneurship). This entrance exam is designed to evaluate candidates' aptitude, analytical skills, logical reasoning, general awareness, and creative thinking abilities.</p>
<p>Established in 1869, St. Xavier&#8217;s College is an autonomous institution affiliated with the University of Mumbai, known for its academic rigor and holistic education approach. The entrance exam for BMS and BBA programs is highly competitive, attracting students from across the country who aspire to build careers in management and business.</p>

<h2 id="st-xavier-8217-s-college-mumbai-entrance-exam-eligibility-criteria">St. Xavier&#8217;s College (Mumbai) Entrance Exam - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for the BMS and BBA programs through St. Xavier&#8217;s College Entrance Exam, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.</li>
<li>Minimum aggregate marks required:</li>
<ul>
<li>General Category: 65%</li>
<li>Christian Minority Category: 60%</li>
<li>SC/ST Candidates: 55%</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must generally be under 22 years of age as of the admission cutoff month (typically June of the admission year, subject to college guidelines).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Board Equivalency</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Students from ISC, CBSE, Maharashtra State Board, IB, or equivalent boards can apply.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Reserved Categories</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Seats are reserved for Christian Minority Candidates as St. Xavier&#8217;s is a minority institution.</li>
<li>SC/ST, OBC, and PwD category students are provided reservations as per Mumbai University norms.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="selection-process-for-admission">Selection Process for Admission</h2>
<p>The admission process for BMS and BBA programs at St. Xavier&#8217;s College consists of the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Test</strong>: Candidates must appear for St. Xavier&#8217;s Entrance Exam.</li>
<li><strong>Shortlisting</strong>: Based on exam scores, candidates are shortlisted for the next round.</li>
<li><strong>Personal Interview (PI)</strong>: Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview round to assess their communication skills, personality, and fit for the program.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong>: Admission is based on the combined score of the Entrance Test (60%) and Personal Interview (40%).</li>
<li><strong>Admission Confirmation</strong>: Selected candidates must complete document verification and fee payment to secure their seat.</li>
</ul>
