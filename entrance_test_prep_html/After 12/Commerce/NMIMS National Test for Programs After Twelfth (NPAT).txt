<h1 id="nmims-national-test-for-programs-after-twelfth-npat">NMIMS National Test for Programs After Twelfth (NPAT) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The NMIMS National Test for Programs After Twelfth (NPAT) is a university-level entrance exam conducted by Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) for admission to its undergraduate (UG) programs. These programs include B.Com (Hons.), BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration), B.Sc. Finance, and Integrated MBA courses. The exam evaluates a candidate&#8217;s quantitative skills, logical reasoning, and verbal ability, ensuring their readiness for commerce and management programs.</p>
<p>NMIMS is renowned for its focus on industry-relevant education, and NPAT serves as a gateway for students to join one of India&#8217;s leading private universities with campuses in Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Navi Mumbai, Indore, Shirpur, and Dhule. The test is designed to identify candidates with strong analytical and communication skills suitable for professional undergraduate programs.</p>

<h2 id="nmims-npat-eligibility-criteria">NMIMS NPAT - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for undergraduate programs through NMIMS NPAT, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.</li>
<li>A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Commerce, Science, or Arts stream is required.</li>
<li>For B.Com (Hons.) and B.Sc. Finance, Mathematics/Statistics is a mandatory subject in Class 12.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must be below 25 years of age at the time of admission.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Additional Notes</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Students appearing for their Class 12 exams in the current year are also eligible to apply, provided they meet the criteria at the time of admission.</li>
<li>Specific programs may have additional eligibility requirements as defined by NMIMS.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="nmims-npat-selection-process">NMIMS NPAT - Selection Process</h2>
<p>The admission process for NMIMS undergraduate programs through NPAT consists of the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Entrance Test</strong>: Candidates must appear for NPAT and secure a competitive score. Multiple attempts are allowed, and the best score is considered for admission.</li>
<li><strong>Merit List</strong>: NMIMS releases a rank-wise merit list based on NPAT performance.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling &amp;amp; Admission</strong>: Shortlisted candidates undergo a selection process, including document verification and final seat allocation at the respective NMIMS campus.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-nmims-npat">How to Apply for NMIMS NPAT?</h2>
<p>The application process for NMIMS NPAT is conducted online through the official website. Below are the detailed steps to apply:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the Official Website</strong>: Navigate to&#160;.</li>
<li><strong>Register &amp;amp; Fill Application Form</strong>: Provide personal, educational, and contact details in the online application form.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong>: Upload a recent passport-size photograph and relevant academic documents such as the 10+2 mark sheet as per the specified format and size.</li>
<li><strong>Pay Application Fee</strong>: Complete the payment online through net banking, credit/debit card, or UPI. The fee amount varies by program and is subject to change (typically around&#160;&#8377;1800&#8722;&#8377;2000&#8377;1800&#8722;&#8377;2000&#160;for a single program; check the official portal for updates).</li>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong>: Once registered and payment is confirmed, candidates can download their NPAT admit card from the website before the exam.</li>
<li><strong>Appear for the Exam</strong>: Candidates must report to the allotted exam center with the admit card and a valid ID proof on the scheduled exam day.</li>
</ul>
