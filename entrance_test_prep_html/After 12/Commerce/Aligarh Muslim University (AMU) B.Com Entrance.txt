<h1 id="aligarh-muslim-university-amu-b-com-entrance-exam">Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) B.Com Entrance Exam </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) conducts the AMU B.Com Entrance Exam for admission into its Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) programs at the university&#8217;s Faculty of Commerce. This university-level entrance exam is designed to assess candidates' knowledge in accounting, business studies, economics, mathematics, and general awareness.</p>
<p>Founded in 1920, AMU is one of India&#8217;s premier central universities, located in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh. It is renowned for its rich history, diverse academic programs, and commitment to fostering excellence in education. The B.Com (Hons.) program at AMU is highly sought after by commerce aspirants due to its comprehensive curriculum and strong industry connections.</p>

<h2 id="amu-b-com-entrance-exam-eligibility-criteria">AMU B.Com Entrance Exam - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for the B.Com (Hons.) program through the AMU B.Com Entrance Exam, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.</li>
<li>A minimum aggregate of 50% marks in Commerce, Science, or Arts stream with Accounts, Economics, Business Studies, or Mathematics as one of the subjects.</li>
<li>Candidates who have completed a Diploma in Secretarial Practice (from AMU) with at least 50% marks can also apply.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>The candidate must not exceed 24 years of age as of the admission cutoff month (typically July of the admission year, subject to university guidelines).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Reservation Criteria</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>50% seats are reserved for internal candidates (AMU students).</li>
<li>Reserved categories such as SC/ST, OBC, PwD, and special quotas are applicable as per AMU admission policies.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="selection-process-for-amu-b-com-admission">Selection Process for AMU B.Com Admission</h2>
<p>The admission process for the B.Com (Hons.) program at AMU consists of the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Exam</strong>: Candidates must appear for the AMU B.Com Entrance Test at designated centers.</li>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Shortlisting</strong>: Selection is based on exam scores and the cutoff set by AMU.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling &amp;amp; Document Verification</strong>: Shortlisted candidates must attend the counselling session for seat allocation.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Candidates must pay fees and complete document submission to confirm admission.</li>
</ul>
