<h1 id="telangana-residential-degree-common-entrance-test-tsrdc-cet">Telangana Residential Degree Common Entrance Test (TSRDC CET) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Telangana Residential Degree Common Entrance Test (TSRDC CET) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by the Telangana Residential Educational Institutions Society (TREIS). This exam serves as a gateway for admission into B.A., B.Com., and B.Sc. programs in residential degree colleges across Telangana. The exam evaluates students on their subject knowledge and aptitude, ensuring admission to top government-funded institutions in the state.</p>
<p>TSRDC CET is designed to provide quality education to students in a residential setup, fostering academic excellence and personal development. These colleges are known for their disciplined environment, experienced faculty, and focus on holistic growth, making them a preferred choice for many students in Telangana.</p>

<h2 id="tsrdc-cet-eligibility-criteria">TSRDC CET - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for the TSRDC CET, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Nationality &amp;amp; Domicile</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>The candidate must be an Indian citizen.</li>
<li>Candidates must be residents of Telangana.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Academic Qualifications</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed the Intermediate (10+2) examination from a recognized board.</li>
<li>General category candidates must secure:</li>
<ul>
<li>40% aggregate marks in English.</li>
<li>50% aggregate marks overall.</li>
</ul>
<li>SC/ST/BC category candidates are given a 5% relaxation in the minimum marks requirement.</li>
<li>Candidates must have cleared the IPE-March examination in a single attempt.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Criteria</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>There is no upper age limit for TSRDC CET.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<ul>

<li>Merit lists will be prepared based on performance in the entrance test.</li>
