<h1 id="jamia-millia-islamia-jmi-b-com-entrance-exam">Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) B.Com Entrance Exam </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), a prestigious central university in India, conducts the JMI Entrance Exam for admission to its Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) program. This university-level entrance test is designed to evaluate candidates' analytical ability, commerce knowledge, numerical aptitude, and general awareness.</p>
<p>Established in 1920, JMI is located in New Delhi and is renowned for its commitment to academic excellence, cultural diversity, and inclusive education. The B.Com (Hons.) program at JMI provides in-depth knowledge of accounting, finance, economics, and business management, preparing students for careers in commerce, banking, taxation, and corporate finance.</p>

<h2 id="jmi-b-com-entrance-exam-eligibility-criteria">JMI B.Com Entrance Exam - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for the B.Com (Hons.) program through the JMI B.Com Entrance Exam, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognized board.</li>
<li>A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Commerce, Science, or Arts streams.</li>
<li>Candidates with Commerce as a subject in 10+2 are preferred.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>The upper age limit is generally 21 years as of the admission cutoff month (typically July of the admission year, subject to university guidelines).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Reservation Criteria</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>50% of seats are reserved for Muslim minority candidates.</li>
<li>Reserved seats for SC/ST, OBC, and PwD categories as per government norms.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="selection-process-for-jmi-b-com-admission">Selection Process for JMI B.Com Admission</h2>
<p>The admission process for the B.Com (Hons.) program at JMI consists of the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Exam</strong>: Candidates must appear for the JMI B.Com Entrance Test at designated centers.</li>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Shortlisting</strong>: Selection is based on exam scores and cutoff marks set by JMI.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling &amp;amp; Document Verification</strong>: Shortlisted candidates must attend counselling for seat allocation.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Candidates must submit documents and pay admission fees to confirm their seat.</li>
</ul>
