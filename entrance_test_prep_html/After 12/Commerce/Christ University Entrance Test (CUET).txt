<h1 id="christ-university-entrance-test-cuet">Christ University Entrance Test (CUET) </h1>
<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Christ University Entrance Test (CUET) is a university-level entrance examination conducted by Christ University, Bangalore, for admissions into its diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs. These programs cover disciplines such as Commerce, Management, Science, Law, Humanities, and Arts. CUET assesses candidates on analytical, logical, verbal, and quantitative skills, along with general awareness and communication proficiency.</p>
<p>The selection process for CUET is multi-staged, ensuring a comprehensive evaluation of candidates. The stages include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Computer-Based Test (CBT)</strong>: An online entrance test.</li>
<li><strong>Micro Presentation (MP)</strong>: A short presentation to assess public speaking and subject knowledge.</li>
<li><strong>Personal Interview (PI)</strong>: An interaction to evaluate communication skills and overall suitability.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="cuet-eligibility-criteria-for-undergraduate-programs">CUET - Eligibility Criteria for Undergraduate Programs</h2>
<p>To apply for undergraduate programs through CUET, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Class 12) from a recognized board in any stream.</li>
<li>Students currently in Class 12 or awaiting their results are also eligible to apply.</li>
<li>While there is no strict minimum percentage requirement for CUET, candidates must fulfill program-specific eligibility criteria as defined by Christ University.</li>
<li>The CUET exam is conducted exclusively in Bengaluru, and all candidates must appear for the test at the designated centers in the city.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="cuet-selection-process">CUET - Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for admission to Christ University through CUET involves multiple stages, designed to assess candidates holistically. The stages are as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Computer-Based Test (CBT) &#8211; CUET Exam</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must appear for the online entrance test as per the scheduled date and time.</li>
<li>The test evaluates verbal, quantitative, analytical, and reasoning skills.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Micro Presentation (MP)</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates are required to deliver a 90-second speech on a randomly assigned topic.</li>
<li>This stage assesses public speaking skills, confidence, and depth of knowledge on the subject.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Personal Interview (PI)</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates are evaluated on their communication skills, general awareness, and subject-specific knowledge.</li>
<li>The interview panel typically consists of faculty members and university representatives.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>The final selection is based on the cumulative performance in the CUET exam, Micro Presentation, and Personal Interview.</li>
<li>Selected candidates receive an admission offer letter from Christ University to complete the formalities.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="cuet-application-process">CUET - Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for CUET is conducted online through the official Christ University website. Below are the detailed steps to apply:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official website:&#160;.</li>
<li>Navigate to the "Admissions" section and select CUET.</li>
<li>Fill in personal, academic, and contact details in the application form.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Upload scanned copies of a recent passport-sized photograph, signature, and relevant academic documents as per the specified format and size.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Payment of Application Fee</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>The application fee varies by course and must be paid online.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Range</strong>: Approximately&#160;&#8377;1500&#8722;&#8377;2000&#8377;1500&#8722;&#8377;2000&#160;(subject to change based on the program).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Once the application is submitted and payment is confirmed, candidates can download their CUET admit card from the official website.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Appear for CUET</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must appear for the online entrance test at the allotted exam center in Bengaluru on the scheduled date.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Attend Micro Presentation &amp;amp; Interview</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Shortlisted candidates are required to participate in the MP and PI rounds at Christ University, Bengaluru.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Admission Offer</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>The final merit list is prepared based on CUET scores, MP, and PI performance.</li>
<li>Selected candidates receive an offer letter and must complete the admission formalities within the stipulated time.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
