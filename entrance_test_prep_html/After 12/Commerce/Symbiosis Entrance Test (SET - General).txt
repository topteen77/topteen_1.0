<h1 id="symbiosis-entrance-test-set-8211-general">Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET &#8211; General) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET &#8211; General) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) [SIU] for admission into various undergraduate programs in Commerce, Management, Humanities, and Law. For commerce aspirants, this test is essential for securing admission to programs such as B.Com (Hons.), BBA, and B.Sc. Economics (Hons.) offered by prestigious Symbiosis institutes across India.</p>
<p>Symbiosis International University is renowned for its academic excellence and industry-oriented curriculum, making SET &#8211; General a highly competitive exam for students aiming to build a career in commerce and business studies. The exam assesses candidates on their verbal, quantitative, analytical, and general awareness skills.</p>

<h2 id="symbiosis-set-general-eligibility-criteria">Symbiosis SET (General) - Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To apply for undergraduate programs through Symbiosis SET (General), candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.</li>
<li>A minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST candidates) is required.</li>
<li>For B.Sc. Economics (Hons.), candidates must have studied Mathematics in Class 12.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>No specific age limit is mentioned for the exam.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Additional Notes</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Students appearing for their Class 12 exams in the current year are also eligible to apply, provided they meet the criteria at the time of admission.</li>
<li>Specific programs or institutes may have additional eligibility requirements as defined by Symbiosis International University.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="symbiosis-set-general-selection-process">Symbiosis SET (General) - Selection Process</h2>
<p>The admission process for Symbiosis undergraduate programs through SET (General) consists of the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Entrance Test (SET &#8211; General)</strong>: Candidates must appear for the CBT exam as per the scheduled date and time.</li>
<li><strong>Shortlisting</strong>: Candidates meeting the cutoff score will be shortlisted for further rounds.</li>
<li><strong>Personal Interview (PI) &amp;amp; Written Ability Test (WAT)</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Some institutes, such as SCMS Pune, Noida, and Nagpur, conduct PI and WAT after the SET score evaluation to assess communication skills and critical thinking.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong>: The final selection is based on SET scores, PI-WAT performance (where applicable), and academic records.</li>
<li><strong>Admission Confirmation</strong>: Selected candidates must complete document verification and fee payment to secure their seat.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-symbiosis-set-general">How to Apply for Symbiosis SET (General)?</h2>
<p>The application process for Symbiosis SET (General) is conducted online through the official website. Below are the detailed steps to apply:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the Official Website</strong>: Navigate to&#160;.</li>
<li><strong>Register &amp;amp; Fill Application Form</strong>: Provide personal, academic, and contact details in the online application form.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong>: Upload a recent passport-size photograph and relevant academic documents such as the 10+2 mark sheet as per the specified format and size.</li>
<li><strong>Pay Application Fee</strong>: Complete the payment online. The fee is approximately&#160;&#8377;1750&#8377;1750&#160;for one test, with an additional&#160;&#8377;1000&#8377;1000&#160;per program if applying to multiple programs (subject to change; check the official portal for updates).</li>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong>: Once registered and payment is confirmed, candidates can download their SET admit card from the website before the exam.</li>
<li><strong>Appear for the Exam</strong>: Candidates must report to the allotted exam center with the admit card and a valid ID proof on the scheduled exam day.</li>
</ul>
