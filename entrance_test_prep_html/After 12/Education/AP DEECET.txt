<h1 id="ap-deecet-andhra-pradesh-diploma-in-elementary-education-common-entrance-test">AP DEECET (Andhra Pradesh Diploma in Elementary Education Common Entrance Test)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The AP DEECET (Andhra Pradesh Diploma in Elementary Education Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by the School Education Department of Andhra Pradesh on behalf of the Government District Institutions. It is designed for candidates who wish to pursue a two-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.Ed), a crucial step towards becoming elementary school educators. The exam is conducted in an online, computer-based mode and serves as a gateway for admission to teacher training programmes across Andhra Pradesh. Only eligible candidates from specific regions, such as Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, can apply, and the exam is administered in Telugu and English mediums to accommodate linguistic diversity.</p>

<h2 id="exam-pattern">Exam Pattern</h2>
<p>The AP DEECET exam is structured into two main parts to assess candidates&#8217; readiness for teacher training at the elementary level. Below is a detailed breakdown of the exam pattern:</p>
<h3 id="structure-of-the-exam">Structure of the Exam</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Part A</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Questions: 60 MCQs</li>
<li>Uniformity: This section is common for all candidates and covers general topics relevant to education.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Part B</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Structure: Divided into four sub-sections:</li>
<ul>
<li>Mathematics</li>
<li>Physical Science</li>
<li>Biological Science</li>
<li>Social Studies</li>
</ul>
<li>Questions: Each sub-section contains 40 questions.</li>
<li>Selection: Candidates must choose one of these four options based on their preferred subject stream for specialization.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for the AP DEECET:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website&#160;&#160;and navigate to the AP DEECET application section. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including academic mark sheets (10+2 or equivalent), proof of age, identification proof, domicile certificate (if applicable), and category certificates (for SC/ST/PH candidates) as specified in the guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using secure online payment modes (credit/debit cards, net banking, etc.) provided on the website. The fee is INR 500 for SC/ST candidates and INR 600 for General Category candidates.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test center, timing, and instructions.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should regularly check the official website for updates on the application process and deadlines.</p>
