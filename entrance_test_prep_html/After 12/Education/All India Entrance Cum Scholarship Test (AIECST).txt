<h1 id="all-india-entrance-cum-scholarship-test-aiecst">All India Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (AIECST)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The All India Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (AIECST) is designed to bridge the gap between aspiration and quality education for students from diverse economic backgrounds. Conducted by APG Shimla University, a well-regarded institution in Himachal Pradesh, this exam offers a gateway to admission into various academic programmes while providing scholarship support to students who may otherwise find higher education financially challenging. Emphasizing academic merit and potential, AIECST is conducted entirely online as a home-based test and is known for its robust evaluation of candidates through multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in key subjects. By offering full or partial scholarships under various categories, AIECST ensures that financially constrained yet meritorious students have the opportunity to pursue higher studies in fields ranging from Engineering and Sciences to Humanities and Education.</p>

<h2 id="syllabus-overview">Syllabus Overview</h2>
<p>The syllabus generally covers topics relevant to quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and current affairs or general knowledge, ensuring that candidates demonstrate both academic proficiency and critical thinking skills. Below is a brief outline of the areas covered:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Quantitative Analysis</strong>: Basic arithmetic, algebra, geometry, data interpretation, and numerical problem-solving skills.</li>
<li><strong>General Knowledge</strong>: Current affairs, history, geography, civics, environmental studies, and basic scientific concepts.</li>
<li><strong>Logical Reasoning</strong>: Analytical puzzles, pattern recognition, logical deductions, and problem-solving exercises to assess critical thinking abilities.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: The exact distribution of questions and syllabus content may be updated periodically by the university. Candidates should refer to the official website for the most current syllabus, sample papers, and additional details on section-wise weightage. The absence of negative marking (if confirmed) allows candidates to attempt all questions without penalty.</p>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for the All India Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (AIECST):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website admission.agu.edu.in/cet and navigate to the AIECST admissions section. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including 10th/12th mark sheets or graduation certificates (as applicable), recent passport-sized photograph, valid identity proof, and category or income certificates (if applicable) as specified in the application guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using secure online payment options (credit/debit cards, net banking, etc.) provided on the website. The fee structure may vary based on category and is detailed on the official portal.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline. In some cases, offline submission is accepted at designated centers as per university instructions.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test instructions and timing.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should have a stable internet connection and compatible device for the online test.</p>
