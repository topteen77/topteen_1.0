<h1 id="ts-deecet-telangana-state-diploma-in-elementary-education-common-entrance-test">TS DEECET (Telangana State Diploma in Elementary Education Common Entrance Test)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The TS DEECET (Telangana State Diploma in Elementary Education Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by the Department of School Education, Government of Telangana. It is designed for candidates interested in pursuing either a Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) or a Diploma in Preschool Education (DPSE), both of which are two-year programmes aimed at training future elementary and preschool educators. The exam is computer-based (online) and facilitates admissions into government District Institutes of Education and Training (DIETs) as well as private unaided elementary teacher education institutions in Telangana. The test is available in multiple mediums&#8212;English, Telugu, and Urdu&#8212;making it accessible to a diverse pool of candidates, including those from both minority and non-minority institutions.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>Candidates applying for TS DEECET must fulfill the following criteria to be eligible for admission to D.El.Ed or DPSE programmes:</p>
<table>
<tr>
<th>
<p><strong>Criteria</strong></p>
</th>
<th>
<p><strong>Specifications</strong></p>
</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>
<p>Citizenship &amp;amp; Local Status</p>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>Must be an Indian citizen and comply with the Telangana Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission) Order, 1947 (as amended).
- Both local and non-local candidates may apply, subject to seat allocation policies.</li></ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>
<p>Age Requirement</p>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>Candidates must be at least 17 years old as of a specified reference month of the entrance year.
- There is no upper age limit for admission to either the D.El.Ed or DPSE programmes, ensuring inclusivity for candidates of all ages.</li></ul>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>
<p>Educational Qualification</p>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>Applicants must have passed the Intermediate (10+2) examination or an equivalent qualifying exam from a recognized board.
- Candidates who are awaiting their results can also apply, provided they clear the qualifying exam by the time of admission.
- A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required for general category candidates, while candidates from SC, ST, or PwD categories require at least 45%.</li></ul>
</td>
</tr>
</table>
<p><strong>Additional Note</strong>: Eligibility criteria, including relaxations in marks and local status requirements, may be subject to updates based on the policies of the Department of School Education, Telangana. Candidates are advised to check the latest admission brochure on the official website for detailed and updated information.</p>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for the TS DEECET:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website&#160;&#160;and navigate to the TS DEECET application section. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including academic certificates (10+2 or equivalent), proof of age, domicile certificate (if claiming local status), identification proof, and category certificates (for SC/ST/PwD candidates) as specified in the guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using secure online payment modes (credit/debit cards, net banking, etc.) provided on the website. The fee structure may vary based on category and is detailed on the official portal.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test center, timing, and instructions.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should regularly check the official website for updates on the application process and deadlines.</p>

<ul>
