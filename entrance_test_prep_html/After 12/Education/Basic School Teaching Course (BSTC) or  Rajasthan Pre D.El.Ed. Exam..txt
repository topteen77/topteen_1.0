<h1 id="basic-school-teaching-course-bstc-rajasthan-pre-d-el-ed-exam">Basic School Teaching Course (BSTC) / Rajasthan Pre D.El.Ed. Exam</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The Basic School Teaching Course (BSTC) exam, also known as the Rajasthan Pre D.El.Ed. Exam, is a state-level entrance test conducted by the Rajasthan Department of Elementary Education (DEED). This exam is designed for candidates residing in Rajasthan who wish to pursue a Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) to become elementary school educators. The BSTC exam aims to implement new elementary education policies, standardize teaching practices, and expand the reach and quality of basic education across the state.</p>
<p>With a total of 600 marks and conducted in a pen-and-paper (offline) format, the BSTC exam evaluates candidates' aptitude in various domains critical to effective teaching. It serves not only as an entrance test but also as a gateway to obtaining quality teacher training, enabling candidates to contribute significantly to the development of elementary education in Rajasthan.</p>

<h2 id="syllabus">Syllabus</h2>
<p>The BSTC exam tests candidates on core areas essential for a teaching career at the elementary level. The main subjects covered in the syllabus are outlined below:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mental Ability</strong>: Focuses on logical reasoning, problem-solving, and analytical skills to assess critical thinking capabilities.</li>
<li><strong>Language Ability</strong>: Assesses proficiency in language comprehension and communication skills in English, Hindi, or Sanskrit (based on the chosen course).</li>
<li><strong>General Awareness (of Rajasthan)</strong>: Evaluates knowledge of Rajasthan&#8217;s culture, history, geography, political structure, and current affairs specific to the state.</li>
<li><strong>Teaching Aptitude</strong>: Measures the candidate&#8217;s understanding of pedagogical principles, classroom management, child psychology, and teaching methodologies tailored for elementary education.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: A detailed syllabus with topic-wise breakdowns for each section is available on the official website predeled.com. Candidates are encouraged to refer to it for comprehensive preparation.</p>

<h2 id="exam-pattern">Exam Pattern</h2>
<p>The BSTC exam is conducted in an offline, pen-and-paper format and consists of Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs). Each correct answer is awarded 3 marks, with no negative marking for incorrect answers. The exam comprises a total of 200 questions, adding up to 600 marks. Candidates have the option to choose between two language combinations based on their course preference:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>General Course</strong>: English + Hindi</li>
<li><strong>Sanskrit Course</strong>: English + Sanskrit</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for the BSTC / Rajasthan Pre D.El.Ed. Exam:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website predeled.com and navigate to the BSTC application section. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including proof of citizenship, Rajasthan domicile certificate (if applicable), academic certificates (HSC mark sheets or equivalent), recent passport-sized photograph, and relevant category certificates (for reserved category applicants) as specified in the guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using secure online payment options provided on the website. The fee structure may vary based on category and is detailed on the official portal.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test center, timing, and instructions.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should regularly check the official website for updates on the application process and deadlines.</p>
