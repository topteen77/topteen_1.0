<h1 id="gd-goenka-aspire-entrance-and-scholarship-test">GD Goenka Aspire Entrance and Scholarship Test</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The GD Goenka Aspire Entrance and Scholarship Test is a centralized evaluation conducted by GD Goenka University for admission into its diverse range of Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) programmes. Designed to assess candidates from various academic backgrounds with strong potential, this test serves a dual purpose: it acts as both an entrance exam and a scholarship screening mechanism. With a significant scholarship fund available, high-scoring candidates can secure tuition fee waivers of up to 100%. Founded in 2013 and located in Haryana, GD Goenka University has grown to include multiple schools and centres of excellence. The exam is administered online via a computer-based test (CBT) and is remotely monitored to ensure fairness and integrity, making it a modern and accessible evaluation platform for aspiring students.</p>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for the GD Goenka Aspire Entrance and Scholarship Test:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website gdgoenkauniversity.com and navigate to the admissions section. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including academic certificates (10th, 12th, and provisional/final degree certificate for undergraduates), institution certificate for final-year students, recent passport-sized photograph, and signature as specified in the application guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using the secure online payment gateway provided on the website. The fee structure may vary based on the programme applied for and is detailed on the official portal.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test instructions and timing.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference. Candidates should have a stable internet connection and compatible device for the online test.</p>
