<h1 id="indian-army-soldier-technical-clerk-store-keeper-recruitment">Indian Army Soldier Technical / Clerk / Store Keeper Recruitment</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Indian Army conducts a competitive recruitment process to induct candidates into various non-officer (soldier) roles such as Soldier Technical, Soldier Clerk, and Soldier Store Keeper Technical (SKT). These positions are essential for the technical, administrative, and logistical support that ensures the smooth functioning of the Army. This recruitment targets young candidates with minimum educational qualifications and specific physical and medical standards, offering them a stable and prestigious career in the defense forces.</p>
<p><strong>Key Objectives:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Efficient Support:</strong>&#160;Recruit personnel for critical technical, administrative, and logistical roles to support the Indian Army&#8217;s operations.</li>
<li><strong>Talent Identification:</strong>&#160;Identify candidates with the aptitude for handling technical tasks, clerical responsibilities, and inventory management.</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunity:</strong>&#160;Provide a stable career path with opportunities for growth, specialized training, and promotions within the Army&#8217;s structured hierarchy.
</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="3-application-process">3. Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for Indian Army Soldier Technical, Clerk, and Store Keeper Technical positions is conducted entirely online through the official Indian Army recruitment portal. Below are the key steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification Release:</strong>&#160;Official notifications are published on the Indian Army recruitment website () and advertised in major newspapers, employment news, and regional recruitment rally announcements.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration:</strong>&#160;Candidates must register on the official portal by providing accurate personal details, academic records, and contact information within the specified application window.</li>
<li><strong>Form Filling &amp;amp; Document Upload:</strong>&#160;Complete the online application form and upload scanned copies of required documents, including:</li>
<ul>
<li>Class 10 and/or 10+2 mark sheet/certificate (as per trade requirement).</li>
<li>Proof of age (e.g., birth certificate or Class 10 certificate).</li>
<li>Recent passport-sized photographs.</li>
<li>Signature.</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable) and any other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee Payment:</strong>&#160;Pay the application fee online (typically around&#160;100100&#160;to&#160;200200, subject to the notification). Exemptions are often available for SC/ST candidates. Payment methods include debit/credit cards or net banking.</li>
<li><strong>Submission &amp;amp; Confirmation:</strong>&#160;Submit the completed application before the deadline and save the confirmation receipt or application number for future reference.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download:</strong>&#160;Admit cards are issued on the official website prior to the exam or rally. Candidates must download and print the admit card to appear for the selection process, along with a valid photo ID.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> In some cases, recruitment for soldier-level posts is conducted through open rallies (Agniveer scheme or traditional recruitment), where online registration may precede physical attendance at a rally location. Check the notification for the specific mode.</p>

<h3 id="syllabus-overview">Syllabus Overview:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>General Knowledge:</strong>&#160;Current affairs (national and international), history, geography, sports, culture, defense-related topics, and basic science.</li>
<li><strong>Mathematics:</strong>&#160;Arithmetic (numbers, percentages, ratios, profit/loss), algebra, geometry, mensuration, and basic trigonometry (Class 10 or 10+2 level, depending on trade).</li>
<li><strong>English:</strong>&#160;Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, and error detection.</li>
<li><strong>Reasoning (if applicable):</strong>&#160;Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, coding-decoding, series, analogy, and logical thinking (especially for Clerk/SKT).</li>
<li><strong>General Science (Soldier Technical):</strong>&#160;Physics (mechanics, electricity, optics), Chemistry (elements, reactions), and Biology (human body, basics) at Class 10+2 level.</li>
<li><strong>Trade-Specific (Clerk/SKT):</strong>&#160;Additional focus on typing skills (tested separately in some cycles), bookkeeping, and data interpretation.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="additional-evaluations">Additional Evaluations:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Physical Efficiency Test (PET)/Physical Measurement Test (PMT):</strong>&#160;Candidates undergo PET/PMT to assess fitness and physical standards, including:</li>
<ul>
<li>1.6 km run (timing varies by category, often under 5:45 to 6:20 minutes for full marks).</li>
<li>Pull-ups (minimum 6-10, depending on category).</li>
<li>9 feet ditch jump (qualifying).</li>
<li>Zig-zag balance walk (qualifying).</li>
<li>Height, weight, and chest measurements (as per regional standards).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;Conducted at designated military hospitals to ensure compliance with Army medical standards (e.g., vision, hearing, dental health, and overall fitness).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents to confirm eligibility.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="5-selection-process">5. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for Indian Army Soldier Technical, Clerk, and Store Keeper Technical positions is multi-staged to ensure only the most suitable candidates are recruited. Below are the stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Initial Screening (Rally or Online Shortlisting):</strong>&#160;For rally-based recruitment, candidates are screened for physical standards (PMT) and document verification at the rally site. For online applications, shortlisting may occur based on academic credentials or preliminary tests.</li>
<li><strong>Physical Efficiency Test (PET):</strong>&#160;Candidates must pass PET to qualify for further stages. PET is scoring or qualifying in nature, depending on the recruitment cycle.</li>
<li><strong>Written Examination (CEE):</strong>&#160;Candidates who clear PET undergo the Common Entrance Examination to test academic and trade-specific knowledge. Performance in CEE is critical for merit listing.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;A thorough medical check to ensure candidates meet the required health standards for Army service.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List:</strong>&#160;The final selection is based on combined performance in PET (if scored), CEE, and medical fitness. Selected candidates are issued a joining letter for enrollment in their respective trades.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The Army may adjust cutoffs or selection criteria based on vacancies, candidate performance, and regional requirements. Recruitment may follow the Agniveer scheme (short-term service with potential retention) as per the latest policy.</p>

<h2 id="6-training-and-career-prospects">6. Training and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Training Program:</strong>&#160;Selected candidates undergo initial training at designated Army Regimental Centres or trade-specific training schools. The training includes:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Basic Military Training:</strong>&#160;Focus on discipline, drill, weapon handling, and military ethos (duration varies, often 6-12 months).</li>
<li><strong>Trade-Specific Training:</strong>&#160;Specialized training based on the role:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Soldier Technical:</strong>&#160;Technical skills for equipment maintenance (e.g., vehicles, weapons, communication systems).</li>
<li><strong>Soldier Clerk:</strong>&#160;Clerical skills, record-keeping, and administrative tasks.</li>
<li><strong>Soldier Store Keeper Technical (SKT):</strong>&#160;Inventory management, logistics, and supply chain operations.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Enrollment</strong><strong>:</strong>&#160;Post-training, candidates are enrolled as soldiers in their respective trades at the entry-level rank (e.g., Sepoy) with a specified pay scale under the 7th Pay Commission (basic pay around&#160;21,70021,700&#160;to&#160;69,10069,100&#160;per month post-training, plus allowances like DA, field area allowance, etc.). Under the Agniveer scheme (if applicable), initial engagement may be for a shorter term with different pay structures.</li>
<li><strong>Career Growth:</strong>&#160;A career in these trades offers:</li>
<ul>
<li>Promotions to higher ranks like Naik, Havildar, Naib Subedar, and Subedar based on service, performance, and exams.</li>
<li>Opportunities to become commissioned officers through internal schemes like the Army Cadet College (ACC) or Special Commissioned Officer (SCO) entry.</li>
<li>Further specialization through advanced courses and skill development programs.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Benefits:</strong>&#160;Includes job security, pension (for permanent recruits), medical facilities for self and family, housing, canteen facilities, and other perks as per Army policies.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="7-preparation-tips">7. Preparation Tips</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Revise Basic Subjects:</strong>&#160;Focus on General Knowledge, Mathematics, English, and trade-specific topics using NCERT textbooks (Class 10/10+2) and standard guides like R.S. Aggarwal for reasoning or Lucent&#8217;s General Knowledge.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Mock Tests:</strong>&#160;Regularly solve previous years&#8217; Army CEE papers and take online mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam pattern and improve time management.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated on Current Affairs:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers, magazines, and online portals to stay informed about national and international events, especially defense-related news.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="physical-preparation">Physical Preparation:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Maintain Fitness:</strong>&#160;Incorporate regular exercises like running (aim for 1.6 km in under 6 minutes), pull-ups, and endurance drills to meet PET standards.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Physical Tasks:</strong>&#160;Simulate PET requirements as per Army guidelines to build confidence and assess fitness levels.</li>

</ul>
