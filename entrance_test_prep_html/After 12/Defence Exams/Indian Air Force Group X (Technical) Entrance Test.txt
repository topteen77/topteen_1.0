<h1 id="indian-air-force-group-x-technical-entrance-test">Indian Air Force Group X (Technical) Entrance Test</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Indian Air Force (IAF) Group X (Technical) Entrance Test is a competitive selection process designed to recruit young talent into the technical trades of the IAF as Airmen. This entry scheme targets candidates with a strong background in science and mathematics, focusing on technical roles that support the operational capabilities of the Air Force. Successful candidates are trained in specialized technical disciplines such as avionics, aircraft maintenance, electronics, and other engineering-related fields. After recruitment, they serve as Airmen in technical trades, with opportunities for career progression and further technical training within the IAF.</p>

<p><strong>Key Objectives:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Technical Excellence:</strong>&#160;To recruit candidates with a strong foundation in science and technical skills for critical roles in maintaining and operating advanced Air Force equipment.</li>
<li><strong>Specialized Training:</strong>&#160;To provide rigorous technical and military training, ensuring competence in handling sophisticated systems and equipment.</li>
<li><strong>Career Advancement:</strong>&#160;To offer a prestigious career path with opportunities for professional growth, advanced technical training, and potential progression to higher ranks or commissioned roles through internal promotions or exams.
</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="additional-evaluations">Additional Evaluations:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phase II - Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Candidates who qualify in the written test are called for PFT at Airmen Selection Centres (ASC). The test includes:</li>
<ul>
<li>1.6 km run (to be completed within a specified time, usually under 6.5 minutes).</li>
<li>Push-ups, sit-ups, and squats (specific numbers as per IAF standards).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Adaptability Test-1 (AT-1):</strong>&#160;A written test to assess suitability for military life through situational response questions.</li>
<li><strong>Adaptability Test-2 (AT-2):</strong>&#160;Group discussion to evaluate communication skills, leadership, and teamwork.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;Conducted at designated Air Force medical centers to ensure compliance with IAF medical standards.
</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="5-selection-process">5. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for IAF Group X (Technical) is multi-staged to ensure only the most capable candidates are recruited as Airmen. Below are the stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phase I - Online Written Test:</strong>&#160;Candidates are evaluated based on their performance in English, Physics, and Mathematics. Those who meet the cutoff are shortlisted for Phase II.</li>
<li><strong>Phase II - Physical Fitness Test (PFT) &amp;amp; Adaptability Tests:</strong>&#160;Shortlisted candidates undergo PFT and Adaptability Tests (AT-1 and AT-2) at designated Airmen Selection Centres to assess physical endurance and suitability for military life.</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents (academic certificates, identity proofs, etc.) to confirm eligibility.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;A thorough medical check at Air Force medical centers to ensure candidates meet the required physical and health standards.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List:</strong>&#160;The final selection is based on combined performance in the written test, PFT, Adaptability Tests, and medical fitness. Selected candidates are issued a joining letter for enrollment as Airmen in Group X (Technical) trades.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The IAF reserves the right to adjust cutoffs or selection criteria based on vacancies and candidate performance.
</p>
<h2 id="6-training-and-career-prospects">6. Training and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Training Program:</strong>&#160;Selected candidates undergo a structured training program at designated IAF training institutes, such as the Basic Training Institute (BTI), Belgaum, or other technical training centers. The training includes:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Basic Military Training:</strong>&#160;Focus on discipline, drill, and military ethos (typically 12-14 weeks).</li>
<li><strong>Technical Training:</strong>&#160;Specialized training in trades like Aircraft Fitter, Electronics Fitter, Mechanical System Fitter, etc., lasting several months based on the trade.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Enrollment</strong><strong>:</strong>&#160;Post-training, candidates are enrolled as Airmen in Group X (Technical) trades, starting at the rank of Leading Aircraftsman (LAC) with a specified pay scale under the 7th Pay Commission (basic pay around&#160;14,60014,600&#160;per month during training, increasing post-training with allowances like DA, TA, etc.).</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunities:</strong>&#160;Airmen in Group X trades work in critical areas such as aircraft maintenance, avionics, radar systems, weapon systems, and IT infrastructure. Career progression includes:</li>
<ul>
<li>Promotions to higher ranks like Corporal, Sergeant, Junior Warrant Officer, and Warrant Officer based on service and performance.</li>
<li>Opportunities to become commissioned officers through internal exams like the Air Force Cadet Entry Scheme or other routes.</li>
<li>Further technical certifications and skill development programs offered by the IAF.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Service Benefits:</strong>&#160;Includes job security, pension, medical facilities, housing, and other perks as per IAF policies.
</li>
</ul>

<li><strong>Improve Problem-Solving Skills:</strong>&#160;Practice logical reasoning and quick calculations to enhance performance in Mathematics and Physics sections.</li>
