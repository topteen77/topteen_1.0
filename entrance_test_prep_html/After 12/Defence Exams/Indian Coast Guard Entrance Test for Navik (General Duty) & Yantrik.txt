<h1 id="indian-coast-guard-entrance-test-for-navik-general-duty-amp-amp-yantrik-technica">Indian Coast Guard Entrance Test for Navik (General Duty) &amp;amp; Yantrik (Technical)</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Indian Coast Guard conducts entrance tests to recruit candidates for various roles essential to maritime security and operations. Two prominent entry schemes under this recruitment process are:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Navik</strong><strong> (General Duty):</strong>&#160;This stream targets candidates for general operational and administrative roles. Naviks are involved in safeguarding coastal areas, assisting in search and rescue operations, enforcing maritime laws, and maintaining security along India&#8217;s coastline.</li>
<li><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong> (Technical):</strong>&#160;This stream is designed for candidates with a technical background to serve in specialized roles such as engineering, maintenance, and technical support. Yantriks handle technical operations in areas like mechanical, electrical, and electronics systems, ensuring the functionality of Coast Guard equipment and vessels.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Key Objectives:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>For </strong><strong>Navik</strong><strong> (General Duty):</strong>&#160;To recruit and train candidates for operational duties and coastal security, emphasizing physical fitness, discipline, and general aptitude.</li>
<li><strong>For </strong><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong> (Technical):</strong>&#160;To recruit candidates with technical proficiency and provide advanced training in engineering and technology to support the Coast Guard&#8217;s technical operations.</li>
<li><strong>Common Goal:</strong>&#160;Both streams aim to build a dedicated, well-trained workforce to enhance India&#8217;s maritime safety, security, and environmental protection.</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="3-application-process">3. Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for both Navik (General Duty) and Yantrik (Technical) entries is conducted entirely online through the official Indian Coast Guard recruitment portal. Below are the key steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification Release:</strong>&#160;Official notifications are published on the Indian Coast Guard website () and advertised in major newspapers, employment news, and educational portals.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration:</strong>&#160;Candidates must register on the official portal by providing personal details, academic records, and contact information within the specified application window.</li>
<li><strong>Form Filling &amp;amp; Document Upload:</strong>&#160;Complete the online application form and upload scanned copies of required documents, such as:</li>
<ul>
<li>Class 10+2 mark sheet/certificate (for Navik GD).</li>
<li>Class 10 mark sheet and Diploma certificate (for Yantrik).</li>
<li>Proof of age (e.g., birth certificate or Class 10 certificate).</li>
<li>Recent passport-sized photographs.</li>
<li>Signature.</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable) and any other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee Payment:</strong>&#160;Pay the application fee online (typically around&#160;200200&#160;to&#160;250250, subject to notification). Exemptions are often available for SC/ST candidates. Payment methods include debit/credit cards or net banking.</li>
<li><strong>Submission &amp;amp; Confirmation:</strong>&#160;Submit the completed application before the deadline and save the confirmation receipt or application number for future reference.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download:</strong>&#160;Admit cards are issued on the official portal prior to the exam. Candidates must download and print the admit card to appear for the examination, along with a valid photo ID.</li>

</ul>

<h3 id="syllabus-overview">Syllabus Overview:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, mensuration, and basic statistics (Class 10 or 10+2 level for Navik GD; slightly advanced for Yantrik).</li>
<li><strong>Physics (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Mechanics, heat, light, sound, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics (Class 10+2 level for Navik GD; diploma-aligned for Yantrik).</li>
<li><strong>English (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, and error detection.</li>
<li><strong>Reasoning &amp;amp; General Awareness (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, coding-decoding, puzzles, current affairs, history, geography, civics, and defense-related topics.</li>
<li><strong>Basic Chemistry (</strong><strong>Navik</strong><strong> GD):</strong>&#160;Elementary concepts of matter, chemical reactions, and periodic table (Class 10+2 level).</li>
<li><strong>Trade-Specific Technical Subjects (</strong><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong> Only):</strong>&#160;Questions based on the candidate&#8217;s diploma branch (e.g., Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics &amp;amp; Telecommunication), covering core concepts and practical applications.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="additional-evaluations">Additional Evaluations:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Candidates who clear the written test undergo PFT, which includes:</li>
<ul>
<li>1.6 km run (to be completed within a specified time, usually under 7 minutes).</li>
<li>20 squats (Uthak Baithak).</li>
<li>10 push-ups.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;Conducted at designated centers to ensure compliance with Coast Guard medical standards (e.g., vision, hearing, dental health, and overall fitness).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents to confirm eligibility.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Unlike some other defense exams, there is typically no interview or group discussion for Navik GD or Yantrik recruitment as per the latest known process, though candidates should check the notification for any updates.</p>

<h2 id="5-selection-process">5. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for both Navik (General Duty) and Yantrik (Technical) is multi-staged to ensure only the most capable candidates are recruited into the Indian Coast Guard. Below are the stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stage I - Written Examination:</strong>&#160;Candidates are evaluated based on their performance in the written test (subjects vary by stream as mentioned above). Those who meet the cutoff are shortlisted for Stage II.</li>
<li><strong>Stage II - Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Shortlisted candidates undergo PFT to assess physical endurance and fitness as per Coast Guard standards.</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents (academic certificates, identity proofs, etc.) to confirm eligibility.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;A thorough medical check at designated centers to ensure candidates meet the required physical and health standards.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List:</strong>&#160;The final selection is based on performance in the written test (normalized scores across zones), PFT (qualifying in nature), and medical fitness. Selected candidates are issued a joining letter for enrollment in their respective streams.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The Coast Guard may adjust cutoffs or selection criteria based on vacancies, candidate performance, and zonal requirements.</p>

<h2 id="6-training-and-career-prospects">6. Training and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Training Program:</strong>&#160;Selected candidates undergo comprehensive training at the Indian Coast Guard Training Centre, typically at INS Chilka, Odisha, or other designated establishments. The training includes:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Basic Training (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Focus on discipline, drill, maritime knowledge, and military ethos (duration varies, often 14-24 weeks).</li>
<li><strong>Trade-Specific Training (</strong><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong>):</strong>&#160;Additional technical training for Yantrik candidates in their respective branches (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics), focusing on equipment maintenance and operations.
</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Enrollment</strong><strong>:</strong>&#160;Post-training, candidates are enrolled as Navik (General Duty) or Yantrik (Technical) at the entry-level rank with a specified pay scale under the 7th Pay Commission (basic pay around&#160;21,70021,700&#160;per month post-training, plus allowances like DA, sea duty allowance, etc.).
</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunities:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Navik</strong><strong> (General Duty):</strong>&#160;Roles include operational support, search and rescue, coastal surveillance, pollution control, and administrative duties on board ships or shore establishments. Career progression includes promotions to ranks like Uttam Navik, Pradhan Navik, and beyond based on service and performance.</li>
<li><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong> (Technical):</strong>&#160;Roles focus on maintenance and operation of technical equipment, engineering systems, electronics, and IT support for Coast Guard vessels and bases. Career progression includes promotions to ranks like Uttam Yantrik, Pradhan Yantrik, and beyond.
</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Long-Term Prospects:</strong>&#160;Both streams offer opportunities for further specialization, professional development (e.g., advanced technical courses for Yantrik), and potential to become commissioned officers through internal exams or schemes. Benefits include job security, pension, medical facilities, and housing as per Coast Guard policies.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="7-preparation-tips">7. Preparation Tips</h2>
<h3 id="academic-preparation">Academic Preparation:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Revise Fundamentals (Both Streams):</strong>&#160;Focus on core subjects like Mathematics, Physics (for Navik GD and Yantrik), English, and Reasoning &amp;amp; General Awareness using NCERT textbooks (Class 10/10+2) and standard guides like R.S. Aggarwal for reasoning or Lucent&#8217;s General Knowledge.</li>
<li><strong>Strengthen Technical Knowledge (</strong><strong>Yantrik</strong><strong> Only):</strong>&#160;Review diploma-level concepts in your specific branch (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics) using relevant textbooks and practical notes.</li>
<li><strong>Take Mock Tests:</strong>&#160;Solve previous years&#8217; Coast Guard papers and take online mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam pattern and improve time management.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="physical-preparation">Physical Preparation:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Maintain Regular Fitness:</strong>&#160;Engage in cardiovascular exercises (e.g., running to complete 1.6 km under 7 minutes), strength training (push-ups, squats), and endurance drills to meet PFT standards.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Physical Tasks:</strong>&#160;Simulate PFT requirements as per Coast Guard guidelines to build confidence and assess fitness levels.</li>

</ul>
