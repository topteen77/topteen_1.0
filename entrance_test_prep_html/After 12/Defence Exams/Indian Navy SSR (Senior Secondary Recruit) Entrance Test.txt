<h1 id="indian-navy-ssr-senior-secondary-recruit-entrance-test">Indian Navy SSR (Senior Secondary Recruit) Entrance Test</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Indian Navy SSR (Senior Secondary Recruit) Entrance Test is a competitive examination conducted by the Indian Navy to recruit young candidates as sailors in various operational and support roles below the officer cadre. The SSR scheme targets candidates who have completed Class 12 (10+2) with a science background and aims to induct them into roles that involve general duties on ships and shore establishments. This recruitment provides an opportunity to serve the nation while building a stable and rewarding career in the Navy.</p>

<p><strong>Key Objectives:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Recruitment:</strong>&#160;To select talented and eligible candidates based on academic ability, general aptitude, and physical fitness.</li>
<li><strong>Operational Support:</strong>&#160;To recruit personnel for roles such as Artificer Apprentice (AA) and general sailors, contributing to the Navy&#8217;s operational and technical needs.</li>
<li><strong>Career Pathway:</strong>&#160;To provide a structured training program that integrates military discipline, skill development, and physical conditioning, leading to a fulfilling career in the Indian Navy.</li>

</ul>

<h3 id="syllabus-overview">Syllabus Overview:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics:</strong>&#160;Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, mensuration, logarithms, set theory, and basic statistics (Class 10+2 level).</li>
<li><strong>Physics:</strong>&#160;Physical world and measurement, kinematics, laws of motion, work, energy, power, thermodynamics, optics, electricity, magnetism, and electronics (Class 10+2 level).</li>
<li><strong>English:</strong>&#160;Comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, error detection, and idioms.</li>
<li><strong>General Knowledge:</strong>&#160;Current affairs (national and international), history, geography, culture, sports, defense-related topics, abbreviations, and basic science.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The syllabus focuses on testing fundamental concepts relevant to Class 12 science subjects and general awareness. Detailed topics are provided in the official notification or on the Indian Navy recruitment portal.</p>

<h3 id="additional-evaluations">Additional Evaluations:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Candidates who qualify in the written test are called for PFT, which includes:</li>
<ul>
<li>1.6 km run (to be completed within 7 minutes).</li>
<li>20 squats (Uthak Baithak).</li>
<li>10 push-ups.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;Conducted at designated naval medical centers to ensure compliance with Navy medical standards (e.g., vision, hearing, dental health, and overall fitness).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents to confirm eligibility.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> There is typically no interview or group discussion for SSR recruitment as per the latest known process, though candidates should check the notification for any updates.</p>

<h2 id="5-selection-process">5. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for the Indian Navy SSR Entrance Test is multi-staged to ensure only the most capable candidates are recruited as sailors. Below are the stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Written Examination:</strong>&#160;Candidates are evaluated based on their performance in the online test covering Mathematics, Physics, English, and General Knowledge. Those who meet the cutoff are shortlisted for the next stage.</li>
<li><strong>Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Shortlisted candidates undergo PFT to assess physical endurance and fitness as per Navy standards. PFT is qualifying in nature.</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents (academic certificates, identity proofs, etc.) to confirm eligibility.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;A thorough medical check at designated centers to ensure candidates meet the required physical and health standards.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List:</strong>&#160;The final selection is based on performance in the written test (normalized scores across batches), PFT (qualifying), and medical fitness. Selected candidates are issued a joining letter for enrollment as SSR sailors.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The Navy may adjust cutoffs or selection criteria based on vacancies and candidate performance. Selection is often zone-wise to ensure regional representation.</p>

<h2 id="6-training-and-career-prospects">6. Training and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Training Program:</strong>&#160;Selected candidates undergo a structured training program at INS Chilka, Odisha, or other designated naval training establishments. The training includes:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Basic Training:</strong>&#160;Focus on naval orientation, discipline, drill, and physical conditioning (typically 22 weeks for SSR).</li>
<li><strong>Professional Training:</strong>&#160;Post-basic training, sailors are assigned to specific branches or trades and receive further on-the-job training.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Enrollment</strong><strong>:</strong>&#160;Post-training, candidates are enrolled as sailors in the SSR category at the entry-level rank of Seaman II with a specified pay scale under the 7th Pay Commission (basic pay around&#160;21,70021,700&#160;to&#160;69,10069,100&#160;per month post-training, plus allowances like DA, sea duty allowance, etc.).</li>
<li><strong>Role Assignment:</strong>&#160;SSR sailors are primarily assigned to general duties involving:</li>
<ul>
<li>Operation and maintenance of equipment on ships and submarines.</li>
<li>Support roles in navigation, communication, and weapon systems.</li>
<li>Other operational tasks on naval bases or vessels.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Career Growth:</strong>&#160;A career as an SSR sailor offers:</li>
<ul>
<li>Promotions to higher ranks like Seaman I, Leading Seaman, Petty Officer, and Chief Petty Officer based on service, performance, and exams.</li>
<li>Opportunities to become commissioned officers through internal schemes like the Commission Worthy (CW) scheme or other routes.</li>
<li>Further specialization through advanced courses and skill development programs.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Benefits:</strong>&#160;Includes job security, pension, medical facilities for self and family, housing, canteen facilities, and other perks as per Navy policies.</li>

</ul>
