<h1 id="indian-air-force-group-y-non-technical-entrance-test">Indian Air Force Group Y (Non-Technical) Entrance Test</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Indian Air Force (IAF) Group Y (Non-Technical) Entrance Test is a competitive recruitment process designed to select young candidates for various non-technical trades as Airmen in the IAF. Unlike the Group X (Technical) trades, which require a background in science and mathematics, Group Y trades are generally open to candidates from any stream, focusing on roles that support administrative, logistical, and operational functions. Successful candidates are trained in specific non-technical disciplines and inducted into trades such as Administration Assistant, Accounts Assistant, Logistics Assistant, and others, contributing to the smooth functioning of the Air Force.</p>

<p><strong>Key Objectives:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Talent Identification:</strong>&#160;To recruit candidates with strong academic credentials, communication skills, and aptitude for administrative and support roles in the IAF.</li>
<li><strong>Holistic Development:</strong>&#160;To assess both intellectual and physical capabilities, ensuring candidates are well-suited for a career in the Air Force.</li>
<li><strong>Career Pathway:</strong>&#160;To provide a rewarding career in non-technical trades, offering job security, professional growth, and the prestige of serving in one of India&#8217;s premier defence forces.
</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="3-application-process">3. Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for the IAF Group Y (Non-Technical) Entrance Test is conducted entirely online through the official IAF recruitment portal. Below are the key steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification Release:</strong>&#160;Official notifications are published on the IAF recruitment website () and advertised in major newspapers and employment news.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration:</strong>&#160;Candidates must register on the official portal by providing accurate personal details, academic records, and contact information within the specified application window.</li>
<li><strong>Form Filling &amp;amp; Document Upload:</strong>&#160;Complete the online application form and upload scanned copies of required documents, including:</li>

<li><strong>Application Fee Payment:</strong>&#160;Pay the application fee online (typically around&#160;100100&#160;to&#160;250250, subject to the notification). Exemptions are often available for SC/ST candidates. Payment methods include debit/credit cards or net banking.</li>

<h3 id="syllabus-overview">Syllabus Overview:</h3>

<ul>

<li><strong>Reasoning and General Awareness (RAGA):</strong></li>

</ul>

<p><strong>Note:</strong> For specific trades like Medical Assistant (if under Group Y), additional subjects like Physics, Chemistry, and Biology may be included. Detailed topics are provided in the official notification or on the IAF recruitment portal.</p>

<h3 id="additional-evaluations">Additional Evaluations:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phase II - Physical Fitness Test (PFT):</strong>&#160;Candidates who qualify in the written test are called for PFT at Airmen Selection Centres (ASC). The test includes:</li>
<ul>
<li>1.6 km run (to be completed within a specified time, usually under 6.5 minutes).</li>
<li>Push-ups, sit-ups, and squats (specific numbers as per IAF standards).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Adaptability Test-1 (AT-1):</strong>&#160;A written test to assess suitability for military life through situational response questions.</li>
<li><strong>Adaptability Test-2 (AT-2):</strong>&#160;Group discussion to evaluate communication skills, leadership, and teamwork.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;Conducted at designated Air Force medical centers to ensure compliance with IAF medical standards.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="5-selection-process">5. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for IAF Group Y (Non-Technical) is multi-staged to ensure only the most capable candidates are recruited as Airmen. Below are the stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phase I - Online Written Test:</strong>&#160;Candidates are evaluated based on their performance in English and Reasoning &amp;amp; General Awareness (RAGA). Those who meet the cutoff are shortlisted for Phase II.</li>
<li><strong>Phase II - Physical Fitness Test (PFT) &amp;amp; Adaptability Tests:</strong>&#160;Shortlisted candidates undergo PFT and Adaptability Tests (AT-1 and AT-2) at designated Airmen Selection Centres to assess physical endurance and suitability for military life.</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of all submitted documents (academic certificates, identity proofs, etc.) to confirm eligibility.</li>
<li><strong>Medical Examination:</strong>&#160;A thorough medical check at Air Force medical centers to ensure candidates meet the required physical and health standards.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List:</strong>&#160;The final selection is based on combined performance in the written test, PFT, Adaptability Tests, and medical fitness. Selected candidates are issued a joining letter for enrollment as Airmen in Group Y (Non-Technical) trades.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The IAF reserves the right to adjust cutoffs or selection criteria based on vacancies and candidate performance.</p>

<h2 id="6-training-and-career-prospects">6. Training and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Training Program:</strong>&#160;Selected candidates undergo a structured training program at designated IAF training institutes, such as the Basic Training Institute (BTI), Belgaum, or other centers. The training includes:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Basic Military Training:</strong>&#160;Focus on discipline, drill, and military ethos (typically 12-14 weeks).</li>
<li><strong>Trade-Specific Training:</strong>&#160;Specialized training in non-technical trades like Administration Assistant, Accounts Assistant, Logistics Assistant, Operations Assistant, or Medical Assistant (if applicable), lasting several weeks based on the trade.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Enrollment</strong><strong>:</strong>&#160;Post-training, candidates are enrolled as Airmen in Group Y (Non-Technical) trades, starting at the rank of Leading Aircraftsman (LAC) with a specified pay scale under the 7th Pay Commission (basic pay around&#160;14,60014,600&#160;per month during training, increasing post-training with allowances like DA, TA, etc.).</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunities:</strong>&#160;Airmen in Group Y trades work in essential support roles such as:</li>
<ul>
<li>Administration: Clerical duties, record-keeping, and office management.</li>
<li>Logistics: Supply chain management and inventory control.</li>
<li>Accounts: Financial record maintenance and auditing.</li>
<li>Operations Assistant: Support in air traffic control or operational planning.</li>
<li>Medical Assistant (if applicable): Basic medical support and patient care. Career progression includes:</li>
<li>Promotions to higher ranks like Corporal, Sergeant, Junior Warrant Officer, and Warrant Officer based on service and performance.</li>
<li>Opportunities to become commissioned officers through internal exams like the Air Force Cadet Entry Scheme or other routes.</li>
<li>Further skill development programs offered by the IAF.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Service Benefits:</strong>&#160;Includes job security, pension, medical facilities, housing, and other perks as per IAF policies.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="7-preparation-tips">7. Preparation Tips</h2>
<h3 id="academic-preparation">Academic Preparation:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Revise Core Subjects:</strong>&#160;Focus on strengthening fundamentals in English and Reasoning &amp;amp; General Awareness using standard textbooks and resources. For English, refer to books like Wren &amp;amp; Martin; for RAGA, use R.S. Aggarwal for reasoning and Lucent&#8217;s General Knowledge.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated on Current Affairs:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers, magazines, and online portals to stay informed about national and international events, defense news, and general trivia.</li>
<li><strong>Take Mock Tests:</strong>&#160;Regularly solve previous years&#8217; IAF Group Y papers and take online mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam pattern and improve time management.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="physical-preparation">Physical Preparation:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Maintain Fitness:</strong>&#160;Incorporate regular exercises like running, push-ups, sit-ups, and squats to meet PFT standards. Aim to complete a 1.6 km run in under 6 minutes.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Physical Tasks:</strong>&#160;Simulate PFT requirements as per IAF guidelines to build endurance and confidence.</li>

</ul>
