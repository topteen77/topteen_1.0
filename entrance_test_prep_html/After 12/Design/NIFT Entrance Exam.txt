<h1 id="nift-entrance-exam">NIFT Entrance Exam</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) conducts a prestigious national-level entrance exam for admission into its undergraduate and postgraduate programs in fashion and design. Established in 1986 under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, NIFT began with a single fashion design program and has grown significantly over the years. Today, it operates across 18 campuses in India, with the headquarters located in New Delhi. Other prominent campuses are situated in Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Kolkata, among others. NIFT is renowned for its cutting-edge curriculum, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and strong industry connections, making it a top choice for aspiring designers and fashion technologists.</p>
<p>In 2006, NIFT was granted statutory status, empowering it to confer degrees and other academic distinctions. The NIFT Entrance Exam serves as the exclusive gateway for admission to a variety of design, fashion technology, and management courses offered by the institute. This exam attracts thousands of candidates each year who dream of building a career in the dynamic and creative world of fashion and design.</p>

<h2 id="nift-exam-pattern">NIFT Exam Pattern</h2>
<p>The NIFT Entrance Exam is conducted in two phases to assess both creative and analytical abilities of candidates. The phases are detailed below:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phase 1: Written Test (Paper-Based Test - PBT)</strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>B.Des</strong><strong> &amp;amp; </strong><strong>M.Des</strong>: Creative Ability Test (CAT) + General Ability Test (GAT)</li>
<li><strong>B.F.Tech</strong><strong>, MFM, </strong><strong>M.F.Tech</strong>: General Ability Test (GAT) only</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Phase 2: Further Evaluations</strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>B.Des</strong><strong> &amp;amp; </strong><strong>M.Des</strong>: Situation Test (for B.Des) or Group Discussion (GD) &amp;amp; Personal Interview (PI) (for M.Des)</li>
<li><strong>B.F.Tech</strong><strong>, MFM, </strong><strong>M.F.Tech</strong>: Group Discussion &amp;amp; Personal Interview (GD/PI)</li>

</ul>
</ul>

<li>Fill in personal, academic, and contact details in the application form.</li>
<li>Upload necessary documents, including a recent passport-sized photograph, signature, and category certificate (if applicable).</li>
