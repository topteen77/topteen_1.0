<h1 id="design-aptitude-test-decode">Design Aptitude Test (DECODE)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The Design Aptitude Test (DECODE) is a national-level entrance examination conducted by the Centre for Design Excellence (CODE) at Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur, Rajasthan. This test is specifically designed to assess the aptitude, creativity, and design sensitivity of candidates aspiring to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate programs in design. DECODE evaluates a candidate&#8217;s logical reasoning, problem-solving abilities, visual perception, and awareness of current trends in the design industry, making it a crucial step for admission to one of India&#8217;s emerging design education hubs.</p>
<p>CODE at VGU is recognized for its innovative curriculum, modern infrastructure, and focus on fostering both technical and creative skills in students. The institute aims to nurture aspiring designers by providing a blend of theoretical knowledge and practical exposure, preparing them for dynamic careers in the design field. DECODE serves as a gateway for students to join this transformative environment, identifying candidates with the potential to excel in various design disciplines.</p>

<h2 id="selection-process">Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for DECODE is multi-staged to evaluate candidates comprehensively. The key stages are as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>DECODE Exam Score</strong>: Performance in the General Ability Test (GAT) and Creative Ability Test (CAT), which together constitute 200 marks.</li>
<li><strong>Interview (Portfolio + Studio Test)</strong>: Shortlisted candidates participate in a personal interaction round, where they present a portfolio of their creative works and engage in a studio test to demonstrate hands-on design skills.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: The final merit list is prepared based on the combined performance in the DECODE exam and the interview round. Selected candidates receive admission offers and must complete document verification and fee payment to confirm their seats.</li>
</ul>
<p>This process ensures that only candidates with a strong blend of analytical skills, creativity, and practical design abilities are admitted to the programs at CODE, VGU.</p>

<h2 id="preparation-tips">Preparation Tips</h2>
<p>To excel in the DECODE exam and subsequent selection rounds, candidates should adopt a strategic preparation approach. Below are some key tips to help applicants succeed:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Understand the Syllabus</strong>: Familiarize yourself with the topics covered in GAT and CAT, focusing on quantitative ability, logical reasoning, and creative skills.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Sketching</strong>: Enhance your drawing and visualization skills through regular practice of perspective drawing, shading, and composition.</li>
<li><strong>Develop a Portfolio</strong>: Compile a diverse portfolio showcasing your creative works, design projects, and sketches for the interview round.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated</strong>: Keep abreast of current affairs, design trends, and industry updates to perform well in the General Knowledge section.</li>
<li><strong>Mock Tests</strong>: Attempt sample papers and mock tests to improve time management, understand the exam pattern, and identify areas for improvement.</li>
<li><strong>Enhance Communication Skills</strong>: Work on vocabulary, sentence structure, and comprehension to excel in the Communication Ability and English Comprehension sections.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Design Thinking</strong>: Practice ideation and problem-solving exercises to build confidence for the Creative Ability Test.</li>
</ul>
<p>These tips can help candidates build confidence and perform effectively across all stages of the DECODE selection process.</p>
