<h2 id="overview-of-swami-vivekananda-merit-cum-means-scholarship">Overview of Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship</h2>
<p>The Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship is a transformative initiative by the Government of West Bengal aimed at fostering academic excellence and ensuring equitable access to education for meritorious students facing financial challenges. Key highlights include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Key Objectives</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Encourage Academic Excellence</strong>: Reward high-performing students from West Bengal based on their academic achievements in Class 10, Class 12, or higher education levels.</li>
<li><strong>Financial Assistance</strong>: Provide monetary support to alleviate the burden of tuition fees, books, and other educational expenses.</li>
<li><strong>Promote Equal Opportunities</strong>: Ensure that deserving students, regardless of their economic background, have access to quality education and career opportunities.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Target Focus</strong>: Primarily aimed at students from economically weaker sections in West Bengal, covering various stages of education from higher secondary to postgraduate levels.</li>
<li><strong>Administered By</strong>: The scholarship is managed by the Higher Education Department, Government of West Bengal, through designated portals and administrative bodies.</li>
<li><strong>Philosophy</strong>: Inspired by Swami Vivekananda&#8217;s vision of education as a tool for empowerment, the program seeks to build a brighter future for students by removing financial barriers and nurturing talent.</li>
</ul>
<p>This scholarship stands as a cornerstone of West Bengal&#8217;s commitment to education and social equity, supporting the state&#8217;s brightest minds.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>

<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong>: The final list of scholarship awardees is compiled based on a combination of academic merit and financial need, prioritized by category (e.g., general, reserved) and level of study, and published on the official portal.</li>
