<h1 id="cbse-merit-scholarship-for-single-girl-child">CBSE Merit Scholarship for Single Girl Child</h1>

<h2 id="overview-of-cbse-merit-scholarship-for-single-girl-child">Overview of CBSE Merit Scholarship for Single Girl Child</h2>
<p>The CBSE Merit Scholarship for Single Girl Child is a targeted program designed to foster academic growth and ensure access to education for single girl children studying in CBSE-affiliated schools. Key highlights include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Key Objectives</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Promote Education</strong>: Encourage the pursuit of higher secondary education among single girl children, fostering gender equity and empowerment through learning.</li>
<li><strong>Financial Support</strong>: Provide monetary assistance to cover tuition fees, books, and other educational expenses for Classes 11 and 12, reducing financial stress on families.</li>
<li><strong>Reward Merit</strong>: Recognize and reward academic excellence among single girl children who have performed well in their Class 10 examinations from CBSE-affiliated schools.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Target Focus</strong>: Specifically aimed at single girl children enrolled in Classes 11 and 12 in CBSE-affiliated schools across India, ensuring continued education post-Class 10.</li>
<li><strong>Administered By</strong>: The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), a national-level board of education in India, under the Ministry of Education, Government of India.</li>
<li><strong>Philosophy</strong>: To support the education of single girl children as a means of empowering young women, contributing to societal development by ensuring equal opportunities for academic and personal growth.</li>
</ul>
<p>This scholarship stands as a vital initiative to promote girl child education and address financial barriers in higher secondary education.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>The CBSE Merit Scholarship for Single Girl Child targets a specific group of students with defined criteria to ensure benefits reach deserving candidates. Below are the detailed eligibility requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Target Group</strong>: Single girl children who have passed Class 10 from CBSE-affiliated schools and are continuing their education in Classes 11 and 12 in the same or another CBSE-affiliated school.</li>
<li><strong>Detailed Criteria</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Domicile/Category</strong>: Must be a single girl child, meaning the only girl child of her parents (no siblings or only male siblings), verified through an affidavit or certificate as per CBSE guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Educational Qualification</strong>: Must have passed the Class 10 examination from a CBSE-affiliated school (public or private) with a strong academic record.</li>
<li><strong>Academic Performance</strong>: Must meet the prescribed minimum marks or percentage in the Class 10 CBSE Board Examination (e.g., typically 60% or above in aggregate, subject to updates), as specified in the official scholarship notification.</li>
<li><strong>Continuing Education</strong>: Must be enrolled in Class 11 in a CBSE-affiliated school at the time of application, with the scholarship extending to Class 12 upon renewal and satisfactory performance.</li>
<li><strong>Other Requirements</strong>: Must be an Indian citizen. Additional conditions, such as not availing of any other scholarship or financial assistance for the same course (exceptions may apply), are outlined in the guidelines.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Additional Note</strong>: Detailed and updated eligibility criteria, including specific percentage cut-offs, documentation requirements, and any relaxations or special provisions, are provided in the latest official notification on the CBSE website:&#160;.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="selection-process">Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for the CBSE Merit Scholarship for Single Girl Child is primarily merit-based, focusing on academic performance in Class 10 and verification of eligibility criteria. Below are the typical stages:</p>
<h3 id="stages-of-selection">Stages of Selection:</h3>
<ul>
<ul>
<li><strong>Academic Evaluation</strong>: Review of the candidate&#8217;s Class 10 academic performance in the CBSE Board Examination against the prescribed minimum marks or percentage criteria (e.g., 60% or above, subject to updates).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification</strong>: Thorough verification of all submitted documents, including Class 10 mark sheets, proof of single girl child status (affidavit/certificate), and other required credentials to confirm eligibility and authenticity.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong>: Preparation of a merit list based on academic performance and fulfillment of all eligibility criteria, prioritizing candidates with higher marks in case of limited slots (number of scholarships may be capped annually).</li>
<li><strong>Award Disbursement</strong>: Scholarship benefits are disbursed to selected candidates as per the schedule mentioned in the official notification, typically via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the student&#8217;s bank account.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<ul>
<li><strong>Outcome</strong>: Selected candidates are notified through the CBSE website, email, or school communication, with instructions for scholarship receipt and renewal processes for Class 12.</li>
<li><strong>Additional Note</strong>: The exact selection methodology, including cut-off marks, number of scholarships awarded, or tie-breaking criteria, may vary based on annual updates or fund availability. Candidates should refer to the latest official guidelines for specifics. There is no separate entrance exam for this scholarship.</li>
</ul>
