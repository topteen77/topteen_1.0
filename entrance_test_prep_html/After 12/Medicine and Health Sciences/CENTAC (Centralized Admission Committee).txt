<h1 id="centac-centralized-admission-committee">CENTAC (Centralized Admission Committee) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Centralized Admission Committee (CENTAC) is a state-level admission process administered by the Government of Puducherry. It facilitates admission to various undergraduate programs offered by 17 collaborating institutions in the Puducherry Union Territory. Unlike many entrance exams, CENTAC does not rely on a separate entrance test for UG courses; instead, admissions are based on academic performance in the 10+2 examination for Puducherry candidates and on JEE Main scores for candidates from other states. CENTAC covers a wide range of professional courses, including medical, engineering, pharmacy, and allied health programs, providing a streamlined single-window system for aspiring students.</p>
<p>CENTAC plays a crucial role in ensuring merit-based and transparent admissions to professional courses in Puducherry, catering to both local and out-of-state candidates. For the latest updates and detailed guidelines, please visit the official website: .</p>

<h2 id="centac-admission-process-no-entrance-exam-for-ug-courses">CENTAC Admission Process (No Entrance Exam for UG Courses)</h2>
<p>Since admission to UG courses under CENTAC is based on academic performance rather than a separate entrance exam, the process is as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>For </strong><strong>Puducherry</strong><strong> Candidates</strong>: Admissions are based on the candidate&#8217;s 10+2 (or equivalent) scores.</li>
<li><strong>For Other State Candidates</strong>: Admissions to specific courses like B.Tech are based on JEE Main scores.</li>
</ul>
<p>The process involves merit list preparation based on the submitted scores, followed by counselling and seat allotment to the participating institutions.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-centac">How to Apply for CENTAC</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website&#160;&#160;and complete the online registration form with your personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, such as a recent photograph, signature, 10+2 mark sheets, domicile certificate (if applicable), and any additional certificates.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using the secure online payment gateway:</li>
<ul>
<li>General Category: INR 1000</li>
<li>Scheduled Caste Category: INR 500</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all details carefully and submit the application before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admission Process</strong>: For Puducherry candidates, admissions are based solely on 10+2 scores, while other state candidates must provide their JEE Main scores for applicable courses. Follow the subsequent steps for document verification, counselling, and seat allotment.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="why-choose-centac">Why Choose CENTAC?</h2>
<p>Participating in the CENTAC admission process offers numerous advantages for aspiring students:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Streamlined Admissions</strong>: A single-window process for multiple undergraduate programs across 17 institutions in Puducherry.</li>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Selection</strong>: Admissions are based on 10+2 or JEE Main scores, ensuring a fair and transparent process.</li>
<li><strong>Diverse Program Options</strong>: Offers a range of courses in medicine, engineering, pharmacy, and allied fields.</li>
<li><strong>Reservation Policies</strong>: Provides reserved seats as per state guidelines, ensuring opportunities for disadvantaged groups.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="admission-preparation-and-strategies">Admission Preparation and Strategies</h2>
<h3 id="for-puducherry-candidates">For Puducherry Candidates:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Academic Performance</strong>: Focus on achieving high scores in the 10+2 examination, as admissions are solely based on these results.</li>
<li><strong>Document Readiness</strong>: Ensure all required documents, including mark sheets and domicile certificates, are prepared and uploaded correctly during the application process.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-other-state-candidates-for-courses-like-b-tech">For Other State Candidates (for courses like B.Tech):</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>JEE Main Preparation</strong>: Develop a structured study plan covering Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics based on the JEE Main syllabus.</li>
<li><strong>Practice MCQs</strong>: Regularly solve mock tests and previous years&#8217; JEE Main papers to improve speed and accuracy.</li>
<li><strong>Time Management</strong>: Practice managing time effectively during preparation and mock exams to perform well under pressure.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="additional-tips">Additional Tips:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Stay updated with any changes in the admission process or eligibility criteria through the official CENTAC website.</li>
<li>Ensure timely submission of applications and fees to avoid last-minute issues.</li>
<li>Maintain a checklist of required documents and deadlines to streamline the application and counselling process.</li>
</ul>
