<h1 id="uttarakhand-ayurveda-pre-medical-test-uapmt">Uttarakhand Ayurveda Pre Medical Test (UAPMT) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Uttarakhand Ayurveda Pre Medical Test (UAPMT), formerly known as the Uttarakhand Ayurveda Pre Medical Test (UAMPT), is a state-level entrance exam conducted by Uttarakhand Ayurvedic University, Dehradun. This exam is designed to select candidates for admission into undergraduate Ayurvedic, Homeopathic, and Unani medical programs&#8212;namely BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery), BHMS (Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery), and BUMS (Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery)&#8212;across the state of Uttarakhand. The test aims to evaluate applicants on their knowledge of core science subjects, ensuring that only qualified candidates who meet the academic and aptitude standards are admitted to these prestigious institutions.</p>
<p>UAPMT serves as a vital gateway for students aspiring to build careers in traditional and complementary systems of medicine, focusing on holistic healthcare practices. For the latest updates and detailed guidelines, please visit the official website:  or contact the helpline at +91-7895305763.</p>

<h2 id="courses-offered-through-uapmt">Courses Offered Through UAPMT</h2>
<p>Candidates who qualify UAPMT can secure admission into the following undergraduate programs offered by Uttarakhand Ayurvedic University and its affiliated colleges:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>BAMS (Bachelor of </strong><strong>Ayurvedic</strong><strong> Medicine and Surgery)</strong>: Training in traditional Ayurvedic medicine and holistic healing practices.</li>
<li><strong>BHMS (Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery)</strong>: Education in homeopathic treatment methods and principles.</li>
<li><strong>BUMS (Bachelor of </strong><strong>Unani</strong><strong> Medicine and Surgery)</strong>: Focus on the Unani system of medicine, emphasizing natural healing techniques.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-uapmt">How to Apply for UAPMT</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Registration</strong>: Visit the official website&#160;&#160;and navigate to the UAPMT registration portal. Complete the online registration form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>: Upload scanned copies of required documents, including a recent photograph, signature, 12th mark sheets, domicile certificate (if applicable), and any additional certificates (e.g., language proficiency certificates).</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee using the secure online payment gateway (options typically include debit/credit cards, net banking, or UPI).</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review all provided details carefully and submit the application form before the deadline.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: Once released, download and print your admit card from the official website. This card is mandatory for appearing in the exam.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="why-choose-uapmt">Why Choose UAPMT?</h2>
<p>Participating in UAPMT offers numerous advantages for aspiring practitioners of traditional medicine:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Specialized Education</strong>: Gain admission to reputed undergraduate programs in Ayurvedic, Homeopathic, and Unani medicine, which emphasize both traditional and modern medical practices.</li>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Selection</strong>: Ensures a transparent, competitive, and fair admission process, rewarding candidates based on performance.</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunities</strong>: Opens avenues for a successful career in healthcare, research, and allied medical fields, with opportunities to serve in government and private sectors.</li>
<li><strong>State-Level Advantage</strong>: Being a state-level exam, UAPMT offers a focused approach to the medical education system in Uttarakhand, benefiting local candidates.</li>
</ul>
