<h1 id="kerala-engineering-architecture-medical-keam-examination">Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical (KEAM) Examination</h1>

<h2 id="about-keam">About KEAM</h2>
<p>The <strong>Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical (KEAM) Examination</strong> is a state-level entrance test conducted by the Commissioner of Entrance Examinations (CEE), Government of Kerala. It serves as a gateway for admissions to engineering, architecture, and pharmacy programs in various government, aided, and private colleges across Kerala. Originally, KEAM also facilitated medical admissions, but since 2016, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) has become the sole qualifying exam for medical and allied health sciences courses in the state.</p>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for KEAM is conducted online, generally beginning in the month of <strong>April</strong>. The steps are as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Visit the official website () and register using personal details.</li>
<li><strong>Filling the Application Form</strong>: Enter academic details, course preferences, and other required information.</li>
<li><strong>Fee Payment</strong>: Pay the application fee online via net banking, credit/debit card, or offline via e-challan at designated banks.</li>
<li><strong>Uploading Documents</strong>: Upload scanned copies of a recent photograph, signature, and relevant certificates (e.g., category, domicile).</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Review the form and submit it. Download the confirmation page for future reference.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>The admit card for KEAM is released online on the official website approximately two to three weeks before the exam, typically in the month of&#160;<strong>May</strong>.</li>
<li>Candidates must download it using their application number and password and carry a printed copy to the exam center along with a valid photo ID.</li>
<li>The admit card contains essential details such as the candidate&#8217;s name, roll number, exam center, and exam timings.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="result-and-rank-list">Result and Rank List</h2>
<ul>
<li>KEAM results are typically declared in the month of&#160;<strong>June</strong>&#160;on the official website.</li>
<li>Candidates can check their scores using their application number and password.</li>
<li>The rank list is prepared by combining 50% weightage from KEAM scores and 50% weightage from 12th board exam marks (standardized as per CEE norms).</li>
<li>Separate rank lists are published for Engineering, Architecture, and Pharmacy courses.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="counselling-and-seat-allotment">Counselling and Seat Allotment</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Counselling Process</strong>: The Centralized Allotment Process (CAP) for KEAM is conducted online, generally in the months of&#160;<strong>July and August</strong>, to allocate seats in various colleges across Kerala.</li>
<li>Candidates are called for counselling based on their rank in the merit list.</li>
<li>During counselling, candidates must choose their preferred course and college based on availability.</li>
<li>Seat allotment is finalized after document verification and fee payment.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="preparation-tips">Preparation Tips</h2>
<p>To excel in KEAM, candidates should adopt a strategic approach to preparation:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Understand the Syllabus</strong>: Thoroughly review the syllabus and prioritize high-weightage topics in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.</li>
<li><strong>Be Mindful of Negative Marking</strong>: Practice accuracy as incorrect answers deduct marks; avoid guesswork.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Mock Tests</strong>: Solve past years&#8217; question papers and mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam pattern and time constraints.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Fundamentals</strong>: Strengthen core concepts using NCERT textbooks for Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.</li>
<li><strong>Time Management</strong>: Practice solving questions within the stipulated time to improve speed and accuracy.</li>
<li><strong>Revise Regularly</strong>: Allocate time for periodic revision of formulas, concepts, and problem-solving techniques.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated</strong>: Keep track of official notifications on the CEE website for any changes in exam pattern or eligibility criteria.</li>
</ul>
