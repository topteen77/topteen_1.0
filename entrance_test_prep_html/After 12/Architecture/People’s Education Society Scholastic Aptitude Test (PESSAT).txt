<h1 id="people-8217-s-education-society-scholastic-aptitude-test-pessat-for-b-arch">People&#8217;s Education Society Scholastic Aptitude Test (PESSAT) for B.Arch</h1>

<h2 id="about-pessat">About PESSAT</h2>
<p>The People&#8217;s Education Society Scholastic Aptitude Test (PESSAT) is a university-level entrance exam conducted annually by PES University, Bangalore, for admissions into various Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) programs. For candidates aspiring to pursue a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) at PES University, PESSAT does not involve a separate entrance test; instead, admission is based on qualifying scores from the National Aptitude Test in Architecture (NATA) or an equivalent recognized architecture entrance exam, submitted through the PESSAT application process. PESSAT serves as a gateway for streamlining applications and admissions into the B.Arch program offered by the university, which is recognized by the Council of Architecture (CoA).</p>

<h2 id="application-process-for-b-arch">Application Process for B.Arch</h2>
<p>The application process for PESSAT B.Arch admissions is conducted online, generally beginning in the tentative month of January. The steps are as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li>Visit the Official Website: Access the PES University admissions portal at&#160;&#160;to start the application process.</li>
<li>Register Online: Provide personal and academic details such as name, email ID, and mobile number to create a login ID and password.</li>
<li>Select the B.Arch Program: Fill in the application form by selecting B.Arch as the preferred course and entering relevant details.</li>
<li>Upload Required Documents: Upload scanned copies of the following in the prescribed format:</li>
<ul>
<li>Class 10 and 12 mark sheets or certificates.</li>
<li>NATA scorecard or equivalent architecture entrance exam result.</li>
<li>Recent passport-sized photograph.</li>
</ul>
<li>Pay the Application Fee: Complete the payment online using a debit card, credit card, or net banking. The fee amount is subject to university guidelines and may vary for different categories.</li>
<li>Submit the Form and Download Confirmation: After successful submission, download and print the confirmation page for future reference.</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="admit-card-not-applicable-for-b-arch">Admit Card (Not Applicable for B.Arch)</h2>
<p>Since PES University does not conduct a separate PESSAT entrance test for B.Arch admissions, no admit card is issued for this program. Candidates applying for B.Arch must focus on obtaining and submitting their NATA scorecard or equivalent architecture entrance exam result through the PESSAT application portal. For other programs like B.Tech or B.Des, admit cards are released online on the official website approximately two weeks before the exam for candidates required to appear for the PESSAT test.</p>

<h2 id="result-and-merit-list-for-b-arch">Result and Merit List for B.Arch</h2>
<p>Since there is no separate PESSAT entrance test for B.Arch, results are not declared in the form of test scores for this program. Instead, PES University prepares a merit list for B.Arch admissions based on the following criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li>NATA Scores: Primary weightage is given to the candidate&#8217;s performance in NATA or an equivalent architecture entrance exam.</li>
<li>Academic Performance: Marks obtained in 10+2 (or equivalent) examinations are also considered for final merit ranking.</li>
</ul>
<p>The merit list is typically published on the official PES University website in the tentative month of June or July, after the application window closes. Candidates can check their status using their application number and login credentials.</p>

<h2 id="counselling-and-seat-allotment-for-b-arch">Counselling and Seat Allotment for B.Arch</h2>
<p>Counselling for PESSAT B.Arch admissions is conducted by PES University, generally in the tentative month of July, after the merit list is published. Shortlisted candidates are invited for counselling based on their rank in the merit list. The process includes the following stages:</p>
<ul>
<li>Document Verification: Candidates must present original documents and certificates, including NATA scorecards, to confirm eligibility.</li>
<li>Seat Allotment: Seats are allocated based on the merit list (combining NATA scores and 10+2 marks) and availability in the B.Arch program.</li>
<li>Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation: Pay the required admission fees to secure the allotted seat and confirm enrolment.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="preparation-strategy-for-b-arch-admissions-nata-based">Preparation Strategy for B.Arch Admissions (NATA-Based)</h2>
<p>Since PESSAT does not involve a separate entrance test for B.Arch, candidates should focus on excelling in NATA or an equivalent architecture entrance exam, as well as maintaining strong academic performance in 10+2. Here are some strategic preparation tips for NATA, which is critical for B.Arch admissions at PES University:</p>
<ul>
<li>Understand the NATA Syllabus: Familiarize yourself with the NATA syllabus, focusing on drawing, visual composition, architectural awareness, and general aptitude.</li>
<li>Practice Drawing Skills: Regularly practice sketching, perspective drawing, and rendering to improve creativity and precision, which are heavily tested in NATA.</li>
<li>Strengthen Fundamentals: Revise basic concepts of Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry from 11th and 12th grade, as these may contribute to the aptitude section of NATA and academic merit for PES University.</li>
<li>Take Mock Tests: Attempt NATA mock tests and sample papers to get accustomed to the exam pattern and time management.</li>
<li>Study Architectural Awareness: Learn about famous architects, iconic buildings, materials, and architectural history to score well in the awareness section of NATA.</li>
<li>Maintain Academic Performance: Aim for high marks in 10+2 examinations, as they contribute to the final merit list for B.Arch admissions at PES University.</li>
<li>Stay Updated: Regularly check the PES University and NATA websites for updates on eligibility criteria, exam patterns, or changes in admission policies.</li>
</ul>
