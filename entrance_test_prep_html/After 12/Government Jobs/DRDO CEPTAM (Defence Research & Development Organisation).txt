<h1 id="drdo-ceptam-defence-research-amp-amp-development-organisation-recruitment">DRDO CEPTAM (Defence Research &amp;amp; Development Organisation) Recruitment</h1>
<h2 id="1-exam-overview">1. Exam Overview</h2>
<p>The DRDO CEPTAM (Centre for Personnel Talent Management) Entrance Test is conducted by the Defence Research &amp;amp; Development Organisation (DRDO) to recruit candidates for various technical and non-technical Group B and Group C posts in its laboratories and establishments across India. This national-level recruitment targets skilled personnel to support DRDO&#8217;s mission of developing cutting-edge defence technologies and systems.</p>

<p><strong>Key Details:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Exam Name:</strong>&#160;DRDO CEPTAM Entrance Test</li>
<li><strong>Conducting Authority:</strong>&#160;Defence Research &amp;amp; Development Organisation (DRDO) through CEPTAM</li>
<li><strong>Purpose:</strong>&#160;Recruitment for various Group B and Group C posts (technical, administrative, and allied categories) in DRDO labs and units</li>
<li><strong>Level of Exam:</strong>&#160;National Level</li>
<li><strong>Mode of Application:</strong>&#160;Online through the official DRDO recruitment portal</li>
<li><strong>Mode of Exam:</strong>&#160;Primarily Computer-Based Test (CBT); may include Skill/Practical Test or Trade Test for certain posts</li>
<li><strong>Posts Offered:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Technical Posts:</strong>&#160;Technician-A (Tech-A) for trades like Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Machinist (requires ITI certification); Senior Technical Assistant-B (STA-B) for diploma/degree holders in engineering or science streams.</li>
<li><strong>Non-Technical Posts:</strong>&#160;Administrative Assistant, Store Assistant, Security Assistant, Clerk, and other support staff roles.</li>
<li><strong>Allied Posts:</strong>&#160;Vehicle Operator, Fireman, Draughtsman, and other specialized roles.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Job Roles:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Technical Posts:</strong>&#160;Assisting in research, development, and maintenance of defence equipment, systems, and technologies; performing skilled trade tasks in labs or workshops.</li>
<li><strong>Non-Technical Posts:</strong>&#160;Handling clerical tasks, record-keeping, store management, administrative support, and security duties in DRDO facilities.</li>
<li><strong>Allied Posts:</strong>&#160;Supporting operational needs like driving, drafting technical drawings, or emergency response (e.g., firefighting).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Official Website:</strong>&#160;&#160;or the dedicated CEPTAM recruitment portal for the latest notifications and updates.</li>

</ul>

<p><strong>Additional Details for Tier-I:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong>&#160;90-120 minutes (varies by post and number of sections); additional time for PwD candidates as per norms.</li>
<li><strong>Mode:</strong>&#160;Online (Computer-Based Test) at designated centers.</li>
<li><strong>Language:</strong>&#160;Questions are typically bilingual (English and Hindi) except for the English Language section.</li>
<li><strong>Marking Scheme:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>1 mark for each correct answer (subject to notification).</li>
<li>Negative marking applies (often -0.25 marks per incorrect answer, as per recent cycles).</li>
<li>No marks for unanswered questions.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Qualifying Nature:</strong>&#160;Tier-I is often a screening stage for posts like STA-B and Technician-A; candidates must secure minimum qualifying marks (category-wise) to proceed to Tier-II or further stages.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Tier-II (Selection Test - for Technical Posts like STA-B, Technician-A)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Format:</strong>&#160;Objective type, focusing on advanced technical knowledge specific to the discipline (e.g., Mechanical Engineering, Electronics) for technical posts.</li>
<li><strong>Marks:</strong>&#160;100-120 marks (varies by post).</li>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong>&#160;90-120 minutes.</li>
<li><strong>Purpose:</strong>&#160;To test in-depth technical expertise; performance in Tier-II contributes to the final merit for technical posts.</li>
<li><strong>Note:</strong>&#160;Tier-II is not applicable for most non-technical posts (e.g., Administrative Assistant), where selection is based on Tier-I performance alone.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="b-skill-practical-test-or-trade-test-if-applicable">B. Skill/Practical Test or Trade Test (if applicable)</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates who qualify in the written examination (Tier-I or Tier-I + Tier-II for technical posts) for certain roles (e.g., Technician-A, Fireman, Vehicle Operator) are called for a Skill Test or Trade Test.</li>
<li><strong>Details for Technical Posts (e.g., Technician-A):</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Practical demonstration of trade skills (e.g., fitting, welding, electrical work) relevant to the post.</li>
<li>Qualifying in nature; candidates must demonstrate proficiency as per DRDO standards.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Details for Allied Posts (e.g., Fireman, Vehicle Operator):</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Physical Efficiency Test (PET) for Fireman (e.g., running, carrying weights, endurance tasks).</li>
<li>Driving Test for Vehicle Operator (e.g., practical driving assessment with valid license).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Details for Non-Technical Posts (e.g., Administrative Assistant):</strong>&#160;Typing Test may be conducted (e.g., 30-35 wpm in English or Hindi) for clerical roles, qualifying in nature.</li>
<li><strong>Evaluation:</strong>&#160;Generally qualifying only; performance may not contribute to final merit but is mandatory to pass.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="c-document-verification">C. Document Verification</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates who qualify in the written exam (and skill/trade test, if applicable) are called for Document Verification.</li>
<li>Original documents to be presented include:</li>
<ul>
<li>Educational certificates (Class 10/12, ITI, Diploma, Degree as applicable).</li>
<li>Age proof (e.g., Class 10 certificate or birth certificate).</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable).</li>
<li>Trade certificates or licenses (for technical/allied posts).</li>
<li>Identity proof and other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li>Discrepancies or failure to produce required documents lead to rejection.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="d-final-merit-list-and-appointment">D. Final Merit List and Appointment</h3>
<ul>
<li>Final selection is based on performance in the written examination (Tier-I for non-technical posts; Tier-I + Tier-II for technical posts), subject to qualification in Skill/Trade Test (if applicable).</li>
<li>A merit list is prepared category-wise and post-wise, and candidates are allocated to DRDO labs/establishments based on merit, preference (if provided during application), and vacancies across the country.</li>
<li>Appointment letters are issued after medical examination (mandatory for all posts to confirm fitness) and completion of formalities like police verification.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> The exact structure, inclusion of Tier-II or Skill/Trade Test, and weightage of stages may vary based on the post and recruitment cycle. Candidates should refer to the latest DRDO CEPTAM notification for precise details.</p>

<h2 id="4-detailed-syllabus">4. Detailed Syllabus</h2>
<p>The syllabus for the DRDO CEPTAM written examination focuses on assessing general aptitude for non-technical posts and technical expertise for relevant roles. Below is a detailed breakdown of common topics.</p>
<h3 id="a-general-intelligence-amp-amp-reasoning">A. General Intelligence &amp;amp; Reasoning</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Analogies, Similarities &amp;amp; Differences, Number/Letter Series, Coding-Decoding, Puzzles (Seating Arrangement, Tabulation), Logical Sequences, Spatial Reasoning (Mirror Images, Paper Folding), Visual Reasoning, Syllogisms, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Non-Verbal Reasoning (Figure Classification, Series).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Practice from standard reasoning books (e.g., R.S. Aggarwal) and solve previous year DRDO CEPTAM papers to build speed and accuracy for logical problem-solving.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="b-quantitative-aptitude">B. Quantitative Aptitude</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Basic Arithmetic (Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division), Number Systems, Fractions, Decimals, Percentages, Ratio &amp;amp; Proportion, Averages, Profit &amp;amp; Loss, Simple &amp;amp; Compound Interest, Time &amp;amp; Work, Speed &amp;amp; Distance, Simple Algebra, Mensuration (Area, Volume), Data Interpretation (Tables, Charts).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Solve quantitative aptitude practice tests (Class 10/12 level NCERT as base), use guides like R.S. Aggarwal, and practice sample papers for time management within the exam duration.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="c-general-awareness">C. General Awareness</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Current Affairs (National and International), History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern India), Geography (Physical, Human, Indian Geography), Indian Polity (Constitution, Governance), Economy (Basic Concepts, Budget), Culture (Art, Literature, Heritage), Sports, Awards, Important Days, Science &amp;amp; Technology (Basic Concepts, Recent Developments), Defence-related Knowledge (DRDO Projects, Defence Systems, Military Technology).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers (e.g., The Hindu, Indian Express), follow monthly current affairs magazines (e.g., Pratiyogita Darpan), and refer to DRDO&#8217;s official website or publications for defence and technology updates.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="d-english-language-amp-amp-comprehension">D. English Language &amp;amp; Comprehension</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Substitution), Grammar (Tenses, Prepositions, Articles, Voice, Narration), Sentence Structure and Correction (Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement), Comprehension Passages (Reading and Interpretation), Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks, Idioms and Phrases.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Practice through language workbooks (e.g., Wren &amp;amp; Martin), read English newspapers, and solve previous question papers to improve grammar and comprehension skills.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="e-technical-section-for-technical-posts-like-technician-a-sta-b">E. Technical Section (for Technical Posts like Technician-A, STA-B)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics (Technician-A):</strong>&#160;Trade-specific knowledge based on ITI syllabus (e.g., Mechanical: Workshop Technology, Machine Tools; Electrical: Circuits, Wiring; Electronics: Basic Electronics, Semiconductors).</li>
<li><strong>Topics (STA-B):</strong>&#160;Discipline-specific knowledge at Diploma/B.Sc. level (e.g., Mechanical Engineering: Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics; Electronics: Digital Circuits, Communication Systems; Computer Science: Programming, Networking).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Refer to ITI textbooks or Diploma/B.Sc. standard books for the relevant trade/discipline. Solve previous DRDO technical papers and practice numerical problems related to the field.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Detailed syllabi and topic-wise weightage are provided in the official DRDO CEPTAM notification or on the DRDO website. The technical section is applicable only for technical posts.</p>

<h2 id="5-application-process">5. Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for DRDO CEPTAM recruitment is conducted entirely online through the official DRDO recruitment portal. Below are the key steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification Release:</strong>&#160;Official notifications are published on the DRDO website () or the dedicated CEPTAM recruitment portal and advertised in major newspapers and employment news.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration:</strong>&#160;Candidates must register on the designated portal by providing accurate personal details, academic records, and contact information within the specified application window. A unique registration ID and password are generated for future logins.</li>
<li><strong>Form Filling &amp;amp; Document Upload:</strong>&#160;Complete the online application form by filling in details like educational qualifications, post preferences (based on eligibility), and exam center choices. Upload scanned copies of:</li>
<ul>
<li>Recent passport-sized photograph (as per specified dimensions).</li>
<li>Signature.</li>
<li>Educational certificates (Class 10/12, ITI, Diploma, Degree as applicable).</li>
<li>Age proof.</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable) and other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee Payment:</strong>&#160;Pay the application fee online (typically around&#160;100100&#160;for General/OBC male candidates, subject to notification). Exemptions are available for SC/ST/PwD/female candidates in most cycles. Payment methods include debit/credit cards, net banking, or e-challan.</li>
<li><strong>Submission &amp;amp; Confirmation:</strong>&#160;Submit the completed application before the deadline and save the confirmation receipt or application number for future reference.</li>
<li><strong>Corrections (if applicable):</strong>&#160;DRDO may allow corrections in specific details (e.g., name, photo) within a limited window for a nominal fee (as per notification).</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download:</strong>&#160;Admit cards for the written exam (and skill/trade test, if applicable) are issued on the DRDO portal prior to each stage. Candidates must download and print the admit card to appear for the test, along with a valid photo ID.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Candidates must ensure compliance with application guidelines to avoid rejection. Always refer to the latest DRDO CEPTAM notification for precise details on deadlines, fee structure, and procedures.</p>

<h2 id="6-selection-process">6. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for DRDO CEPTAM is multi-staged to evaluate candidates&#8217; aptitude, technical skills (where applicable), and job suitability. Below are the typical stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Written Examination (Tier-I - CBT):</strong>&#160;An objective test serving as the primary screening stage for all posts. Candidates who meet the cutoff (category-wise, post-wise) are shortlisted for the next stage or final merit, depending on the post.</li>
<li><strong>Written Examination (Tier-II - CBT, for Technical Posts):</strong>&#160;Conducted for technical posts like STA-B and Technician-A to assess in-depth technical knowledge. Performance in Tier-II contributes to the final merit for these posts.</li>
<li><strong>Skill/Practical Test or Trade Test (if applicable):</strong>&#160;Conducted for shortlisted candidates for posts requiring hands-on skills (e.g., Technician-A, Fireman, Vehicle Operator). This test is qualifying in nature (e.g., practical trade skills for Technician-A, physical endurance for Fireman, driving test for Vehicle Operator).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Verification of original documents (educational certificates, age proof, category certificates, trade licenses, ID proof) for shortlisted candidates after clearing written and skill tests (if applicable).</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List and Appointment:</strong>&#160;Final selection is based on performance in the written examination (Tier-I for non-technical posts; Tier-I + Tier-II for technical posts). A merit list is prepared for each post and category, and candidates are allocated to DRDO labs/establishments based on merit, preference, and vacancies. Appointment is subject to medical fitness (mandatory) and completion of formalities like police verification.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> DRDO CEPTAM recruitment does not include an interview stage in recent cycles, focusing on written exams and skill tests for selection. The exact process may vary based on the post and recruitment cycle, so refer to the latest notification for confirmation.</p>

<h2 id="8-preparation-strategies-and-resources">8. Preparation Strategies and Resources</h2>
<p><strong>Study Tips (Written Examination):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Understand the Syllabus:</strong>&#160;Break down topics for each section (General Intelligence &amp;amp; Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness, English) and prioritize high-weightage areas like General Awareness (including defence knowledge) and Reasoning for Tier-I. For technical posts, focus on discipline-specific content for Tier-II.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Regularly:</strong>&#160;Solve previous years&#8217; DRDO CEPTAM question papers and online mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam format, improve speed, and manage time effectively within the 90-120 minute duration.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Weak Areas:</strong>&#160;Identify challenging topics (e.g., Quantitative Aptitude mensuration, Reasoning puzzles) and devote extra time to mastering them through targeted practice.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated on Current Affairs:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers (e.g., The Hindu, Indian Express), watch news channels, and use current affairs apps or magazines (e.g., Pratiyogita Darpan) to stay informed on national events, especially defence and technology developments related to DRDO.</li>
<li><strong>Enhance English Skills:</strong>&#160;Practice comprehension passages, vocabulary exercises, and grammar tests (e.g., Wren &amp;amp; Martin) to excel in the English section, crucial for all posts.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Technical Preparation (for Technical Posts like STA-B, Technician-A):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Review Core Concepts:</strong>&#160;Study ITI syllabus for Technician-A (trade-specific) or Diploma/B.Sc. textbooks for STA-B (discipline-specific) to build a strong foundation in relevant subjects (e.g., Mechanical Engineering, Electronics).</li>
<li><strong>Solve Technical Problems:</strong>&#160;Practice numerical and conceptual questions from standard reference books (e.g., R.K. Jain for Mechanical, B.L. Theraja for Electrical) and previous DRDO papers for Tier-II preparation.</li>
