<h1 id="ssc-multi-tasking-staff-mts-recruitment">SSC Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) Recruitment</h1>

<h2 id="1-exam-overview">1. Exam Overview</h2>
<p>The SSC Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) Recruitment is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) to fill non-technical Group C posts in various ministries, departments, and organizations under the Government of India. This national-level recruitment targets candidates for support roles, offering stable central government jobs with attractive benefits.</p>

<p><strong>Key Details:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Exam Name:</strong>&#160;SSC Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) Recruitment</li>
<li><strong>Conducting Authority:</strong>&#160;Staff Selection Commission (SSC)</li>
<li><strong>Level of Exam:</strong>&#160;National Level</li>
<li><strong>Posts Offered:</strong>&#160;Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) positions, classified under Group C (Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial)</li>
<li><strong>Job Roles:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>Handling general administrative and support tasks such as file management, data entry, and clerical duties.</li>
<li>Assisting with day-to-day office activities, maintenance (e.g., cleaning, carrying files), and logistical support (e.g., mail delivery, peon duties) in central government offices, departments, or attached/subordinate offices.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Mode of Application:</strong>&#160;Online through the official SSC portal</li>
<li><strong>Mode of Exam:</strong>&#160;Computer-Based Test (CBT) for written examination; no Physical Efficiency Test (PET) or interview in recent cycles for MTS roles</li>
<li><strong>Official Website:</strong>&#160;&#160;for the latest notifications and updates.</li>

</ul>

<p><strong>Additional Details for Paper-I:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong>&#160;90 minutes (1.5 hours); additional time for PwD candidates as per norms (e.g., 30 minutes extra).</li>
<li><strong>Mode:</strong>&#160;Online (Computer-Based Test) at designated centers.</li>
<li><strong>Language:</strong>&#160;Questions are available in English, Hindi, and other regional languages as specified by SSC (candidates choose their preferred language during application, except for the English section).</li>
<li><strong>Marking Scheme:</strong></li>
<ul>
<li>1 mark for each correct answer.</li>
<li>Negative marking applies (-0.25 marks per incorrect answer, subject to notification).</li>
<li>No marks for unanswered questions.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Qualifying Nature:</strong>&#160;Candidates must secure minimum qualifying marks (e.g., 40% for General, relaxations for reserved categories) to be considered for further stages or final selection.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Paper-II (Descriptive Type - CBT, if applicable)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Format:</strong>&#160;Short Essay or Letter Writing in English or any language included in the 8th Schedule of the Constitution.</li>
<li><strong>Marks:</strong>&#160;50 marks (subject to notification).</li>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong>&#160;30 minutes (additional time for PwD candidates as per norms).</li>
<li><strong>Purpose:</strong>&#160;To test basic language and writing skills; qualifying in nature only (marks not added to final merit in most cycles).</li>
<li><strong>Note:</strong>&#160;In recent SSC MTS recruitment cycles, Paper-II has been omitted or made qualifying only, with final merit based solely on Paper-I performance. Check the latest notification for confirmation.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="b-document-verification">B. Document Verification</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates who qualify in the written examination (Paper-I, and Paper-II if applicable) based on cutoffs (category-wise, state-wise, and department-wise) are called for Document Verification.</li>
<li>Original documents to be presented include:</li>
<ul>
<li>Educational certificates (Class 10 mark sheet or equivalent).</li>
<li>Age proof (e.g., Class 10 certificate or birth certificate).</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable).</li>
<li>Identity proof and other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li>Discrepancies or failure to produce required documents lead to rejection.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="c-final-merit-list-and-appointment">C. Final Merit List and Appointment</h3>
<ul>
<li>Final selection is based on performance in Paper-I (objective CBT), subject to qualification in Paper-II (if conducted and applicable).</li>
<li>A merit list is prepared category-wise and state/UT-wise (SSC allocates MTS posts across various states and central government departments), and candidates are allocated departments and locations based on merit, preference (if provided during application), and vacancies.</li>
<li>Appointment letters are issued after medical examination (if included by the department) and completion of formalities like police verification.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Unlike some other SSC exams, MTS recruitment in recent cycles does not include a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) or interview, focusing primarily on written exam performance. The exact structure may vary based on the recruitment cycle, so candidates should refer to the latest notification for precise details.</p>

<h2 id="4-detailed-syllabus">4. Detailed Syllabus</h2>
<p>The syllabus for the SSC MTS written examination (Paper-I) focuses on assessing basic academic skills and general awareness at the Class 10 level. Below is a detailed breakdown of the topics.</p>

<h3 id="a-general-intelligence-amp-amp-reasoning">A. General Intelligence &amp;amp; Reasoning</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Analogies, Similarities &amp;amp; Differences, Number/Letter Series, Coding-Decoding, Puzzles (Seating Arrangement, Tabulation), Logical Sequences, Spatial Reasoning (Mirror Images, Paper Folding), Visual Reasoning, Syllogisms, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Non-Verbal Reasoning (Figure Classification, Series).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Practice from standard reasoning books (e.g., R.S. Aggarwal) and solve previous year SSC MTS papers to build speed and accuracy for logical problem-solving.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="b-quantitative-aptitude">B. Quantitative Aptitude</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Basic Arithmetic (Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division), Number Systems, Fractions, Decimals, Percentages, Ratio &amp;amp; Proportion, Averages, Profit &amp;amp; Loss, Discount, Simple Interest, Time &amp;amp; Work, Speed &amp;amp; Distance, Data Interpretation (Tables, Charts), Mensuration (Area, Perimeter of Basic Shapes), Simplification.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Solve quantitative aptitude practice tests (Class 10 level NCERT as base), use guides like R.S. Aggarwal, and practice sample papers for time management within the exam duration.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="c-general-awareness">C. General Awareness</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Current Affairs (National and International), History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern India), Geography (Physical, Human, Indian Geography), Indian Polity (Constitution, Governance), Economy (Basic Concepts, Budget), Culture (Art, Literature, Heritage), Sports, Awards, Important Days, Science &amp;amp; Technology (Basic Concepts, Recent Developments), General Knowledge about Government Schemes and Policies.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers (e.g., The Hindu, Indian Express), follow monthly current affairs magazines (e.g., Pratiyogita Darpan), and refer to static GK books (e.g., Lucent&#8217;s General Knowledge) for comprehensive coverage.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="d-english-language-amp-amp-comprehension">D. English Language &amp;amp; Comprehension</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word Substitution), Grammar (Tenses, Prepositions, Articles, Voice, Narration), Sentence Structure and Correction (Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement), Comprehension Passages (Reading and Interpretation), Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks, Idioms and Phrases.</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Practice through language workbooks (e.g., Wren &amp;amp; Martin), read English newspapers, and solve previous question papers to improve grammar and comprehension skills.</li>

</ul>
<h3 id="e-paper-ii-descriptive-test-if-applicable">E. Paper-II Descriptive Test (if applicable)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Topics:</strong>&#160;Writing a short essay (150-200 words) or letter (formal/informal) on a given topic in English or any language listed in the 8th Schedule of the Constitution (e.g., Hindi, Tamil).</li>
<li><strong>Preparation Tips:</strong>&#160;Practice writing essays and letters on common topics (e.g., social issues, personal experiences) to improve expression and coherence. Focus on basic grammar and format for letters.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Detailed syllabi and topic-wise weightage are provided in the official notification or on the SSC website. Paper-II (descriptive) may not be conducted in some cycles, as per recent trends.</p>

<h2 id="5-application-process">5. Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for SSC MTS recruitment is conducted entirely online through the official SSC portal. Below are the key steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Notification Release:</strong>&#160;Official notifications are published on the SSC website () and advertised in major newspapers and employment news.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration:</strong>&#160;Candidates must register on the SSC portal by providing accurate personal details, academic records, and contact information within the specified application window. A unique registration ID and password are generated for future logins.</li>
<li><strong>Form Filling &amp;amp; Document Upload:</strong>&#160;Complete the online application form by filling in details like educational qualifications, category, and exam center choices. Upload scanned copies of:</li>
<ul>
<li>Recent passport-sized photograph (as per specified dimensions).</li>
<li>Signature.</li>
<li>Educational certificates (Class 10 mark sheet or equivalent).</li>
<li>Age proof.</li>
<li>Category certificate (if applicable) and other documents specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee Payment:</strong>&#160;Pay the application fee online (typically around&#160;100100&#160;for General/OBC male candidates, subject to notification). Exemptions are available for SC/ST/PwD/female candidates. Payment methods include debit/credit cards, net banking, or e-challan.</li>
<li><strong>Submission &amp;amp; Confirmation:</strong>&#160;Submit the completed application before the deadline and save the confirmation receipt or application number for future reference.</li>
<li><strong>Corrections (if applicable):</strong>&#160;SSC may allow corrections in specific details (e.g., name, photo) within a limited window for a nominal fee (as per notification).</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download:</strong>&#160;Admit cards for the written exam (Paper-I and Paper-II, if applicable) are issued on the SSC portal prior to each stage. Candidates must download and print the admit card to appear for the test, along with a valid photo ID.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Candidates must ensure compliance with application guidelines to avoid rejection. Always refer to the latest SSC MTS notification for precise details on deadlines, fee structure, and procedures.</p>

<h2 id="6-selection-process">6. Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for SSC MTS is streamlined to evaluate candidates&#8217; basic aptitude for support roles in central government offices. Below are the typical stages based on recent cycles:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Written Examination (Paper-I - CBT):</strong>&#160;An objective test serving as the primary screening stage. Candidates who meet the cutoff (category-wise, state-wise, and department-wise) are shortlisted for the next stage or final merit, depending on the cycle.</li>
<li><strong>Written Examination (Paper-II - Descriptive, if applicable):</strong>&#160;A qualifying test to assess basic writing skills. Performance in Paper-II does not contribute to the final merit in most recent cycles; candidates must simply pass it (if conducted).</li>
<li><strong>Document Verification:</strong>&#160;Shortlisted candidates must present original documents at designated centers for verification, including educational certificates, age proof, category certificates, and ID proof.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List and Appointment:</strong>&#160;Final selection is based on performance in Paper-I (objective CBT). A merit list is prepared for each state/UT and category, and candidates are allocated departments and locations based on merit, preference (if provided), and vacancies. Appointment is subject to medical fitness (if tested by the department) and completion of formalities like police verification.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> SSC MTS recruitment does not include a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) or interview in recent cycles, focusing solely on written exam performance for merit. The exact process may vary based on the recruitment cycle, so refer to the latest notification for confirmation.</p>

<h2 id="8-preparation-strategies-and-resources">8. Preparation Strategies and Resources</h2>
<p><strong>Study Tips (Written Examination):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Understand the Syllabus:</strong>&#160;Break down topics for each section (General Intelligence &amp;amp; Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness, English) and prioritize high-weightage areas like General Awareness and Reasoning, which are crucial for scoring.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Regularly:</strong>&#160;Solve previous years&#8217; SSC MTS question papers and online mock tests to familiarize yourself with the exam format, improve speed, and manage time effectively within the 90-minute duration.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Weak Areas:</strong>&#160;Identify challenging topics (e.g., Quantitative Aptitude simplification, English grammar) and devote extra time to mastering them through targeted practice.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated on Current Affairs:</strong>&#160;Read newspapers (e.g., The Hindu, Indian Express), watch news channels, and use current affairs apps or magazines (e.g., Pratiyogita Darpan) to stay informed on national and international events for General Awareness.</li>
<li><strong>Enhance English Skills:</strong>&#160;Practice comprehension passages, vocabulary exercises, and grammar tests (e.g., Wren &amp;amp; Martin) to excel in the English section, important for clerical aptitude.</li>
<li><strong>Descriptive Writing (if applicable for Paper-II):</strong>&#160;Practice writing short essays and letters on common topics to improve clarity and coherence for Paper-II, if included in the cycle.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Resources:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Books and Guides:</strong>&#160;Use SSC MTS-specific preparation books from reputed publishers like Arihant, Disha Publications, or R.S. Aggarwal (for Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning).</li>
<li><strong>Online Platforms:</strong>&#160;Leverage websites and mobile apps (e.g., Gradeup, Testbook) that offer mock tests, video tutorials, and practice exercises tailored for SSC exams.</li>
<li><strong>Current Affairs:</strong>&#160;Follow daily news updates through apps, monthly compilations, or dedicated portals for General Awareness preparation.</li>
<li><strong>General Knowledge:</strong>&#160;Refer to static GK books (e.g., Lucent&#8217;s General Knowledge) for history, geography, and polity basics at the Class 10 level.</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>General Tips:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Time Management:</strong>&#160;Develop a study schedule that allocates sufficient time to each section of Paper-I and periodic revision to ensure retention.</li>
<li><strong>Join Study Groups:</strong>&#160;Engage with peers in online forums (e.g., SSC-focused Telegram groups, Quora spaces) or local study groups to exchange tips, resources, and stay motivated.</li>
<li><strong>Practice Computer Skills:</strong>&#160;Since the exam is CBT-based, ensure comfort with using a computer and navigating online interfaces through mock tests or practice sessions.</li>
