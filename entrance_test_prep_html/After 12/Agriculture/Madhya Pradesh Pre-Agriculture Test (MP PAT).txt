<h1 id="madhya-pradesh-pre-agriculture-test-mp-pat">Madhya Pradesh Pre-Agriculture Test (MP PAT)</h1>

<h2 id="about-mp-pat">About MP PAT</h2>
<p>The Madhya Pradesh Pre-Agriculture Test (MP PAT) is a state-level entrance exam for admissions to agriculture and allied courses in Madhya Pradesh. It is conducted offline and assesses candidates based on their 10+2 level knowledge. The exam is typically held in the month of June every year.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Academic Qualification</strong>: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (40% for reserved categories).</li>
<li><strong>Subjects Required</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>For B.Sc. Agriculture/Horticulture/Forestry: Physics, Chemistry, and any one of Biology, Forestry, Agriculture, or Mathematics.</li>
<li>For B.Tech. Agriculture Engineering: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>: Minimum 17 years as of the admission year; no upper limit.</li>
<li><strong>Domicile</strong>: Preference for Madhya Pradesh residents, but all-India candidates can apply under certain quotas.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official website esb.mp.gov.in and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.</li>
<li>Fill in personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li>Upload scanned documents (photograph, signature, and certificates).</li>
<li>Pay the application fee online via Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI.</li>
<li>Submit the form and download the confirmation page.</li>
</ul>
<p>The application process typically starts in the month of May every year.</p>

<h2 id="exam-pattern">Exam Pattern</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type of Questions</strong>: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)</li>
<li><strong>Number of Questions</strong>: 200</li>
<li><strong>Marking Scheme</strong>: +1 for each correct answer; no negative marking</li>
<li><strong>Sections</strong>: Varies by group (e.g., PCB for biology-based courses, PCM for engineering).</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="syllabus">Syllabus</h2>
<p>Based on Madhya Pradesh Board's 11th and 12th curriculum:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Physics</strong>: Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Optics, Electricity.</li>
<li><strong>Chemistry</strong>: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Organic Chemistry.</li>
<li><strong>Biology</strong>: Cell Biology, Genetics, Plant Physiology, Ecology.</li>
<li><strong>Mathematics</strong>&#160;(for PCM): Algebra, Calculus, Geometry.</li>
<li><strong>Agriculture</strong>: Crop Production, Soil Science, Horticulture.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>Released a week before the exam on the official website.</li>
<li>Download using application number and date of birth.</li>
<li>Contains exam details like center, timing, and instructions.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="result-and-counselling">Result and Counselling</h2>
<ul>
<li>Results declared online within a month post-exam.</li>
<li>Counselling is centralized, involving choice filling, document verification, and seat allotment based on merit.</li>
<li>Multiple rounds conducted for vacant seats.</li>

</ul>
