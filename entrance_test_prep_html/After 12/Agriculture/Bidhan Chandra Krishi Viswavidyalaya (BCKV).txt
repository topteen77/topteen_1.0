<h1 id="bidhan-chandra-krishi-viswavidyalaya-bckv-b-sc-hons-agriculture">Bidhan Chandra Krishi Viswavidyalaya (BCKV) B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture</h1>

<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>Bidhan Chandra Krishi Viswavidyalaya (BCKV), established in 1974, is a premier agricultural university in West Bengal dedicated to education, research, and extension services in agriculture and allied sciences. Located in Mohanpur, Nadia, it offers a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Agriculture, a four-year undergraduate program designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in agricultural sciences, including crop production, soil science, plant breeding, and sustainable farming practices. The university also emphasizes research-oriented learning and field training.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Academic Qualification</strong>: Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) or equivalent examination from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English as compulsory subjects. A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required for general category students, and 40% for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD). Mathematics or Agriculture as an additional subject may be preferred.</li>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>: Candidates must be at least 17 years old as of the admission year, with no upper age limit.</li>
<li><strong>Domicile</strong>: Preference is given to West Bengal domiciles, but all-India candidates can apply through ICAR quota.</li>
<li><strong>Additional Requirements</strong>: Candidates appearing in the qualifying exam can apply provisionally, provided they meet criteria at the time of admission. For ICAR quota, qualifying CUET-UG is mandatory.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Registration</strong>: Visit the official BCKV website bckv.edu.in and register using a valid email ID and mobile number.</li>
<li><strong>Filling Application Form</strong>: Provide personal, academic, and communication details as required, including choice of quota if applicable.</li>
<li><strong>Uploading Documents</strong>: Upload scanned images of photograph, signature, 10+2 mark sheet, category certificate (if applicable), and other necessary documents in specified formats (e.g., JPEG/PDF, size limits apply).</li>
<li><strong>Payment of Application Fee</strong>: Pay the application fee online through Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or other available methods.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>: Verify all details and submit the application form. Download and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.</li>
</ul>
<p>The application process typically starts in the month of June every year.</p>

<h2 id="selection-process">Selection Process</h2>
<p>Admission to the B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture program is primarily merit-based, considering the candidate's performance in the qualifying examination (10+2). A merit list is prepared based on aggregate marks in relevant subjects.</p>
<ul>
<li>Approximately 15-20% seats (around 30 seats) are reserved for candidates applying through the All India Entrance Examination, specifically CUET (ICAR-UG) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), following ICAR and university norms.</li>
<li>Shortlisted candidates are invited for counselling. There is no separate entrance test by BCKV; however, physical fitness and document verification are mandatory.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="syllabus-for-preparation-based-on-10-2-level">Syllabus for Preparation (Based on 10+2 Level)</h2>
<p>While admission is merit-based, candidates should be well-versed in 10+2 subjects for a strong foundation:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Physics</strong>: Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electricity, Magnetism, Optics.</li>
<li><strong>Chemistry</strong>: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Organic Chemistry, Biochemistry, Environmental Chemistry.</li>
<li><strong>Biology</strong>: Cell Biology, Genetics, Plant Physiology, Animal Husbandry, Ecology, Biotechnology.</li>
<li><strong>English</strong>: Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary (for qualifying purposes).</li>
<li><strong>Additional Topics</strong>: Basic Agriculture, Soil Science, Crop Production (useful for merit evaluation).</li>
</ul>
<p>Candidates under ICAR quota should prepare for CUET-UG syllabus, which includes domain-specific subjects like Agriculture.</p>
<h2 id="counselling-process">Counselling Process</h2>
<p>Shortlisted candidates based on the merit list are called for the counselling process, which is conducted online or offline depending on university guidelines. It includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Registration for counselling on the university portal.</li>
<li>Choice filling for course preferences (if multiple options available).</li>
<li>Document verification and seat allotment based on merit rank, category, and availability.</li>
<li>Payment of admission fees to confirm the seat.</li>
</ul>
<p>Multiple rounds may be held if seats remain vacant. Candidates must report to the university for final admission formalities.</p>

<h2 id="campus-facilities-and-career-prospects">Campus Facilities and Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Facilities</strong>: BCKV offers modern classrooms, well-equipped labs, research farms, library with digital resources, hostels, sports facilities, and extension centers for practical training. The university has collaborations with ICAR institutes for advanced research.</li>
<li><strong>Career Prospects</strong>: Graduates can pursue careers in agricultural research, agribusiness, extension services, banking (agri-loans), NGOs, or higher studies like M.Sc. Agriculture. Employment opportunities include roles as Agricultural Officers, Farm Managers, or entrepreneurs in agro-industries. Placement cell assists with internships and jobs, with average starting salary around &#8377;3-5 lakhs per annum.</li>

</ul>
