<h1 id="assam-agricultural-university-veterinary-entrance-test-aau-vet">Assam Agricultural University Veterinary Entrance Test (AAU VET)</h1>
<h2 id="about-aau-vet">About AAU VET</h2>
<p>The Assam Agricultural University Veterinary Entrance Test (AAU VET) is a state-level entrance examination conducted annually by Assam Agricultural University (AAU) for candidates seeking admission to undergraduate veterinary and animal husbandry programs. The exam is held offline as a Pen-and-Paper-Based Test (PBT) and serves as the primary gateway for admission to B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H. (Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry) and other agriculture-related undergraduate programs.</p>
<p>Apart from veterinary sciences, Assam Agricultural University also offers undergraduate programs in Agriculture, Horticulture, Sericulture, Fisheries Science, and Community Science. Admissions to these courses are granted based on AAU VET scores and other university-specific criteria. The university also facilitates postgraduate programs in related fields through separate admission processes.</p>
<p><strong>Latest Update</strong>: AAU VET continues to be a significant examination for students aspiring to pursue veterinary and agricultural sciences in Assam, with updates regarding application processes and counseling being regularly announced on the official AAU website.</p>

<h2 id="aau-vet-eligibility-criteria">AAU VET Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<h3 id="educational-qualification">Educational Qualification</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed the 10+2 examination from a recognized board with subjects Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Agriculture/Forestry.</li>
<li>Minimum aggregate marks required:</li>
<ul>
<li>General/OBC/UPS candidates: 50%</li>
<li>SC/ST/DAC candidates: 40%</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<h3 id="age-criteria">Age Criteria</h3>
<ul>
<li>For all undergraduate programs (except B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H.), candidates must be at least 16 years old as of the admission year.</li>
<li>For B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H., candidates must meet the age requirements as per the Veterinary Council of India (VCI) guidelines.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="additional-eligibility">Additional Eligibility</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates serving as Junior Research Fellows (JRFs), Senior Research Fellows (SRFs), or Research Associates (RAs) at AAU may apply for postgraduate programs, provided they fulfill all other requirements.</li>
<li>Domicile requirements may apply for certain reserved seats as per state government norms.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Candidates are advised to review the latest eligibility updates on the official website to ensure compliance with any revised criteria.</p>

<h2 id="aau-vet-application-process">AAU VET Application Process</h2>
<p>Candidates can apply for AAU VET by following these steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official website:&#160;.</li>
<li>Create a login ID and fill in personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Uploading Documents</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Recent passport-size photograph</li>
<li>Scanned signature</li>
<li>Class 10 and 12 mark sheets</li>
<li>Caste certificate (if applicable)</li>
<li>Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) / Nativity Certificate</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee Payment</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must pay the application fee via online payment modes (Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI).</li>
<li>Tentative Fee: Around&#160;10001000&#160;(subject to change based on category and university notification).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Submission &amp;amp; Admit Card Download</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>After successful submission, download the AAU VET Admit Card when available from the official portal.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application.</p>
<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>The admit card for AAU VET is released on the official website a few weeks before the exam.</li>
<li>Candidates must download and print the admit card, which contains details such as name, roll number, exam center, and timings.</li>
<li>A valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, etc.) must be carried along with the admit card to the exam center.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="result-amp-amp-counselling-process">Result &amp;amp; Counselling Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Result Declaration</strong>: Results for AAU VET are announced on the official website a few weeks after the test. Candidates are ranked based on their scores.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling Process</strong>: Shortlisted candidates are called for the counselling process, which may include multiple rounds (including mop-up rounds for vacant seats).</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Steps</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Register for counselling and pay the required fee.</li>
<li>Attend document verification at designated centers or online, as notified.</li>
<li>Choose preferred programs and colleges during the choice-filling window.</li>
<li>Seat allotment based on rank, category, and availability.</li>
<li>Report to the allotted college with original documents for final admission.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Documents Required</strong>: 10+2 mark sheets, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), AAU VET admit card, and rank card.</li>
<li><strong>Latest Update</strong>: Notifications regarding counselling phases and vacant seats are regularly updated on the university portal.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-choose-aau-vet">Why Choose AAU VET?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gateway to Veterinary and Agricultural Sciences</strong>: AAU VET provides direct admission to prestigious programs like B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H. and other agriculture-related courses.</li>
<li><strong>State-Level Recognition</strong>: Conducted by Assam Agricultural University, a leading institution in agricultural education in Northeast India.</li>
<li><strong>Diverse Program Options</strong>: Offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses in agriculture, veterinary, and allied sciences.</li>
<li><strong>Career Opportunities</strong>: Graduates can explore roles in veterinary healthcare, agricultural research, and government sectors.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Regional Needs</strong>: Curriculum and training are tailored to address agricultural and veterinary challenges specific to Assam and the Northeast region.</li>
<li><strong>Affordable Education</strong>: Government-supported programs ensure quality education at a reasonable cost.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="career-prospects">Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Veterinary Sector</strong>: Careers as veterinarians, animal health consultants, or livestock managers in government and private sectors.</li>
<li><strong>Agricultural Sector</strong>: Opportunities in agricultural research, farm management, and extension services.</li>
<li><strong>Fisheries and Sericulture</strong>: Roles in aquaculture, fish farming, and silk production industries.</li>
<li><strong>Community Science</strong>: Focus on rural development, nutrition, and family resource management.</li>
<li><strong>Government Jobs</strong>: Eligibility for positions in state and central agricultural and veterinary departments.</li>
<li><strong>Entrepreneurship</strong>: Start ventures in farming, animal husbandry, or agricultural consultancy services.</li>
</ul>
