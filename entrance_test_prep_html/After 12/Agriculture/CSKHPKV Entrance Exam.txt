<h1 id="cskhpkv-entrance-exam">CSKHPKV Entrance Exam</h1>
<h2 id="about-cskhpkv">About CSKHPKV</h2>
<p>Chaudhary Sarwan Kumar Himachal Pradesh Krishi Vishvavidyalaya (CSKHPKV), officially known as CSK Himachal Pradesh Agricultural University (CSK HPAU), is a premier agricultural university located in Palampur, Himachal Pradesh. Established in 1978, the university specializes in hill agriculture and is accredited by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). CSKHPKV offers undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral (PhD) programs in agriculture, veterinary sciences, and related disciplines. The university is renowned for its research and education tailored to the unique agricultural challenges of hilly terrains.</p>
<p><strong>Latest Update</strong>: CSKHPKV continues to be a leading institution for agricultural education, with ongoing updates for admissions to various programs being announced on the official website.</p>
<h2 id="cskhpkv-admission-process">CSKHPKV Admission Process</h2>
<p>Admission to UG and PG programs at CSKHPKV is based on a combination of entrance test scores and academic performance:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Weightage for UG and PG Admissions</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>60% weightage is given to the CSKHPKV Entrance Test results.</li>
<li>40% weightage is given to the marks obtained in the qualifying examination (10+2 for UG, graduation for PG).</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>National-Level Tests</strong>: For certain programs, admissions are also conducted based on national-level entrance tests such as NEET (for B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H.), CUET, or ICAR AIEEA (for UG and PG agriculture-related courses), alongside university-conducted exams.</li>
<li><strong>Merit-Based Admissions</strong>: Some programs may offer merit-based admissions without an entrance test, based on academic performance and specific eligibility criteria.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Official Website for Registration</strong>: Candidates can apply online through the official website: .</p>

<h2 id="cskhpkv-eligibility-criteria">CSKHPKV Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<h3 id="for-undergraduate-ug-programs">For Undergraduate (UG) Programs</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>B.Sc. (</strong><strong>General &amp;amp;</strong><strong> </strong><strong>Hons</strong><strong>.) Agriculture</strong></li>
<li>Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Science stream) from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics as core subjects.</li>
<li>Selection is based on the CSKHPKV Entrance Exam conducted by the university or through ICAR AIEEA scores in some cases.</li>
<li><strong>B.V.Sc</strong><strong>. &amp;amp; A.H. (Bachelor of Veterinary Science &amp;amp; Animal Husbandry)</strong></li>
<li>Candidates must have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English as subjects.</li>
<li>Minimum aggregate marks: 50% for General category (40% for SC/ST/DA candidates).</li>
<li>Must qualify for the CSKHPKV Entrance Test or NEET-UG as per the admission guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>B.Tech</strong><strong> (Food Technology)</strong></li>
<li>Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.</li>
<li>Selection is based on the university entrance test or merit, depending on the specific admission cycle.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-postgraduate-pg-programs">For Postgraduate (PG) Programs</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>M.Sc. (Master of Science) in Agriculture and Allied Fields</strong></li>
<li>Candidates must have a bachelor&#8217;s degree in a relevant discipline (e.g., Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry) from a recognized university.</li>
<li>Minimum marks required: 60% for General/OBC, 50% for SC/ST/DA candidates.</li>
<li>Admission is based on CSKHPKV Entrance Exam scores or ICAR AIEEA PG scores.</li>
<li><strong>M.V.Sc</strong><strong>. (Master of Veterinary Science)</strong></li>
<li>Bachelor&#8217;s degree in Veterinary Science &amp;amp; Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H.) from a recognized institution.</li>
<li>Minimum marks: 60% for General/OBC (50% for SC/ST/DA candidates).</li>
<li>Admission through the university entrance exam or merit-based selection as notified.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-doctoral-phd-programs">For Doctoral (PhD) Programs</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must hold a master&#8217;s degree in a relevant field (e.g., M.Sc. Agriculture, M.V.Sc.) from a recognized university.</li>
<li>Minimum marks required: 60% for General/OBC, 50% for SC/ST/DA candidates.</li>
<li>Admission is based on the entrance test conducted by CSKHPKV, followed by an interview or merit evaluation in some cases.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Eligibility criteria may vary slightly based on specific program requirements or updates in admission policies. Candidates are advised to check the latest prospectus on the official website.</p>
<h2 id="cskhpkv-entrance-test-exam-centers">CSKHPKV Entrance Test: Exam Centers</h2>
<p>The entrance exam for B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H. and B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programs is conducted at the following centers across Himachal Pradesh:</p>
<ul>
<li>Chamba</li>
<li>Hamirpur</li>
<li>Mandi</li>
<li>Nurpur</li>
<li>Palampur</li>
<li>Rampur</li>
<li>Solan</li>
<li>Una</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Exam centers may be subject to change based on the number of applicants or logistical considerations. Candidates should confirm the allocated center on their admit card.</p>

<li><strong>Medium of Exam</strong>: English only</li>

<li><strong>Total Marks</strong>: 180</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for CSKHPKV Entrance Exam is conducted online:</p>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official website:&#160;.</li>
<li>Navigate to the admissions section and register using a valid email ID and phone number.</li>
<li>Fill out the application form with personal, educational, and category details.</li>
<li>Upload required documents, such as scanned copies of photographs, signatures, and certificates.</li>
<li>Pay the application fee through online payment methods (debit/credit card, net banking).</li>
<li>Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>The admit card for the CSKHPKV Entrance Exam is released on the official website a few weeks before the exam.</li>
<li>Candidates must download and print the admit card, which contains details such as name, roll number, exam center, and timings.</li>
<li>A valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, etc.) must be carried along with the admit card to the exam center.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="result-amp-amp-counselling-process">Result &amp;amp; Counselling Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Result Declaration</strong>: Results for the entrance exam are announced on the official website a few weeks after the test. Candidates are ranked based on their scores and academic merit.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling Process</strong>: Shortlisted candidates must participate in the counselling process, which may be conducted online or offline.</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Steps</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Register for counselling and pay the required fee.</li>
<li>Attend document verification (physical or online, as notified).</li>
<li>Choose preferred programs and colleges during the choice-filling window.</li>
<li>Seat allotment based on rank, category, and availability.</li>
<li>Report to the allotted college with original documents for final admission.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Documents </strong><strong>Required</strong>: 10+2 mark sheets, graduation certificates (for PG), domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), entrance exam admit card, and rank card.</li>
<li><strong>Latest Update</strong>: Notifications regarding document verification and registration for various programs are regularly updated on the university portal.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-choose-cskhpkv">Why Choose CSKHPKV?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>ICAR Accreditation</strong>: Recognized by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), ensuring high standards of education and research.</li>
<li><strong>Specialization in Hill Agriculture</strong>: One of India's premier institutes focusing on agricultural research and practices suited for hilly terrains.</li>
<li><strong>State-of-the-Art Facilities</strong>: Advanced research centers, well-equipped laboratories, experimental farms, and modern infrastructure for practical learning.</li>
<li><strong>High Placement Rate</strong>: Graduates find employment in government sectors (agriculture and veterinary departments), private agricultural firms, research institutions, and international organizations.</li>
<li><strong>Scholarship Opportunities</strong>: Various state and central government scholarships are available for meritorious and economically weaker students, including ICAR-sponsored fellowships.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Sustainability</strong>: Emphasis on sustainable farming practices, organic agriculture, and climate-resilient crop varieties relevant to Himalayan agriculture.</li>
<li><strong>Collaborative Research</strong>: Opportunities for students to engage in national and international research projects through university partnerships.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="career-prospects">Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Agriculture Sector</strong>: Graduates can pursue careers in agricultural research, agribusiness, farm management, extension services, and government roles in agriculture departments.</li>
<li><strong>Veterinary Sciences</strong>: B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H. and M.V.Sc. graduates can work in veterinary hospitals, animal husbandry sectors, wildlife conservation, and livestock management.</li>
<li><strong>Food Technology</strong>: B.Tech graduates can find opportunities in food processing industries, quality control, and product development.</li>
<li><strong>Higher Studies</strong>: Students can opt for postgraduate and doctoral programs for specialization or pursue competitive exams for research fellowships.</li>
<li><strong>Entrepreneurship</strong>: Opportunities to start ventures in organic farming, horticulture, seed production, or agricultural consultancy services.</li>
</ul>
