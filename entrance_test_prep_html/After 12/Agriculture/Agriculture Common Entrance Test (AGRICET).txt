<h1 id="agricet-agriculture-common-entrance-test">AGRICET (Agriculture Common Entrance Test)</h1>
<h2 id="about-agricet">About AGRICET</h2>
<p>The Agriculture Common Entrance Test (AGRICET) is a state-level entrance exam conducted by Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU) for admission into four-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture programs. This test is specifically designed for diploma holders in Agriculture, Seed Technology, and Organic Farming who wish to pursue higher studies in these fields. The exam is conducted in offline mode with a duration of 1 hour and 30 minutes. Successful candidates secure admission to various affiliated agricultural colleges in Andhra Pradesh based on their performance in the exam.</p>
<p><strong>Latest Update</strong>: AGRICET continues to be a key pathway for diploma holders to enter degree programs, with ongoing updates regarding application processes and counselling phases being announced on the official ANGRAU website.</p>

<h2 id="agricet-eligibility-criteria">AGRICET Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>To be eligible for AGRICET, candidates must fulfill the following criteria:</p>
<h3 id="educational-qualification">Educational Qualification</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed a two-year diploma in Agriculture, Seed Technology, or Organic Farming from:</li>
<ul>
<li>Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU), or</li>
<li>Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University (PJTSAU).</li>
</ul>
<li>Final-year diploma students are also eligible to apply, provided they complete their diploma before the counseling process.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="age-criteria">Age Criteria</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Minimum Age</strong>: 17 years</li>
<li><strong>Maximum Age</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>22 years for General/OBC candidates</li>
<li>25 years for SC/ST candidates</li>
<li>27 years for Physically Challenged (PwD) candidates</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Age limits are calculated as of the admission year, and candidates must provide valid proof during counseling.</p>

<h2 id="agricet-cut-off-marks-amp-amp-seat-reservation">AGRICET Cut-off Marks &amp;amp; Seat Reservation</h2>
<p>The AGRICET cut-off score determines a candidate&#8217;s eligibility for counseling and seat allotment.</p>
<h3 id="minimum-qualifying-marks">Minimum Qualifying Marks</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>General/OBC Candidates</strong>: 25% (3030&#160;out of&#160;120120&#160;marks)</li>
<li><strong>SC/ST Candidates</strong>: No minimum cut-off, but admission depends on seat availability and merit ranking.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="seat-distribution">Seat Distribution</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Local Category</strong>: 85% seats are reserved for candidates belonging to Andhra Pradesh.</li>
<li><strong>Non-Local (Unreserved) Category</strong>: 15% seats are allocated for non-local candidates.</li>
<li><strong>Seat Division During </strong><strong>Counseling</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>65% seats for the Convener Quota (filled through counseling based on AGRICET merit).</li>
<li>35% seats for Management Quota in affiliated private agricultural colleges.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Reservation policies for categories such as SC, ST, OBC, and PwD are applied as per Andhra Pradesh government norms during seat allotment.</p>
<h2 id="agricet-syllabus">AGRICET Syllabus</h2>
<p>The AGRICET syllabus is based on the diploma curriculum in Agriculture, Seed Technology, and Organic Farming as prescribed by ANGRAU. The syllabus for each specialization is outlined below:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>1. Agriculture Specialization</strong></li>
<li>Agronomy</li>
<li>Genetics &amp;amp; Plant Breeding</li>
<li>Soil Science &amp;amp; Agricultural Chemistry</li>
<li>Entomology</li>
<li>Agricultural Economics</li>
<li>Agricultural Engineering</li>
<li>English</li>
<li>Plant Pathology</li>
<li>Horticulture</li>
<li>Agricultural Extension</li>
<li><strong>2. Seed Technology Specialization</strong></li>
<li>Agronomy</li>
<li>Genetics &amp;amp; Plant Breeding</li>
<li>Soil Science &amp;amp; Agricultural Chemistry</li>
<li>English</li>
<li>Seed Technology</li>
<li>Entomology</li>
<li>Statistics</li>
<li>Plant Pathology</li>
<li>Agricultural Extension</li>
<li>Agricultural Economics</li>
<li><strong>3. Organic Farming Specialization</strong></li>
<li>Topics aligned with diploma-level coursework in organic farming practices, soil management, and sustainable agriculture.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: The syllabus ensures that the questions align with the diploma-level knowledge in the respective specializations. Candidates are advised to refer to the official brochure for detailed topics and updates.</p>
<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>The application process for AGRICET is conducted online through the official website:</p>
<ul>
<li>Visit the official website:&#160;&#160;or the dedicated AGRICET portal.</li>
<li>Register using a valid email ID and phone number.</li>
<li>Fill out the application form with personal, educational, and category details.</li>
<li>Upload required documents, such as scanned copies of photographs, signatures, and diploma certificates.</li>
<li>Pay the application fee through online payment methods (debit/credit card, net banking).</li>
<li>Submit the form and download the confirmation page for future reference.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>The admit card for AGRICET is released on the official website a few weeks before the exam.</li>
<li>Candidates must download and print the admit card, which contains details such as name, roll number, exam center, and timings.</li>
<li>A valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, etc.) must be carried along with the admit card to the exam center.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="result-amp-amp-counselling-process">Result &amp;amp; Counselling Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Result Declaration</strong>: Results for AGRICET are announced on the official website a few weeks after the test. Candidates are ranked based on their scores.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling Process</strong>: Shortlisted candidates must participate in the counselling process, which may include multiple phases (manual or online).</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Steps</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Register for counselling and pay the required fee.</li>
<li>Attend document verification at designated centers or online, as notified.</li>
<li>Choose preferred colleges during the choice-filling window.</li>
<li>Seat allotment based on rank, category, and availability.</li>
<li>Report to the allotted college with original documents for final admission.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Documents Required</strong>: Diploma mark sheets, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), AGRICET admit card, and rank card.</li>
<li><strong>Latest Update</strong>: Notifications regarding counselling phases and vacant seats are regularly updated on the university portal.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-choose-agricet">Why Choose AGRICET?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gateway to B.Sc. (</strong><strong>Hons</strong><strong>.) Agriculture</strong>: AGRICET provides diploma holders with direct admission into four-year agriculture degree programs.</li>
<li><strong>State-Level Recognition</strong>: Conducted by ANGRAU, a prestigious agricultural university in Andhra Pradesh.</li>
<li><strong>Diverse Career Paths</strong>: Graduates can explore careers in agricultural research, agribusiness, seed technology, organic farming, and government agriculture departments.</li>
<li><strong>Higher Studies Opportunities</strong>: AGRICET graduates can pursue M.Sc. (Agriculture), Ph.D., and specialized agricultural courses.</li>
<li><strong>Affordable Education</strong>: Government agricultural colleges offer quality education at a lower cost compared to private institutions.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Practical Skills</strong>: The curriculum and exam structure emphasize practical knowledge gained during diploma studies, ensuring a smooth transition to degree-level learning.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="career-prospects">Career Prospects</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Agriculture Sector</strong>: Graduates can pursue roles in agricultural research, farm management, extension services, and government jobs in agriculture departments.</li>
<li><strong>Seed Technology</strong>: Opportunities in seed production, quality control, and certification agencies.</li>
<li><strong>Organic Farming</strong>: Careers in sustainable agriculture, organic product certification, and eco-friendly farming ventures.</li>
<li><strong>Agribusiness</strong>: Roles in agricultural marketing, supply chain management, and rural development projects.</li>
<li><strong>Entrepreneurship</strong>: Start ventures in farming, seed distribution, or agricultural consultancy services.</li>
</ul>
