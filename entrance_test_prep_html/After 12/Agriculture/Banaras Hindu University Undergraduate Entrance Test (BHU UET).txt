<h1 id="banaras-hindu-university-undergraduate-entrance-test-bhu-uet-cuet-ug">Banaras Hindu University Undergraduate Entrance Test (BHU UET) / CUET UG</h1>
<h2 id="about-bhu-uet-cuet-ug">About BHU UET / CUET UG</h2>
<p>The Banaras Hindu University Undergraduate Entrance Test (BHU UET) was historically a university-level entrance examination conducted for admission to various undergraduate programs offered by Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi. However, in recent years, BHU has transitioned to using the Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) for admissions to its undergraduate courses. The CUET UG is a national-level examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) and is used for admissions to multiple central and participating universities, including BHU. The test is conducted online as a Computer-Based Test (CBT) for multiple courses such as B.A. (Hons.), B.Sc. (Hons.), B.Com. (Hons.), B.Ed., LL.B., B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H., B.P.Ed., B.F.A., and B.P.A.</p>
<p><strong>Latest Update</strong>: Admissions to undergraduate programs at BHU are now conducted through the CUET UG, as per the latest guidelines. Candidates are advised to regularly check the official BHU and CUET websites for updates on the application and examination process.</p>

<h2 id="eligibility-criteria">Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<h3 id="general-eligibility">General Eligibility</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Final Year Candidates</strong>: Students appearing in the final year of their qualifying exam can apply, provided they submit their mark sheets during counseling.</li>
<li><strong>Re-Appearance Rule</strong>: Candidates already admitted to BHU for Part 1/Semester 1 of any course through previous entrance exams cannot reappear for the same course without permission from the concerned faculty.</li>
<li><strong>Grade Conversion</strong>: If the qualifying exam mark sheet displays grades instead of marks, the candidate must provide a valid conversion certificate from their board/university.</li>
<li><strong>Indian Universities Accepted</strong>: Degrees and certificates from any recognized Indian university or board are considered valid for application.</li>
<li><strong>Multiple Course Applications</strong>: Candidates may apply for multiple courses, provided that the exam schedules do not overlap.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="syllabus-cuet-ug-for-bhu-admissions">Syllabus (CUET UG for BHU Admissions)</h2>
<p>The CUET UG syllabus for BHU admissions is based on the 12th-grade curriculum and includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Science Courses</strong>: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology</li>
<li><strong>Commerce Courses</strong>: Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies</li>
<li><strong>Arts Courses</strong>: History, Political Science, Sociology, General Knowledge, Mental Ability</li>
<li><strong>General Test</strong>: Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness</li>
</ul>
<p>The difficulty level aligns with the 10+2 standard. Candidates should refer to the NCERT syllabus and CUET UG guidelines for preparation.</p>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Specific courses like B.V.Sc. &amp;amp; A.H. or B.Ed. may require additional subject combinations or practical components as specified by BHU.</p>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<p>Candidates can apply for CUET UG (for BHU admissions) by following these steps:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the CUET Registration Portal</strong>: Access&#160;&#160;for initial registration.</li>
<li><strong>Fill the Application Form</strong>: Enter personal, academic details, and select BHU as a preferred university along with desired courses.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong>: Scanned passport-size photo, signature, and relevant certificates (as per CUET guidelines).</li>
<li><strong>Pay the Application Fee</strong>: Online payment via debit/credit card, net banking, or UPI (fee varies based on number of subjects and category).</li>
<li><strong>Submit the Form</strong>: Review carefully before final submission.</li>
<li><strong>Download Confirmation</strong>: Print the confirmation page for reference.</li>
<li><strong>BHU-Specific Registration</strong>: After CUET UG results, candidates must register on the BHU counseling portal&#160;&#160;for seat allocation.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: For detailed instructions, candidates should visit the CUET UG website and BHU Admission Guidelines at .</p>

<h2 id="admit-card">Admit Card</h2>
<ul>
<li>The admit card for CUET UG is released on the official CUET website a few weeks before the exam.</li>
<li>Candidates must download and print the admit card, which contains details such as name, roll number, exam center, and timings.</li>
<li>A valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar card, voter ID, etc.) must be carried along with the admit card to the exam center.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="result-amp-amp-counselling-process">Result &amp;amp; Counselling Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Result Declaration</strong>: Results for CUET UG are announced on the official CUET website by NTA. BHU uses these scores to prepare merit lists for counselling.</li>
<li><strong>Counselling Process</strong>: BHU conducts online counselling for shortlisted candidates based on CUET UG scores.</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Steps</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Register on the BHU counselling portal and pay the required fee.</li>
<li>Attend document verification (online or physical, as notified).</li>
<li>Choose preferred programs and colleges during the choice-filling window.</li>
<li>Seat allotment based on rank, category, and availability.</li>
<li>Report to the allotted college with original documents for final admission.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<li><strong>Documents Required</strong>: 10+2 mark sheets, domicile certificate (if applicable), category certificate (if applicable), CUET UG admit card, and scorecard.</li>
<li><strong>Latest Update</strong>: Notifications regarding counselling phases and seat allotment results are regularly updated on the BHU portal.</li>

</ul>
