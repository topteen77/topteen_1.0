<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The IPU CET BBA (Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test for BBA) is the entrance examination for admission into Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programs at Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) and its affiliated colleges, many of which offer marketing as a specialization option. This exam is meticulously designed to assess candidates&#8217; aptitude in quantitative reasoning, verbal skills, logical analysis, and general business awareness, ensuring that only the most capable and promising candidates secure admission into these highly competitive programs. GGSIPU, a leading public university in Delhi, is renowned for its industry-oriented curriculum, strong placement records, and diverse affiliated institutions, making it a top choice for students aspiring to build a career in business management with a focus on marketing. For the latest updates and detailed guidelines, please visit the IPU Official Website at .</p>
