<ul>
<li><strong>Establish a Uniform Benchmark</strong>: Create a consistent standard to evaluate candidates from diverse academic backgrounds, ensuring fairness in the selection process.</li>
<li><strong>Streamline the Admission Process</strong>: Serve as the principal criterion for shortlisting applicants for specialized marketing programs at NMIMS.</li>
<li><strong>Identify High-Potential Candidates</strong>: Recognize students with strong analytical, communicative, and critical thinking skills essential for success in marketing and business management.</li>
<li><strong>Promote Transparency</strong>: Utilize a computer-based testing format to minimize errors and ensure an impartial, unbiased evaluation for all candidates.</li>
</ul>
