<h1 id="ipmat-iim-indore-amp-amp-iim-rohtak-8211-5-year-integrated-bba-mba-entrance-test">IPMAT (IIM Indore &amp;amp; IIM Rohtak) &#8211; 5-Year Integrated BBA+MBA Entrance Test</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The IPMAT (Integrated Program in Management Aptitude Test) is the gateway for admission into the prestigious five-year integrated management program offered by the Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore and IIM Rohtak. This unique program combines undergraduate studies in business administration (BBA) with a Master of Business Administration (MBA), allowing students to specialize in areas such as marketing, finance, or operations in the later years. Designed to assess candidates&#8217; managerial aptitude, quantitative skills, verbal ability, and logical reasoning, IPMAT is one of the most sought-after entrance exams for young aspirants aiming to build a strong foundation for a career in management directly after high school. IIM Indore and IIM Rohtak, as premier management institutes in India, are renowned for their rigorous academic standards, industry-oriented curricula, and exceptional placement records. For the latest updates and detailed guidelines, please visit the IIM Indore IPMAT Portal at  and the IIM Rohtak IPMAT Portal at .</p>

<h2 id="exam-objectives">Exam Objectives</h2>
<p>The IPMAT scorecard is a crucial component of the integrated program&#8217;s admission process, serving the following key objectives:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Uniform Assessment</strong>: To standardize the evaluation of candidates from various educational backgrounds, ensuring fairness in selection across diverse academic streams.</li>
<li><strong>Admission Gateway</strong>: To serve as the primary criterion for shortlisting candidates for the integrated BBA+MBA program at IIM Indore and IIM Rohtak.</li>
<li><strong>Talent Identification</strong>: To identify individuals with strong managerial potential, analytical aptitude, and communication skills who are capable of excelling in the dynamic field of management.</li>
<li><strong>Transparent Evaluation</strong>: To ensure a fair, computer-based assessment that minimizes bias and errors, providing an objective measure of a candidate&#8217;s readiness for a rigorous management education.</li>
</ul>
