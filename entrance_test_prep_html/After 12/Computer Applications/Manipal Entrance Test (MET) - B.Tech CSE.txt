<h1 id="manipal-entrance-test-met-for-b-tech-computer-science-amp-amp-engineering-cse">Manipal Entrance Test (MET) for B.Tech Computer Science &amp;amp; Engineering (CSE) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Manipal Entrance Test (MET) is a university-level entrance exam conducted by Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) for admission to its B.Tech Computer Science &amp;amp; Engineering (CSE) program. MET assesses a candidate's proficiency in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Logical Reasoning, and English. The test is held in online Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode and is open to students who have completed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as mandatory subjects.</p>
<p>The exam is highly competitive, with thousands of students applying annually for a limited number of seats. Aspirants must go through a rigorous selection process, including an entrance test and counseling, to secure admission to Manipal's prestigious engineering programs, known for their industry-aligned curriculum and excellent placement records.</p>

<h2 id="selection-process">Selection Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Exam Performance</strong>: Candidates must qualify MET with a competitive score.</li>
<li><strong>Merit List &amp;amp; Rank Declaration</strong>: The university releases a rank list based on the test performance.</li>
<li><strong>Counseling</strong><strong> &amp;amp; Seat Allocation</strong>: Shortlisted candidates are invited for online counseling based on their ranks.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Selected candidates must verify documents, confirm their seat, and complete the fee payment process.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="application-process">Application Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Registration</strong>: Candidates must register on the official website&#160;&#160;and create a profile.</li>
<li><strong>Filling the Application Form</strong>: Provide personal, academic, and contact details along with document uploads.</li>
<li><strong>Payment of Application Fee</strong>: The fee must be paid online using debit/credit card, net banking, or UPI.</li>
<li><strong>Slot Booking</strong>: Once the application is submitted, candidates can book their exam slot based on availability.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>: After slot booking, the admit card will be generated, which must be downloaded and carried to the exam center.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="preparation-tips">Preparation Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics &amp;amp; Logical Reasoning</strong>: Strengthen problem-solving speed and accuracy. Focus on topics like probability, calculus, algebra, and data interpretation.</li>
<li><strong>Physics &amp;amp; Chemistry</strong>: Understand key concepts, formulas, and their applications. Practice numerical-based problems regularly.</li>
<li><strong>English &amp;amp; Aptitude</strong>: Improve reading comprehension, vocabulary, and logical reasoning through daily practice.</li>
<li><strong>Mock Tests &amp;amp; Previous Papers</strong>: Solve MET mock tests and previous years&#8217; question papers to understand the exam pattern and improve time management.</li>
</ul>
