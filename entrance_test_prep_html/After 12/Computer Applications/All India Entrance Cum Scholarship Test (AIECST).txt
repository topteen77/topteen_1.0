<h1 id="all-india-entrance-cum-scholarship-test-aiecst">All India Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (AIECST) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The All India Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (AIECST) is a university-level entrance and scholarship examination conducted by APG Shimla University. The exam serves dual purposes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Test</strong>: For admission into undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and diploma programs.</li>
<li><strong>Scholarship Test</strong>: To provide financial assistance to meritorious and financially weaker students.</li>
</ul>
<p>AIECST is conducted entirely online, allowing students to take the test from the comfort of their homes, making it accessible to candidates across India. This initiative by APG Shimla University aims to promote higher education by reducing financial barriers and ensuring quality learning opportunities.</p>

<h2 id="benefits-of-taking-aiecst">Benefits of Taking AIECST</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Access to Quality Higher Education</strong>: Gain admission to reputed UG, PG, and diploma courses at APG Shimla University.</li>
<li><strong>Scholarship Support</strong>: Reduce tuition fees with merit-based and financial aid scholarships, making education more affordable.</li>
<li><strong>Online Exam Format</strong>: Students can take the test from home, ensuring accessibility across India, especially for those in remote areas.</li>
<li><strong>Transparent Selection Process</strong>: Fair evaluation based on performance, with clear criteria for admission and scholarships.</li>
<li><strong>Wide Range of Career Options</strong>: Choose from multiple fields such as IT, law, management, healthcare, and engineering, aligning with diverse career aspirations.</li>
</ul>
