<h1 id="dayananda-sagar-admission-test-dsat">Dayananda Sagar Admission Test (DSAT) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>Dayananda Sagar University (DSU), Bengaluru, conducts the Dayananda Sagar Admission Test (DSAT), a university-level entrance exam for admission to various Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) programs. The test is open to Indian, NRI, and overseas candidates and is available in both online and offline modes.</p>
<p>DSU offers programs in Engineering, Computer Applications, Basic and Applied Sciences, Commerce, Arts, Management, and Health Sciences. The university was established in 1960 by Sri Dayananda Sagar and is managed by the Mahatma Gandhi Vidya Peeta Trust, known for its commitment to quality education and innovation.</p>

<h2 id="dsat-eligibility-criteria-for-bca-amp-amp-it-courses">DSAT Eligibility Criteria for BCA &amp;amp; IT Courses</h2>
<h3 id="for-bca-bachelor-of-computer-applications">For BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications):</h3>
<ul>
<li>Must have passed 10+2 (Class 12) from a recognized state or central board.</li>
<li>A minimum of 45% aggregate marks is required.</li>
<li>Candidates from any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) are eligible.</li>
<li>Preference is given to students who have studied Mathematics or Computer Science at the 10+2 level.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-b-tech-in-computer-science-amp-amp-it">For B.Tech in Computer Science &amp;amp; IT:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Must have completed 10+2 (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).</li>
<li>A minimum of 45% aggregate marks is required from a recognized board.</li>
<li>No age limit for admission.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="age-criteria">Age Criteria:</h3>
<ul>
<li>There is no upper age limit for any course under DSAT.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="dsat-counselling-and-admission-process">DSAT Counselling and Admission Process</h2>
<p>Candidates who clear the DSAT exam must participate in the counselling process, which includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Online Registration for counselling.</li>
<li>Choice Filling: Select preferred courses and colleges.</li>
<li>Seat Allotment: Based on DSAT rank and seat availability.</li>
<li>Document Verification: Submit academic and identity documents.</li>
<li>Fee Payment &amp;amp; Admission Confirmation: Pay the course fees to secure admission.</li>
</ul>
