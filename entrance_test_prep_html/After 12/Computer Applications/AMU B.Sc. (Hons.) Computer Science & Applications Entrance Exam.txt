<h1 id="amu-entrance-test-for-b-sc-hons-computer-science-amp-amp-applications">AMU Entrance Test for B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science &amp;amp; Applications </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) offers a Bachelor of Science (Honors) in Computer Science &amp;amp; Applications, a four-year undergraduate program aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. Admission to this program is based on the AMU Entrance Test, a university-level examination conducted annually by the university.</p>
<p>This program is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science and its applications, preparing them for careers in the rapidly evolving IT industry. AMU, with its rich academic heritage and modern infrastructure, offers an ideal environment for students to excel in this field through a blend of theoretical and practical learning.</p>

<h2 id="selection-process">Selection Process</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Entrance Exam Performance</strong>: Candidates must appear for the AMU Entrance Test.</li>
<li><strong>Merit List &amp;amp; Score Declaration</strong>: Results are released, and candidates are ranked based on their scores.</li>
<li><strong>Counseling</strong><strong> &amp;amp; Seat Allotment</strong>: Qualified candidates participate in counseling sessions for seat allocation.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Selected candidates complete document verification and fee payment to confirm admission.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="syllabus-for-b-sc-hons-computer-science-amp-amp-applications">Syllabus for B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science &amp;amp; Applications</h2>
