<h1 id="symbiosis-entrance-test-set-for-bca-bba-it">Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET) for BCA / BBA-IT </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET) is a university-level entrance examination conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) for admission to various undergraduate programs, including the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and Bachelor of Business Administration in Information Technology (BBA-IT). The exam assesses candidates' proficiency in logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English language, and general awareness. Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research (SICSR), Pune, offers these programs under Symbiosis International (Deemed University).</p>
<p>SET serves as a gateway for students aspiring to build careers in computer applications and IT-enabled business management at one of India&#8217;s premier institutions. With a focus on both technical and managerial skills, the BCA and BBA-IT programs at SICSR are designed to meet industry demands and foster innovation.</p>

<h2 id="symbiosis-set-bca-bba-it-eligibility-criteria">Symbiosis SET BCA / BBA-IT Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed the 10+2 examination (or equivalent) in any stream from a recognized board.</li>
<li>General category candidates must have secured a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in their qualifying examination.</li>
<li>SC/ST category candidates must have secured a minimum of 45% aggregate marks.</li>
<li>Candidates appearing for their 12th board examinations can also apply.</li>
<li>There is no age limit for applying to the program.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="set-selection-process">SET Selection Process</h2>
<p>The selection process for admission to BCA and BBA-IT at Symbiosis International (Deemed University) involves multiple stages:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Symbiosis Entrance Test (SET)</strong>: Candidates must appear for the SET General exam.</li>
<li><strong>Shortlisting for PI-WAT</strong>: Based on SET scores, shortlisted candidates are invited for the next round.</li>
<li><strong>Personal Interaction &amp;amp; Written Ability Test (PI-WAT)</strong>: Candidates are assessed on communication skills, writing ability, and overall aptitude.</li>
<li><strong>Final Merit List</strong>: The final selection is based on SET scores, PI-WAT performance, and academic background.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="set-for-bca-bba-it-syllabus">SET for BCA / BBA-IT Syllabus</h2>
<p>The syllabus for the Symbiosis SET (BCA / BBA-IT) includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>General English</strong>: Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading Comprehension, Synonyms &amp;amp; Antonyms, Sentence Correction.</li>
<li><strong>Quantitative Aptitude</strong>: Arithmetic, Algebra, Probability, Statistics, Time &amp;amp; Work, Percentage, Profit &amp;amp; Loss.</li>
<li><strong>General Awareness</strong>: Current Affairs, Business &amp;amp; Economy, Science &amp;amp; Technology, Sports, Awards &amp;amp; Honors.</li>
<li><strong>Analytical &amp;amp; Logical Reasoning</strong>: Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Series, Puzzles, Syllogisms.</li>
</ul>
