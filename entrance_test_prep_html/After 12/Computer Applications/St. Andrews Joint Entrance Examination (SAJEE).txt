<h1 id="st-andrews-joint-entrance-examination-sajee">St. Andrews Joint Entrance Examination (SAJEE) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>SAJEE, or the St. Andrews Joint Entrance Examination, is a national-level entrance test conducted annually by the St. Andrews Institute of Technology and Management (SAITM). This exam serves as a gateway for admission to undergraduate courses in B.Tech, BBA, and BCA, affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).</p>
<p>SAJEE aims to admit students who exhibit strong moral, intellectual, artistic, and athletic values while emphasizing practical training, self-learning, and discipline. Successful candidates are also eligible for scholarships of up to &#8377;2&#8377;2 lakh, making it an attractive opportunity for students across India to pursue quality education in diverse fields.</p>

<h2 id="sajee-eligibility-criteria">SAJEE Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<h3 id="for-bba-bca">For BBA/BCA:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed or be appearing for their 10+2 examinations.</li>
<li>A minimum of 45% aggregate marks is required.</li>
<li>Mathematics is not mandatory for BCA applicants.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-b-tech-4-year-program">For B.Tech (4-Year Program):</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates should have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology/Computer Science as core subjects.</li>

<h3 id="for-b-tech-lateral-entry-8211-direct-admission-into-2nd-year">For B.Tech (Lateral Entry &#8211; Direct Admission into 2nd Year):</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have completed a Diploma (Polytechnic) or a B.Sc. degree from a recognized university.</li>
<li>A minimum of 50% marks is required in the Diploma/Bachelor's degree.</li>
<li>Selection is based on SAJEE score, interview performance, and counseling process.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="why-choose-sajee">Why Choose SAJEE?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Recognized Programs</strong>: Courses affiliated with MDU and AICTE-approved, ensuring credibility and quality.</li>
<li><strong>Flexible Admission Criteria</strong>: Admission based on 12th marks, SAJEE score, or JEE Main rank, offering multiple pathways.</li>
<li><strong>Generous Scholarships</strong>: Financial assistance of up to&#160;&#8377;2&#8377;2&#160;lakh for eligible students, reducing the burden of tuition fees.</li>
<li><strong>Diverse Course Options</strong>: Engineering, Business, and IT programs tailored for future careers in high-demand sectors.</li>
<li><strong>National-Level Exam</strong>: Open to students from all across India, promoting inclusivity and diversity.</li>
</ul>
