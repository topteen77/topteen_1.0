<h1 id="indraprastha-university-common-entrance-test-ipu-cet">Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test (IPU CET) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>IPU CET (Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test) is a university-level entrance exam conducted by Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), Delhi, for admission to various Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) programs, including Computer Applications and IT-related courses. The exam assesses candidates on their subject knowledge, logical reasoning, and analytical skills.</p>
<p>IPU CET serves as the primary admission gateway for courses like BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) and MCA (Master of Computer Applications), offering candidates opportunities in IT, software development, and related fields. The admission process includes online registration, entrance exam, result declaration, and counselling, ensuring a streamlined path to higher education at GGSIPU.</p>

<h2 id="ipu-cet-eligibility-criteria-for-bca-amp-amp-mca">IPU CET Eligibility Criteria for BCA &amp;amp; MCA</h2>
<h3 id="for-bca-bachelor-of-computer-applications">For BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications):</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Class 12) from a recognized board.</li>
<li>A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required.</li>
<li>Must have English as a core, elective, or functional subject.</li>
<li>Mathematics or one of the following subjects is mandatory:</li>
<ul>
<li>Computer Science</li>
<li>Informatics Practices</li>
<li>Computer Applications</li>
<li>Multimedia &amp;amp; Web Technology</li>
<li>Data Management Applications</li>
<li>Web Application</li>
</ul>
<li>Candidates with a three-year Diploma in Engineering (approved by AICTE) with a minimum of 50% marks are also eligible.</li>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>: Minimum Age: 18 years; Maximum Age: 21 years.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="for-mca-master-of-computer-applications">For MCA (Master of Computer Applications):</h3>
<ul>
<li>Candidates must have a Bachelor&#8217;s degree (BCA/B.Sc./B.Tech/B.Com/B.A.) with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognized university.</li>
<li>Mathematics must have been studied at 10+2 or graduation level.</li>
<li><strong>Age Limit</strong>: No age limit for MCA admissions.</li>
</ul>
