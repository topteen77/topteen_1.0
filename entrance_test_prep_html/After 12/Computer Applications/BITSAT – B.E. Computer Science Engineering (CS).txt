<h1 id="birla-institute-of-technology-and-science-admission-test-bitsat-for-b-e-computer">Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT) for B.E. Computer Science </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test (BITSAT) is a national-level entrance examination conducted by BITS Pilani for admission to its various undergraduate engineering programs, including B.E. in Computer Science. BITS Pilani is one of India's most prestigious private engineering institutions, renowned for its rigorous curriculum, research-driven approach, and exceptional placement records.</p>
<p>BITSAT is a highly competitive exam that evaluates candidates on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Logical Reasoning. Admission is offered at BITS campuses in Pilani, Goa, and Hyderabad, providing students with access to world-class education and opportunities in the field of computer science and technology.</p>

<h2 id="bitsat-selection-process-for-b-e-computer-science">BITSAT Selection Process for B.E. Computer Science</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>BITSAT Examination</strong>: Candidates must appear for the online test, which assesses their aptitude in PCM, English, and Logical Reasoning.</li>
<li><strong>Merit List &amp;amp; Score Announcement</strong>: BITS releases a merit list based on candidates' performance in BITSAT.</li>
<li><strong>Counseling</strong><strong> &amp;amp; Seat Allotment</strong>: Shortlisted candidates are invited for the admission process, where they select their preferred campus and specialization.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Candidates must complete document verification and fee payment to confirm admission.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="bitsat-syllabus-for-b-e-computer-science">BITSAT Syllabus for B.E. Computer Science</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics</strong>: Algebra, Trigonometry, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Probability, Vector Algebra, Differential Equations.</li>
<li><strong>Physics</strong>: Mechanics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Optics, Modern Physics, Thermodynamics.</li>
<li><strong>Chemistry</strong>: Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Electrochemistry.</li>
<li><strong>English Proficiency</strong>: Reading Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Sentence Correction.</li>
<li><strong>Logical Reasoning</strong>: Data Interpretation, Puzzles, Series, Pattern Recognition, Critical Thinking.</li>
</ul>

<li>B.E. Computer Science with Cyber Security</li>

<p>These programs focus on advanced areas such as algorithms, machine learning, big data, cybersecurity, and software development.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-bitsat">How to Apply for BITSAT?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the Official Website</strong>: Go to&#160;.</li>
<li><strong>Register Online</strong>: Create an account by providing your email ID and mobile number.</li>
<li><strong>Fill in the Application Form</strong>: Enter personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong>: Upload scanned copies of photograph and signature.</li>
<li><strong>Pay the Application Fee</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Rs. 3400 for male candidates</li>
<li>Rs. 2900 for female candidates</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Book Exam Slot</strong>: Choose the preferred date and time for the exam.</li>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong>: Once the slot is booked, the admit card will be available for download.</li>
<li><strong>Appear for the Exam</strong>: Take the test as per the allotted schedule.</li>
</ul>
