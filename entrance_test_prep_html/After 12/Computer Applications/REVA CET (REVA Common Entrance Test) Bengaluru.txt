<h1 id="reva-common-entrance-test-reva-cet">REVA Common Entrance Test (REVA CET) </h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details">Overview and Details</h2>
<p>REVA CET is the Common Entrance Test conducted by REVA University, Bengaluru, for admission to its Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and Ph.D. programs, along with scholarships for meritorious students. The exam is designed to evaluate candidates based on their aptitude, subject knowledge, and logical reasoning skills. Apart from research and doctoral programs, REVA University currently offers 34 UG programs, 26 full-time PG programs, and 2 part-time PG programs in various disciplines across its multiple schools.</p>
<p>The exam is conducted annually and serves as a gateway for admissions into a wide range of courses offered by the university, providing students with opportunities to pursue quality education in a dynamic and innovative environment.</p>

<h2 id="scholarship-opportunities">Scholarship Opportunities</h2>
<p>REVA University provides merit-based scholarships to students who perform exceptionally well in REVA CET. The scholarship percentage is based on the candidate&#8217;s score in the entrance exam:</p>
<ul>
<li>Top 5% candidates: 100% Tuition Fee Waiver</li>
<li>5% - 10% candidates: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver</li>
<li>10% - 20% candidates: 25% Tuition Fee Waiver</li>

</ul>
<p>Apart from merit scholarships, other financial aid options include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Need-based Scholarships</li>
<li>Sports Scholarships</li>
<li>Alumni-based Concessions</li>
<li>Government Scholarships for Reserved Categories</li>
</ul>
