<h1 id="srm-joint-engineering-entrance-examination-srmjeee-for-b-tech-cse">SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination (SRMJEEE) for B.Tech CSE</h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details"> Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination (SRMJEEE) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) for admission to various B.Tech programs, including B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE). The exam is conducted in an online Computer-Based Test (CBT) format and evaluates candidates on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Aptitude.</p>
<p>SRMJEEE serves as the primary admission test for SRMIST campuses in Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani (all in Tamil Nadu), and NCR (Delhi). SRMIST is recognized for its strong industry-oriented curriculum, high placement records, international collaborations, and advanced research facilities, making it a preferred destination for engineering aspirants.</p>

<h2 id="srmjeee-selection-process-for-b-tech-cse">SRMJEEE Selection Process for B.Tech CSE</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>SRMJEEE Examination</strong>: Candidates must appear for the SRMJEEE exam in online mode.</li>
<li><strong>Merit List &amp;amp; Rank Announcement</strong>: SRMIST releases a merit list based on exam scores. Candidates are assigned ranks accordingly.</li>
<li><strong>Counseling</strong><strong> &amp;amp; Seat Allotment</strong>: Shortlisted candidates are called for counseling, where they choose their preferred campus and specialization based on rank and seat availability.</li>
<li><strong>Final Admission</strong>: Candidates must complete document verification and pay the required tuition fees to confirm admission.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="srmjeee-syllabus-for-b-tech-cse">SRMJEEE Syllabus for B.Tech CSE</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics</strong>: Algebra, Trigonometry, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Probability, Vector Algebra, Differential Equations.</li>
<li><strong>Physics</strong>: Mechanics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Optics, Modern Physics, Thermodynamics.</li>
<li><strong>Chemistry</strong>: Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Electrochemistry.</li>
<li><strong>English</strong>: Reading Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, Sentence Correction.</li>
<li><strong>Aptitude</strong>: Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation, Numerical Ability, Puzzle Solving.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="srmjeee-specializations-in-computer-science-and-engineering">SRMJEEE Specializations in Computer Science and Engineering</h2>
<p>SRMIST offers multiple specializations in B.Tech CSE, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (Core)</li>
<li>B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning</li>
<li>B.Tech CSE with Cyber Security</li>
<li>B.Tech CSE with Data Science</li>
<li>B.Tech CSE with Software Engineering</li>
<li>B.Tech CSE with Cloud Computing and Big Data Analytics</li>
</ul>
<p>These specializations provide students with skills in AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, data analytics, and software development.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-apply-for-srmjeee">How to Apply for SRMJEEE?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Visit the Official Website</strong>: Go to&#160;.</li>
<li><strong>Register Online</strong>: Create an account by providing your email ID and mobile number.</li>
<li><strong>Fill in the Application Form</strong>: Enter personal, academic, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Upload Documents</strong>: Upload scanned copies of photograph and signature.</li>
<li><strong>Pay the Application Fee</strong>: The application fee is approximately Rs. 1200, payable online.</li>
<li><strong>Book Exam Slot</strong>: Select the preferred date and time for the exam.</li>
<li><strong>Download Admit Card</strong>: Once the slot is booked, the admit card will be generated.</li>
<li><strong>Appear for the Exam</strong>: Take the test as per the allotted date and time.</li>
</ul>
