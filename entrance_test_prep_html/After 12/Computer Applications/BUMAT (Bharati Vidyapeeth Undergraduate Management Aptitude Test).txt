<h1 id="bharati-vidyapeeth-undergraduate-management-aptitude-test-bumat">Bharati Vidyapeeth Undergraduate Management Aptitude Test (BUMAT)</h1>

<h2 id="overview-and-details"> Overview and Details</h2>
<p>The Bharati Vidyapeeth Undergraduate Management Aptitude Test (BUMAT) is a national-level entrance examination conducted by Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University) for admission to its Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) programs. The exam is held annually in multiple locations across India and is open only to Indian citizens. BUMAT is conducted in an online mode as a Computer-Based Test (CBT).</p>
<p>This entrance exam evaluates candidates on their numerical aptitude, reasoning ability, verbal ability, and reading comprehension skills. Admission to BBA and BCA courses at Bharati Vidyapeeth is based on the candidate&#8217;s performance in BUMAT, making it a critical step for aspiring students seeking quality education in management and computer applications.</p>

<h2 id="bumat-eligibility-criteria">BUMAT Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li>The candidate must be an Indian citizen.</li>
<li>There is no upper age limit for appearing in BUMAT.</li>
<li>Candidates must have passed the 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board.</li>
<li><strong>Minimum Qualifying Marks</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>General Category: 45%</li>
<li>SC/ST Category: 40%</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<li><strong>Fill in Details</strong>: Provide personal and academic details accurately.</li>

</ul>
