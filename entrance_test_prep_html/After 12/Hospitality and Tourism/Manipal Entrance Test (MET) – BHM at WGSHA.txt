<h1 id="manipal-entrance-test-met-8211-bhm-at-welcomgroup-school-of-hotel-administration">Manipal Entrance Test (MET) &#8211; BHM at Welcomgroup School of Hotel Administration (WGSHA)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The Manipal Entrance Test (MET) &#8211; BHM at Welcomgroup School of Hotel Administration (WGSHA) is the gateway for admission into the esteemed Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) program offered by WGSHA, a premier hospitality education institute under the Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) in Manipal, Karnataka. Known for its rigorous academic standards, state-of-the-art facilities, and strong industry linkages with leading hospitality organizations like the ITC Hotels group, WGSHA has established itself as a leader in hospitality education in India. The MET &#8211; BHM evaluates candidates on their quantitative skills, verbal proficiency, logical reasoning, and industry-related knowledge, ensuring that only the most capable individuals are prepared for a successful career in the dynamic hospitality sector. For the most recent updates and detailed instructions, please refer to the WGSHA Official Website at .</p>

<h2 id="objective-of-the-exam">Objective of the Exam</h2>
<p>The MET &#8211; BHM scorecard is an essential criterion for enrollment into the BHM program at WGSHA. Its primary aims are to:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ensure Uniform Assessment</strong>: Provide a consistent evaluation framework for all applicants from diverse academic backgrounds, maintaining fairness in the selection process.</li>
<li><strong>Facilitate Admission Decisions</strong>: Serve as the key basis for shortlisting candidates for the BHM program at WGSHA, streamlining the admission procedure.</li>
<li><strong>Identify Talent Early</strong>: Recognize individuals with strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving abilities, along with a keen interest in hospitality, which are crucial for thriving in the industry.</li>
<li><strong>Guarantee Transparent Testing</strong>: Utilize a computer-based (or as specified) format to minimize errors and bias, ensuring an impartial and objective assessment process.</li>
</ul>
