<h1 id="ipu-cet-bba">IPU CET (BBA)</h1>
<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The IPU CET (BBA) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (IPU) for admission to its integrated BBA programs. Designed to assess candidates&#8217; quantitative aptitude, verbal proficiency, logical reasoning, and business awareness, this exam serves as the gateway to a comprehensive management education that blends undergraduate studies with real-world business insights. Many of these programs offer specialized tracks in areas such as Marketing, Finance, and Entrepreneurship, preparing students for dynamic careers in the business world. IPU, based in Delhi, is recognized for its quality education and industry-oriented curriculum across its affiliated colleges. For the most up-to-date information and complete guidelines, please visit the IPU Official Website at .</p>

<h2 id="purpose-amp-amp-objectives">Purpose &amp;amp; Objectives</h2>
<p>The IPU CET (BBA) score is a vital criterion in the admission process, serving the following key objectives:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Establish a Common Benchmark</strong>: Provide a standardized method to evaluate candidates from diverse academic backgrounds, ensuring fairness in selection.</li>
<li><strong>Streamline Admissions</strong>: Serve as the primary tool for shortlisting applicants for integrated BBA programs offered by IPU and its affiliated institutes.</li>
<li><strong>Identify Future Leaders</strong>: Detect candidates with strong quantitative, verbal, and analytical skills, along with an aptitude for business and entrepreneurship, who can excel in management roles.</li>
<li><strong>Ensure Transparency</strong>: Utilize an online, computer-based test format to deliver an objective, unbiased assessment for all participants.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="application-procedure">Application Procedure</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to apply for IPU CET (BBA) for integrated BBA programs, noting that specific processes may be updated periodically by IPU:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Visit the IPU CET portal on the official website at&#160;&#160;and navigate to the BBA application section.</li>
<li>Complete the online application form by filling in all required personal, academic, and contact details accurately.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Document Submission</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Upload scanned copies of required documents, including academic transcripts (10+2 mark sheets or equivalent), identity proofs, a recent passport-sized photograph, and category certificates (for reserved categories) if applicable, in the prescribed format.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Application Fee</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Remit the prescribed fee via the online payment gateway provided on the portal (credit/debit cards, net banking, etc.). The fee amount may vary based on category or program, with exact details available on the official website.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Submission</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Review all details carefully and submit your application before the deadline.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Admit Card Download</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Once released, download and print your admit card from the IPU website. The admit card is mandatory for exam entry and contains essential details like test center, timing, and instructions.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure that all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements to avoid rejection of the application. Keep a copy of the submitted application and payment receipt for future reference.
