<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>The St. Xavier&#8217;s BMS Entrance (Mumbai) is the official entrance exam conducted by St. Xavier&#8217;s College, Mumbai, for admission to its flagship Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) program.
