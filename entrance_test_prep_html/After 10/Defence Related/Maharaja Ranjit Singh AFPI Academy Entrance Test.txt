<h1 id="maharaja-ranjit-singh-armed-forces-preparatory-institute-afpi-punjab">Maharaja Ranjit Singh Armed Forces Preparatory Institute (AFPI Punjab)</h1>

<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The Maharaja Ranjit Singh Armed Forces Preparatory Institute (AFPI Punjab) is a premier institution dedicated to identifying and nurturing young talent for a career in the armed forces. Located in Mohali, Punjab, the institute prepares candidates for various army entrance examinations, primarily focusing on the National Defence Academy (NDA) and other military selection processes, by offering a specialized two-year training program that integrates academic coaching, physical fitness, and leadership development.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Key Objectives</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Holistic Preparation</strong>: Equip students with the academic knowledge, physical endurance, and leadership qualities needed for success in army entrance exams.</li>
<li><strong>Rigorous Training</strong>: Provide structured coaching sessions in subjects relevant to entrance tests (such as Mathematics, English, General Knowledge, and Reasoning) alongside comprehensive physical training programs.</li>
<li><strong>Competitive Edge</strong>: Ensure that candidates not only clear the entrance exams but also excel in subsequent selection rounds like interviews and group discussions.&#160;<strong>Note</strong>: Always refer to the latest official AFPI Punjab recruitment notification on the official website (afpipunjab.org) for the most accurate and updated information before applying. Official notifications provide detailed eligibility updates, region-specific requirements, and test schedules , , .</li>
</ul>
</ul>
