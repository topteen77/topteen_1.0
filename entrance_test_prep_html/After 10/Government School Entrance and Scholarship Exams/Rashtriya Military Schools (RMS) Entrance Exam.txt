<h1 id="rashtriya-military-school-entrance-exam-cet">Rashtriya Military School Entrance Exam (CET)</h1>
<h2 id="1-introduction">1. Introduction</h2>
<p>Rashtriya Military Schools (RMS) are residential public schools under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, aimed at preparing students for future careers in the Armed Forces and other leadership roles. Established as King George&#8217;s Royal Indian Military Schools in the early 20th century, RMS currently operate in five locations across India: RMS Chail (Himachal Pradesh), RMS Ajmer (Rajasthan), RMS Belgaum (Karnataka), RMS Bangalore (Karnataka), and RMS Dholpur (Rajasthan). The Common Entrance Test (CET) for admission to Class 6 and Class 9 is held annually, offering successful candidates access to high-quality academics, a strong military ethos, sports, and extracurricular activities crucial for all-round development. RMS focuses on holistic growth through academics, sports, leadership, and character-building, preparing cadets for entry into the National Defence Academy (NDA), Indian Naval Academy, other officer training academies, or higher civilian career paths.</p>

<h2 id="5-syllabus-outline">5. Syllabus Outline</h2>
<ul>
<li>Though RMS does not issue a fixed syllabus, questions align with the NCERT curriculum of Classes 4&#8211;5 (for Class 6 exam) and 7&#8211;8 (for Class 9 exam):</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mathematics</strong>: Basic arithmetic, algebraic expressions, factors &amp;amp; multiples, geometry, data handling, mensuration.</li>
<li><strong>English</strong>: Grammar (tenses, prepositions, articles), vocabulary, comprehension passages, sentence formation.</li>
<li><strong>Science (Class 9 only)</strong>: Physical/chemical changes, motion, force &amp;amp; pressure, cells &amp;amp; organisms, basic environmental science.</li>
<li><strong>Social Studies (Class 9 only)</strong>: Indian &amp;amp; world geography, medieval/modern history basics, civics structure, fundamental economics concepts.</li>
<li><strong>General Intelligence</strong>: Puzzles, coding-decoding, sequences, analogies, figure classification, pattern recognition.</li>
<li><strong>GK &amp;amp; Current Affairs</strong>: National &amp;amp; international events, general knowledge about India (states, capitals, culture), basic scientific facts.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Note</strong>: Candidates should focus on NCERT textbooks and practice reasoning skills for the intelligence section.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="6-reservation-amp-amp-seat-allocation">6. Reservation &amp;amp; Seat Allocation</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Defence Category</strong>: A certain percentage of seats are reserved for children of serving/ex-servicemen (Army, Navy, Air Force).</li>
<li><strong>SC/ST/OBC</strong>: Governed by Government of India&#8217;s reservation norms.</li>
<li><strong>Open / General Category</strong>: Remaining seats are allocated to general category candidates.</li>
<li><strong>Girls</strong>: Rashtriya Military Schools have traditionally been for boys, but some schools now admit girls subject to policy changes and seat availability.</li>
<li><strong>Note</strong>: Check the latest notification or official RMS website for updated reservation policies.</li>
</ul>

<ul>

<ul>
<li>Forms are typically available on the Rashtriya Military School website (rashtriyamilitaryschools.edu.in).</li>
<li>Notifications are usually released a few months before the exam (tentatively September&#8211;November).</li>

<li><strong>Application Fee</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Varies by category (General/Defence/SC/ST).</li>
<li>Payment modes include Demand Draft, online bank transfer, or as specified in the notification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Documents Required</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Birth certificate, ID proof, category certificate (if applicable), previous class mark sheets, etc.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Note</strong>: Ensure accurate submission of details and documents during registration to avoid rejection.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="8-exam-day-amp-amp-result">8. Exam Day &amp;amp; Result</h2>
<ul>

<ul>

<h2 id="9-selection-amp-amp-admission">9. Selection &amp;amp; Admission</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Merit List</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Based on CET score and category-wise cutoffs.</li>
<li>Some RMS might conduct a short interview/viva for final verification.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Medical Examination</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>Conducted at designated military/government hospitals; physical fitness and good health are critical.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Final Offer</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li>After clearing the written exam, interview (if any), and medical exam, candidates are allotted seats based on merit and reservation.</li>
<li>Students must complete admission formalities, pay fees, and join the school when the session begins.</li>
</ul>
</ul>

<h2 id="11-preparation-tips">11. Preparation Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Focus on NCERT Basics</strong>: Review Class 4&#8211;5 (for Class 6 exam) or 7&#8211;8 (for Class 9 exam) thoroughly, especially Math and Science.</li>
<li><strong>Regular Practice</strong>: Solve sample papers, previous years&#8217; CET question papers if available, and standard MCQ-based test books.</li>
<li><strong>Time Management</strong>: Practice finishing subject sections within allocated time; attempt easier questions first.</li>
<li><strong>Improve Language Skills</strong>: Read short stories, practice grammar exercises, and increase vocabulary.</li>
<li><strong>Stay Updated with GK</strong>: Read newspapers, watch educational news segments, and memorize basic facts about Indian states, capitals, and recent events.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="12-significance-of-rashtriya-military-schools">12. Significance of Rashtriya Military Schools</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Military Ethos &amp;amp; Discipline</strong>: Students develop leadership qualities, camaraderie, and discipline aligning with defence services culture.</li>
<li><strong>CBSE Curriculum</strong>: Prepares them well for board exams and future competitive exams (NDA, JEE, NEET, etc.).</li>
<li><strong>Holistic Development</strong>: Emphasis on sports, extracurriculars, and moral education alongside academics.</li>
<li><strong>Defence Career Path</strong>: RMS alumni frequently join the National Defence Academy (NDA) and other officer training institutes, benefiting from early exposure to military values.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="14-conclusion">14. Conclusion</h2>
<p>The Rashtriya Military School Entrance Exam (CET) is a gateway to a highly disciplined, academically rigorous, and value-driven educational environment. Success in the Common Entrance Test requires a strong foundation in core subjects, adept reasoning skills, and consistent practice with mock tests. Prospective students who clear the written exam, interview (if applicable), and medical checks can look forward to an enriching journey that blends scholastic excellence, character-building, and military-oriented training, paving the way for future leadership roles in the Armed Forces or civilian sectors.</p>
