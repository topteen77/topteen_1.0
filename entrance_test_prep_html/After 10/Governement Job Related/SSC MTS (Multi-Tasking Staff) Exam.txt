<h1 id="ssc-mts-multi-tasking-staff-examination">SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff) Examination</h1>
<h2 id="1-overview">1. Overview</h2>
<p>The SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff) exam is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) to recruit candidates for various non-technical, clerical, and support staff positions in central government departments and offices across India. These roles encompass a wide range of responsibilities, including peon, daftary, jamadar, junior gestetner operator, chowkidar, safaiwala, mali, and other similar support functions such as Havaldar (under CBIC and CBN). The examination process is designed to evaluate candidates on their basic academic abilities and aptitude, ensuring they are equipped to handle the administrative and support tasks required in a government setting.</p>
<h2 id="2-eligibility-criteria">2. Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<h3 id="2-1-educational-qualification">2.1 Educational Qualification</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Minimum Requirement</strong>: Candidates must have passed Class 10 (Matriculation) or an equivalent examination from a recognized board. There is no requirement for higher education for SSC MTS posts.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="2-2-age-limit">2.2 Age Limit</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>General Age Range</strong>: Candidates are typically required to be within a specific age bracket, generally between 18 and 25 years for most posts. For certain departments or roles like Havaldar, the upper age limit may extend to 27 years. Exact details are provided in the official notification.</li>
<li><strong>Age Relaxation</strong>: Relaxations in the upper age limit are generally available for reserved categories as per government norms:</li>
<ul>
<li>OBC: 3 years</li>
<li>SC/ST: 5 years</li>
<li>PwD (Persons with Disabilities): 10 years (additional relaxations for reserved categories)</li>
<li>Other categories (e.g., ex-servicemen, widows, etc.): As per government rules mentioned in the notification.</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<h3 id="2-3-nationality-amp-amp-other-requirements">2.3 Nationality &amp;amp; Other Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Citizenship</strong>: Only Indian citizens are eligible to apply. In some cases, subjects of Nepal, Bhutan, or Tibetan refugees who came to India before a specified date (as per government norms) may also be eligible, subject to conditions in the notification.</li>
<li><strong>Additional Criteria</strong>: Candidates should have a clean record with no criminal background and meet any other conditions stated in the official notification, such as physical standards for certain roles.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="3-exam-pattern-and-syllabus">3. Exam Pattern and Syllabus</h2>
<h3 id="3-1-written-examination">3.1 Written Examination</h3>
<p>The SSC MTS exam is conducted in two sessions, both of which are computer-based tests (CBT).</p>

<p><strong>Session 1</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mode</strong>: Computer-Based Test (Online)</li>
<li><strong>Question Type</strong>: Objective Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)</li>
<li><strong>Duration</strong>: Approximately 45 minutes</li>
<li><strong>Sections Covered</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>Numerical and Mathematical Ability</strong>: Basic arithmetic, percentages, ratios, averages, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance, etc.</li>
<li><strong>Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving</strong>: Logical reasoning, puzzles, series, analogies, coding-decoding, pattern recognition, etc.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Marking Scheme</strong>: No negative marking in Session 1. Each question carries equal marks (typically&#160;33&#160;marks per question).</li>

</ul>
<p><strong>Session 2</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mode</strong>: Computer-Based Test (Online)</li>
<li><strong>Question Type</strong>: Objective Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)</li>
<li><strong>Duration</strong>: Approximately 45 minutes</li>
<li><strong>Sections Covered</strong>:</li>
<ul>
<li><strong>General Awareness</strong>: Current affairs, history, geography, polity, economics, basic science, and cultural topics.</li>
<li><strong>English Language and Comprehension</strong>: Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, and reading comprehension.</li>
</ul>
<li><strong>Marking Scheme</strong>: Negative marking is applicable in Session 2 (typically&#160;11&#160;mark deducted for each wrong answer).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: The exact number of questions, total marks, and sectional weightage may vary with each recruitment cycle. Always refer to the latest official notification for precise details. Both sessions are conducted on the same day, and qualifying Session 1 is mandatory to have Session 2 evaluated.</p>
<h3 id="3-2-other-considerations">3.2 Other Considerations</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Skill Test or Physical Efficiency Test (PET)/Physical Standard Test (PST)</strong>: In some instances, depending on the specific nature of the support role (e.g., Havaldar under CBIC and CBN), there might be an additional on-site skill test or practical assessment such as walking, cycling, or other physical tasks, along with height and chest measurements as per norms specified in the notification. For most SSC MTS posts, however, the primary evaluation is based on the written exam.</li>
<li><strong>Computer Proficiency</strong>: As the exam is computer-based, basic computer literacy and comfort with online test interfaces are beneficial.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="4-application-process">4. Application Process</h2>
<h3 id="4-1-steps-to-apply">4.1 Steps to Apply</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Official Notification</strong>: The recruitment notice is published on the official SSC website (ssc.nic.in) and other government portals. It contains all necessary details regarding vacancies, exam dates, and application procedures.</li>
<li><strong>Online Registration</strong>: Applications are submitted online through the SSC portal. Applicants must fill in their details accurately, upload scanned images of required documents (photograph, signature, etc.), and pay the application fee (if applicable).</li>
<li><strong>Application Fee</strong>: A nominal fee (usually around&#160;100100&#160;INR) may be required. Exemptions or concessions might be provided for reserved category candidates (SC/ST, PwD, ex-servicemen, and female candidates) as per government norms.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="4-2-important-dates-tentative">4.2 Important Dates (Tentative)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Application Window</strong>: The notification specifies the opening and closing dates for application submissions, typically spanning a month, often around February-March or as per the SSC calendar.</li>
<li><strong>Admit Card Release</strong>: Admit cards are issued on the SSC website a few weeks before the exam. It is mandatory to download and carry the admit card to the exam center along with a valid ID proof.</li>
<li><strong>Exam Date</strong>: The written examination is generally conducted in multiple shifts over several days, often between April and June, depending on the recruitment cycle.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="5-document-verification">5. Document Verification</h2>
<h3 id="5-1-required-documents">5.1 Required Documents</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Educational Certificates</strong>: Original Class 10 certificate or mark sheet along with a photocopy.</li>
<li><strong>Proof of Age</strong>: Birth certificate, Class 10 certificate, or any other official document that confirms the applicant&#8217;s age.</li>
<li><strong>Proof of Identity &amp;amp; Citizenship</strong>: Documents such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport that establish Indian citizenship.</li>
<li><strong>Additional Certificates</strong>: Any other documents as specified in the notification, for example, caste/category certificates for reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD), ex-servicemen certificates, etc.</li>
<li><strong>Photograph and Signature</strong>: Recent passport-sized photographs and scanned signatures as per the format mentioned in the application guidelines.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="5-2-verification-process">5.2 Verification Process</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Timing</strong>: Candidates will be required to present original documents along with their copies at designated verification centers, either on the day of the exam or at a later stage after being shortlisted based on written exam (and PET/PST, if applicable) performance.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="6-preparation-tips">6. Preparation Tips</h2>
<h3 id="6-1-for-the-written-examination">6.1 For the Written Examination</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Study Materials</strong>: Utilize standard textbooks and practice materials focusing on quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, English language skills, and general awareness. Recommended resources include books by R.S. Aggarwal for Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning, and Lucent&#8217;s for General Knowledge. Online platforms also offer study materials and video tutorials.</li>
<li><strong>Mock Tests &amp;amp; Previous Papers</strong>: Practicing with sample tests and previous year question papers can help you become familiar with the exam pattern and improve your time management.</li>
<li><strong>Time Management</strong>: Develop strategies to handle the time pressure during the exam, ensuring you answer all questions within the allotted duration. Prioritize accuracy in Session 2 due to negative marking.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="6-2-general-advice">6.2 General Advice</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stay Updated</strong>: Keep an eye on the official SSC website (ssc.nic.in) and trusted sources for any updates or changes in the exam pattern or schedule.</li>
<li><strong>Document Organization</strong>: Ensure all your documents are up-to-date, properly scanned, and ready to be uploaded during the application process or for verification.</li>
<li><strong>Physical Preparation (if applicable)</strong>: For roles requiring PET/PST, maintain physical fitness and practice tasks like walking or cycling as per the specified standards.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="7-conclusion">7. Conclusion</h2>
<p>The SSC MTS recruitment test is a gateway to clerical and support staff positions within Central Government organizations. With the primary eligibility requirement being a Class 10 pass, the exam tests fundamental skills across quantitative aptitude, reasoning, language, and general awareness. A thorough understanding of the exam pattern, regular practice, and meticulous preparation&#8212;both academically and in managing application formalities&#8212;will help candidates maximize their chances of success.</p>
<p>For the most current details, including specific dates, vacancy numbers, or changes in exam patterns for the upcoming cycle, candidates are advised to refer to the latest official SSC notification on ssc.nic.in.</p>
