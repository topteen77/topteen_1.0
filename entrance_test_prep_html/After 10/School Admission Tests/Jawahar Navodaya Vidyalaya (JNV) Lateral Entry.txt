<ul>
<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) are premier residential schools established under the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti to provide quality education to talented students, predominantly from rural areas of India. The lateral entry scheme for Class 11 is designed for students who have successfully completed Class 10 (or equivalent) and wish to join a JNV at the senior secondary level. This pathway offers an opportunity to access high-quality education, residential facilities, and comprehensive academic and extracurricular programs at no or minimal cost. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam patterns, syllabus, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission through this esteemed program.</p>

<li><strong>3. Key Features of JNV Lateral Entry</strong></li>
<li><strong>Free Quality Education</strong>: Comprehensive academic curriculum provided at no or minimal cost.</li>
<li><strong>Residential Facilities</strong>: Free boarding and lodging to ensure a conducive learning environment.</li>
<li><strong>Holistic Development</strong>: Access to extracurricular programs, sports, and cultural activities alongside academics.</li>
<li><strong>Scholarship Opportunities</strong>: Merit-based financial aid or stipends may be available for deserving students.</li>
<li><strong>Focus on Rural Talent</strong>: Special emphasis on identifying and nurturing talent from rural and underprivileged backgrounds.</li>
</ul>
