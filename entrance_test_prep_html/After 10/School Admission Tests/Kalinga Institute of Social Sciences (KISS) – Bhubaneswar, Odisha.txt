<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Kalinga Institute of Social Sciences (KISS), located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, is a pioneering institution dedicated to transforming the lives of underprivileged tribal students through free, high-quality residential education. Recognized globally as a model for social transformation, KISS empowers students academically, culturally, and socially, with a mission to bridge educational gaps and promote social equity. The institute provides education from primary to higher levels, including Class 11 admissions, focusing on holistic development. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, selection process, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission to this transformative institution.</p>

<li><strong>3. Key Features of KISS Admissions</strong></li>
<li><strong>Free Residential Education</strong>: No tuition fees or boarding charges, ensuring access to quality education for economically disadvantaged tribal communities.</li>
<li><strong>Holistic Development</strong>: Emphasis on academics, extracurricular activities, vocational training, sports, and overall personality development.</li>
<li><strong>Social </strong><strong>Upliftment</strong>: Special focus on students from tribal backgrounds to bridge educational gaps, promote social equity, and preserve cultural heritage.</li>
<li><strong>Comprehensive Support</strong>: Provides not only education but also life skills training, healthcare, and nutritional support to students.</li>
<li><strong>Global Recognition</strong>: KISS is recognized internationally for its unique model of empowering marginalized communities through education.</li>
</ul>
