<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Mayo College, located in Ajmer, Rajasthan, is one of India&#8217;s premier residential schools, celebrated for its rich heritage, academic excellence, and holistic development approach. Established in 1875, it has a long-standing tradition of nurturing leaders through a balanced educational environment. The lateral admission process allows students who have completed a certain level (typically Class 10 or Class 11, depending on the admission scheme) to join Mayo College at a higher class. The selection process generally includes an entrance exam, academic evaluation, document verification, and sometimes an interview or counseling session, ensuring that candidates possess strong academic records and the potential to thrive in a rigorous residential setting. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam pattern, selection process, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing lateral admission to this esteemed institution.</p>
