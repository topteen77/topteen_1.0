<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Doon School, located in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, is one of India&#8217;s most prestigious residential schools, renowned for its academic excellence, strong extracurricular programs, and emphasis on overall personality development. Established in 1935, it has a storied legacy of nurturing leaders across various fields. The school primarily admits students at Class 7 and Class 8 levels as standard entry points, with occasional lateral entry opportunities for Class 11 based on seat availability and merit. The admission process is designed to identify talented students who can excel academically and thrive in a rigorous residential environment. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam pattern, selection process, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission to this esteemed institution.</p>
