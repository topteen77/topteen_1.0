While regular admissions occur at lower levels, lateral entry into higher classes (typically after Class 10 or Class 11) is not a standard process but may be offered on a case-by-case basis when vacancies arise. The selection process for lateral entry is designed to identify students with strong academic records and the potential to integrate into the Welham system. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam pattern (if applicable), selection process, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing lateral admission to these esteemed institutions.</p>
