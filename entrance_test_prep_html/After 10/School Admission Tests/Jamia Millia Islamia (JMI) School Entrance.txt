<ul>
<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI) School Entrance exam is designed for students seeking admission to Class 11 at JMI-affiliated schools. This entrance test selects candidates for three streams: Science, Commerce, and Humanities, assessing their academic readiness and aptitude for higher secondary education. Admission through this exam offers students access to a reputed institution known for academic excellence, holistic development, and a diverse, culturally enriched learning environment with modern facilities. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam patterns, syllabus, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission through this prestigious program.</p>
