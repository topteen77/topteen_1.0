<p><strong>Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) School Entrance for Class 11</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) School Entrance exam is designed for students who have passed Class 10 and are seeking admission to Class 11 in AMU-affiliated schools. This entrance test assesses candidates&#8217; academic readiness and aptitude for higher secondary education in three major streams: Science, Arts, and Commerce. Admission through this exam offers students access to a reputed institution with a rich heritage, quality education, modern facilities, and a structured environment that promotes academic excellence and character building. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam patterns, syllabus, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission through this prestigious program.</p>
