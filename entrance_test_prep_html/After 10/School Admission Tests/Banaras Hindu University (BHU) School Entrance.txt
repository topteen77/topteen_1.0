<p><strong>Banaras Hindu University (BHU) School Entrance for Class 11</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>1. Introduction</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The Banaras Hindu University (BHU) School Entrance exam is designed for students seeking admission to Class 11 at Central Hindu Boys&#8217; School or Central Hindu Girls&#8217; School, prestigious institutions affiliated with BHU. These schools offer quality education with a rich tradition, focusing on holistic development through academic excellence and personality growth. The entrance exam evaluates candidates&#8217; academic abilities and aptitude to ensure they are well-prepared for their chosen stream: Science, Commerce, or Arts. The following sections provide detailed insights into eligibility criteria, application process, exam patterns, syllabus, preparation strategies, and other essential information to support students in securing admission through this esteemed program.</p>

<li><strong>Admission to Prestigious Schools</strong>: Access to Central Hindu Boys&#8217; School and Central Hindu Girls&#8217; School, known for their academic rigor and heritage under BHU.</li>
