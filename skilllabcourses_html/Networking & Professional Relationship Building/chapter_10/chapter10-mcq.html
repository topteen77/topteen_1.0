MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of role-playing scenarios in networking exercises?

Correct Answer: B) To practice real-life networking situations

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an essential component of a strong introduction during networking?

Correct Answer: C) Mentioning your name and a relevant detail

 

Question 3: When simulating a networking event, what is a recommended practice for participants?

Correct Answer: C) To rotate partners and meet as many people as possible

 

Question 4: What is a key benefit of setting personal networking goals?

Correct Answer: B) It provides direction and motivation

 

Question 5: What should you include in your networking journal to track progress effectively?

Correct Answer: C) Names, contact information, and conversation notes
