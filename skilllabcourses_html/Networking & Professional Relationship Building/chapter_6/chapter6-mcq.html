MCQs

Question 1: What is mentorship primarily defined as?

Correct Answer: B) A relationship in which a more experienced person guides a less experienced individual.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is NOT a benefit of having a mentor?

Correct Answer: B) Access to exclusive job offers

 

Question 3: What is one key difference between formal and informal mentors?

Correct Answer: B) Formal mentors are part of structured programs, while informal mentors arise from personal connections.

 

Question 4: When approaching a potential mentor, what is an important step to take?

Correct Answer: B) Send a brief message introducing yourself and explaining why you are reaching out.
