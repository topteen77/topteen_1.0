MCQs

Question 1: What is the main difference between competitive and collaborative negotiation?

A) Competitive focuses on mutual gain, while collaborative focuses on winning at all costs

B) Competitive is about one side winning, while collaborative aims for both sides to benefit

C) Competitive builds long-term relationships, while collaborative is for one-time deals

D) Competitive involves teamwork, while collaborative involves hiding information

Correct Answer: B) Competitive is about one side winning, while collaborative aims for both sides to benefit

 

Question 2: Why is it important to identify your interests before a negotiation?

A) It helps you hide your true goals from the other party

B) It allows you to focus on the deeper reasons behind what you want and find flexible solutions

C) It ensures you always get exactly what you ask for without compromise

D) It makes the other party agree with you immediately

Correct Answer: B) It allows you to focus on the deeper reasons behind what you want and find flexible solutions

 

Question 3: What is an effective way to understand the other party’s perspective during a negotiation?

A) Assume you know their reasons and push your own agenda

B) Interrupt them to explain why your side is more important

C) Ask open-ended questions and actively listen to their concerns

D) Ignore their feelings to stay focused on your goals

Correct Answer: C) Ask open-ended questions and actively listen to their concerns

 

Question 4: What is a key characteristic of a win-win solution in negotiation?

A) One party gets everything they want, while the other compromises completely

B) Both parties feel satisfied because their core interests are addressed

C) It only works in competitive negotiations where winning is the goal

D) It avoids any form of compromise to keep things simple

Correct Answer: B) Both parties feel satisfied because their core interests are addressed
