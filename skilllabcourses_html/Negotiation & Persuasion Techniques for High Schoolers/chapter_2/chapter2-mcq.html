MCQs

Question 1: What is a key component of active listening that shows you are fully engaged in the conversation?

A) Checking your phone occasionally to stay updated

B) Interrupting to share your own opinion quickly

C) Nodding and paraphrasing what the speaker said

D) Thinking about what to say next while the other person talks

Correct Answer: C) Nodding and paraphrasing what the speaker said

 

Question 2: When asking a teacher for extra help, which approach demonstrates effective verbal communication?

A) Mumbling, “Uh, I don’t get this… can you help or whatever?”

B) Saying clearly, “I’m struggling with this topic. Could we go over it together?”

C) Complaining, “This is too hard, I can’t do it at all!”

D) Whispering, “I need help,” so quietly the teacher can’t hear

Correct Answer: B) Saying clearly, “I’m struggling with this topic. Could we go over it together?”

 

Question 3: How can non-verbal communication impact a conversation during a group project discussion?

A) Slouching and avoiding eye contact can show you’re confident

B) Smiling and leaning forward can show interest and encourage others to share

C) Crossing your arms and frowning can make you seem more approachable

D) Looking at your phone constantly can show you’re a good multitasker

Correct Answer: B) Smiling and leaning forward can show interest and encourage others to share

 

Question 4: What is an empathetic response if a friend tells you they’re upset about not getting picked for a school play?

A) “Just try again next time, it’s not a big deal.”

B) “I know how disappointing that feels. I’m here if you want to talk about it.”

C) “Why didn’t you practice more? That’s probably why.”

D) “Let’s not talk about it, you’ll get over it soon.”

Correct Answer: B) “I know how disappointing that feels. I’m here if you want to talk about it.”
