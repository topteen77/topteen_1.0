Explore the Course Outline:

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to Negotiation and Persuasion - Discover the core concepts and importance of these vital skills.

  • Chapter 3: Understanding Negotiation Basics - Grasp the essentials of negotiation styles and strategies.
  • Chapter 4: Strategies for Successful Negotiation - Master techniques for preparation, bargaining, and conflict resolution.
  • Chapter 5: The Art of Persuasion - Uncover the principles of influence and ethical persuasion.
  • Chapter 6: Persuasion in Group Settings - Develop skills to lead and influence in team environments.
  • Chapter 7: Applying Skills in Real-World Contexts - Practice negotiation and persuasion in school, home, and digital spaces.
  • Chapter 8: Overcoming Challenges and Building Confidence - Build resilience and self-assurance to handle setbacks.
  • Chapter 9: Ethics and Responsibility in Negotiation and Persuasion - Understand the importance of fairness and respect in influence.
  • Chapter 10: Capstone Project and Review - Apply your learning in a culminating project and reflect on your growth.
