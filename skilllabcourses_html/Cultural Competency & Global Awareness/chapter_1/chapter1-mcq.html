MCQs

Question 1: What is cultural competency?

A) The ability to speak multiple languages fluently

B) The ability to interact effectively and respectfully with people from different cultures

C) The knowledge of every culture in the world

D) The practice of ignoring cultural differences to treat everyone the same

Correct Answer: B) The ability to interact effectively and respectfully with people from different cultures

 

Question 2: How does cultural understanding help reduce conflict?

A) It teaches you to avoid people from different cultures

B) It helps you understand the reasons behind people’s behaviors based on their cultural norms

C) It ensures everyone agrees on the same rules

D) It eliminates all differences between people

Correct Answer: B) It helps you understand the reasons behind people’s behaviors based on their cultural norms

 

Question 3: What is a stereotype?

A) A true fact about a group of people

B) A personal opinion based on extensive research

C) A fixed, often oversimplified idea about a group that is usually based on assumptions

D) A detailed and accurate description of a culture

Correct Answer: C) A fixed, often oversimplified idea about a group that is usually based on assumptions

 

Question 4: Why is self-reflection important in developing cultural competency?

A) It helps you prove that you have no biases

B) It allows you to understand your own cultural background and biases to better connect with others

C) It focuses on changing other people’s perspectives

D) It teaches you to hide your cultural identity

Correct Answer: B) It allows you to understand your own cultural background and biases to better connect with others

 

Question 5: Which of the following is an example of cultural awareness?

A) Assuming everyone celebrates the same holidays as you do

B) Recognizing that some cultures view direct eye contact as disrespectful while others see it as a sign of respect

C) Insisting that your way of greeting people is the best way

D) Refusing to try foods from other cultures because they seem strange

Correct Answer: B) Recognizing that some cultures view direct eye contact as disrespectful while others see it as a sign of respect
