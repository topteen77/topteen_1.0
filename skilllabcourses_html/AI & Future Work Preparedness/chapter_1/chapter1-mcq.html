MCQs

Question 1: What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

A) A type of hardware that makes computers faster

B) A branch of computer science where machines learn from data to make decisions

C) A programming language used to create websites

D) A tool for designing video games only

Correct Answer: B) A branch of computer science where machines learn from data to make decisions

 

Question 2: When was the term "Artificial Intelligence" first coined?

A) 1997

B) 1956

C) 2011

D) 1980

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of AI in everyday life for teens?

A) Using a calculator for math homework

B) Snapchat filters that detect and transform your face

C) Typing a document on a computer

D) Charging your phone

Correct Answer: B) Snapchat filters that detect and transform your face

 

Question 4: Which statement is a common myth about AI?

A) AI can make mistakes if trained on bad data

B) AI will take over the world and control humans

C) AI is a tool that humans design and manage

D) AI creates new job opportunities in tech fields

Correct Answer: B) AI will take over the world and control humans

 

Question 5: What is an ethical concern related to AI?

A) AI makes games more fun to play

B) AI collects personal data, raising privacy issues

C) AI helps predict weather more accurately

D) AI improves smartphone camera quality

Correct Answer: B) AI collects personal data, raising privacy issues
