MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of research as described in the chapter?

A) To randomly search for information on the internet without a plan

B) To ask questions and hunt for answers in a structured, curious way

C) To copy information directly from books for school projects

D) To avoid asking questions and rely only on personal opinions

Correct Answer: B) To ask questions and hunt for answers in a structured, curious way

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of primary research according to the chapter?

A) Reading an article on the history of the Indian Freedom Struggle on IndiaHistory.com

B) Watching a YouTube documentary about gaming trends in India

C) Surveying 15 classmates about their stress levels before exams using a Google Form

D) Checking NCERT.org for timelines of historical events

Correct Answer: C) Surveying 15 classmates about their stress levels before exams using a Google Form

 

Question 3: How can research skills help in everyday life decisions as mentioned in the chapter?

A) By helping you guess the best option without any data

B) By enabling you to compare prices and reviews before buying a phone worth ₹20,000 (approx. $240)

C) By ensuring you always agree with your friends’ opinions

D) By avoiding the need to check facts or information

Correct Answer: B) By enabling you to compare prices and reviews before buying a phone worth ₹20,000 (approx. $240)

 

Question 4: Which step of the research process involves spotting patterns or surprises in the collected data?

A) Identify Your Question or Topic

B) Plan Your Approach

C) Gather Information

D) Analyze Your Findings

Correct Answer: D) Analyze Your Findings
