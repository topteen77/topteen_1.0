MCQs

Question 1: What is a key way to respect intellectual property and copyright laws when using someone else’s work in your project, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Using the content without giving any credit to the creator

B) Copying large sections of text or media and claiming it as your own

C) Citing the source or asking for permission before using the material

D) Ignoring copyright rules since it’s just for a school project

Correct Answer: C) Citing the source or asking for permission before using the material

 

Question 2: What is an important ethical consideration when conducting surveys or collecting data for a school project, according to the chapter?

A) Collecting data without telling participants the purpose of your research

B) Sharing participants’ personal information publicly to make your report detailed

C) Getting informed consent and protecting participants’ privacy

D) Asking only personal and uncomfortable questions to get dramatic results

Correct Answer: C) Getting informed consent and protecting participants’ privacy

 

Question 3: How can you avoid fabrication and falsification of data in your research, based on the chapter’s guidance?

A) Making up results to make your project look more impressive

B) Changing data to match what you hoped to find

C) Reporting only the actual data you collected, even if it’s not perfect

D) Ignoring data that doesn’t support your hypothesis

Correct Answer: C) Reporting only the actual data you collected, even if it’s not perfect

 

Question 4: Why is cultural sensitivity important in research, as explained in the chapter?

A) It allows you to make assumptions and stereotypes about different groups

B) It helps you respect diverse backgrounds and avoid offending others in your work

C) It encourages you to ignore cultural differences for simplicity

D) It is only necessary when researching your own culture

Correct Answer: B) It helps you respect diverse backgrounds and avoid offending others in your work
