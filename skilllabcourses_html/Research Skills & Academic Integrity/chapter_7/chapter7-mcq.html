MCQs

Question 1: What is a key step to avoid plagiarism when paraphrasing or summarizing, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Changing just a few words without understanding the original idea

B) Rewriting the content in your own words and citing the source

C) Copying the original text and adding quotation marks

D) Using the idea without mentioning where it came from

Correct Answer: B) Rewriting the content in your own words and citing the source

 

Question 2: When should you use a direct quote in your writing, according to the chapter?

A) For every piece of information you find from a source

B) Only when the original wording is impactful or precise and worth highlighting

C) Whenever you want to make your essay longer

D) When you don’t have time to paraphrase or summarize

Correct Answer: B) Only when the original wording is impactful or precise and worth highlighting

 

Question 3: What is an effective way to integrate sources into your writing, as explained in the chapter?

A) Copying large sections of text without any citation

B) Using signal phrases like “According to” and connecting the source to your own ideas

C) Listing all sources at the end without mentioning them in the text

D) Ignoring sources and focusing only on your opinions

Correct Answer: B) Using signal phrases like “According to” and connecting the source to your own ideas

 

Question 4: Which of the following is a common citation mistake to avoid, based on the chapter’s guidance?

A) Citing every borrowed idea or quote immediately after using it

B) Forgetting to include a citation after paraphrasing a source’s idea

C) Double-checking the format of your citation style for accuracy

D) Including full details of the source in your bibliography

Correct Answer: B) Forgetting to include a citation after paraphrasing a source’s idea
