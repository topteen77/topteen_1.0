MCQs

Question 1: According to the chapter, how can research skills help with college and career readiness?

A) By helping you avoid all applications and interviews

B) By providing solid facts for essays and showing initiative in interviews

C) By focusing only on hobbies unrelated to your goals

D) By spending large amounts like ₹50,000 (approx. $600) on resources

Correct Answer: B) By providing solid facts for essays and showing initiative in interviews

 

Question 2: What is one way the chapter suggests using research to solve everyday problems?

A) Ignoring small issues and hoping they resolve themselves

B) Searching for product reviews before buying items like a ₹3,000 (approx. $36) phone case

C) Relying solely on friends’ opinions without checking facts

D) Spending ₹1,000 (approx. $12) on professional help for every issue

Correct Answer: B) Searching for product reviews before buying items like a ₹3,000 (approx. $36) phone case

 

Question 3: What does the chapter recommend for staying updated with credible news and information?

A) Believing every WhatsApp forward without verification

B) Sticking to trusted sources like BBC India and fact-checking viral claims

C) Only reading random blogs with sensational headlines

D) Avoiding news altogether to save time and ₹100 (approx. $1.20) data

Correct Answer: B) Sticking to trusted sources like BBC India and fact-checking viral claims

 

Question 4: How does the chapter describe lifelong learning through research?

A) As something that stops after high school ends

B) As a way to fuel curiosity and learn new skills or hobbies for free or low cost like ₹200 (approx. $2.40)

C) As a process that requires expensive courses worth ₹5,000 (approx. $60)

D) As unimportant compared to earning money immediately

Correct Answer: B) As a way to fuel curiosity and learn new skills or hobbies for free or low cost like ₹200 (approx. $2.40)
