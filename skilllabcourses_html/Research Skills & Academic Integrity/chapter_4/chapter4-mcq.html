MCQs

Question 1: What is a key benefit of using the Cornell Method for note-taking, as discussed in the chapter?

A) It focuses on drawing detailed pictures instead of writing notes

B) It organizes notes into sections for keywords, main ideas, and summaries, making review easier

C) It requires writing every word from a source without summarizing

D) It is only useful for digital note-taking on apps like Evernote

Correct Answer: B) It organizes notes into sections for keywords, main ideas, and summaries, making review easier

 

Question 2: What is the main purpose of creating an annotated bibliography, according to the chapter?

A) To list sources without any additional information for quick reference

B) To provide a summary, evaluation, and relevance of each source used in research

C) To copy and paste full articles into a single document

D) To avoid citing sources properly in a research paper

Correct Answer: B) To provide a summary, evaluation, and relevance of each source used in research

 

Question 3: How can digital tools likeZoterohelp with research organization, based on the chapter’s explanation?

A) They automatically write essays and reports for students

B) They manage sources, store citations, and auto-format bibliographies, saving time

C) They only work for storing unrelated personal photos and videos

D) They prevent students from accessing online articles and PDFs

Correct Answer: B) They manage sources, store citations, and auto-format bibliographies, saving time

B) Breaking down tasks into smaller goals with a timeline and using techniques like Pomodoro
