MCQs

Question 1: What is a core principle of academic integrity, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Copying others’ work to save time on assignments

B) Being honest, fair, and responsible in your schoolwork

C) Submitting work without citing any sources

D) Only focusing on getting high grades regardless of methods

Correct Answer: B) Being honest, fair, and responsible in your schoolwork

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of plagiarism, according to the chapter?

A) Quoting a source on “Indian Economy” with proper citation in your essay

B) Rewriting a YouTube video’s ideas on “Best Laptops under ₹30,000” without giving credit

C) Writing an original essay based on your own research and thoughts

D) Mentioning a friend’s idea in a project with their permission and a shoutout

Correct Answer: B) Rewriting a YouTube video’s ideas on “Best Laptops under ₹30,000” without giving credit

 

Question 3: What is a key aspect of the ethical use of information and ideas, as explained in the chapter?

A) Using others’ work without mentioning them to make your project look original

B) Giving credit to creators through citations or mentions when using their content

C) Only using your own ideas without ever referencing external sources

D) Copying exact paragraphs from websites to improve your writing

Correct Answer: B) Giving credit to creators through citations or mentions when using their content

 

Question 4: How can honestybedemonstrated in academic collaboration, based on the chapter’s guidance?

A) Letting one teammate do all the work while claiming equal credit

B) Contributing fairly to group tasks and clearly defining each person’s role

C) Copying a teammate’s ideas without acknowledging their input

D) Avoiding group work entirely to ensure individual honesty

Correct Answer: B) Contributing fairly to group tasks and clearly defining each person’s role
