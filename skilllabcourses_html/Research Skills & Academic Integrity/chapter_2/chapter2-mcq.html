MCQs

Question 1: What is the role of curiosity in developing a research mindset as described in the chapter?

A) To accept information without questioning it

B) To spark interest and ask specific, actionable questions that guide investigation

C) To focus only on topics already known to you

D) To avoid digging deeper into complex issues

Correct Answer: B) To spark interest and ask specific, actionable questions that guide investigation

 

Question 2: How does critical thinking help in research according to the chapter?

A) By encouraging you to believe every claim you read online

B) By helping you question information, analyze its credibility, and decide if it’s trustworthy

C) By limiting your research to only one source of information

D) By ignoring data that doesn’t match your opinion

Correct Answer: B) By helping you question information, analyze its credibility, and decide if it’s trustworthy

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a characteristic of a reliable source as mentioned in the chapter?

A) It comes from a random blog with no author or evidence

B) It is backed by evidence and comes from credible organizations like government or educational websites

C) It is full of advertisements and clickbait headlines

D) It provides outdated information without any references

Correct Answer: B) It is backed by evidence and comes from credible organizations like government or educational websites

 

Question 4: What is the purpose of setting research goals and objectives in the research process?

A) To make research more confusing and time-consuming

B) To provide a clear roadmap with a big-picture goal and specific steps to stay focused

C) To avoid planning and randomly collect information

D) To focus only on small details without a larger purpose

Correct Answer: B) To provide a clear roadmap with a big-picture goal and specific steps to stay focused
