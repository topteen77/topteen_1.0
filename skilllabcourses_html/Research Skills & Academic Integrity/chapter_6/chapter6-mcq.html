MCQs

Question 1: Why is citation important in academic writing, as discussed in the chapter?

A) It helps you avoid doing your own research for assignments

B) It prevents plagiarism and builds credibility for your work

C) It makes your project look longer without adding content

D) It is only necessary for college students, not high schoolers

Correct Answer: B) It prevents plagiarism and builds credibility for your work

 

Question 2: Which citation style is most commonly used for English and humanities subjects like literature, according to the chapter?

A) APA (American Psychological Association)

B) Chicago (Chicago Manual of Style)

C) MLA (Modern Language Association)

D) Harvard Style

Correct Answer: C) MLA (Modern Language Association)

 

Question 3: How would you cite a website in MLA style for a project on “Indian Street Foods under ₹100,” based on the chapter’s guidance?

A) Author. “Title of Page.”Website Name, Day Month Year, URL.

B) Author, Year, Website Name, URL.

C) Website Name, “Title of Page,” Author, Year.

D) URL, Author, “Title of Page,” Day Month Year.

Correct Answer: A) Author. “Title of Page.”Website Name, Day Month Year, URL.

 

Question 4: What is a key tip for using citation generators responsibly, as explained in the chapter?

A) Rely on the tool completely without checking the output

B) Use only paid versions of citation tools for accuracy

C) Double-check the generated citation for errors or missing details

D) Avoid using citation generators since they are always inaccurate

Correct Answer: C) Double-check the generated citation for errors or missing details
