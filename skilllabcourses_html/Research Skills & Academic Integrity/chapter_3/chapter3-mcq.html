MCQs

Question 1: What is a key advantage of using databases like JSTOR for research, as discussed in the chapter?

A) They provide free entertainment videos and memes

B) They offer access to peer-reviewed studies and articles, often through school subscriptions

C) They focus only on outdated information from the past decade

D) They are mainly used for finding random blog posts

Correct Answer: B) They offer access to peer-reviewed studies and articles, often through school subscriptions

 

Question 2: How can advanced search techniques on search engines help with research, according to the chapter?

A) By increasing the number of irrelevant results to scroll through

B) By using tools like quotation marks and “site:” to find specific, relevant information faster

C) By limiting searches to only paid advertisements

D) By automatically downloading unrelated apps

Correct Answer: B) By using tools like quotation marks and “site:” to find specific, relevant information faster

 

Question 3: What is a sign of bias in a source when evaluating information, as mentioned in the chapter?

A) The source uses neutral language and presents multiple perspectives

B) The source includes data and citations from credible studies

C) The source pushes a single viewpoint with loaded words like “horrible” without evidence

D) The source is published on a government or educational website

Correct Answer: C) The source pushes a single viewpoint with loaded words like “horrible” without evidence

 

Question 4: Why is cross-referencing important in the research process, based on the chapter’s explanation?

A) It ensures you only use one source for all your information

B) It helps confirm the accuracy of information by comparing it across multiple reliable sources

C) It focuses on finding the most biased sources to balance opinions

D) It guarantees that all sources will disagree with each other

Correct Answer: B) It helps confirm the accuracy of information by comparing it across multiple reliable sources
