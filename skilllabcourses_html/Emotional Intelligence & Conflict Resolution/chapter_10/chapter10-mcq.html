MCQs

Question 1: Managing Stress During Exams (Section 1)What is a key Emotional Intelligence (EI) skill to use when feeling stressed before a big test?

A) Empathy – Understanding your teacher’s perspective on the test.

B) Self-Regulation – Taking deep breaths to calm down before studying.

C) Social Skills – Asking a friend to argue with the teacher for you.

D) Motivation – Ignoring the stress and focusing only on the grade.

Correct Answer: B) Self-Regulation – Taking deep breaths to calm down before studying.

 

Question 2: Dealing with Teacher Conflicts (Section 1)How can EI help when you disagree with a teacher about a grade?

A) Use self-awareness to ignore your feelings and accept the grade.

B) Use empathy and social skills to calmly ask for feedback on how to improve.

C) Use motivation to argue until the teacher changes the grade.

D) Use self-regulation to avoid talking to the teacher altogether.

Correct Answer: B) Use empathy and social skills to calmly ask for feedback on how to improve.

 

Question 3: Managing Family Disagreements (Section 2)What is an effective EI strategy to use during an argument with a parent about rules?

A) Interrupt them to explain why they’re wrong.

B) Use active listening to show you understand their concerns before sharing your side.

C) Walk away without saying anything to avoid the conflict.

D) Blame them for not trusting you to make decisions.

Correct Answer: B) Use active listening to show you understand their concerns before sharing your side.

 

Question 4: Communication with Friends or Siblings (Section 2)Which communication tip based on EI is most helpful when discussing a problem with a sibling?

A) Use “I” statements to express how you feel without blaming them.

B) Talk about the issue when they’re already upset to get it over with.

C) Tell them they’re wrong to make your point clear.

D) Avoid the conversation and hope the problem goes away.

Correct Answer: A) Use “I” statements to express how you feel without blaming them.

 

Question 5: Navigating Online Interactions (Section 3)How can EI help you respond to a rude comment on your social media post?

A) Use self-regulation to pause before replying and avoid reacting in anger.

B) Immediately argue back to defend yourself.

C) Delete your account to avoid further comments.

D) Post something mean about the person to get even.

Correct Answer: A) Use self-regulation to pause before replying and avoid reacting in anger.

 

Question 6: Maintaining a Positive Online Presence (Section 3)What is a way to apply EI to create a positive online presence?

A) Post whatever comes to mind without thinking about the impact.

B) Share kind or uplifting content and think before posting to avoid hurting others.

C) Follow accounts that make you feel bad to challenge yourself.

D) Ignore negative comments and never respond to anyone online.

Correct Answer: B) Share kind or uplifting content and think before posting to avoid hurting others.
