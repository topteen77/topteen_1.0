MCQs

Question 1: What does the "SMART" framework for goal-setting stand for?

A) Simple, Motivated, Active, Realistic, Timely

B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

C) Strong, Manageable, Ambitious, Reliable, Targeted

D) Strategic, Meaningful, Attainable, Resourceful, Timed

Correct Answer: B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

 

Question 2: How can connecting emotions to goals enhance motivation?

A) It makes goals easier to achieve automatically.

B) It helps you ignore setbacks and challenges.

C) It provides a personal “why” that fuels commitment and drive.

D) It ensures you never feel frustrated while pursuing goals.

Correct Answer: C) It provides a personal “why” that fuels commitment and drive.

 

Question 3: Which strategy is recommended in Section 2 to overcome procrastination by making starting a task less intimidating?

A) The 5-Minute Rule

 

Question 4: What is a key time management tip tailored for teens mentioned in Section 2?

A) Focus on one task at a time instead of multitasking.

B) Spend all free time studying to avoid falling behind.

C) Avoid using planners as they are too restrictive.

D) Work on tasks only when you feel motivated.

Correct Answer: A) Focus on one task at a time instead of multitasking.

 

Question 5: What is the purpose of reframing negative thoughts, as discussed in Section 3?

A) To pretend challenges don’t exist and stay overly optimistic.

B) To replace harsh, self-critical thoughts with balanced, growth-focused ones.

C) To avoid thinking about personal goals altogether.

D) To focus only on past successes and ignore current struggles.

Correct Answer: B) To replace harsh, self-critical thoughts with balanced, growth-focused ones.

 

Question 6: Which activity is suggested in Section 3 to build a habit of positivity?

A) Setting stricter deadlines for all tasks

B) Keeping a positivity journal to record daily good things and lessons learned

C) Comparing your progress with others to stay competitive

D) Avoiding reflection on challenges to stay upbeat

Correct Answer: B) Keeping a positivity journal to record daily good things and lessons learned
