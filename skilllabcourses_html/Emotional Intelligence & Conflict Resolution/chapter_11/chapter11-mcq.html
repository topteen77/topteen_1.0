MCQ Questions

Question 1: Which of the following is a commonphysicalindicator of stress in teens?

A) Feeling irritable or snapping at others

B) Worrying constantly about the future

C) Experiencing frequent headaches or stomachaches

D) Avoiding social situations or responsibilities

Correct Answer: C) Experiencing frequent headaches or stomachaches

 

Question 2: When should a teen consider seeking support for stress?

A) When they feel stressed for a single day

B) When stress feels unmanageable or lasts for weeks

C) Only when they have a big test coming up

D) When they feel a little tired after school

Correct Answer: B) When stress feels unmanageable or lasts for weeks

 

Question 3: What is one benefit of practicing mindfulness as a stress-relief technique?

A) It helps build physical strength and endurance

B) It quiets a racing mind and reduces anxiety

C) It organizes your schoolwork and deadlines

D) It improves your grades automatically

Correct Answer: B) It quiets a racing mind and reduces anxiety

 

Question 4: Which strategy can help balance school and personal life for a teen?

A) Studying all night to finish assignments

B) Setting a cutoff time for schoolwork to allow personal time

C) Ignoring friends to focus only on grades

D) Taking on extra activities even when overwhelmed

Correct Answer: B) Setting a cutoff time for schoolwork to allow personal time

 

Question 5: Why is downtime considered an important part of self-care for teens?

A) It allows you to avoid all responsibilities permanently

B) It helps recharge your energy and prevent burnout

C) It guarantees better grades without studying

D) It means spending all day on social media

Correct Answer: B) It helps recharge your energy and prevent burnout

 

Question 6: Which of the following is a healthy habit that supports emotional well-being?

A) Staying up late every night to finish homework

B) Skipping meals to save time for activities

C) Aiming for 8-10 hours of sleep per night

D) Spending all free time on social media

Correct Answer: C) Aiming for 8-10 hours of sleep per night
