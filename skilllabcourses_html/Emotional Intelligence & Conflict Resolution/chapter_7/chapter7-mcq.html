MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary definition of conflict as discussed in the chapter?

A) A physical fight between two or more people

B) A disagreement or clash between differing opinions, needs, or goals

C) A situation where someone always wins and another loses

D) An unnatural occurrence that should be avoided at all costsCorrect Answer:B) A disagreement or clash between differing opinions, needs, or goals

 

Question 2: Why is conflict considered a natural part of relationships according to the chapter?

A) It shows that people don’t care about each other

B) It happens because no two people will always have the same views or needs

C) It only occurs in unhealthy relationships

D) It prevents relationships from growing strongerCorrect Answer:B) It happens because no two people will always have the same views or needs

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of an internal conflict as described in the chapter?

A) Arguing with a friend over weekend plans

B) Disagreeing with parents about curfew rules

C) Feeling torn between joining a new club or staying with a sports team

D) Having a dispute in a group chat over a misinterpreted commentCorrect Answer:C) Feeling torn between joining a new club or staying with a sports team

 

Question 4: What is a common cause of conflict among teens highlighted in the chapter?

A) Lack of interest in social activities

B) Miscommunication, especially over text or social media

C) Always agreeing with peers and family

D) Avoiding emotional discussionsCorrect Answer:B) Miscommunication, especially over text or social media

 

Question 5: How can emotions escalate a conflict, based on the chapter’s discussion?

A) By encouraging calm and thoughtful responses

B) By causing someone to use “I” statements to express feelings

C) By leading to impulsive reactions like blaming or yelling

D) By helping both parties understand each other’s perspectivesCorrect Answer:C) By leading to impulsive reactions like blaming or yelling

 

Question 6: What is an effective way to recognize and manage emotional triggers during a disagreement, according to the chapter?

A) Ignoring your feelings to avoid making the situation worse

B) Reflecting on past conflicts to identify patterns that provoke strong emotions

C) Immediately confronting the other person about their behavior

D) Pretending the trigger doesn’t exist to keep the peaceCorrect Answer:B) Reflecting on past conflicts to identify patterns that provoke strong emotions
