MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of creating a Personal Community Integration Plan (PCIP)?

A) To focus solely on academic success and ignore social connections

B) To structure your approach to blending into Canadian community life through social, cultural, and practical steps

C) To avoid interacting with local customs and maintain only your home culture

D) To plan your travel itinerary across Canada during holidaysCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of a SMART goal for a new international student in Canada?

A) “I want to do well in my studies.”

B) “I will join a study group within the first two weeks to achieve a B+ average by the end of the semester.”

C) “I hope to make some friends someday.”

D) “I will attend every event on campus.”Correct Answer: b)

 

Question 3: How can international students balance maintaining their cultural identity while adapting to Canadian customs?

A) By completely abandoning their home traditions to fit in

B) By participating in Canadian events while also celebrating their own traditions, such as hosting cultural gatherings

C) By refusing to engage with Canadian culture to preserve their identity

D) By only interacting with people from their home countryCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 4: What is a common sign of culture shock that international students might experience in Canada?

A) Feeling excited about every new experience without any challenges

B) Feeling irritable, lonely, or confused due to unfamiliar customs or environment

C) Immediately understanding all social norms and fitting in seamlessly

D) Having no interest in connecting with others or seeking supportCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 5: What is one way to build a support network as a new international student in Canada?

A) Avoid attending orientation events to focus only on studies

B) Actively participate in orientation week, join student associations, and connect with mentors through university programs

C) Rely solely on family back home without seeking local connections

D) Wait for others to approach you without making any effortCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 6: Why is continuous learning important for long-term success as an international student in Canada?

A) It helps students focus only on short-term goals without planning for the future

B) It ensures students can adapt, grow skills, and prepare for career and personal goals beyond the first semester

C) It prevents students from reflecting on their progress or making adjustments

D) It limits students to classroom learning without exploring other opportunitiesCorrect Answer: b)
