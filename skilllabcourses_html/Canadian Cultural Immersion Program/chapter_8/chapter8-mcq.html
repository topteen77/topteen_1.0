MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of mandatory university health insurance plans like UHIP for international students in Canada?

A) To cover travel expenses during holidays

B) To provide coverage for basic medical services like doctor visits and hospital stays

C) To pay for university tuition fees

D) To offer discounts on gym memberships

Correct Answer: B) To provide coverage for basic medical services like doctor visits and hospital stays

 

Question 2: If you have a mild fever and sore throat but can’t get a doctor’s appointment for a few days, what is the most appropriate first step to seek care in Canada?

A) Go directly to the emergency room (ER)

B) Visit a walk-in clinic or call a provincial telehealth service for advice

C) Wait until the symptoms worsen before seeking help

D) Order prescription medicine online without a consultation

Correct Answer: B) Visit a walk-in clinic or call a provincial telehealth service for advice

 

Question 3: What is a common free resource offered by Canadian universities to support student mental health?

A) Paid private therapy sessions off-campus

B) Free counseling services and workshops at campus wellness centers

C) Mandatory fitness memberships for stress relief

D) Exclusive access to international travel programs

Correct Answer: B) Free counselling services and workshops at campus wellness centers

 

Question 4: Which clothing item is essential for staying safe and warm during a Canadian winter with temperatures below -20°C?

A) A light rain jacket

B) A high-quality insulated winter coat rated for extreme cold

C) Cotton t-shirts as base layers

D) Open-toed sandals with socks

Correct Answer: B) A high-quality insulated winter coat rated for extreme cold

 

Question 5: When should you call 9-1-1 for a medical emergency in Canada?

A) For a mild headache that lasts a few hours

B) For life-threatening conditions like chest pain or severe bleeding

C) For scheduling a routine doctor’s appointment

D) For advice on over-the-counter medications

Correct Answer: B) For life-threatening conditions like chest pain or severe bleeding

 

Question 6: What is a recommended strategy to combat Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD) during Canada’s dark winter months?

A) Avoid all outdoor activities until spring

B) Use a light therapy lamp to mimic natural sunlight

C) Skip regular sleep schedules to stay up later

D) Limit social interactions to focus on studies

Correct Answer: B) Use a light therapy lamp to mimic natural sunlight
