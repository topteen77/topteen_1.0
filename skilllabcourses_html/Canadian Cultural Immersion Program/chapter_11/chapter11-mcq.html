MCQs

Question 1: What is a key benefit of learning to use public transit before arriving in Canada?

A) It guarantees you’ll never get lost in a new city

B) It reduces stress and saves time when navigating to school or errands

C) It ensures you won’t need to ask for help from locals

D) It allows you to avoid using transit apps or mapsCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 2: Why is setting up a Canadian bank account important for international students?

A) It allows you to avoid paying any fees for transactions

B) It simplifies payments, avoids high international fees, and helps manage daily expenses

C) It guarantees immediate access to a credit card without any checks

D) It is only necessary if you plan to work in CanadaCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 3: What is the primary purpose of a land acknowledgement for international students in Canada?

A) To fulfill a mandatory university assignment without personal reflection

B) To show respect for Indigenous Peoples and recognize their traditional territories

C) To focus solely on Canadian history without connecting to current contexts

D) To replace learning about local customs with a written statementCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 4: How does volunteering benefit international students integrating into Canadian life?

A) It ensures students avoid interacting with local communities to focus on studies

B) It builds skills, fosters connections, and aligns with Canadian values of giving back

C) It is only useful for students who want to work in non-profit organizations

D) It prevents students from needing to participate in campus activitiesCorrect Answer: b)

 

Question 5: What is one purpose of creating a cultural immersion documentation portfolio?

A) To track and reflect on personal growth and cultural experiences in Canada

B) To submit as a formal academic requirement for all courses

C) To focus only on challenges without celebrating achievements

D) To avoid sharing experiences with family or friends back homeCorrect Answer: a)

 

Question 6: Why is it important to regularly measure and adjust your integration progress in Canada?

A) To compare your success with other students and compete for recognition

B) To recognize achievements, identify challenges, and adapt strategies for better integration

C) To focus only on failures without celebrating small wins

D) To avoid seeking support from mentors or university resourcesCorrect Answer: b)
