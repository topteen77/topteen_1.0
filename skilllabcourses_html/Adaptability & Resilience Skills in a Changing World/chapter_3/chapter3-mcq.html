MCQs

Question 1: What is a key benefit of identifying your emotions during a setback?

A) It makes the setback disappear instantly.

B) It helps you understand what you’re feeling and manage it better.

C) It ensures you never feel upset again.

D) It prevents you from ever facing challenges.

Correct Answer: B) It helps you understand what you’re feeling and manage it better.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of a mindfulness technique to calm your mind?

A) Writing a long essay about your problems.

B) Using the “5-4-3-2-1” method to focus on your surroundings.

C) Ignoring your feelings and distracting yourself with games.

D) Thinking about all the things that could go wrong in the future.

Correct Answer: B) Using the “5-4-3-2-1” method to focus on your surroundings.

 

Question 3: How can breathing exercises help during tough times?

A) They make you forget why you were upset.

B) They slow your heart rate and help you feel calmer.

C) They solve all your problems immediately.

D) They stop you from needing to talk to anyone.

Correct Answer: B) They slow your heart rate and help you feel calmer.

 

Question 4: Why is it important to lean on friends, family, or mentors during difficult moments?

A) They can do everything for you so you don’t have to try.

B) They provide support, perspective, and help you feel less alone.

C) They will always agree with everything you say.

D) They can prevent any tough times from happening.

Correct Answer: B) They provide support, perspective, and help you feel less alone.

 

Question 5: What is a practical way to express emotions safely after a setback?

A) Keeping all your feelings bottled up to avoid conflict.

B) Yelling at someone to let out your frustration.

C) Writing a letter about your feelings or talking to a trusted person.

D) Pretending nothing happened and moving on without reflection.

Correct Answer: C) Writing a letter about your feelings or talking to a trusted person.
