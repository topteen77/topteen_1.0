MCQs

Question 1: How does recognizing progress help boost self-esteem, according to the chapter?

A) It guarantees you’ll never fail again.

B) It shows you that you’re better than everyone else.

C) It reminds you that you’re stronger than you think by noticing small victories.

D) It means you don’t have to try hard anymore.

Correct Answer: C) It reminds you that you’re stronger than you think by noticing small victories.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is a tool mentioned in the chapter to quiet negative thoughts?

A) Ignoring your feelings completely.

B) Using the “Thought Swap” technique to replace negative thoughts with kinder, realistic ones.

C) Comparing yourself to others on social media.

D) Avoiding challenges to prevent self-doubt.

Correct Answer: B) Using the “Thought Swap” technique to replace negative thoughts with kinder, realistic ones.

 

Question 3: In the story of Kayla from the chapter, how did she build confidence through resilience?

A) She ignored the bullying and pretended it didn’t happen.

B) She joined a mental health awareness club and started a campaign against cyberbullying.

C) She quit social media and avoided all school events permanently.

D) She fought back by bullying others online.

Correct Answer: B) She joined a mental health awareness club and started a campaign against cyberbullying.

 

Question 4: What is the purpose of the “Power Pose” trick described in the chapter?

A) To impress others with your physical strength.

B) To lower stress and boost confidence by standing in a confident posture for a short time.

C) To avoid doing difficult tasks by focusing on your body instead.

D) To make you feel like a superhero without actually helping your mindset.

Correct Answer: B) To lower stress and boost confidence by standing in a confident posture for a short time.

 

Question 5: What lesson can high school students learn from Marcus’ story about his basketball injury?

A) If you get injured, you should give up on your dreams.

B) Resilience can help you discover new strengths, like leadership, even during setbacks.

C) Confidence only comes from being the best at something.

D) You should avoid taking risks to prevent injuries.

Correct Answer: B) Resilience can help you discover new strengths, like leadership, even during setbacks.
