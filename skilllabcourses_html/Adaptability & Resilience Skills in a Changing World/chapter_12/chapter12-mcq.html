MCQs

Question 1: What is a key strategy for adapting to the fast-paced world of social media and technology without stress?

A) Trying to master every new app or trend immediately.

B) Learning new tools at your own pace and focusing on what’s useful to you.

C) Ignoring all technology updates to avoid change.

D) Spending all your time online to stay in the loop.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: How can maintaining a healthy digital balance help build resilience?

A) It increases your screen time to stay connected.

B) It prevents online stress from overwhelming your emotions and boosts focus.

C) It ensures you never miss a social media post or notification.

D) It stops you from having any offline activities.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: What is a common source of online stress for high school students?

A) Spending too much time on hobbies offline.

B) Constant notifications and comparison on social media.

C) Avoiding all digital platforms completely.

D) Having too few online friends.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is a resilient response to cyberbullying or negative online interactions?

A) Arguing with the person to prove them wrong.

B) Deleting all your social media accounts permanently.

C) Blocking the person, not engaging, and seeking support if needed.

D) Ignoring the issue and hoping it disappears on its own.Correct Answer:C

 

Question 5: Why is setting time limits for social media use important for a healthy digital balance?

A) It ensures you spend all your time online without distractions.

B) It helps reduce stress and frees up time for real-life connections and activities.

C) It prevents you from connecting with friends online.

D) It makes you miss out on important trends and updates.Correct Answer:B
