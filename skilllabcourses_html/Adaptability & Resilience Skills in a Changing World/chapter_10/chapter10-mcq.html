MCQs

Question 1: Why is organizing tasks important when facing unexpected challenges?

A) It makes you look busy to others.

B) It helps you stay focused and reduces stress by breaking tasks into manageable pieces.

C) It ensures you never have to deal with surprises.

D) It guarantees you’ll finish everything on time.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a key strategy for balancing school, personal life, and unexpected disruptions?

A) Ignoring personal life to focus only on school.

B) Setting boundaries and using a weekly calendar to allocate time for different areas.

C) Doing everything at the last minute to save time.

D) Avoiding communication with others to stay focused.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: How does the Eisenhower Matrix help with time management?

A) It sorts tasks by urgency and importance to decide what to do first.

B) It helps you avoid doing any tasks at all.

C) It focuses only on urgent tasks, ignoring long-term goals.

D) It makes all tasks seem equally important.Correct Answer:A

 

Question 4: What is a benefit of setting realistic deadlines under pressure?

A) It ensures you’ll never miss a deadline.

B) It avoids last-minute panic and helps maintain the quality of your work.

C) It allows you to ignore tasks that take too long.

D) It makes others do your work for you.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 5: Why is adjusting priorities important when unexpected events occur?

A) It helps you focus on what’s most urgent and adapt to changes without losing control.

B) It means you can skip all your responsibilities.

C) It prevents you from ever having to plan ahead.

D) It guarantees you’ll always have extra time.Correct Answer:A
