MCQs

Question 1: Why is clear communication important during times of change?

A) It makes people like you more.

B) It reduces confusion and helps everyone stay on the same page.

C) It ensures you always get your way.

D) It prevents any change from happening.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a key part of active listening?

A) Thinking about what you’ll say next while the other person talks.

B) Focusing fully on the speaker without distractions.

C) Interrupting to share your own story.

D) Looking away to avoid making things awkward.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: How does empathy help during a challenging situation?

A) It allows you to ignore someone’s feelings.

B) It helps you understand how others feel and builds stronger connections.

C) It makes you focus only on your own problems.

D) It stops you from having to listen to others.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is an effective way to express your needs or concerns in an uncertain situation?

A) Using “I” statements to focus on your feelings without blaming others.

B) Yelling to make sure you’re heard.

C) Staying silent and hoping the issue goes away.

D) Complaining without suggesting any solutions.Correct Answer:A

 

Question 5: Which of the following is a benefit of expressing needs and concerns effectively?

A) It guarantees everyone will agree with you.

B) It prevents any conflict from ever happening.

C) It helps get your needs met and encourages collaboration.

D) It makes others stop talking to you.Correct Answer:C
