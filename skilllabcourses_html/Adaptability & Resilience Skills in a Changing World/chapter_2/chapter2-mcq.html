MCQs

Question 1: What is the main difference between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset?

A) A fixed mindset believes effort is unnecessary, while a growth mindset ignores challenges.

B) A fixed mindset sees abilities as unchangeable, while a growth mindset believes abilities can develop with effort.

C) A fixed mindset focuses on learning, while a growth mindset avoids mistakes.

D) A fixed mindset encourages taking risks, while a growth mindset sticks to what’s easy.

Correct Answer: B) A fixed mindset sees abilities as unchangeable, while a growth mindset believes abilities can develop with effort.

 

Question 2: Why does a growth mindset matter for high school students?

A) It guarantees perfect grades without studying.

B) It helps students avoid all challenges and obstacles.

C) It encourages persistence and resilience in facing academic and personal challenges.

D) It ensures students never make mistakes.

Correct Answer: C) It encourages persistence and resilience in facing academic and personal challenges.

 

Question 3: How can obstacles be viewed through a growth mindset?

A) As proof that you’re not good enough to succeed.

B) As annoying problems that should be ignored.

C) As chances to learn new skills or strategies.

D) As reasons to give up on your goals.

Correct Answer: C) As chances to learn new skills or strategies.

 

Question 4: What is a practical way to turn mistakes into stepping stones, according to the chapter?

A) Pretend the mistake never happened and move on.

B) Blame someone else for the mistake to avoid feeling bad.

C) Break down what went wrong and brainstorm how to improve next time.

D) Avoid trying anything new to prevent future mistakes.

Correct Answer: C) Break down what went wrong and brainstorm how to improve next time.

 

Question 5: Which of the following is an example of a daily growth mindset habit?

A) Telling yourself you’re not good at something and giving up.

B) Starting your day with a positive mantra like, “I’m ready to learn something new today.”

C) Comparing yourself to others and feeling discouraged.

D) Focusing only on results and ignoring the effort you put in.

Correct Answer: B) Starting your day with a positive mantra like, “I’m ready to learn something new today.”
