MCQs

Question 1: What is the first step in the step-by-step approach to tackling big challenges?

A) Taking immediate action without planning.

B) Defining the problem by figuring out what’s really going on.

C) Brainstorming solutions without understanding the issue.

D) Giving up if the problem seems too big.

Correct Answer: B) Defining the problem by figuring out what’s really going on.

 

Question 2: How can thinking outside the box help when solutions aren’t obvious?

A) It encourages you to stick to the same old methods.

B) It helps you look at the problem from a fresh, creative angle.

C) It guarantees you’ll solve the problem instantly.

D) It prevents you from ever needing to ask for help.

Correct Answer: B) It helps you look at the problem from a fresh, creative angle.

 

Question 3: What does “pivoting” mean in the context of problem-solving?

A) Giving up on your goal completely.

B) Changing your plan or approach when the current one isn’t working.

C) Ignoring the problem and hoping it goes away.

D) Sticking to the original plan no matter what.

Correct Answer: B) Changing your plan or approach when the current one isn’t working.

 

Question 4: Why is it important to reflect and adjust after trying a solution?

A) To criticize yourself for making mistakes.

B) To see if the solution worked and make changes if needed.

C) To avoid trying any new ideas in the future.

D) To prove that the first solution is always the best.

Correct Answer: B) To see if the solution worked and make changes if needed.

 

Question 5: Which of the following is a way to think outside the box when solving a problem?

A) Only considering the most obvious solutions.

B) Asking “What if” questions to challenge the usual rules.

C) Refusing to try anything new or different.

D) Focusing only on why the problem can’t be solved.

Correct Answer: B) Asking “What if” questions to challenge the usual rules.
