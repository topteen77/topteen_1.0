MCQ Questions

Question 1: What is adaptability, as described in Chapter 5?

A) The ability to stick to a plan no matter what happens.

B) The skill of changing your approach or mindset to fit new situations.

C) The habit of avoiding challenges to stay comfortable.

D) The talent of predicting every possible change in life.Correct Answer:b) The skill of changing your approach or mindset to fit new situations.

 

Question 2: In the real-life example of Mia’s group project, how did adaptability help her succeed?

A) She ignored the problem and hoped it would resolve itself.

B) She quickly reassigned tasks and simplified the presentation to make it work with fewer teammates.

C) She refused to change the original plan and worked alone all night.

D) She canceled the project and asked for an extension.Correct Answer:b) She quickly reassigned tasks and simplified the presentation to make it work with fewer teammates.

 

Question 3: What is the purpose of the “Switch It Up” Challenge in the chapter’s practical exercises?

A) To make students feel uncomfortable for no reason.

B) To encourage students to never change their routines.

C) To help students practice flexibility by trying small, new experiences.

D) To force students to abandon all their habits permanently.Correct Answer:c) To help students practice flexibility by trying small, new experiences.

 

Question 4: Which strategy from Chapter 5 helps you stay calm during a sudden change by grounding yourself in the moment?

A) Make a Quick Plan B with the 3-Step Pivot.

B) Reframe the Change to find a hidden win.

C) Use the 5-4-3-2-1 Trick to focus on your senses.

D) Create a Plan B Playlist for motivation.Correct Answer:c) Use the 5-4-3-2-1 Trick to focus on your senses.

 

Question 5: How does reframing a sudden change, as discussed in the chapter, help with adaptability?

A) It encourages you to ignore the change and pretend it didn’t happen.

B) It shifts your mindset to find a positive opportunity or benefit in the situation.

C) It forces you to stick to the original plan no matter the cost.

D) It makes you focus only on what went wrong.Correct Answer:b) It shifts your mindset to find a positive opportunity or benefit in the situation.
