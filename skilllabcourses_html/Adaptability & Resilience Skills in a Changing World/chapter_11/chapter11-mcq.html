MCQ Questions

Question 1: How does physical health impact emotional strength and resilience?

A) It has no effect on emotions or stress levels.

B) A healthy body boosts mood, focus, and the ability to handle stress better.

C) It only matters for athletes and not for regular students.

D) It makes you immune to all emotional challenges.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: Which of the following is a simple habit to boost resilience through physical wellness?

C) Getting 8-10 hours of sleep most nights.

 

Question 3: What is a common sign of burnout that high school students should watch for?

A) Feeling energized and motivated every day.

B) Constant tiredness, irritability, or losing interest in usual activities.

C) Wanting to spend more time with friends and family.

D) Completing all tasks with ease and no stress.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is a proactive step to recharge when you’re feeling burned out?

A) Pushing harder to finish everything without breaks.

B) Taking mini-breaks or doing something fun to lift your mood.

C) Ignoring how you feel and hoping it goes away.

D) Adding more tasks to distract yourself from stress.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 5: Why does nutrition play a role in building resilience?

A) It keeps your energy steady, helping you stay calm and focused under pressure.

B) It only matters if you’re trying to lose weight.

C) It makes you study longer without needing sleep.

D) It prevents you from ever feeling stressed.Correct Answer:A
