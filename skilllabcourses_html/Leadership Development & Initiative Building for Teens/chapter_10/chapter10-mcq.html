MCQs

Question 1: What does integrity in leadership primarily involve?

Correct Answer: B) Being honest and adhering to moral principles

 

Question 2: Why is fairness important in leadership?

Correct Answer: B) It ensures that all team members feel valued and included

 

Question 3: What is one practical strategy for embracing accountability as a leader?

Correct Answer: C) Asking for feedback from team members

 

Question 4: How can a leader use their influence for positive social impact?

Correct Answer: B) By engaging in community service and advocacy

 

Question 5: What is social responsibility?

Correct Answer: B) The idea that individuals should act in ways that benefit society

 

Question 6: What is a recommended action when a leader makes a mistake?

Correct Answer: C) Reflect on the mistake and discuss improvements with the team
