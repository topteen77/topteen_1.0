MCQs

Question 1: What is a key way to inspire others through positive examples as discussed in the chapter?

A) Giving long speeches about success

B) Demonstrating resilience and integrity in your actions

C) Telling peers what they should do to improve

D) Focusing only on your own achievements

Correct Answer: B) Demonstrating resilience and integrity in your actions

 

Question 2: Why is specific praise more effective than generic compliments when offering encouragement?

A) It sounds more professional

B) It shows you’re paying attention to their unique efforts

C) It takes less time to say

D) It makes you seem more authoritative

Correct Answer: B) It shows you’re paying attention to their unique efforts

 

Question 3: What is a recommended strategy for offering guidance to a peer who is struggling?

A) Tell them exactly how to solve their problem

B) Focus on their mistakes to help them learn

C) Suggest solutions while encouraging them to find their own way

D) Avoid giving advice so they don’t feel pressured

Correct Answer: C) Suggest solutions while encouraging them to find their own way

 

Question 4: What does the chapter identify as a core component of empathy in peer-to-peer coaching?

A) Solving problems for your peer quickly

B) Understanding and acknowledging their emotions and perspective

C) Telling them how you would handle their situation

D) Keeping conversations short to avoid discomfort

Correct Answer: B) Understanding and acknowledging their emotions and perspective

 

Question 5: How can active listening help in building empathy with a peer, according to the chapter?

A) It shows you’re in a hurry to move on to solutions

B) It allows you to interrupt with helpful advice

C) It demonstrates that you’re fully engaged and value their thoughts

D) It helps you avoid asking too many questions

Correct Answer: C) It demonstrates that you’re fully engaged and value their thoughts

 

Question 6: What should you do if a peer shares a personal struggle during a coaching conversation?

A) Share your own story immediately to relate to them

B) Promise to keep it confidential and avoid judgment

C) Suggest they talk to someone else for help

D) Change the topic to make them feel more comfortable

Correct Answer: B) Promise to keep it confidential and avoid judgment
