Chapter 10: Real-World Applications and Practice – Put It All to Work

Hey there, high school trailblazers! Welcome to the hands-on, let’s-make-it-happen part of your project management journey! You’ve learned the basics, tackled risks, and mastered communication—now it’s time to see how all of this plays out in the real world. Think of this chapter as your chance to test-drive your skills, whether you’re dreaming up a school fundraiser, leading a club, or planning something big for your future (like a startup idea). Project management isn’t just theory; it’s a superpower for getting stuff done. In Chapter 10, we’re diving into three exciting topics to bridge the gap between learning and doing: case studies of student projects, a hands-on project simulation, and preparing for bigger challenges.
