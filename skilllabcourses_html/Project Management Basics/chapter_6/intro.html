Chapter 6: Executing and Monitoring Your Project – Making It Happen

Hey there, high school go-getters! Welcome to the action-packed part of your project management journey! You’ve got your plan, tools, and team ready—now it’s time to roll up your sleeves and bring your project to life. Think of this chapter as your playbook for getting things done, whether you’re running a school fundraiser, nailing a group presentation, or working on a personal mission (like organizing a charity drive). Executing and monitoring a project is all about starting strong, staying on course, and handling bumps along the way. In Chapter 6, we’re covering three essential topics to keep your project moving forward: starting your project, tracking progress, and dealing with delays and issues. With practical advice, relatable scenarios, and a sprinkle of encouragement, this chapter will help you take charge and keep your project on track. Let’s jump in and make it happen!

What’s Ahead in This Chapter:

  • Starting Your Project: Learn how to launch your project with confidence and get everyone on the same page.
    • Kickoff Meetings or Initial Steps: Discover the power of a great start with a team huddle or first action steps.
    • Setting the Tone for Success: Find out how to build momentum and keep the energy positive from day one.
  • Tracking Progress: Stay in control by keeping an eye on how far you’ve come and what’s still ahead.
    • Checking Off Completed Tasks: Feel the satisfaction of marking things done and seeing your progress.
    • Using Milestones to Measure Advancement: Break your project into big wins to celebrate along the way.
  • Dealing with Delays and Issues: Learn how to tackle challenges without losing your cool or your focus.
    • Identifying Problems Early: Spot issues before they turn into major headaches.
    • Brainstorming Solutions as a Team: Work together to come up with creative fixes and keep moving forward.

Get ready to turn your plans into action and keep your project cruising toward the finish line. By the end of this chapter, you’ll know how to start strong, monitor your progress like a pro, and handle any curveballs that come your way. So, gather your team (or just your awesome self) and let’s dive into executing and monitoring—your project is officially in motion!
