MCQs

Question 1: Which of the following is an example of a renewable energy source that harnesses natural elements to generate power?

A) Coal-powered plants

B) Wind turbines

C) Gasoline engines

D) Nuclear reactorsCorrect Answer:B) Wind turbines

 

Question 2: What is a common economic barrier to adopting sustainable practices on a large scale?

A) Lack of public interest in green technologies

B) High upfront costs of installing solutions like solar panels

C) Too many renewable energy options available

D) Overproduction of eco-friendly productsCorrect Answer:B) High upfront costs of installing solutions like solar panels

 

Question 3: Why is it challenging to balance development with environmental protection in growing cities?

A) Development always leads to cleaner air and water

B) Building infrastructure often harms natural habitats or increases pollution

C) Environmental protection laws are too easy to enforce

D) Cities have unlimited resources to protect the environmentCorrect Answer:B) Building infrastructure often harms natural habitats or increases pollution

 

Question 4: What does “carbon neutrality” mean in the context of a sustainable future?

A) Producing more carbon dioxide to balance global temperatures

B) Ensuring no net carbon dioxide emissions by offsetting pollution

C) Completely stopping all industrial activities

D) Ignoring carbon emissions to focus on waste reductionCorrect Answer:B) Ensuring no net carbon dioxide emissions by offsetting pollution
