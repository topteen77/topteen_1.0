MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary goal of sustainability?

A) To use up all resources as quickly as possible for economic growth

B) To meet current needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs

C) To focus only on environmental protection and ignore social needs

D) To increase pollution to support industrial developmentCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 2: Which of the following is NOT one of the three pillars of sustainability?

A) Environmental Sustainability

B) Social Sustainability

C) Technological Sustainability

D) Economic SustainabilityCorrect Answer: C

 

Question 3: Why is sustainability especially important for teens?

A) Teens are the only ones who can solve environmental problems

B) Teens will inherit the future impacted by today’s environmental issues

C) Teens are not affected by sustainability challenges

D) Teens have no role in creating change for the environmentCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 4: Which of the following is an example of a global environmental challenge mentioned in the chapter?

A) Lack of social media awareness

B) Deforestation in the Amazon rainforest

C) Increase in school homework

D) Development of new video gamesCorrect Answer: B
