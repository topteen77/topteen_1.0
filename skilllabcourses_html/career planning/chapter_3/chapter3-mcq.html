MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of researching potential careers and job profiles?

A) To immediately decide on a lifelong career path

B) To gather detailed information about roles and see if they align with your skills and interests

C) To impress potential employers with your knowledge

D) To avoid taking career assessments like Holland Codes

Correct Answer: B) To gather detailed information about roles and see if they align with your skills and interests

 

Question 2: What does the Holland Code (RIASEC) assessment primarily help you identify?

A) The exact salary you will earn in a specific career

B) Your personality type and matching career categories based on interests

C) The best college major for your future job

D) How to write a resume for job applications

Correct Answer: B) Your personality type and matching career categories based on interests

 

Question 3: If your Holland Code result is “SAE” (Social, Artistic, Enterprising), which career might be a good match?

A) Software Engineer

B) Event Planner

C) Automotive Technician

D) Data Analyst

Correct Answer: B) Event Planner

 

Question 4: When evaluating a career’s working conditions, what factor should you consider to ensure it fits your lifestyle?

A) The company’s stock price

B) Whether the job offers a fast-paced or relaxed environment and matches your preferred work hours

C) The number of social media followers the job role typically has

D) The color of the office walls

Correct Answer: B) Whether the job offers a fast-paced or relaxed environment and matches your preferred work hours

 

Question 5: Why is it important to research salary data for a career using tools like BLS.gov or Glassdoor.com?

A) To compare it with your friends’ earnings

B) To determine if the starting pay and growth potential can support your financial needs and goals

C) To decide if you should ask for a raise immediately

D) To know how much to spend on work clothes

Correct Answer: B) To determine if the starting pay and growth potential can support your financial needs and goals

 

Question 6: What makes a career “future-proof” as discussed in the chapter?

A) It guarantees a high salary from the start

B) It is expected to grow and remain relevant due to global trends like technology and sustainability

C) It requires no education or training to enter

D) It is a traditional job that has existed for centuries

Correct Answer: B) It is expected to grow and remain relevant due to global trends like technology and sustainability

 

Question 7: Which of the following is an example of a future-proof career in the Green Energy sector, and why?

A) Coal Miner, because it’s a hands-on job

B) Solar Photovoltaic Installer, because of high growth due to sustainability goals

C) Travel Blogger, because it involves outdoor work

D) Retail Cashier, because it’s in high demand everywhere

Correct Answer: B) Solar Photovoltaic Installer, because of high growth due to sustainability goals

 

Question 8: How can combining insights from a Holland Code assessment and research on future-proof careers help in career planning?

A) It ensures you pick the highest-paying job immediately

B) It helps you identify careers that match your personality while focusing on growing, relevant fields

C) It eliminates the need to evaluate job tasks or salary data

D) It guarantees you’ll get hired in your chosen field

Correct Answer: B) It helps you identify careers that match your personality while focusing on growing, relevant fields
