Course Title: Career Planning

Introductory Module: Growth Mindset for Career Planning

  • Understanding the Importance of a Growth Mindset in Career Development
  • Overcoming Fear of Failure and Embracing Adaptability
  • Activity: Reflecting on Personal Mindset Challenges and Opportunities

Chapter 1: Introduction to Career Planning

  • Overview and Purpose of Career Planning
  • Key Components of the Career Planning Process
  • The Importance of Self-Awareness and Informed Decision-Making
  • Common Myths About Career Planning (e.g., "You Must Choose One Career for Life")
  • Activity: Defining Personal Career Success ("What Does Career Success Mean to You?")
  • Worksheet: Career Planning Introduction (Overall Process, Importance, Key Elements, Initial Aspirations/Fears)

Chapter 2: Self-Exploration

  • Understanding Your Skills, Interests, Values, and Experiences
  • Reflecting on Your Personal and Cultural Background
  • Exploring Societal Expectations and Family Influences on Career Choices
  • Introduction to Digital Tools for Personality and Skills Assessments (e.g., Myers-Briggs, StrengthsFinder)
  • Worksheets: "Things I Have Done" (Working with People, Data, Things), "Skills Identification Worksheet", "Job Values Inventory Worksheet" (Rank and Understand Job Qualities)

Chapter 3: Occupational Exploration

  • Researching Potential Careers and Job Profiles
  • Using Online Career Assessments (e.g., Holland Codes)
  • Evaluating Job Tasks, Working Conditions, and Salary Data
  • Exploring Future-Proof Careers (e.g., Green Energy, Cybersecurity)
  • Activity: Guided Informational Interviews or Job Shadowing
  • Worksheet: Occupational Exploration and Matching Your Interests (Including Comparison Chart for Occupations)

Chapter 4: Educational Planning

  • Aligning Your Educational Goals with Career Aspirations
  • Researching Degrees, Certifications, Licenses, and Training Options
  • Exploring Non-Traditional Education Paths (e.g., Bootcamps, Online Courses like Coursera/Udemy, Micro-Credentials)
  • Planning for Higher Education and Alternative Training Paths
  • Checklist: Evaluating Educational Programs (Cost, Accreditation, Outcomes)
  • Worksheet: Educational Planning for Career Preparation

Chapter 5: Career and Educational Goal Setting

  • Setting Short-Term and Long-Term Goals for Your Career and Education
  • Applying the SMART Goals Framework in a Career Context (Examples for Different Career Stages)
  • Strategies for Overcoming Setbacks and Adjusting Goals to Life Changes
  • Worksheets: "My Career Plan"

Chapter 6: Action Planning and Decision Making

  • Developing a Detailed Action Plan for Your Career Goals
  • The Decision-Making Process (e.g., PACED Process)
  • Creating a Roadmap for Success
  • Contingency Planning for Unexpected Challenges (e.g., Job Loss, Industry Shifts)
  • Templates: Timeline for Short-Term and Long-Term Action Steps
  • Worksheets: Action Plan Templates, Decision-Making Tools (Including Decision-Making Matrix for Weighing Options)

Chapter 7: Essential Work Skills and Professional Development

  • Identifying and Developing Transferable Skills
  • Emphasizing Digital Literacy (e.g., Collaboration Tools like Slack/Trello, Basic Data Analysis)
  • Strategies for Skill Enhancement and Lifelong Learning
  • Building a Professional Portfolio, Resume, and Networking Skills
  • Personal Branding: Creating a Professional Online Presence (e.g., LinkedIn, Personal Websites)
  • Preparation for the Workforce (Interview Skills, Workplace Etiquette)
  • Worksheet: Professional Development and Skills Checklist

Chapter 8: Next Steps and Continuous Career Development

  • Planning for Future Growth and Career Transitions
  • Strategies for Career Pivots and Re-Skilling in Changing Industries
  • Establishing a System for Regular Review and Update of Your Career Plan
  • Additional Resources: Professional Organizations, Online Communities, Podcasts for Career Inspiration
  • Support Networks and Mentoring Opportunities
  • Worksheet: Career Review and Update Plan

Capstone Project: Comprehensive Career Portfolio

  • Compile a Career Portfolio Including: Action Plan, Updated Resume, Personal Reflection on Career Planning Journey
