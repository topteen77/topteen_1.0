What You’ll Learn: Course Chapters at a Glance

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to Advanced Soft Skills: Building on the Basics - Recap core skills and set personal goals for post-high school growth.
  • Chapter 2: Advanced Communication and Digital Etiquette: Navigating Real-World Interactions - Refine communication and master online professionalism.
  • Chapter 3: Teamwork, Leadership, and Initiative: Thriving in Group Settings - Excel in collaboration and lead with confidence in diverse teams.
  • Chapter 4: Critical Thinking and Adaptability: Solving Problems in a Changing World - Develop innovative solutions and resilience for life’s transitions.
  • Chapter 5: Time Management and Stress Balance: Preparing for Independence - Balance responsibilities and manage stress for personal success.
  • Chapter 6: Professionalism and Networking: Creating Opportunities After High School - Build a professional image and connect for future growth.
  • Chapter 7: Emotional Intelligence and Conflict Resolution: Managing Relationships - Strengthen relationships through empathy and conflict strategies.
  • Chapter 8: Financial Literacy and Goal Setting: Planning for the Future - Master budgeting and set actionable goals for long-term success.
  • Chapter 9: Public Speaking and Presentation Skills: Communicating with Impact - Gain confidence to present ideas effectively to any audience.
  • Chapter 10: Cultural Awareness and Lifelong Learning: Embracing Growth and Diversity - Respect diversity and cultivate a mindset of continuous learning.
