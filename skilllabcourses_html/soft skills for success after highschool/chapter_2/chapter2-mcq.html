MCQs

Question 1: What is a key tip for speaking with confidence during a job interview?

A) Speak as quickly as possible to show enthusiasm.

B) Use slang to make the conversation casual.

C) Speak clearly and slowly to ensure your message is understood.

D) Avoid making eye contact to stay focused on your thoughts.

Correct Answer: C) Speak clearly and slowly to ensure your message is understood.

 

Question 2: When writing a professional email to a teacher, what should you include to make it effective?

A) Use emojis to express your emotions.

Correct Answer: C) Include a clear subject line and a polite greeting.

 

Question 3: Which non-verbal cue can make you appear more confident in a professional setting?

A) Crossing your arms and looking down.

B) Standing tall with an open posture and making eye contact.

C) Fidgeting with your hands while speaking.

D) Avoiding smiles to seem serious.

Correct Answer: B) Standing tall with an open posture and making eye contact.

 

Question 4: What is an important guideline for respectful online interactions in a group chat for a school project?

A) Send multiple messages if no one replies immediately.

B) Type in all caps to get attention quickly.

C) Use polite language and start with a greeting.

D) Post personal complaints to vent frustration.

Correct Answer: C) Use polite language and start with a greeting.

 

Question 5: How can you maintain a positive digital footprint for future employers or colleges to see?

A) Post everything publicly to show transparency.

B) Share content that reflects your achievements and values.

C) Avoid using social media altogether.

D) Ignore privacy settings since they don’t matter.

Correct Answer: B) Share content that reflects your achievements and values.

 

Question 6: What is a practical step to protect your personal information online?

A) Use the same password for all accounts for easy recall.

B) Share your location on social media to connect with friends.

C) Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your accounts.

D) Click on links in emails to verify your account details.

Correct Answer: C) Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your accounts.
