MCQs

Question 1: How Ads Target TeensWhy are teens considered a prime target for advertisers?

A) Teens have unlimited money to spend on products.

B) Teens are less active online compared to adults.

C) Teens are shaping their identities and are influenced by trends, making them open to ads.

D) Teens don’t use social media platforms where ads are common.Correct Answer:C) Teens are shaping their identities and are influenced by trends, making them open to ads.

 

Question 2: Marketing Tactics on PlatformsHow do ads on TikTok often blend in with regular content to target teens?

A) They appear as long, formal commercials with detailed product info.

B) They mimic viral content like challenges or dances using trending sounds.

C) They are only shown in separate sections away from user posts.

D) They avoid using hashtags to stay unnoticed by users.Correct Answer:B) They mimic viral content like challenges or dances using trending sounds.

 

Question 3: Influencer Endorsements and Hidden SponsorshipsWhat is a common issue with influencer endorsements on social media?

A) Influencers always clearly label every post as an ad with #ad or #sponsored.

B) Influencers never get paid to promote products to their followers.

C) Influencers may not disclose they were paid, making a promotion seem like a genuine opinion.

D) Influencers only promote products they dislike to be honest with fans.Correct Answer:C) Influencers may not disclose they were paid, making a promotion seem like a genuine opinion.

 

Question 4: Recognizing Persuasive Techniques – Emotional AppealsHow do emotional appeals in ads work to influence teen consumers?

A) They provide detailed facts and statistics about a product’s quality.

B) They create a sense of urgency by claiming limited stock is available.

C) They connect a product to strong feelings like happiness or insecurity to motivate a purchase.

D) They show that everyone else is buying the product to avoid missing out.Correct Answer:C) They connect a product to strong feelings like happiness or insecurity to motivate a purchase.

 

Question 5: Recognizing Persuasive Techniques – Scarcity TacticsWhat is the main goal of scarcity tactics like “Limited time offer!” in advertisements?

A) To give consumers plenty of time to research the product before buying.

B) To create urgency and pressure teens to buy quickly before they miss out.

C) To ensure the product is always available at a low price.

D) To encourage teens to ignore the ad and look for better deals elsewhere.Correct Answer:B) To create urgency and pressure teens to buy quickly before they miss out.

 

Question 6: Making Informed Consumer ChoicesWhat is a practical strategy for teens to avoid impulse buying after seeing an ad?

A) Buy the product immediately to take advantage of a limited offer.

B) Follow the 24-hour rule by waiting a day before deciding to purchase.

C) Trust the ad’s claims without checking reviews or comparing prices.

D) Ask friends to buy the product for you to avoid personal regret.Correct Answer:B) Follow the 24-hour rule by waiting a day before deciding to purchase.
