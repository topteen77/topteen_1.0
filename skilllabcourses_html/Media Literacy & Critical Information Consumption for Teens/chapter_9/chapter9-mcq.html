MCQs

Question 1: Teens as Media CreatorsWhat is one key benefit of teens using platforms like TikTok and YouTube to share content?

A) They can avoid doing schoolwork by focusing on social media.

B) They can express their unique perspectives and connect with others.

C) They are guaranteed to become famous and make money.

D) They don’t need to worry about the impact of their posts.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 2: Teens as Media CreatorsWhen creating content, why is it important to balance creativity with accuracy?

A) To ensure your posts always go viral.

B) To avoid spreading false information that could mislead or harm others.

C) To make sure your content looks professional with fancy editing.

D) To impress teachers and parents with your skills.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 3: Crafting Messages with PurposeHow should you tailor content differently for TikTok compared to a blog post?

A) TikTok content should be long and detailed, while blog posts should be short and funny.

B) TikTok content should be short and engaging, while blog posts can be longer and in-depth.

C) TikTok content should be formal, while blog posts should use slang and memes.

D) TikTok content should avoid visuals, while blog posts need lots of videos.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 4: Crafting Messages with PurposeWhat is a major risk of using clickbait titles in your personal posts?

B) It can cause followers to lose trust if the title doesn’t match the content.

C) It guarantees more likes and shares every time.

D) It helps build a professional reputation as a creator.Correct Answer: B
