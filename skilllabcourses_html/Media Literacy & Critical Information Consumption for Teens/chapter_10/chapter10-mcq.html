Question 1: Case Studies of Teen Media Literacy WinsWhat was a key factor in the success of Sarah’s TikTok video debunking a health myth?

A) She used humor and credible sources to make her content engaging and trustworthy.

B) She posted the video without checking any facts to get quick views.

C) She focused on a topic unrelated to her peers’ interests.

D) She avoided using any visuals or slogans in her video.Correct Answer: A

 

Question 2: Case Studies of Teen Media Literacy WinsWhy did Jamal’s #LogOffHate Instagram campaign gain traction among students?

A) It ignored personal stories and focused only on random memes.

B) It shared real stories and resources about cyberbullying, connecting with his audience.

C) It criticized victims of cyberbullying to get attention.

D) It was only promoted to teachers and not students.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 3: Hands-On Project SimulationWhat is a primary goal of planning a school campaign to combat misinformation in the group activity?

A) To create posters without researching any facts.

B) To educate peers and stop the spread of false information using credible sources.

C) To focus solely on blaming individuals for spreading rumors.

D) To avoid teamwork and work individually on the project.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 4: Hands-On Project SimulationWhy is ethical messaging important when designing a school campaign?

A) It ensures the campaign gets the most likes on social media.

B) It focuses on educating without shaming or bullying others.

C) It allows the group to ignore facts and focus on opinions.

D) It encourages pointing fingers at specific students.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 5: Preparing for a Media-Savvy FutureHow can media literacy skills help with college research?

A) By teaching you to rely on unverified social media posts for information.

B) By helping you find trustworthy sources like academic journals for stronger arguments.

C) By encouraging you to avoid citing sources in your papers.

D) By focusing only on personal opinions instead of facts.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 6: Preparing for a Media-Savvy FutureWhat is one way teens can lead a media literacy initiative in their school?

A) By ignoring misinformation and focusing only on entertainment content.

B) By starting a media literacy club to discuss fake news and fact-checking with peers.

C) By refusing to share any content or ideas with classmates.

D) By creating campaigns that mock others for not knowing media skills.Correct Answer: B
