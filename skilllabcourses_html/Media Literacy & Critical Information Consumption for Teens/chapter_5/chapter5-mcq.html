MCQs

Question 1: Howdoes social mediaoften influence teen self-esteem?

A) It has no impact since teens focus on real-life interactions.

B) It boosts self-esteem only through offline validation.

C) It can affect self-esteem through likes, comments, and shares, tying worth to online feedback.

D) It always improves self-esteem by connecting teens with friends.

Correct Answer: C) It can affect self-esteem through likes, comments, and shares, tying worth to online feedback.

 

Question 2: What is meant by “social comparison” on social media?

A) Comparing the number of followers between different platforms.

B) Comparing one’s own life to the curated, often perfect-looking lives of others online, this can lower self-esteem.

C) Comparing privacy settings to ensure safety online.

D) Comparing the quality of posts to improve content creation.

Correct Answer: B) Comparing one’s own life to the curated, often perfect-looking lives of others online, which can lower self-esteem.

 

Question 3: What is a digital footprint?

A) The physical space your phone takes up when using social media.

B) The trail of data left behind by your online activities, like posts and likes, which can be permanent.

C) The number of apps you have downloaded on your device.

D) The time you spend scrolling through social media daily.

Correct Answer: B) The trail of data left behind by your online activities, like posts and likes, which can be permanent.

 

Question 4: Why is oversharing on social media considered risky?

A) It can make your posts less popular among friends.

B) It can expose personal information to strangers, increasing dangers like identity theft or harassment.

C) It always leads to losing followers on your accounts.

D) It prevents you from updating privacy settings.

Correct Answer: B) It can expose personal information to strangers, increasing dangers like identity theft or harassment.

 

Question 5: What is a recommended first step when dealing with online trolls or cyberbullying?

A) Argue with the troll to defend yourself and prove them wrong.

B) Ignore the hate and avoid engaging, as trolls often want a reaction.

C) Delete your social media accounts immediately to avoid further issues.

D) Post a public response to shame the troll in front of others.

Correct Answer: B) Ignore the hate and avoid engaging, as trolls often want a reaction.

 

Question 6: How can teens contribute to a positive online environment?

A) By posting as much personal information as possible to build trust.

B) By hyping up friends’ posts, supporting targets of bullying, and reporting toxic behaviour.

C) By joining every group chat to stay updated on drama.

D) By following accounts that spread rumours to stay informed.

Correct Answer: B) By hyping up friends’ posts, supporting targets of bullying, and reporting toxic behaviour.
