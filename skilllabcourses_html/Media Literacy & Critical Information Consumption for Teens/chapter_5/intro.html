Chapter 5: Social Media and Digital Footprints

Hey there, high school students! Welcome to Chapter 5, where we’re diving into the wild world of social media and the digital trails you leave behind without even realizing it. As teens, you’re probably on platforms like Instagram, TikTok, or Snapchat every day, but do you know how much of your life is shaped—or exposed—by what you post and interact with? In this chapter, we’ll explore how social media impacts your identity, relationships, and privacy, and how to navigate its challenges like a pro. Let’s jump in and take control of your online world!

  • How Social Media Shapes Teen Identity and Relationships Social media isn’t just about memes and trends—it’s a huge part of how you see yourself and connect with others. Likes, comments, and shares can boost your confidence one minute and crush it the next, messing with your self-esteem. Plus, scrolling through curated, “perfect” lives online often makes reality feel like it doesn’t measure up. We’ll unpack how these platforms influence who you are and how you relate to friends, so you can keep a healthy balance between your online and offline self.
  • Understanding Your Digital Footprint Everything you do online—every post, like, or share—leaves a mark called your digital footprint, and it’s way more permanent than you might think. Even “private” stuff can stick around forever, and oversharing can come back to bite you. We’ll also look at privacy settings and how companies collect your data, often without you noticing. For teens who grew up digital, knowing how to manage your footprint is crucial to protecting your future, whether it’s for college apps or just personal safety.
  • Dealing with Online Trolls and Toxicity Let’s face it: the internet isn’t always a nice place. Cyberbullying, hate comments, and toxic group chats can hit hard, and misinformation can spread drama fast. As teens, you’re often on the front lines of this negativity, so we’ll cover strategies to handle trolls—whether it’s clapping back with confidence or knowing when to disengage safely. Learning how to navigate online toxicity will help you keep your mental health and relationships intact in the digital space.

In this chapter, we’ll explore these topics and get hands-on with a “Digital Footprint Audit” activity, where you’ll review your social media profiles to spot privacy risks and questionable content. By the end, you’ll have the know-how to use social media smartly, protect your digital presence, and handle online drama like a champ. Let’s get started on mastering your digital life!
