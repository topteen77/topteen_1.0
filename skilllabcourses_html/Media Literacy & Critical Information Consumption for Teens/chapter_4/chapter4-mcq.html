MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary difference between misinformation and disinformation?

A) Misinformation is always about politics, while disinformation is about health.

B) Misinformation is shared unintentionally, while disinformation is spread deliberately to deceive.

C) Misinformation is created by teens, while disinformation is created by adults.

D) Misinformation is fake news, while disinformation is a type of meme.

Correct Answer: B) Misinformation is shared unintentionally, while disinformation is spread deliberately to deceive.

 

Question 2: Why are teens particularly vulnerable to misinformation on social media?

A) They lack access to fact-checking tools.

B) They rely heavily on platforms that prioritize engagement over accuracy and are influenced by peer pressure.

C) They are not allowed to use credible news sources.

D) They do not understand how the internet works.

Correct Answer: B) They rely heavily on platforms that prioritize engagement over accuracy and are influenced by peer pressure.

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a common trick used by creators of fake news to deceive readers?

A) Providing detailed sources for every claim.

B) Using misleading headlines to grab attention with clickbait phrases.

C) Sharing content only in long, detailed articles.

D) Avoiding the use of images or videos.

Correct Answer: B) Using misleading headlines to grab attention withclickbaitphrases.

 

Question 4: What is a practical tool mentioned in the chapter for verifying the authenticity of a suspicious photo?

A) Posting the photo on social media to ask friends.

B) Using a reverse image search tool like Google Images or TinEye.

C) Ignoring the photo if it looks suspicious.

D) Editing the photo to see if it changes.

Correct Answer: B) Using a reverse image search tool like Google Images orTinEye.

 

Question 5: What is a real-world consequence of sharing health-related misinformation, as discussed in the chapter?

A) It can improve public awareness of health issues.

B) It can lead to dangerous behaviors, like ingesting harmful substances during a health crisis.

C) It only affects online communities and not real life.

D) It encourages people to visit doctors more often.

Correct Answer: B) It can lead to dangerousbehaviors, like ingesting harmful substances during a health crisis.

 

Question 6: What personal risk might a teen face by sharing false information online?

A) Gaining more followers on social media.

B) Damaging their reputation or facing legal issues for spreading defamatory content.

C) Receiving awards for creative content.

D) Being praised by teachers for online activity.

Correct Answer: B) Damaging their reputation or facing legal issues for spreading defamatory content.
