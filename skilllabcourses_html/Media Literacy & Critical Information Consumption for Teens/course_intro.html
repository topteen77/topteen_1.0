Course Introduction: Media Literacy &amp; Critical Information Consumption for Teens

Welcome to Media Literacy &amp; Critical Information Consumption for Teens, a transformative and engaging course designed exclusively for high school students in grades 9-12. In today’s digital age, where information bombards us from every corner of the internet, mastering the art of navigating media with a critical mindset is not just a skill—it’s an essential superpower! This course empowers teens to discern credible sources, combat misinformation, and understand the profound influence media has on opinions, trends, and personal choices.

Over the course of 11 impactful weeks, you’ll explore vital topics such as:

  • Introduction to Media Literacy, where you’ll uncover why it matters in your world;
  • Understanding Media Influence and Bias, to see how content shapes perspectives;
  • Identifying Credible Sources, to ensure you’re building knowledge on solid ground;
  • Combating Misinformation and Fake News, to become a detective of truth;
  • Social Media and Digital Footprints, to protect your online presence;
  • Advertising and Consumer Manipulation, to resist clever marketing traps;
  • News Literacy for Teens, to engage with current events critically;
  • Media Ethics and Responsible Sharing, to share content with integrity;
  • Media Creation and Critical Expression, to unleash your creativity responsibly;
  • Real-World Applications and Practice, to apply skills in meaningful ways;
  • Building Your Media Literacy Toolkit, our capstone chapter to craft a personalized guide for the future.

Through interactive activities, real-world case studies, and inspiring projects, you’ll learn to question what you see, verify what you hear, and share content with purpose. Whether you’re scrolling through Instagram, catching up on breaking news, or crafting your own viral posts, this course will shape you into a confident digital citizen ready to excel in academics, future careers, and civic life.

Enroll today and embark on a journey to sharpen your critical thinking, safeguard against online pitfalls, and harness the power of media for positive change. Your voice is powerful—let us help you make it resonate with impact! Join now and lead the charge against misinformation!
