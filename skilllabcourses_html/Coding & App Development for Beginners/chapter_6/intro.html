Chapter 6: Introduction to Web Development with HTML and CSS

Dive into the creative world of building websites with Introduction to Web Development with HTML and CSS, tailored for high school students eager to craft their own corner of the internet. Web development is the art of designing and creating websites, combining structure and style to bring ideas to life online. This chapter introduces you to the foundational tools—HTML for content and CSS for design—that power the web as we know it.

Learn how websites function, master the basics of structuring pages with HTML, and add flair with CSS styling techniques. You’ll build your very own webpage, such as a personal portfolio or blog, and discover how to share it with the world using free hosting tools. This chapter is your gateway to creating digital experiences that can reach a global audience.

Chapter Content Overview

  • What is Web Development? How Websites Work - Understand the fundamentals of web development and website mechanics.
  • Basics of HTML: Structure and Tags - Learn to create the skeleton of a webpage using HTML.
  • Styling with CSS: Colours, Fonts, and Layouts - Add visual appeal to your pages with CSS design elements.
  • Creating Your First Webpage (Personal Portfolio or Blog) - Build a unique webpage to showcase your skills or ideas.
  • Hosting and Sharing Your Webpage Online (Free Tools) - Discover how to publish your site for others to see using free platforms.

Get excited to design and launch your own website with HTML and CSS! This chapter will ignite your creativity, giving you the power to build online spaces that reflect who you are. Let’s jump into web development together, create something amazing, and share your digital masterpiece with the world!
