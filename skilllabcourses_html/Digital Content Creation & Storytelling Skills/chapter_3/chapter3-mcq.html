MCQs

Question 1: What is the main advantage of using a smartphone for photography compared to a DSLR camera?

A) Smartphones always produce higher quality images than DSLRs

B) Smartphones are more expensive but have better lenses

C) Smartphones are portable, always with you, and have easy-to-use features like portrait mode

D) Smartphones allow for more manual control over settings than DSLRs

Correct Answer: C) Smartphones are portable, always with you, and have easy-to-use features like portrait mode

 

Question 2: What does the "Rule of Thirds" composition technique suggest for creating a balanced photo?

A) Place the subject in the exact center of the frame

B) Place the subject along the lines or intersections of an imaginary 3x3 grid

C) Always shoot from a low angle to make the subject look bigger

D) Fill the entire frame with the subject to avoid empty space

Correct Answer: B) Place the subject along the lines or intersections of an imaginary 3x3 grid

 

Question 3: When is the best time to shoot photos using natural light for a warm, flattering glow?

A) Midday when the sun is directly overhead

B) Late at night using moonlight

C) During golden hour, just after sunrise or before sunset

D) On a completely overcast day with no visible sun

Correct Answer: C) During golden hour, just after sunrise or before sunset

Question 5: Why is creating a consistent visual style important for your digital content?

A) It makes your content look boring and predictable

B) It helps your content become instantly recognizable to followers and builds your brand identity

C) It ensures all your photos are taken with the same camera

D) It prevents you from experimenting with new photography techniques

Correct Answer: B) It helps your content become instantly recognizable to followers and builds your brand identity
