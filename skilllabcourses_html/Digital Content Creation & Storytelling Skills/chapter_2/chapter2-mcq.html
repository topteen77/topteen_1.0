MCQs

Question 1: What is the purpose of a "conflict" in a story?

A) To introduce the main character

B) To make the story boring and predictable

C) To create a problem or challenge that keeps the audience interested

D) To wrap up the story with a happy ending

Correct Answer: C) To create a problem or challenge that keeps the audience interested

 

Question 2: Which narrative structure starts with the end of the story and then flashes back to show how it happened?

A) Linear Structure

B) Non-Linear Structure

C) Three-Act Structure

D) Circular Structure

Correct Answer: B) Non-Linear Structure

 

Question 3: What is the main goal of a "hook" in digital content?

A) To explain the entire story in the first few seconds

B) To grab the audience’s attention and make them want to keep watching

C) To show the resolution of the story right at the start

D) To confuse the audience so they rewatch the content

Correct Answer: B) To grab the audience’s attention and make them want to keep watching

 

Question 4: How can emotional storytelling help connect with your audience?

A) By using only funny content to make them laugh

B) By sharing genuine feelings and relatable experiences that make them feel something

C) By avoiding personal stories to keep the content professional

D) By using loud music to cover up any emotional moments

Correct Answer: B) By sharing genuine feelings and relatable experiences that make them feel something
