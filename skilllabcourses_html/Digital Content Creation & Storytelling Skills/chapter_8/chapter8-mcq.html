MCQs

Question 1: What is the main purpose of checking analytics like views, watch time, and engagement metrics for your online content?

A) To compare your stats with popular creators and feel discouraged

B) To understand how your audience interacts with your content and identify what works or flops

C) To ignore the numbers and focus only on making random videos

D) To change your niche every week based on small changes in data

Correct Answer: B) To understand how your audience interacts with your content and identify what works or flops

 

Question 2: How can insights about your audience, such as their age range and active times, help improve your content on platforms like TikTok or Instagram?

A) They tell you to post at random times to confuse your followers

B) They help you create content that matches their vibe and post when they’re most likely to see it

C) They are useless since all audiences behave the same way

D) They only matter if you already have thousands of followers

Correct Answer: B) They help you create content that matches their vibe and post when they’re most likely to see it

 

Question 3: Why is it important to jump on trends and understand platform algorithms when creating content for social media?

A) To copy every trend exactly without adding your personal style

B) To stay relevant, increase visibility, and get pushed to more viewers by the platform’s system

C) To avoid using trending hashtags or sounds that could boost your reach

D) To focus only on old content styles that are no longer popular

Correct Answer: B) To stay relevant, increase visibility, and get pushed to more viewers by the platform’s system

 

Question 4: What is a key benefit of collaborations and cross-promotions with other creators for growing your online presence?

A) They make your content less interesting by involving too many people

B) They help you reach new audiences, build creator friendships, and create fun, engaging content

C) They only work if the other creator has millions of followers

D) They are a waste of time compared to working solo on every post

Correct Answer: B) They help you reach new audiences, build creator friendships, and create fun, engaging content
