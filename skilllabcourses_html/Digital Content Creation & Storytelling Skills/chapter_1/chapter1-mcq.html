MCQs

Question 1: What is digital content creation primarily about?

A) Writing essays for school assignments

B) Creating videos, photos, or posts to share online

C) Designing physical products for sale

D) Playing video games without recording them

Correct Answer: B) Creating videos, photos, or posts to share online

 

Question 2: Which platform is best suited for short, trendy videos lasting 15-60 seconds?

C) TikTok

D) Twitter (X)

 

Question 3: Why is identifying a niche important for a digital content creator?

A) It helps you use every platform at once

B) It makes your content stand out and builds a loyal following

C) It ensures you never have to learn new skills

D) It guarantees you’ll make money immediately

Correct Answer: B) It makes your content stand out and builds a loyal following

 

Question 4: What is an ethical consideration when creating digital content?

A) Using any music or video clip you like without permission

B) Sharing personal information about others without consent

C) Respecting copyright and privacy rules

D) Spreading unverified information to gain views

Correct Answer: C) Respecting copyright and privacy rules
