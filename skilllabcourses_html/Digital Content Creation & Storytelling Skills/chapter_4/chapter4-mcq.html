MCQs

Question 1: What is a key advantage of using a smartphone for video creation compared to a DSLR camera?

A) Smartphones always have better video quality than DSLRs

B) Smartphones are more expensive but offer superior manual controls

C) Smartphones are portable, easy to use, and can shoot HD video right from your pocket

D) Smartphones allow for interchangeable lenses for cinematic effects

Correct Answer: C) Smartphones are portable, easy to use, and can shoot HD video right from your pocket

 

Question 2: What is the purpose of a storyboard in video planning?

A) To write detailed dialogue for every scene in the video

B) To create a visual map of shots and scenes to organize the video’s flow

C) To list all the equipment needed for filming

D) To edit the video after filming is complete

Correct Answer: B) To create a visual map of shots and scenes to organize the video’s flow

 

Question 3: Which shooting technique would you use to make a subject look powerful or intimidating in a video?

A) Eye-Level Angle

B) High Angle (Looking Down)

C) Low Angle (Looking Up)

D) Wide Shot

Correct Answer: C) Low Angle (Looking Up)

 

Question 4: Why is recording quality audio important for videos, and how can you improve it on a budget?

A) Audio doesn’t matter as long as the visuals are good; use any mic

B) Clear audio keeps viewers engaged; use a cheap lavalier mic or earbuds with a built-in mic

C) Audio is only important for music videos; record in noisy areas for realism

D) Good audio requires expensive studio equipment; avoid recording without it

Correct Answer: B) Clear audio keeps viewers engaged; use a cheap lavalier mic or earbuds with a built-in mic

 

Question 5: What is a simple way to build confidence and engage your audience when appearing on camera?

A) Memorize a long script to avoid mistakes and sound professional

B) Avoid looking at the camera to feel less nervous

C) Talk to the camera like it’s a friend, make eye contact with the lens, and ask viewers questions

D) Keep videos silent to focus on visuals instead of speaking

Correct Answer: C) Talk to the camera like it’s a friend, make eye contact with the lens, and ask viewers questions
