MCQs

Question 1: What is the main purpose of creating a personal or channel brand identity for your online content?

A) To copy popular creators and gain quick followers

B) To make your content recognizable and unique so people remember and connect with your vibe

C) To change your style constantly to confuse your audience

D) To focus only on visuals without considering your personality or niche

Correct Answer: B) To make your content recognizable and unique so people remember and connect with your vibe

 

Question 2: Why are thumbnails, logos, and channel art important for your online presence?

A) They make your profile look cluttered and hard to navigate

B) They act as the first impression to grab attention and reflect your brand before viewers click

C) They are unnecessary if your content is good enough

D) They should be changed daily to keep things fresh

Correct Answer: B) They act as the first impression to grab attention and reflect your brand before viewers click

 

Question 3: What is a key strategy for writing effective titles, descriptions, and tags to improve visibility on platforms like YouTube or TikTok?

A) Use long, complicated titles to explain every detail of the video

B) Include catchy phrases, keywords, and calls-to-action to attract clicks and boost searchability

C) Avoid using hashtags or tags to keep your content exclusive

D) Write descriptions that are unrelated to the video to surprise viewers

Correct Answer: B) Include catchy phrases, keywords, and calls-to-action to attract clicks and boost searchability

 

Question 4: How does engaging with your audience through comments, polls, and community building benefit your online presence?

A) It discourages followers from interacting with your content

B) It builds a loyal community that feels connected, increasing likes, shares, and growth

C) It only works if you ignore negative comments completely

D) It takes too much time and has no real impact on your following

Correct Answer: B) It builds a loyal community that feels connected, increasing likes, shares, and growth
