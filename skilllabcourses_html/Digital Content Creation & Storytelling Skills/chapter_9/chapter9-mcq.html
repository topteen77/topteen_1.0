MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of monetization for high school content creators?

A) To focus only on making money and ignore creative content

B) To turn digital content into a source of income while pursuing a passion

C) To stop creating content once you earn a small amount

D) To spend all earnings immediately without saving or planning

Correct Answer: B) To turn digital content into a source of income while pursuing a passion

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of earning through ad revenue as a teen creator?

A) Promoting a brand’s product in a video for a one-time payment

B) Sharing an affiliate link for a product and earning a commission per sale

C) Getting paid by YouTube for views on your videos after joining the Partner Program

D) Selling your own merchandise directly to followers

Correct Answer: C) Getting paid by YouTube for views on your videos after joining the Partner Program

 

Question 3: Why is building a portfolio of digital content important for high school students?

A) It helps showcase your best work to impress brands, clients, or schools for future opportunities

B) It is only useful if you already have millions of followers

C) It prevents you from creating new content by focusing on old posts

D) It is a way to delete old content and start fresh

Correct Answer: A) It helps showcase your best work to impress brands, clients, or schools for future opportunities

 

Question 4: How can exploring careers in content creation, filmmaking, or digital marketing benefit high school students?

A) It guarantees an immediate full-time job without finishing school

B) It helps identify potential passions or career paths and builds skills for college or future gigs

C) It forces students to choose a career before they are ready

D) It is irrelevant until after high school graduation

Correct Answer: B) It helps identify potential passions or career paths and builds skills for college or future gigs

 

Question 5: What is a key strategy for balancing content creation with studies and personal life as a high schooler?

A) Ignoring schoolwork to focus entirely on creating content for more views

B) Setting a schedule for content, studies, and downtime while prioritizing mental health

C) Posting content every day regardless of exams or personal stress

D) Avoiding breaks from content creation to maintain follower growth

Correct Answer: B) Setting a schedule for content, studies, and downtime while prioritizing mental health
