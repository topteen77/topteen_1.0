MCQs

Question 1: Which video editing software is free, beginner-friendly, and ideal for quick social media content like TikTok or Instagram Reels?

A) Adobe Premiere Pro

B) DaVinci Resolve

C) CapCut

D) iMovie (only for Windows users)

Correct Answer: C) CapCut

 

Question 2: What is the main purpose of trimming a video clip during editing?

A) To remove an entire section from the middle of the clip

B) To shorten a clip by adjusting its start or end points for better pacing

C) To add special effects between two clips

D) To overlay text or graphics on the video

Correct Answer: B) To shorten a clip by adjusting its start or end points for better pacing

 

Question 3: Why is Colour grading used in video editing, and how can it impact your content?

A) It fixes audio issues to make voices clearer

B) It adjusts brightness and contrast to correct lighting mistakes

C) It stylizes the video with specific Colour tones to create a mood or cinematic look

D) It adds text and graphics for better storytelling

Correct Answer: C) It stylizes the video with specific Colour tones to create a mood or cinematic look

 

Question 4: When adding audio to a video, what should you consider to avoid copyright issues on platforms like YouTube or TikTok?

A) Use any popular song as long as it’s trending

B) Always add your own recorded music without checking platform rules

C) Choose royalty-free music from app libraries or use platform-approved sounds

D) Keep the music volume high to cover any copyright claims

Correct Answer: C) Choose royalty-free music from app libraries or use platform-approved sounds
