MCQs

Question 1: You’re at a school lunch, and your friend starts clutching their throat, unable to speak, with a panicked look. Their face is turning red. What should you do first?

A) Tell them to drink water to clear their throat.

B) Ask, “Are you choking?” and if they can’t answer but signal yes, perform the Heimlich maneuver.

C) Wait a few minutes to see if they cough it out on their own.

D) Pat them on the back gently until they stop choking.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 2: While at a park, you see someone collapse and become unresponsive. You check, and their chest isn’t moving—they’re not breathing normally. What is the first thing you should do?

A) Start chest compressions without calling for help to save time.

B) Check for a pulse to confirm if their heart has stopped.

C) Call 911 or yell for someone to call while you prepare to start hands-only CPR.

D) Wait for a trained adult to arrive before doing anything.Correct Answer: C

 

Question 3: During gym class, a classmate has a seizure and starts shaking on the floor. What is the most important action to take while the seizure is happening?

A) Hold them down to stop the shaking so they don’t hurt themselves.

B) Put a spoon in their mouth to prevent them from biting their tongue.

C) Clear the area of objects and protect their head with something soft like a jacket.

D) Leave them alone and go find a teacher to handle it.Correct Answer: C

 

Question 4: At a hot outdoor event, your friend faints and falls to the ground. They’re unresponsive for a moment. What should you do immediately after ensuring they’re on the ground safely?

A) Splash cold water on their face to wake them up quickly.

B) Lay them flat on their back and elevate their legs to improve blood flow to the brain.

C) Make them sit up right away to get air.

D) Give them food to raise their blood sugar.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 5: You’re at a party, and a friend who ate a cookie starts wheezing, their face swells, and they say their throat feels tight. You suspect anaphylaxis. They have an EpiPen in their bag. What is the correct sequence of actions?

A) Wait to see if symptoms get worse before using the EpiPen, then call for help.

B) Use the EpiPen on their thigh, call 911 immediately, and stay with them until help arrives.

C) Call 911 first, wait for paramedics to arrive, and let them use the EpiPen.

D) Give them water to drink and use the EpiPen only if they stop breathing.Correct Answer: B
