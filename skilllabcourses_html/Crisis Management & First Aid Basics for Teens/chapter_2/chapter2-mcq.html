MCQs

Question 1: What is a key sign that a situation might be an emergency?

A) Someone is laughing and joking around after a small fall.

B) A person is unconscious and not responding after collapsing.

C) Your friend is annoyed about losing a game.

D) The room is a bit messy after a party.Correct Answer: B) A person is unconscious and not responding after collapsing.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of aminoremergency?

A) A classmate faints during gym class and doesn’t wake up quickly.

B) A small paper cut from flipping through a notebook in class.

C) A fire starts spreading in the school cafeteria.

D) Someone is choking and can’t breathe or speak.Correct Answer: B) A small paper cut from flipping through a notebook in class.

 

Question 3: What is the best first step to take if a friend faints during a hot day at a school event?

A) Shake them hard to wake them up immediately.

B) Lay them flat, elevate their legs if possible, and get an adult.

C) Tell them to stand up and walk it off.

D) Leave them alone to rest while you go get a snack.Correct Answer: B) Lay them flat, elevate their legs if possible, and get an adult.

 

Question 4: Which item shouldalwaysbe included in a teen’s emergency kit for on-the-go situations?

A) A gaming console for entertainment during a crisis.

B) A phone charger or power bank to keep communication open.

C) A heavy textbook to use as a weight.

D) A large bag of perishable snacks that spoil quickly.Correct Answer: B) A phone charger or power bank to keep communication open.

 

Question 5: When should you call 911 instead of a parent or school nurse?

A) When you need help with homework after school hours.

B) When a small cut on your finger won’t stop bleeding a little.

C) When someone is not breathing after a serious accident.

D) When you’re late for class and need a quick ride.Correct Answer: C) When someone is not breathing after a serious accident.
