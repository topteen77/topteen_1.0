MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of first aid?

A) To replace professional medical treatment completely

B) To provide immediate, temporary care to prevent worsening of an injury or illness

C) To diagnose the exact medical condition of the injured person

D) To perform complex medical procedures in an emergencyCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 2: What do Good Samaritan laws generally protect you from when providing first aid?

A) Any mistakes, even if you act recklessly

B) Legal liability if you help in good faith and act reasonably

C) The need to ask for consent before helping someone

D) Having to call 911 after assisting someoneCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 3: Which item is NOT typically recommended for a teen’s first aid kit?

A) Antiseptic wipes for cleaning wounds

B) Disposable gloves to protect against germs

C) Prescription medication to share with friends

D) Instant cold packs for swelling or sprainsCorrect Answer: C

 

Question 4: What is the FIRST step you should take before helping someone in an emergency?

A) Immediately apply a bandage to any visible wound

B) Call 911 even if the situation seems minor

C) Assess the scene to ensure it’s safe for you to approach

D) Move the injured person to a more comfortable locationCorrect Answer: C

 

Question 5: When using an instant cold pack for a sprain, what is an important step to avoid injury?

A) Place it directly on the skin for maximum effect

B) Wrap it in a cloth or shirt before applying to the area

C) Keep it on the injury for at least an hour without a break

D) Squeeze it as hard as possible to activate the cooling fasterCorrect Answer: B
