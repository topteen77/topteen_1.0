MCQs

Question 1: When making an emergency call, what is the FIRST piece of information you should provide to the operator?

A) Your name and phone number

B) The condition of the victim

C) Your exact location

D) What you think caused the emergency

Correct Answer: C) Your exact location

 

Question 2: If you’re in the United States and witness a friend collapse at a school event, which number should you dial for emergency help?

A) 112

B) 999

C) 911

D) 000

 

Question 3: While waiting for help to arrive, how should you approach calming an injured friend who is panicking after falling off a bike?

A) Tell them, “Stop crying, it’s not a big deal.”

B) Say, “I’m here with you, help is on the way.”

C) Ask a lot of detailed questions about how they fell.

D) Ignore their emotions and focus on fixing their bike.

Correct Answer: B) Say, “I’m here with you, help is on the way.”

 

Question 4: Before checking a classmate’s twisted ankle during a gym class injury, what is the BEST way to ask for their permission?

A) “I’m going to look at your ankle now, okay?”

B) “Can I take a look at your ankle to see how bad it is?”

C) “You have to let me help you, just hold still.”

D) “I’m checking it whether you like it or not.”

Correct Answer: B) “Can I take a look at your ankle to see how bad it is?”

 

Question 5: During a crisis at a school picnic where someone gets hurt, how should you delegate tasks to your friends to ensure help arrives quickly?

A) Yell, “Someone do something, hurry up!”

B) Say, “Jake, call 911 and tell them we’re at the west field. Mia, stay with her and keep her calm.”

C) Tell everyone to stand back and let you handle it alone.

D) Ask, “Who wants to help?” and wait for volunteers.

Correct Answer: B) Say, “Jake, call 911 and tell them we’re at the west field. Mia, stay with her and keep her calm.”
