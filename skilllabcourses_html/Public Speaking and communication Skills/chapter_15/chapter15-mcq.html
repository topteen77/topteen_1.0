Question 1: According to the chapter, why is reflecting on progress important for high school students in their communication journey?

A) It allows students to ignore their past mistakes and focus only on the present

B) It helps assess growth in confidence, delivery, and audience engagement, building self-awareness and motivation for future challenges

C) It is only necessary for professional speakers, not for students

D) It encourages students to compare themselves to others to feel betterCorrect Answer:B. It helps assess growth in confidence, delivery, and audience engagement, building self-awareness and motivation for future challenges

 

Question 2: What does the chapter suggest as a key purpose of showcasing skills through a final presentation or speech event?

A) To avoid using any learned techniques and just speak casually

B) To demonstrate the communication techniques learned throughout the course, build pride, and prepare for future high-stakes moments

C) To focus only on entertaining the audience without structure

D) To skip preparation since the course is already completeCorrect Answer:B. To demonstrate the communication techniques learned throughout the course, build pride, and prepare for future high-stakes moments
