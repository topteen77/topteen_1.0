Question 1: According to the chapter, what is the primary purpose of understanding persuasion using ethos, pathos, and logos?

A) To manipulate audiences into agreeing without evidence

B) To build convincing arguments by balancing credibility, emotion, and logic for effective influence

C) To focus only on emotional appeals to win over listeners

D) To avoid using facts in order to seem more relatableCorrectAnswer:B. To build convincing arguments by balancing credibility, emotion, and logic for effective influence

Question 3: How does the chapter recommend handling opposing views during a debate?

B) Attack the opponent personally to discredit their argument

C) Rebut respectfully with phrases like “I see your point, but here’s why…” and support with evidence

Question 4: What is a core principle of ethical communication as discussed in the chapter?
