MCQs

Question 1: According to the chapter, why are rehearsal techniques important for high school students preparing for a speech?

A) They allow students to avoid practicing altogether

B) They help improve fluency, confidence, and delivery through structured practice, ensuring a smoother performance

C) They focus only on memorizing lines without considering delivery

D) They are unnecessary if a student is naturally confidentCorrect Answer:B. They help improve fluency, confidence, and delivery through structured practice, ensuring a smoother performance

 

Question 2: What does the chapter suggest as a key benefit of recording and self-review when polishing speech delivery?

A) It allows students to avoid feedback from others

B) It provides an outside perspective to spot strengths and areas for improvement, like filler words or gestures

C) It is only useful for professional speakers, not students

D) It discourages students by focusing only on flawsCorrect Answer:B. It provides an outside perspective to spot strengths and areas for improvement, like filler words or gestures

 

Question 3: How does the chapter recommend structuring peer feedback sessions to make them effective?

A) By only giving negative feedback to highlight mistakes

B) By using a feedback sandwich—starting with a positive, suggesting a fix, and ending with praise—to balance encouragement and improvement

C) By avoiding specific suggestions to keep things vague

D) By refusing to accept feedback to maintain confidenceCorrect Answer:B. By using a feedback sandwich—starting with a positive, suggesting a fix, and ending with praise—to balance encouragement and improvement

 

Question 4: What is a primary focus of time management in speech delivery, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Speaking as quickly as possible to finish early

B) Staying within time limits by pacing content and editing for efficiency, ensuring key points are covered without rushing or dragging

C) Ignoring time limits to include all possible details

D) Adding extra stories to fill time even if irrelevantCorrect Answer:B. Staying within time limits by pacing content and editing for efficiency, ensuring key points are covered without rushing or dragging
