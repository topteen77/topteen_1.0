MCQs

Question 1: According to the chapter, why is preparing for questions important for a speaker?

A) It ensures the audience won’t ask any questions

B) It helps anticipate audience queries and practice thoughtful responses, boosting credibility and calmness

C) It allows the speaker to avoid answering difficult questions

D) It is only necessary for professional speakers, not studentsCorrect Answer:B. It helps anticipate audience queries and practice thoughtful responses, boosting credibility and calmness

 

Question 2: What is a key component of active listening skills as described in the chapter?

A) Thinking about your response while the other person is speaking

B) Fully focusing on and understanding the question before answering, showing respect through attention and body language

C) Interrupting to answer quickly and show confidence

D) Looking at notes or the audience instead of the askerCorrect Answer:B. Fully focusing on and understanding the question before answering, showing respect through attention and body language

 

Question 3: How does the chapter suggest responding with confidence to a tough or unexpected question?

A) Immediately say “I don’t know” and move on

B) Pause briefly, stay calm, use a confident tone, and structure a short, clear answer even if unsure

C) Avoid answering by changing the topic

D) Speak as quickly as possible to hide uncertaintyCorrect Answer:B. Pause briefly, stay calm, use a confident tone, and structure a short, clear answer even if unsure

 

Question 4: What does the chapter recommend for accepting and using feedback effectively?

A) Ignore criticism and focus only on positive comments

B) Listen without defending, ask for specifics, and apply actionable points to improve skills

C) Argue with the person giving feedback to clarify your intent

D) Avoid seeking feedback to prevent hearing negative commentsCorrect Answer:B. Listen without defending, ask for specifics, and apply actionable points to improve skills

 

Question 5: What is a common challenge teens face when handling feedback, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Always receiving too much positive feedback, making it hard to improve

B) Feeling embarrassed or defensive about criticism, often brushing it off instead of using it for growth

C) Never receiving feedback because they don’t ask for it

D) Finding feedback too easy to apply without effortCorrect Answer:B. Feeling embarrassed or defensive about criticism, often brushing it off instead of using it for growth
