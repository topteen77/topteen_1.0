MCQs

Question 1: What is stage fright, as described in the chapter?

A) A rare condition that only affects professional speakers

B) A normal reaction involving physical and mental nervousness when speaking in front of others

C) A permanent fear that cannot be overcome with practice

D) A sign that someone is not meant to be a public speakerCorrect Answer:B. A normal reaction involving physical and mental nervousness when speaking in front of others

 

Question 2: According to the chapter, what is one benefit of using positive self-talk as a mindset shift for confidence?

A) It eliminates all physical symptoms of nervousness instantly

B) It helps replace negative thoughts with encouraging ones to reduce stress

C) It guarantees a perfect speech every time

D) It prevents the need for any practice before speakingCorrect Answer:B. It helps replace negative thoughts with encouraging ones to reduce stress

 

Question 3: How does the chapter suggest posture can impact a speaker’s confidence?

A) Slouching makes a speaker appear more relatable to the audience

B) Standing tall with shoulders back projects control and can make a speaker feel stronger

C) Posture has no effect on how a speaker is perceived by others

D) Only professional speakers need to worry about postureCorrect Answer:B. Standing tall with shoulders back projects control and can make a speaker feel stronger

 

Question 4: What is one purpose of recording oneself as a practice strategy, according to the chapter?

A) To share videos on social media for popularity

B) To spot habits like saying “um” or looking down and track improvement over time

C) To avoid practicing in front of others completely

D) To memorize speeches without needing to understand themCorrect Answer:B. To spot habits like saying “um” or looking down and track improvement over time

 

Question 5: Why does the chapter emphasize the importance of eye contact in body language?

A) It distracts the audience from noticing mistakes in the speech

B) It shows engagement and builds trust with listeners

C) It is only necessary for large audiences, not small groups

D) It makes the speaker appear less nervous without any effortCorrect Answer:B. It shows engagement and builds trust with listeners

 

Question 6: What is the main goal of reframing failure as growth, as discussed in the chapter?

A) To ignore mistakes and pretend they didn’t happen

B) To view mistakes as lessons that help improve future performances

C) To blame others for any errors during a speech

D) To avoid taking risks in public speaking altogetherCorrect Answer:B. To view mistakes as lessons that help improve future performances
