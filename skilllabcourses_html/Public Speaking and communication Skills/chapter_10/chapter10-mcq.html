MCQs

Question 1: According to the chapter, what is a primary reason public speaking anxiety matters for high school students?

A) It prevents students from ever speaking in public

B) It can disrupt focus and performance, but can be managed with tools to stay calm and deliver effectively

C) It only affects professional speakers, not students

D) It is an unavoidable barrier that cannot be overcomeCorrect Answer:B. It can disrupt focus and performance, but can be managed with tools to stay calm and deliver effectively

 

Question 2: What is one practical tool the chapter suggests for dealing with public speaking anxiety?

A) Avoiding practice to reduce stress

B) Using deep breathing exercises, like inhaling for 4 seconds, holding for 4, and exhaling for 4, to calm nerves

C) Speaking as quickly as possible to finish sooner

D) Ignoring physical symptoms like shaky handsCorrect Answer:B. Using deep breathing exercises, like inhaling for 4 seconds, holding for 4, and exhaling for 4, to calm nerves

 

Question 3: How does the chapter recommend managing mistakes during a speech, such as forgetting lines?

A) Stop speaking entirely and apologize repeatedly to the audience

B) Pause briefly, take a breath, use a recovery line like “Let me rephrase that,” and continue smoothly

C) Ignore the mistake and pretend it didn’t happen

D) Laugh loudly to distract the audience from the errorCorrect Answer:B. Pause briefly, take a breath, use a recovery line like “Let me rephrase that,” and continue smoothly

 

Question 4: What strategy does the chapter offer for handling difficult audiences, such as hecklers?

A) Argue with them immediately to defend your point

B) Stay calm, keep a steady tone, and respond with a polite line like “Thanks for the input, let’s discuss after”

C) Stop the speech and ask them to leave

D) Ignore them completely and focus only on your notesCorrect Answer:B. Stay calm, keep a steady tone, and respond with a polite line like “Thanks for the input, let’s discuss after”

 

Question 5: According to the chapter, what does building resilience in public speaking involve?

A) Avoiding all setbacks by not speaking in public

B) Viewing setbacks as learning opportunities and maintaining motivation to improve over time

C) Focusing only on perfect performances to build confidence

D) Ignoring feedback to prevent feeling discouragedCorrect Answer:B. Viewing setbacks as learning opportunities and maintaining motivation to improve over time

 

Question 6: What is a common challenge teens face when building resilience, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Always receiving positive feedback, which prevents growth

B) Taking setbacks personally or losing motivation after a flop, instead of seeing them as chances to learn

C) Never experiencing setbacks in public speaking

D) Finding resilience too easy to develop without effortCorrect Answer:B. Taking setbacks personally or losing motivation after a flop, instead of seeing them as chances to learn
