MCQs

Question 1: What is a key characteristic of a strong password?

A) It should be short and easy to remember, like your name

B) It should be at least 12 characters long with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

C) It should be the same for all your accounts to avoid forgetting

D) It should include your birthdate for personal significance

Correct Answer: B) It should be at least 12 characters long with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

 

Question 2: How does a password manager help with account security?

A) It automatically logs you into websites without a password

B) It stores unique, complex passwords securely so you don’t have to remember them

C) It shares your passwords with trusted friends for backup

D) It creates weak passwords that are easy to type quickly

Correct Answer: B) It stores unique, complex passwords securely so you don’t have to remember them

 

Question 3: What is the purpose of Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

A) To make logging in faster by skipping passwords

B) To add an extra layer of security by requiring a second step, like a code sent to your phone

C) To allow others to access your account with a shared code

D) To store your passwords in a second location for safety

Correct Answer: B) To add an extra layer of security by requiring a second step, like a code sent to your phone

 

Question 4: Which of the following is a common sign of a phishing attempt?

A) An email from a trusted company with no links or urgent requests

B) A message claiming “Your account will be deleted in 24 hours!” with a suspicious link

C) A text from a friend asking how you’re doing without any links

D) A notification from an app you use with correct spelling and branding

Correct Answer: B) A message claiming “Your account will be deleted in 24 hours!” with a suspicious link
