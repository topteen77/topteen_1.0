MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of cyber security?

A) To make the internet faster for users

B) To protect devices, data, and online accounts from digital threats

C) To create new social media platforms

D) To design better video games

Correct Answer: B) To protect devices, data, and online accounts from digital threats

 

Question 2: Why is digital safety especially important for teens in their everyday life?

A) Teens spend a lot of time online, increasing their exposure to risks

B) Teens don’t use the internet as much as adults

C) Teens are not allowed to use social media

D) Digital safety only matters for schoolwork

Correct Answer: A) Teens spend a lot of time online, increasing their exposure to risks

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of a common digital risk?

A) Using a strong password for your accounts

B) Clicking on a fake email link that asks for your login details

C) Updating your device software regularly

D) Sharing only with trusted friends on social media

Correct Answer: B) Clicking on a fake email link that asks for your login details

 

Question 4: What is a specific cyber threat that often targets teenagers on social media?

A) Updating privacy settings

B) Cyberbullying through mean comments or rumors

C) Using two-factor authentication

D) Posting only educational content

Correct Answer: B) Cyberbullying through mean comments or rumors

 

Question 5: What can happen if you overshare personal information on social media as a teen?

A) Your account becomes more popular

B) Strangers might track you or misuse your information

C) Your privacy settings automatically improve

D) You get more followers instantly

Correct Answer: B) Strangers might track you or misuse your information
