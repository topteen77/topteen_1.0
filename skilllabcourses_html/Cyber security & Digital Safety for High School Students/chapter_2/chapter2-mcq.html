MCQs

Question 1: What is considered personal data?

A) A random meme you found online

B) Your full name and home address

C) A public news article

D) A funny video with no identifiable info

Correct Answer: B) Your full name and home address

 

Question 2: Why is privacy important for teens online?

A) It helps you get more followers on social media

B) It protects you from risks like identity theft and stalking

C) It makes your posts go viral faster

D) It ensures your phone battery lasts longer

Correct Answer: B) It protects you from risks like identity theft and stalking

 

Question 3: What is a safe way to use privacy settings on social media?

A) Keep your account public so everyone can see your posts

B) Set your account to private so only approved followers can view your content

C) Share your location in every post to connect with friends

D) Allow strangers to send you direct messages without restrictions

Correct Answer: B) Set your account to private so only approved followers can view your content

 

Question 4: Which of the following should you avoid posting online to stay safe?

A) A picture of your pet without any location tags

B) Your home address and school schedule in a public post

C) A funny meme with no personal information

D) A group photo where everyone agreed to be shared

Correct Answer: B) Your home address and school schedule in a public post

 

Question 5: What is a potential risk of oversharing personal data on social media?

A) Your account might get more likes and shares

B) Strangers could use your info for cyberbullying or scams

C) Your friends will automatically protect your privacy

D) Your posts will be deleted by the app automatically

Correct Answer: B) Strangers could use your info for cyberbullying or scams
