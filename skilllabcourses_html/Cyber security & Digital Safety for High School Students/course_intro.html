Introduction to Cyber Security &amp; Digital Safety for High School Students

Dive into the digital age with confidence and power! Welcome to "Cyber Security &amp; Digital Safety for High School Students", a groundbreaking skill enhancement course crafted exclusively for students in classes 9-12. In a world where every click, share, and download shapes your digital identity, knowing how to protect yourself online is your ultimate superpower. This course is your gateway to mastering the art of safeguarding personal data, outsmarting online threats, and embracing safe internet habits. With captivating lessons, real-world challenges, and hands-on activities, students will not only learn to defend their digital lives but also become champions of a secure online community. Whether you're just starting out or already a tech enthusiast, this course equips you with the critical skills to thrive in the fast-paced, ever-changing digital universe.

Course Outline: Chapters at a Glance

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to Cyber Security and Digital Safety - Discover the fundamentals of staying safe online.
  • Chapter 2: Understanding Personal Data and Privacy - Learn what personal data is and why protecting it matters.
  • Chapter 3: Passwords and Account Security - Master the art of creating and managing secure passwords.
  • Chapter 4: Recognizing Online Threats - Identify and respond to dangers like malware and cyberbullying.
  • Chapter 5: Safe Internet Habits - Develop practices for secure browsing and app usage.
  • Chapter 6: Protecting Devices and Data - Secure your gadgets with updates, backups, and antivirus tools.
  • Chapter 7: Social Media and Digital Footprint - Manage your online presence and its long-term impact.
  • Chapter 8: Cyber Laws and Ethical Online Behaviour - Understand legal consequences and ethical digital conduct.
  • Chapter 9: Responding to Cyber Incidents - Know how to act if hacked or facing online harassment.
  • Chapter 10: Building a Cyber-Safe Future - Prepare for lifelong digital safety and explore cyber careers.
