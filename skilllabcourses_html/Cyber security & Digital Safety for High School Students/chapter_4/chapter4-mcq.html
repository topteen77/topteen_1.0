MCQs

Question 1: What is malware, and how does it typically infiltrate a device?

A) A type of hardware that boosts internet speed; installed through official stores

B) Malicious software designed to harm devices; often enters through sketchy links or downloads

C) A secure app for protecting passwords; installed by default on all devices

D) A digital currency used by hackers; transferred through online payments

Correct Answer: B) Malicious software designed to harm devices; often enters through sketchy links or downloads

 

Question 2: What makes ransomware particularly dangerous for teens?

A) It speeds up your device but deletes unimportant files

B) It locks your device or files and demands payment to unlock them, risking data loss

C) It improves gaming performance but requires a subscription

D) It automatically updates apps without user permission

Correct Answer: B) It locks your device or files and demands payment to unlock them, risking data loss

 

Question 3: How can you identify a potential phishing attempt in a message or email?

A) It comes from a verified account with a blue checkmark and no links

B) It uses urgent language like “Act now!” and has suspicious links or typos

C) It offers a free subscription to a trusted app with clear instructions

D) It includes a personal greeting from someone you know without any requests

Correct Answer: B) It uses urgent language like “Act now!” and has suspicious links or typos

 

Question 4: Which of the following is an example of cyberbullying?

A) A friend sending you a funny meme in a private chat

B) Someone repeatedly posting mean comments about you on social media for others to see

C) A classmate asking for help with homework in a group chat

D) An app notification reminding you to update your profile picture

Correct Answer: B) Someone repeatedly posting mean comments about you on social media for others to see

 

Question 5: What is a recommended step to take if you’re experiencing online harassment or cyberbullying?

A) Respond with insults to defend yourself and show strength

B) Delete all your social media accounts immediately without telling anyone

C) Save evidence like screenshots, block the person, and tell a trusted adult

D) Ignore the messages completely and hope they stop on their own

Correct Answer: C) Save evidence like screenshots, block the person, and tell a trusted adult

 

Question 6: How can you verify if a viral social media post is fake news or misinformation?

A) Share it immediately to see if others agree it’s true

B) Check the source, cross-check with trusted news outlets, and use fact-checking sites

C) Trust the post if it has a lot of likes and comments

D) Assume it’s real if it matches your personal opinions

Correct Answer: B) Check the source, cross-check with trusted news outlets, and use fact-checking sites
