MCQs

Question 1: What does the “S” in HTTPS stand for, and why is it important when browsing the internet?

A) Speed; it makes websites load faster for better streaming

B) Secure; it encrypts your data to protect it from hackers

C) Simple; it simplifies the website design for easier use

D) Social; it connects you to social media platforms safely

Correct Answer: B) Secure; it encrypts your data to protect it from hackers

 

Question 2: What is a major risk of using public Wi-Fi, and how can you stay safe?

A) It’s too slow for gaming; use a wired connection instead

B) It’s often unsecured, allowing hackers to spy; use a VPN for protection

C) It costs money to connect; avoid it by using mobile data always

D) It limits app downloads; only download apps at home

Correct Answer: B) It’s often unsecured, allowing hackers to spy; use a VPN for protection

 

Question 3: When downloading apps, what is a key safety tip to follow to avoid malware or scams?

A) Download from random websites for exclusive free versions

B) Only use official stores like Google Play or Apple App Store and check reviews

C) Install apps that ask for access to all your data for full features

D) Always download “cracked” versions to save money on paid apps

Correct Answer: B) Only use official stores like Google Play or Apple App Store and check reviews

 

Question 4: How can you identify clickbait or a suspicious link before clicking on it?

A) It promises realistic rewards and comes from a trusted friend

B) It uses over-the-top headlines like “SHOCKING!” and has a weird URL

C) It’s posted by a verified account with a blue checkmark

D) It leads to a well-known website with an HTTPS lock icon

Correct Answer: B) It uses over-the-top headlines like “SHOCKING!” and has a weird URL

 

Question 5: What should you do if you receive a message with a link saying, “Win Free Robux Now!” from an unknown sender?

A) Click it immediately to claim the reward before it’s gone

B) Ignore or delete it, as it’s likely clickbait or a scam, and don’t click the link

C) Share it with friends to see if they think it’s real

D) Enter your gaming login to verify the offer is legit

Correct Answer: B) Ignore or delete it, as it’s likely clickbait or a scam, and don’t click the link
