MCQs

Question 1: What is a common reason high school students procrastinate due to fear of failure?

A) They enjoy the task too much to start early.

B) They worry their work won’t be good enough, so they delay starting.

C) They have too many immediate consequences for delaying.

D) They always have plenty of time to complete tasks.Correct Answer: B. They worry their work won’t be good enough, so they delay starting.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of how distractions contribute to procrastination?

A) Setting clear goals for a study session.

B) Breaking tasks into smaller, manageable steps.

C) Spending time on social media instead of studying.

D) Creating a daily routine for homework.Correct Answer: C. Spending time on social media instead of studying.

 

Question 3: What is the main idea behind the “2-Minute Rule” as a strategy to overcome procrastination?

A) Work on a task for 2 hours without stopping.

B) Commit to starting a task for just 2 minutes to overcome initial resistance.

C) Take a 2-minute break after every hour of work.

D) Spend 2 minutes planning every task before starting.Correct Answer: B. Commit to starting a task for just 2 minutes to overcome initial resistance.

 

Question 4: How can “eating the frog” help combat procrastination?

A) By taking frequent breaks to avoid stress.

B) By tackling the easiest tasks first to build confidence.

C) By starting with the hardest or least appealing task when energy is highest.

D) By delaying difficult tasks until the deadline approaches.Correct Answer: C. By starting with the hardest or least appealing task when energy is highest.

 

Question 5: What is a key step in building self-discipline through consistency?

A) Starting with large, complex habits to challenge yourself.

B) Beginning with small, manageable habits and repeating them daily.

C) Changing routines frequently to keep things interesting.

D) Avoiding tracking progress to reduce pressure.Correct Answer: B. Beginning with small, manageable habits and repeating them daily.

 

Question 6: Why is practicing delayed gratification important for self-discipline?

A) It encourages immediate rewards to stay motivated.

B) It helps you choose long-term benefits over short-term pleasures.

C) It allows you to avoid setting goals altogether.

D) It focuses on taking breaks whenever you feel tired.Correct Answer: B. It helps you choose long-term benefits over short-term pleasures.
