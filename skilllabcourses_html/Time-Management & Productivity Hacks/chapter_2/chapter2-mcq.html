Question 1: What is the primary purpose of conducting a “time audit” as described in the chapter?

Question 2: Which activity is recommended in the chapter to get a detailed view of how you spend a single day?

A) Weekly Overview

C) 24-Hour Time Log

C) 24-Hour Time Log

 

Question 3: According to the chapter, which of the following is a common time-waster for high school students?

A) Attending classes regularly

B) Excessive screen time on social media or videos

C) Completing homework on time

D) Participating in extracurricular activitiesCorrect Answer:B) Excessive screen time on social media or videos

 

Question 4: What is one way the chapter suggests to minimize a time-waster like social media?

B) Set a specific time limit, such as 20 minutes per day, using a timer

C) Use social media only during peak productivity hours
