Chapter 2: Assessing Your Current Time Habits

Welcome to Chapter 2 of the "Time-Management &amp; Productivity Hacks" course! In Chapter 1, we explored why time is such a valuable and unique resource, how it impacts your daily life as a high school student, and the benefits of managing it effectively. Now, it’s time to take a closer look at your relationship with time. Before you can improve how you manage your hours and minutes, you need to understand how you’re currently spending them. This chapter will help you assess your current time habits through self-reflection, identify activities that waste your time, and recognize patterns that boost or hinder your productivity. Let’s dive in and get a clear picture of where your time goes!
