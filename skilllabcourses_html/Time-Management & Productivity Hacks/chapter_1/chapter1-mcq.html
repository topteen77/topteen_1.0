MCQs

Question 1: What makes time a unique resource compared to other assets like money or possessions?

A) Time can be saved for future use.

B) Time is infinite and always available.

C) Time is finite and cannot be replaced once spent.

D) Time can be shared with others easily.Correct Answer:c) Time is finite and cannot be replaced once spent.

 

Question 2: How does the chapter suggest thinking about time to better understand its value?

A) As a tool that can be borrowed.

B) As a currency you invest in activities.

C) As a reward for hard work.

D) As something that grows with age.Correct Answer:b) As a currency you invest in activities.

 

Question 3: According to the chapter, how does time impact academic performance for high school students?

A) It has no effect since grades depend only on intelligence.

B) It determines how well students can prepare for exams and complete assignments.

C) It only affects students who are in extracurricular activities.

D) It impacts only the amount of time spent in class.Correct Answer:b) It determines how well students can prepare for exams and complete assignments.

 

Question 4: What is one way poor time management can affect a student’s personal life, as mentioned in the chapter?

A) It can lead to better focus on hobbies.

B) It can improve relationships with teachers.

C) It can strain relationships with family and friends due to being “too busy.”

D) It can create more time for relaxation.Correct Answer:c) It can strain relationships with family and friends due to being “too busy.”

 

Question 5: What is a key benefit of effective time management for high school students, according to the chapter?

A) It guarantees higher test scores without studying.

B) It reduces stress by providing a sense of control over tasks.

C) It eliminates the need for sleep and self-care.

D) It ensures students never have to prioritize tasks.Correct Answer:b) It reduces stress by providing a sense of control over tasks.

 

Question 6: How does the chapter suggest effective time management prepares students for the future?

A) It helps them avoid all responsibilities.

B) It builds skills like discipline and prioritization valued in college and careers.

C) It ensures they never face challenges.

D) It teaches them to rely only on digital tools.Correct Answer:b) It builds skills like discipline and prioritization valued in college and careers.
