MCQ Questions

Question 1: What is a primary reason digital distractions like smartphones are so disruptive to high school students?

A) They are rarely used for schoolwork.

B) They provide constant notifications that interrupt focus.

C) They are only used for educational purposes.

D) They have no impact on study habits.Correct Answer: B. They provide constant notifications that interrupt focus.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of an internal distraction as described in the chapter?

A) Background noise from family members.

B) Receiving a text message during homework.

C) Feeling anxious about an upcoming test.

D) A cluttered desk in your study area.Correct Answer: C. Feeling anxious about an upcoming test.

 

Question 3: What is a recommended strategy for optimizing a physical workspace to minimize distractions?

A) Keep the desk cluttered to feel more comfortable.

B) Study in a high-traffic area to stay motivated.

C) Choose a quiet, dedicated space away from relaxation areas like a bed.

D) Keep all devices on and nearby for quick access.Correct Answer: C. Choose a quiet, dedicated space away from relaxation areas like a bed.

 

Question 4: How can students mentally prepare to maintain focus in a distraction-free environment?

A) Start tasks without any specific goals in mind.

B) Set clear intentions by defining what to accomplish before beginning.

C) Keep distracting apps open to take quick breaks.

D) Avoid preparing materials in advance to stay spontaneous.Correct Answer: B. Set clear intentions by defining what to accomplish before beginning.

 

Question 5: What is the purpose of establishing tech-free zones or times, according to the chapter?

A) To completely eliminate the use of technology from daily life.

B) To encourage focus or rest by limiting device access in specific areas or periods.

C) To increase screen time in other parts of the day.

D) To make technology more difficult to use for schoolwork.Correct Answer: B. To encourage focus or rest by limiting device access in specific areas or periods.

 

Question 6: How can students address the fear of missing out (FOMO) when setting boundaries with technology?

A) Check social media constantly to stay updated.

B) Schedule specific times to catch up on online content rather than checking all day.

C) Avoid communicating boundaries to friends to prevent conflict.

D) Ignore the feeling and delete all social media accounts.Correct Answer: B. Schedule specific times to catch up on online content rather than checking all day.
