MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary structure of a single cycle in thePomodoroTechnique?

A) 50 minutes of work followed by a 10-minute break

B) 25 minutes of work followed by a 5-minute break

C) 30 minutes of work followed by a 15-minute break

D) 20 minutes of work followed by a 10-minute breakCorrect Answer: B) 25 minutes of work followed by a 5-minute break

 

Question 2: What is one key benefit of thePomodoroTechnique for students?

A) It eliminates the need for planning study sessions

B) It increases focus by working in short, manageable intervals

C) It encourages multitasking during study time

D) It allows students to study for hours without breaksCorrect Answer: B) It increases focus by working in short, manageable intervals

 

Question 3: When applying thePomodoroTechnique to study sessions, what is a recommended strategy for maximizing focus?

A) Work on multiple subjects during a single Pomodoro

B) Check social media briefly to stay motivated

C) Focus on only one task or topic per Pomodoro

D) Stop working if the task isn’t completed in 25 minutesCorrect Answer: C) Focus on only one task or topic per Pomodoro

 

Question 4: How can thePomodoroTechnique be adapted for a student who finds 25-minute intervals too short for complex tasks?

A) Skip breaks entirely to maintain momentum

B) Extend work intervals to 30 or 50 minutes with proportional breaks

C) Reduce work intervals to 15 minutes for easier focus

D) Ignore the timer and work until the task is completeCorrect Answer: B) Extend work intervals to 30 or 50 minutes with proportional breaks

 

Question 5: What is a recommended activity during a short 5-minute break to prevent burnout?

A) Review notes from the previous Pomodoro to reinforce learning

B) Scroll through social media to relax quickly

C) Stand up and stretch to improve blood flow

D) Start planning the next study task immediatelyCorrect Answer: C) Stand up and stretch to improve blood flow

 

Question 6: Why are breaks an essential part of thePomodoroTechnique in preventing burnout?

A) They allow students to complete more tasks in less time

B) They provide a cognitive reset and reduce mental fatigue

C) They encourage students to take longer naps during the day

D) They help students avoid studying altogetherCorrect Answer: B) They provide a cognitive reset and reduce mental fatigue
