MCQs

Question 1: Why is goal-setting important for time management?

A) It guarantees instant success without effort.

B) It helps prioritize tasks and provides direction.

C) It eliminates the need to plan daily activities.

D) It ensures you never face any challenges.Correct Answer: B) It helps prioritize tasks and provides direction.

 

Question 2: How does achieving a small goal impact a student’s confidence?

A) It has no effect on confidence.

B) It can discourage them from setting bigger goals.

C) It boosts self-esteem and proves they can succeed.

D) It makes them overconfident and careless.Correct Answer: C) It boosts self-esteem and proves they can succeed.

 

Question 3: What does the “M” in SMART stand for, and why is it important?

A) Motivated – It ensures you stay excited about the goal.

B) Measurable – It allows you to track progress with specific criteria.

C) Manageable – It ensures the goal isn’t too hard.

D) Meaningful – It connects the goal to personal values.Correct Answer: B) Measurable – It allows you to track progress with specific criteria.

 

Question 4: Which of the following is an example of a SMART goal for a high school student?

A) I want to get better at science.

B) I will study science every day.

C) I will improve my science grade by studying 30 minutes daily after school for the next 4 weeks to score at least 85% on the next test.

D) I will become the best science student in my class.Correct Answer: C) I will improve my science grade by studying 30 minutes daily after school for the next 4 weeks to score at least 85% on the next test.

 

Question 5: What is a key strategy for aligning daily actions with long-term goals?

A) Setting unrealistic deadlines to push yourself harder.

B) Breaking goals into smaller, actionable steps and scheduling them.

C) Ignoring distractions completely without planning for them.

D) Working on multiple big goals at once without prioritizing.Correct Answer: B) Breaking goals into smaller, actionable steps and scheduling them.

 

Question 6: If a student’s goal is to prepare for a math competition but they keep getting distracted by social media, what should they do to align their actions with their goal?

A) Give up on the goal since distractions are unavoidable.

B) Spend more time on social media to relax before studying.

C) Identify and minimize distractions by using apps to limit screen time or studying in a quiet place.

D) Ignore the goal and focus on something easier.Correct Answer: C) Identify and minimize distractions by using apps to limit screen time or studying in a quiet place.
