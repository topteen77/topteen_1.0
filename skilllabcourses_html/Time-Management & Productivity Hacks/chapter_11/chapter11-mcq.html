MCQ Questions

Question 1: What is a key difference between stress and burnout as described in the chapter?

A) Stress is always long-term, while burnout is short-term.

B) Stress often involves over-engagement, while burnout involves feeling depleted and detached.

C) Stress only affects physical health, while burnout affects emotions.

D) Stress is unrelated to burnout and cannot lead to it.Correct Answer: B. Stress often involves over-engagement, while burnout involves feeling depleted and detached.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is a common sign of burnout rather than typical stress, according to the chapter?

A) Feeling anxious before a test.

B) Having occasional headaches from studying.

C) Feeling emotionally exhausted and detached from school or activities for a prolonged period.

D) Worrying about an upcoming deadline.Correct Answer: C. Feeling emotionally exhausted and detached from school or activities for a prolonged period.

 

Question 3: What is the purpose of the 4-7-8 breathing technique mentioned in the chapter as a quick stress-relief method?

A) To increase heart rate and energize the body before a test.

B) To calm the heart rate and signal the brain to relax in under 5 minutes.

C) To replace the need for sleep or exercise.

D) To focus on future tasks instead of current stress.Correct Answer: B. To calm the heart rate and signal the brain to relax in under 5 minutes.

 

Question 4: Which long-term stress management habit does the chapter recommend to prevent burnout?

A) Avoiding all social interactions to focus on schoolwork.

B) Using time management with a planner to break tasks into smaller steps.

C) Ignoring stress symptoms until they go away on their own.

D) Taking long breaks without planning, regardless of deadlines.Correct Answer: B. Using time management with a planner to break tasks into smaller steps.

 

Question 5: Why does the chapter emphasize sleep as critical for high school students in managing stress?

A) It allows students to avoid responsibilities by sleeping more.

B) It consolidates memories, regulates emotions, and lowers stress hormone levels.

C) It is only necessary for students who are physically active.

D) It replaces the need for stress-relief techniques like meditation.Correct Answer: B. It consolidates memories, regulates emotions, and lowers stress hormone levels.

 

Question 6: What is one way the chapter suggests incorporating exercise into a busy high school schedule?

A) Only exercising if you’re on a sports team or in PE class.

B) Starting with small, manageable sessions like a 10-minute walk during study breaks.

C) Exercising intensely every day, even if it means sacrificing sleep.

D) Avoiding exercise if it doesn’t fit into a gym routine.Correct Answer: B. Starting with small, manageable sessions like a 10-minute walk during study breaks.
