MCQs

Question 1: What is the main difference between kinetic energy and potential energy?

A) Kinetic energy is stored energy, while potential energy is energy of motion.

B) Kinetic energy is energy of motion, while potential energy is stored energy.

C) Kinetic energy is only found in living things, while potential energy is in objects.

D) Kinetic energy is caused by gravity, while potential energy is caused by friction.Correct Answer: b) Kinetic energy is energy of motion, while potential energy is stored energy.

 

Question 2: What happens during a chemical reaction?

A) Substances mix together without changing their properties.

B) Substances transform into new substances with different properties.

C) Substances only change their physical shape or size.

D) Substances disappear completely into energy.Correct Answer: b) Substances transform into new substances with different properties.

 

Question 3: Which of the following best describes the role of the circulatory system in the human body?

A) It helps you think and react by sending signals through nerves.

B) It breaks down food into energy for the body to use.

C) It transports oxygen and nutrients to cells via blood.

D) It supports the body with bones and allows movement.Correct Answer: c) It transports oxygen and nutrients to cells via blood.

 

Question 4: What is the purpose of forming a hypothesis in the scientific method?

A) To prove your experiment will work before testing it.

B) To make an educated guess about what might happen in an experiment.

C) To write down the final results of your experiment.

D) To ask a random question without any research.Correct Answer: b) To make an educated guess about what might happen in an experiment.

 

Question 5: In the “Pendulum Power” experiment from the chapter, what factor most likely affects the time it takes for a pendulum to complete a swing?

A) The color of the string used in the pendulum.

B) The length of the string holding the weight.

C) The temperature of the room where the experiment is conducted.

D) The type of material the weight is made of.Correct Answer: b) The length of the string holding the weight.
