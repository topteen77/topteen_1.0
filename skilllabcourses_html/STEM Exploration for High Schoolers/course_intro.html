What You’ll Learn – Course Chapters at a Glance:

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to STEM – Uncover the essence of STEM and its impact on everyday life.
  • Chapter 2: Foundations of Science – Master the basics of physics, chemistry, and biology through experiments.
  • Chapter 3: Technology and Innovation – Dive into coding, AI, and cybersecurity with practical projects.
  • Chapter 4: Engineering Principles – Learn design thinking and build structures like bridges or robots.
  • Chapter 5: Mathematics in Action – Apply math to real-world problems and explore data analysis.
  • Chapter 6: Interdisciplinary STEM Projects – Combine STEM fields to create solutions like sustainable city models.
  • Chapter 7: Environmental and Social Impact of STEM – Use STEM to address climate change and social challenges.
  • Chapter 8: Robotics for Beginners – Build and program robots for simple tasks like obstacle avoidance.
  • Chapter 9: Introduction to Space Science and Astronomy – Explore the universe with model rockets and star charts.
  • Chapter 10: Biomedical Science and Engineering – Discover health tech by designing medical device models.
  • Chapter 11: Game Design and Virtual Reality – Create games and explore VR/AR with coding platforms.
  • Chapter 12: Preparing for a Future in STEM – Build a portfolio and propose solutions for real-world issues.
