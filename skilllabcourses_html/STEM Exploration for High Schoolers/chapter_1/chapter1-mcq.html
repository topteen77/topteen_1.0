MCQs

Question 1: What does STEM stand for, and how do its components work together?

A) Science, Technology, Education, Math; they work independently to solve different problems.

B) Science, Technology, Engineering, Math; they combine to create solutions like designing a self-driving car.

C) Systems, Tools, Engineering, Mechanics; they focus only on building physical structures.

D) Science, Teaching, Electronics, Math; they are used only in classroom settings.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 2: How does STEM impact everyday life for high school students?

A) It only affects activities outside of school, like playing video games.

B) It has no real impact on daily routines since it’s just for scientists.

C) It shapes daily life through things like smartphones, clean water, and school buses.

D) It is only relevant when studying for exams or doing homework.Correct Answer: C

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of a STEM career that combines creativity and technology?

A) A civil engineer designing bridges.

B) A game developer coding virtual worlds like Minecraft.

C) A marine biologist studying ocean life.

D) An actuary calculating insurance risks.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 4: What is a key element of the STEM mindset, and why is it important?

A) Memorizing facts; it helps in passing tests easily.

B) Curiosity; it drives discovery and leads to asking questions like “Why does this happen?”

C) Following instructions; it ensures you never make mistakes.

D) Avoiding challenges; it keeps learning stress-free.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 5: How can STEM help address global challenges like climate change, as discussed in the chapter?

A) By ignoring the problem since it’s too big for teens to solve.

B) By using science to study carbon levels and engineering to build wind turbines.

C) By focusing only on local issues and avoiding global concerns.

D) By relying solely on math without involving other STEM fields.Correct Answer: B
