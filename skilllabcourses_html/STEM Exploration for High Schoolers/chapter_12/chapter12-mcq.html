MCQ Questions

Question 1: Exploring STEM Education PathwaysWhat is a primary benefit of taking AP or IB STEM courses in high school?

A) They guarantee admission to top colleges.

B) They provide an opportunity to earn college credit and prepare for advanced studies.

C) They are easier than regular high school courses.

D) They focus only on theoretical knowledge without practical skills.

Correct Answer: B) They provide an opportunity to earn college credit and prepare for advanced studies.

 

Question 2: Skills for SuccessWhich skill is most essential when working on a group STEM project, such as building a robot for a competition?

A) Working alone to ensure perfection.

B) Teamwork to collaborate and combine diverse skills like coding and design.

C) Avoiding communication to prevent conflicts.

D) Focusing only on personal tasks without considering others’ contributions.

Correct Answer: B) Teamwork to collaborate and combine diverse skills like coding and design.

 

Question 3: Building a STEM PortfolioWhat is a key purpose of including a “failures” section in a STEM portfolio?

A) To show that you give up easily when projects don’t work.

B) To demonstrate grit and growth by explaining lessons learned from challenges.

C) To prove that STEM is too difficult for most students.

D) To avoid showcasing successful projects.

Correct Answer: B) To demonstrate grit and growth by explaining lessons learned from challenges.

 

Question 4: Inspiring StoriesWhat lesson can high school students learn from teen innovator Gitanjali Rao, who created a device to detect lead in water?

A) STEM achievements are only possible for adults with advanced degrees.

B) High school projects are unimportant and cannot make a real impact.

C) Age is no barrier to innovation, and small projects can address big problems.

D) STEM should only focus on theoretical research, not practical solutions.

Correct Answer: C) Age is no barrier to innovation, and small projects can address big problems.

 

Question 5: Final Project - Proposing a STEM SolutionWhat is a critical first step in proposing a STEM solution for a real-world problem?

A) Immediately building a prototype without planning.

B) Identifying a specific problem in your community or the world that you care about.

C) Copying an existing solution without research.

D) Focusing only on global issues that are impossible to solve locally.

Correct Answer: B) Identifying a specific problem in your community or the world that you care about.
