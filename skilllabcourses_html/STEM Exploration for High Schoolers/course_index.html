Course Title: STEM Exploration for High Schoolers

 

Chapter 1: Introduction to STEM

  • What is STEM? Understanding Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math
  • Importance of STEM in Everyday Life
  • Exploring STEM Careers and Opportunities
  • Developing a STEM Mindset: Curiosity, Creativity, and Critical Thinking

 

Chapter 2: Foundations of Science

  • Basics of Physics: Motion, Forces, and Energy

  • Hands-On Activity: Simple Experiments to Understand Scientific Principles

  • Introduction to Technology: From Tools to Digital Systems
  • Basics of Coding: Writing Your First Program (e.g., Python or Scratch)
  • Understanding Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Cybersecurity: Staying Safe in a Digital World
  • Hands-On Activity: Building a Simple App or Game

 

Chapter 4: Engineering Principles

  • What is Engineering? Types of Engineering Disciplines

  • Introduction to Robotics: Components and Simple Builds
  • Hands-On Activity: Designing and Testing a Small Bridge or Robot

  • Data Analysis: Collecting, Interpreting, and Visualizing Data

  • Introduction to Coding with Math: Algorithms and Logic

Chapter 7: Environmental and Social Impact of STEM

  • STEM for Sustainability: Addressing Climate Change
  • Renewable Energy: Solar, Wind, and Beyond
  • Ethical Considerations in Technology and Engineering
  • STEM for Social Good: Solving Local and Global Challenges
  • Hands-On Activity: Brainstorming Solutions for Environmental Issues

 

Chapter 8: Robotics for Beginners

  • What is Robotics? Understanding Robots and Their Applications
  • Components of Robots: Sensors, Motors, and Microcontrollers

  • Building a Simple Robot: Using Kits or Everyday Materials
  • Hands-On Activity: Designing a Robot to Perform a Simple Task (e.g., Line Following or Obstacle Avoidance)

 

Chapter 9: Introduction to Space Science and Astronomy

  • Exploring the Universe: Basics of Astronomy and Space Exploration
  • Understanding Planets, Stars, and Galaxies
  • Space Technology: Satellites, Rockets, and Space Missions

Chapter 10: Biomedical Science and Engineering

  • What is Biomedical Science? Intersection of Biology and Technology

Chapter 11: Game Design and Virtual Reality

Chapter 12: Preparing for a Future in STEM
