MCQs

Question 1: What is Biomedical Science primarily focused on?

A) Designing bridges and buildings using engineering principles

B) Combining biology and technology to solve health-related problems

C) Studying weather patterns and climate change

D) Developing new software for gaming applications

Correct Answer: B) Combining biology and technology to solve health-related problems

 

Question 2: Which body system is responsible for pumping blood and delivering oxygen throughout the body?

B) Nervous System

Correct Answer: C) Circulatory System

 

Question 3: What is the primary function of an MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging) machine in medical technology?

A) To take images of bones using radiation

B) To provide detailed images of soft tissues like muscles and brain using magnets and radio waves

C) To replace damaged limbs with robotic parts

D) To measure heart rate and steps through wearable sensors

Correct Answer: B) To provide detailed images of soft tissues like muscles and brain using magnets and radio waves

 

Question 4: In the context of engineering solutions for health, what is the first step in the design process for creating a medical device?

A) Building a prototype of the device

B) Testing the device in real-world conditions

C) Identifying a specific health problem to solve

D) Finalizing the product for mass production

Correct Answer: C) Identifying a specific health problem to solve

 

Question 5: What is a key ethical concern related to gene editing in Biomedical Science?

A) Whether it is too expensive to develop new medical devices

B) Whether it should be used to alter non-medical traits like appearance in humans

C) Whether it requires too much time to test for safety

D) Whether it can be used to improve computer software

Correct Answer: B) Whether it should be used to alter non-medical traits like appearance in humans
