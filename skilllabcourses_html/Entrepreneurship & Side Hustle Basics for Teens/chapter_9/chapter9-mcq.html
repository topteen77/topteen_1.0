MCQs

Question 1: Which of the following is a clear sign that your side hustle is ready to scale up?

A) You’re feeling bored with your hustle and want a change.

B) You’re consistently making a profit and have a high demand for your product.

C) You’ve just started your hustle and want to grow immediately.

D) You’re struggling to keep up with orders and missing deadlines.Correct Answer:B) You’re consistently making a profit and have a high demand for your product.

 

Question 2: What is an important step when forming a small team for your side hustle?

A) Choosing friends who are popular, even if they’re not reliable.

B) Keeping all tasks to yourself to avoid sharing profits.

C) Defining clear roles for each team member to avoid confusion.

D) Avoiding discussions about money to prevent arguments.Correct Answer:C) Defining clear roles for each team member to avoid confusion.

 

Question 3: How can feedback help improve your side hustle?

A) It tells you to completely change your product without testing.

B) It helps identify small issues or patterns to fix for better customer satisfaction.

C) It should be ignored if it’s negative to stay confident.

D) It only matters if it comes from your best friends.Correct Answer:B) It helps identify small issues or patterns to fix for better customer satisfaction.

 

Question 4: What is a practical way to balance your growing hustle with school responsibilities?

A) Skip homework to focus on hustle tasks when orders increase.

B) Set specific hours for hustle work and stick to a schedule.

C) Take on every order, even during exam weeks, to earn more.

D) Stop all school activities to make time for your hustle.Correct Answer:B) Set specific hours for hustle work and stick to a schedule.
