MCQs

Question 1: What is a key purpose of creating a portfolio for your side hustle as a teen entrepreneur?

A) To show off only how much money you’ve made.

B) To document your journey and build credibility with customers or others.

C) To keep all your business secrets hidden from competitors.

D) To avoid sharing your work with anyone until it’s perfect.Correct Answer:B) To document your journey and build credibility with customers or others.

 

Question 2: What is an essential element of a strong pitch for your hustle?

A) Talking for as long as possible to explain every detail.

B) Clearly explaining the problem you solve and why your solution is unique.

C) Avoiding any mention of challenges to seem flawless.

D) Focusing only on how much money you want to make.Correct Answer:B) Clearly explaining the problem you solve and why your solution is unique.

 

Question 3: What is a benefit of organizing or participating in a Teen Entrepreneur Fair?

A) It guarantees you’ll make a lot of money on the spot.

B) It allows you to showcase your hustle and learn from other teen hustlers.

C) It’s a way to avoid working on your hustle individually.

D) It’s only useful if you have a perfect product to sell.Correct Answer:B) It allows you to showcase your hustle and learn from other teen hustlers.

 

Question 4: Why is setting a 6-month goal important after completing this course?

A) It ensures you’ll achieve instant success with no effort.

B) It helps you stay focused and keep momentum with your hustle.

C) It forces you to stick to one idea forever without changing.

D) It’s only necessary if you failed during the course.Correct Answer:B) It helps you stay focused and keep momentum with your hustle.
