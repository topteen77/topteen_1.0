MCQs

Question 1: What is one major advantage of starting a digital side hustle for teens, as discussed in Section 1?

A) It requires a large budget to start.

B) It offers a massive global reach compared to offline hustles.

C) It can only be done during fixed working hours.

D) It limits your audience to your local area.Correct Answer:b) It offers a massive global reach compared to offline hustles.

 

Question 2: According to Section 2, what is an important step in setting up a strong online presence?

A) Posting blurry photos to keep things casual.

B) Using a consistent, catchy username across platforms for branding.

C) Sharing personal details like your home address in your bio.

D) Posting only once a year to avoid spamming.Correct Answer:b) Using a consistent, catchy username across platforms for branding.

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a popular online side hustle for teens mentioned in Section 3?

A) Opening a physical store in a mall.

B) Selling digital products like printable planners on Etsy.

C) Working a full-time office job remotely.

D) Buying expensive equipment to start a business.Correct Answer:b) Selling digital products like printable planners on Etsy.

 

Question 4: In Section 4, what is the primary benefit of using free tools like Canva and Google Sheets for your hustle?

A) They require advanced technical skills to use.

B) They help save money and time while looking professional.

C) They are only useful for offline businesses.

D) They cost a lot but guarantee success.Correct Answer:b) They help save money and time while looking professional.

 

Question 5: What is a key online safety tip for teens from Section 5?

A) Share your full name and address on public profiles to build trust.

B) Click on every link sent in DMs to check for opportunities.

C) Use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to secure your accounts.
