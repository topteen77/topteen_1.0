MCQs

Question 1: What is a Business Plan?

A) A detailed 50-page document required for all businesses

B) A written guide that acts like a GPS for your side hustle, showing where you’re headed and how to get there

C) A playlist of motivational songs to inspire your hustle

D) A list of products you want to buy for your business

Correct Answer: B) A written guide that acts like a GPS for your side hustle, showing where you’re headed and how to get there

 

Question 2: What does the 'M' in SMART goals stand for?

A) Motivational

B) Measurable

C) Manageable

D) Marketable

Correct Answer: B) Measurable

 

Question 3: Why is budgeting important for a teen’s side hustle?

A) It helps you spend all your money on cool packaging

B) It ensures you make every dollar (or rupee) count by tracking costs and income

C) It guarantees you’ll make a profit immediately

D) It’s only necessary for big companies, not small hustles

Correct Answer: B) It ensures you make every dollar (or rupee) count by tracking costs and income

 

Question 4: Which of the following is a good time management tip for balancing school and a side hustle?

A) Work on your hustle all night and skip sleep

B) Set specific hustle hours, like 1-2 hours a few days a week, to avoid clashing with schoolwork

C) Take on as many orders as possible, even during exam week

D) Ignore school deadlines to focus on your business

Correct Answer: B) Set specific hustle hours, like 1-2 hours a few days a week, to avoid clashing with schoolwork

 

Question 5: What is a key part of a mini business model for a teen’s side hustle?

A) Writing a 100-page report on market trends

B) Outlining how you deliver your product or service to customers

C) Creating a playlist for motivation while working

D) Buying expensive equipment before starting

Correct Answer: B) Outlining how you deliver your product or service to customers
