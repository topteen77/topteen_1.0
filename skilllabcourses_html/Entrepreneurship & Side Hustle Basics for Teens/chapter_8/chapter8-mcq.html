MCQs

Question 1: What is profit in the context of a side hustle?

A) The total money you earn from sales

B) The money you spend on materials and supplies

C) The money left after subtracting expenses from income

D) The money you save for personal goalsCorrect Answer:c) The money left after subtracting expenses from income

 

Question 2: What does reinvesting mean for a teen entrepreneur?

A) Spending all your profit on personal items like gadgets

B) Putting money back into your hustle to grow it, like buying better tools

C) Saving all your earnings in a bank without using them

D) Giving money to friends to help with their hustlesCorrect Answer:b) Putting money back into your hustle to grow it, like buying better tools

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a simple way for a teen to track their hustle’s finances?

A) Ignoring small transactions since they don’t matter

B) Using a notebook to write down income and expenses

C) Guessing how much money is earned or spent each month

D) Only checking bank statements once a yearCorrect Answer:b) Using a notebook to write down income and expenses

 

Question 4: In India, when might a teen hustler need to worry about income tax?

A) If they earn less than ₹1,000 per year from their hustle

B) If their total yearly income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh

C) If they sell only one product online

D) If they use a free app to track moneyCorrect Answer:b) If their total yearly income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh
