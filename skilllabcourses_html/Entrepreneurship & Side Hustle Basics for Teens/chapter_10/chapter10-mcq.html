MCQs

Question 1: What is a practical way to manage the challenge of limited time as a teen hustler?

A) Skip school activities to focus solely on your hustle.

B) Set a specific “Power Hour” each day to work on your hustle tasks.

C) Work on your hustle only when you feel like it, without a schedule.

D) Ignore homework to prioritize customer orders.Correct Answer:B) Set a specific “Power Hour” each day to work on your hustle tasks.

 

Question 2: How should a teen entrepreneur handle rejection from a potential customer or business?

A) Take it personally and stop trying to avoid further rejection.

B) Ignore the rejection and keep doing things the same way.

C) Ask for feedback and use it to improve or pivot to a new approach.

D) Give up on that idea completely and start something unrelated.Correct Answer:C) Ask for feedback and use it to improve or pivot to a new approach.

 

Question 3: Why is setting small milestones important for staying motivated in your side hustle?

A) They make your big goals feel less important and easier to ignore.

B) They help you feel progress and keep your energy up with achievable wins.

C) They allow you to avoid celebrating until you reach the final goal.

D) They distract you from focusing on the challenges you face.Correct Answer:B) They help you feel progress and keep your energy up with achievable wins.

 

Question 4: Where can a teen entrepreneur look for mentorship if they don’t know anyone locally to guide them?

A) Only ask friends who may not have business experience.

B) Avoid seeking help since no one nearby can offer advice.

C) Join online communities or follow entrepreneurs on social media for tips.

D) Wait until they’re older to find a mentor in person.Correct Answer:C) Join online communities or follow entrepreneurs on social media for tips.
