MCQs

Question 1: What is the main purpose of brainstorming when coming up with side hustle ideas?

A) To immediately pick the best idea and start working on it

B) To generate as many ideas as possible without judging them at first

C) To criticize and eliminate bad ideas right away

D) To create a detailed business plan for one ideaCorrect Answer:B) To generate as many ideas as possible without judging them at first

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an effective way to identify problems in your community that could inspire a side hustle?

A) Ignoring complaints because they’re not your responsibility

B) Only focusing on problems that affect adults in your area

C) Observing daily frustrations at school or in your neighborhood and asking others what bugs them

D) Waiting for someone else to solve local issues before starting a businessCorrect Answer:C) Observing daily frustrations at school or in your neighborhood and asking others what bugs them

 

Question 3: How can social media polls help in researching a side hustle idea?

A) They provide a way to quickly get feedback from peers about the demand for your idea

B) They are only useful for advertising your product after you start selling

C) They help you design a logo for your business

D) They automatically tell you how much profit you’ll makeCorrect Answer:A) They provide a way to quickly get feedback from peers about the demand for your idea

 

Question 4: What is the benefit of conducting a competitor analysis for your side hustle?

A) It helps you copy exactly what others are doing to guarantee success

B) It allows you to learn from others’ strengths and weaknesses to find your unique angle

C) It discourages you from starting a business if others are already doing something similar

D) It focuses only on criticizing other businesses without improving your own ideaCorrect Answer:B) It allows you to learn from others’ strengths and weaknesses to find your unique angle

 

Question 5: Why is a feasibility check important before starting a side hustle as a teen?

A) It ensures you’ll make a lot of money right away

B) It helps you decide if you have the time, resources, and skills to make the idea work with your busy schedule

C) It guarantees that your idea will never fail

D) It focuses only on getting permission from parents to start a businessCorrect Answer:B) It helps you decide if you have the time, resources, and skills to make the idea work with your busy schedule
