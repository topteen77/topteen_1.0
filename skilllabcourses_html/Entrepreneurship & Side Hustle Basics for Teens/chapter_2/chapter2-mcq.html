MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of self-assessment in discovering your passion for a side hustle?

A) To compare your skills with your friends’ skills

B) To identify your personal interests, hobbies, and strengths that can guide your hustle

C) To decide how much money you want to earn immediately

D) To copy successful entrepreneurs’ ideas

Correct Answer: B) To identify your personal interests, hobbies, and strengths that can guide your hustle

 

Question 2: How can a hobby like cooking be turned into a hustle according to the chapter?

A) By cooking only for family and not charging anything

B) By starting a YouTube channel without any planning

C) By offering products like mini idli packs or services like teaching recipes to neighbors

D) By spending a lot of money on a professional kitchen setup first

Correct Answer: C) By offering products like mini idli packs or services like teaching recipes to neighbors

 

Question 3: Why is it important to consider market needs when planning a side hustle?

A) Because your passion alone guarantees success

B) Because a hustle only works if people want or need what you offer

C) Because market needs are irrelevant to teen businesses

D) Because you should ignore your skills and only focus on trends

Correct Answer: B) Because a hustle only works if people want or need what you offer

 

Question 4: What is a practical step suggested in the chapter for brainstorming your first hustle idea?

A) Waiting for someone else to give you an idea

B) Combining your passion, hobby, skill, and a market need to create a simple plan

C) Spending a large amount of money on advertising before starting

D) Ignoring feedback from friends and family

Correct Answer: B) Combining your passion, hobby, skill, and a market need to create a simple plan

 

Question 5: Which of the following is an example of a skill that a teen might turn into a hustle, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Watching TV all day without any goal

B) Organizing events like Durga Puja or school projects for others

C) Avoiding schoolwork and responsibilities

D) Spending money on expensive gadgets without a plan

Correct Answer: B) Organizing events like Durga Puja or school projects for others
