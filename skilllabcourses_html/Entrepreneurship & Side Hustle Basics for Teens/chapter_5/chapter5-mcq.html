MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of branding for a teen’s side hustle?

A) To make products more expensive

B) To create recognition and trust among customers

C) To copy big companies like Nike or Coca-Cola

D) To confuse customers with complicated designs

Correct Answer: B) To create recognition and trust among customers

 

Question 2: When choosing a brand name for your hustle, what is an important factor to consider?

A) Making it as long and detailed as possible

B) Ensuring it is short, catchy, and easy to remember

C) Using a name that no one can pronounce

D) Copying a popular brand’s name to gain attention

Correct Answer: B) Ensuring it is short, catchy, and easy to remember

 

Question 3: Which free tool is recommended in the chapter for teens to create a simple logo for their hustle?

A) Adobe Photoshop

B) Microsoft Word

C) Canva

D) Paintbrush

Correct Answer: C) Canva

 

Question 4: How can incorporating cultural elements into branding help a teen’s side hustle?

A) It makes the brand look outdated and boring

B) It creates an emotional connection with the local audience

C) It confuses customers about the product

D) It only works for international markets

Correct Answer: B) It creates an emotional connection with the local audience

 

Question 5: What is a key aspect of building a personal brand as a teen entrepreneur?

A) Pretending to be someone you’re not to impress others

B) Being authentic and consistent in how you present yourself online and offline

C) Avoiding social media to keep your hustle private

D) Only focusing on your product and ignoring your reputation

Correct Answer: B) Being authentic and consistent in how you present yourself online and offline
