MCQs

Question 1: What is Entrepreneurship?

A) Working for a large company to earn a salary

B) Creating a product, service, or solution and turning it into a business

C) Following someone else’s business plan without changes

D) Avoiding risks and sticking to safe ideas only

Correct Answer: B) Creating a product, service, or solution and turning it into a business

 

Question 2: Which of the following is NOT a key trait of a young entrepreneur’s mindset?

A) Resilience

B) Creativity

C) Fear of failure

D) Problem-solving

Correct Answer: C) Fear of failure

 

Question 3: What is a side hustle, as described in the chapter?

A) A full-time job that replaces school

B) A small, manageable business or gig done alongside other activities

C) A business that requires a large investment to start

D) A hobby with no intention of earning money

Correct Answer: B) A small, manageable business or gig done alongside other activities

 

Question 4: Which teen entrepreneur started a delivery service using Mumbai’sdabbawalas?

C) Elon Musk

Correct Answer: B) Tilak Mehta

 

Question 5: Why is starting a hustle as a teen beneficial, according to the chapter?

A) It guarantees instant wealth and success

B) It allows teens to avoid school and responsibilities

C) It builds skills, confidence, and financial independence early in life

D) It eliminates the need to learn from mistakes

Correct Answer: C) It builds skills, confidence, and financial independence early in life
