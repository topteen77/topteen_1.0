MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of building a personal goal system, as discussed in Chapter 10?

B) To create a repeatable, personalized process for setting and achieving goals that fits your lifestyle

C) To focus only on academic goals without considering other life areas

Question 2: According to Chapter 10, what is a key benefit of conducting an annual goal review?

B) It helps you reflect on wins and setbacks, celebrate progress, and reset for the year ahead

D) It eliminates the need to adapt goals to changing circumstancesCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 3: What is a crucial step in adapting goals to a changing world, as outlined in Chapter 10?

A) Sticking to the original plan no matter the circumstances

B) Assessing the core idea of your goal and brainstorming new ways to achieve it when faced with change

D) Ignoring external changes and focusing only on personal effortCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 4: Why is becoming a goal mentor important, according to Chapter 10?
