MCQs

Question 1: What is a key benefit of using digital tools like Todoist or Habitica for goal-setting?

A) They automatically complete your goals without any effort.

B) They provide reminders and help organize tasks to keep you on track.

C) They replace the need for setting SMART goals.

D) They focus only on tracking progress without planning.Correct Answer:B) They provide reminders and help organize tasks to keep you on track.

 

Question 2: How can building a small habit help achieve a larger goal?

A) It replaces the need for a goal by focusing on random actions.

B) It breaks the goal into tiny, repeatable steps that build momentum over time.

C) It guarantees instant success without any planning.

D) It makes the goal less important by focusing on daily routines.Correct Answer:B) It breaks the goal into tiny, repeatable steps that build momentum over time.

 

Question 3: What is the main purpose of tracking progress toward a goal?

A) To make the goal more complicated with extra steps.

B) To show how far you’ve come and keep you motivated with visible results.

C) To avoid setting deadlines or making adjustments.

D) To focus only on failures instead of successes.Correct Answer:B) To show how far you’ve come and keep you motivated with visible results.

 

Question 4: How does having an accountability partner help with achieving goals?

A) They do the work for you so you don’t have to try.

B) They provide support and motivation by checking in on your progress.

C) They make the goal less important by sharing the responsibility.

D) They focus only on criticizing your mistakes.Correct Answer:B) They provide support and motivation by checking in on your progress.

 

Question 5: Why is it important to set rewards for milestones in a goal journey?

A) Rewards make the goal unnecessary by focusing on fun instead of work.

B) Rewards give you something to look forward to and boost motivation.

C) Rewards ensure you never fail at any step of the goal.

D) Rewards replace the need for tracking progress or planning.Correct Answer:B) Rewards give you something to look forward to and boost motivation.

 

Question 6: What is the primary function of a personal goal dashboard?

A) To list every possible goal you might ever have without focus.

B) To act as a visual command center for organizing and tracking active goals.

C) To replace the need for digital tools or accountability partners.

D) To focus only on rewards without showing progress or plans.Correct Answer:B) To act as a visual command center for organizing and tracking active goals.
