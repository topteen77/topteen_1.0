MCQs

Question 1: What does the "S" in SMART Goals 2.0 stand for, and why is it important?

A) Simple – It ensures goals are easy to achieve.

B) Specific – It provides clarity by defining exactly what you want to do.

C) Strong – It focuses on building confidence in goal-setting.

D) Strategic – It emphasizes planning over action.Correct Answer:B) Specific – It provides clarity by defining exactly what you want to do.

 

Question 2: How does making a goal "Measurable" help in achieving it?

A) It connects the goal to your personal values.

B) It sets a deadline to create urgency.

C) It allows you to track progress with numbers or milestones.

D) It ensures the goal is realistic and doable.Correct Answer:C) It allows you to track progress with numbers or milestones.

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of an "Achievable" goal for a high school student who has never run before?

A) Run a full marathon next week.

B) Run 1 mile twice a week for a month.

C) Win a national running competition this year.

D) Run 10 miles every day starting tomorrow.Correct Answer:B) Run 1 mile twice a week for a month.

 

Question 4: What is the purpose of making a goal "Relevant" in the SMART framework?

A) To ensure it has a clear deadline.

B) To make sure it connects to your personal "why" or values.

C) To break it into smaller, measurable steps.

D) To keep it specific and detailed.Correct Answer:B) To make sure it connects to your personal "why" or values.

 

Question 5: Why is setting a "Time-Bound" goal important for high school students?

A) It makes the goal easier to achieve with less effort.

B) It creates urgency and helps fight procrastination.

C) It ensures the goal is specific and clear.

D) It guarantees the goal will be relevant to your life.Correct Answer:B) It creates urgency and helps fight procrastination.

 

Question 6: Which of the following best represents a transformation from a vague wish to a SMART goal?

A) "I want to be popular" to "I’ll be the most liked person in school."

B) "I want to get fit" to "I’ll exercise every day somehow."

C) "I want to save money" to "I’ll save $5 a week from my allowance in a jar every Sunday, aiming for $50, because I want new sneakers, by March 1st."

D) "I want good grades" to "I’ll study a lot this year."Correct Answer:C) "I want to save money" to "I’ll save $5 a week from my allowance in a jar every Sunday, aiming for $50, because I want new sneakers, by March 1st."

 

Question 7: How can a digital tool like the SMART Goal Generator App help with goal-setting?

A) It automatically achieves goals for you without effort.

B) It provides guided prompts to build SMART goals and reminders for deadlines.

C) It replaces the need to understand the SMART framework.

D) It focuses only on setting deadlines without other SMART elements.Correct Answer:B) It provides guided prompts to build SMART goals and reminders for deadlines.
