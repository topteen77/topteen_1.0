MCQs

Question 1: What is a key difference between a growth mindset and a fixed mindset when facing a challenge?

A) A growth mindset avoids challenges to protect self-image, while a fixed mindset embraces them.

B) A growth mindset sees challenges as opportunities to learn, while a fixed mindset views them as proof of inability.

C) A growth mindset believes effort is unnecessary, while a fixed mindset focuses on hard work.

D) A growth mindset ignores feedback, while a fixed mindset seeks it constantly.Correct Answer:B) A growth mindset sees challenges as opportunities to learn, while a fixed mindset views them as proof of inability.

 

Question 2: How can mental toughness help a student achieve a difficult goal like improving grades?

A) It encourages giving up when tasks feel too hard to avoid stress.

B) It helps the student stay committed and push through frustration by focusing on small steps and their ‘why.’

C) It ensures the student never feels tired or unmotivated while studying.

D) It replaces the need for planning or breaking goals into smaller tasks.Correct Answer:B) It helps the student stay committed and push through frustration by focusing on small steps and their ‘why.’

 

Question 3: What is the main purpose of using positive self-talk when working toward a goal?

A) To ignore challenges and pretend everything is easy.

B) To replace negative thoughts with encouraging, realistic statements that boost confidence.

C) To avoid setting goals by focusing only on feelings.

D) To criticize past mistakes in a constructive way.Correct Answer:B) To replace negative thoughts with encouraging, realistic statements that boost confidence.

 

Question 4: How does visualization help in achieving a goal, such as performing well in a school play?

A) It guarantees success without any real practice or preparation.

B) It allows you to mentally rehearse success in detail, building confidence and focus before the actual event.

C) It distracts you from the goal by focusing on unrelated daydreams.

D) It replaces the need for setting specific goals or taking action.Correct Answer:B) It allows you to mentally rehearse success in detail, building confidence and focus before the actual event.

 

Question 5: What is one way mindfulness can support goal achievement for a high school student?

A) It helps eliminate all distractions permanently without any effort.

B) It encourages multitasking to complete goals faster.

C) It helps the student stay present and reduce anxiety, making it easier to concentrate on goal tasks.

D) It focuses only on past mistakes to avoid repeating them.Correct Answer:C) It helps the student stay present and reduce anxiety, making it easier to concentrate on goal tasks.
