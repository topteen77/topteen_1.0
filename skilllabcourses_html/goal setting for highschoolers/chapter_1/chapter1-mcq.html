Quiz Questions

Question 1: What is the main difference between a dream and a goal?

A) A dream is specific, while a goal is vague.

B) A dream is a broad idea or wish, while a goal is a specific, actionable target with a timeframe.

C) A dream is something you can achieve quickly, while a goal takes years.

D) A dream is always unrealistic, while a goal is always practical.

Correct Answer: B) A dream is a broad idea or wish, while a goal is a specific, actionable target with a timeframe.

 

Question 2: Why is goal setting especially important during your teen years?

A) It helps you avoid doing homework and focus on fun activities.

B) It prepares you for adulthood, builds confidence, and reduces stress by giving direction.

C) It guarantees you will become successful overnight.

D) It stops you from having any challenges or failures.

Correct Answer: B) It prepares you for adulthood, builds confidence, and reduces stress by giving direction.

 

Question 3: What does the “SMART” in SMART goals stand for?

A) Simple, Meaningful, Amazing, Realistic, Timely

B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

C) Strong, Motivated, Active, Reliable, Tough

D) Smart, Modern, Attractive, Rapid, True

Correct Answer: B) Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

 

Question 4: What can you learn from the real stories of teen goal-setters like Jake and Mia?

A) You need to aim for huge goals right from the start.

B) Small, consistent goals can lead to big achievements over time.

C) Only talented teens can achieve their goals.

D) Goal setting only works for sports and arts, not other areas.

Correct Answer: B) Small, consistent goals can lead to big achievements over time.
