Chapter 8: From Planning to Action: Your 4-Week Launch

 

Section 1: Week 1 – Assessment &amp; Goal Selection

Why It Matters

Before you dive into action, you need to know where you stand and what to aim for. Week 1 is about taking stock of your life, skills, and dreams to pick goals that excite you and fit your current reality. For teens, this step helps you avoid overwhelm by focusing on what’s doable right now, whether it’s school, hobbies, or personal growth.
