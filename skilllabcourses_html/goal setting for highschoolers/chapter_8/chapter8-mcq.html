MCQ Questions

Question 1: What is the main purpose of Week 1: Assessment &amp; Goal Selection in the 4-week launch plan?

A) To start taking action on your goals immediately

B) To evaluate your strengths, weaknesses, and schedule to choose 1-3 realistic goals

C) To create a detailed 90-day plan for long-term success

D) To celebrate early wins and adjust your action planCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 2: Why is breaking down goals into weekly actions during Week 2 important for high school students?

A) It makes goals feel less intimidating and fits progress into a busy schedule

B) It ensures you complete all goals within one week

C) It helps you avoid having a support system for accountability

D) It focuses only on long-term 90-day planningCorrect Answer: A

 

Question 3: What is a key benefit of tracking and adjusting in real-time during Week 3?

A) It allows you to set new goals every day without a plan

B) It helps you identify issues early and make changes before getting derailed

C) It focuses solely on celebrating wins without reviewing progress

D) It encourages skipping actions if they are too hardCorrect Answer: B

 

Question 4: What is the primary focus of the 90-Day Goal Achievement Plan as the final project of Chapter 8?

A) To review and celebrate only the past 4 weeks’ achievements

B) To create a short-term plan for the next week of actions

C) To extend the 4-week launch into a 3-month vision with monthly milestones

D) To focus on selecting new focus areas without specific goalsCorrect Answer: C
