Chapter 6: Planning for the Future

Welcome to the sixth chapter of your "Path to My Future" Career Readiness Program! After learning about money and finances, it’s time to look ahead and think about where you want to go in life. In this chapter, we’ll explore how to set goals, connect your education to career dreams, find people to support you, and create a personal plan for success. These skills are like a compass, helping you navigate your journey through school and beyond, whether you dream of becoming a scientist, an artist, or a business owner.

Here’s what you’ll discover in this chapter:

  • Setting Personal and Academic Goals: Learn to create clear, achievable goals using the SMART method to succeed in school and life.
  • Education and Career Pathways: Understand how your school choices today can open doors to exciting career opportunities tomorrow.
  • Finding Mentors and Support: Identify trusted adults, like family or teachers, who can guide you in making big decisions.
  • Creating a Future Roadmap: Build a personal plan with goals, skills, and steps to shape the coming years.

This chapter is all about helping you take charge of your future by thinking ahead and making thoughtful plans. Planning now will support you in school projects, hobbies, and any career you choose. Through fun activities, real-life examples, and personal reflection, you’ll start building a vision for who you want to be. So, let’s get started on this exciting step of your journey to a bright and amazing future!
