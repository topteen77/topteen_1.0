Welcome to Path to My Future: Career Readiness for Grades 6-8

Embark on an exciting journey of self-discovery and exploration with our innovative Career Readiness Course, "Path to My Future," specially crafted for middle school students in grades 6-8. At this pivotal stage of life, young minds are brimming with curiosity and potential, ready to uncover their unique talents and dreams. Our course is designed to ignite that spark, guiding students through a transformative experience that lays the foundation for a fulfilling future.

Through engaging activities, hands-on projects, and inspiring lessons, "Path to My Future" empowers students to understand themselves, explore diverse career possibilities, and build essential life skills. Our comprehensive curriculum spans eight dynamic chapters:

  • Understanding Myself
  • Exploring the World of Careers
  • Developing Essential Skills
  • Building Emotional and Social Skills
  • Learning About Money and Finances
  • Planning for the Future
  • Technology and Tomorrow’s Jobs
  • Experiencing Careers Hands-On

From discovering personal strengths and passions to navigating the ever-evolving world of work, this course equips young learners with the confidence and tools to shape their own paths.
