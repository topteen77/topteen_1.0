Chapter 5: Learning About Money and Finances

Welcome to the fifth chapter of your "Path to My Future" Career Readiness Program! After building your emotional and social skills, it’s time to dive into something that plays a big role in everyone’s life—money and how to manage it. In this chapter, we’ll explore the basics of handling money, making smart choices, and planning for your future. These skills are like a map, guiding you to make decisions that help you achieve your dreams, whether you want to buy something special, save for a big goal, or prepare for a career.

Here’s what you’ll discover in this chapter:

  • Basics of Money Management: Learn how to earn, save, and spend wisely through fun budgeting activities that put you in control.
  • Needs vs. Wants: Understand the difference between things you must have and things you’d like to have, so you can make smart choices with your money.
  • Careers and Income: Explore how different jobs can affect how much you earn and the kind of life you can build.
  • Setting Money Goals: Create clear, achievable plans for saving and spending, both for now and for the future.

This chapter is all about giving you the tools to be confident with money, from small everyday decisions to big plans down the road. Knowing how to manage finances will help you at home, in school projects, and in any career you choose, whether you dream of being an engineer, a chef, or an entrepreneur. Through exciting activities, real-life examples, and thoughtful reflection, you’ll start building a strong foundation for financial success. So, let’s get started on this important step of your journey to the future!
