Chapter 10: Capstone Project – Solving a High School Challenge with Tech

Introduction: Become a Tech-Savvy Problem Solver!

Hey, high schoolers! Welcome to Chapter 10, the grand finale of your Design Thinking journey—your Capstone Project! This chapter is all about putting everything you’ve learned into action by solving a real high school challenge using technology. Think of this as your chance to be a tech-savvy innovator, creating solutions that can make life easier for you and your classmates. Whether it’s tackling messy study schedules, reducing exam stress, or solving another common teen struggle, you’ll use digital tools or AI to design something awesome.

Technology is everywhere, and as high schoolers, you’re already pros at using apps, social media, and gadgets. Now, let’s harness that power to solve real problems with Design Thinking—empathizing with others, brainstorming creative ideas, and building practical solutions. In this chapter, we’ll guide you through picking a high school issue to solve, using tech tools like apps or AI to create a solution, thinking critically about how to use tech responsibly, sharing your idea with classmates, and setting goals to keep growing your tech skills before graduation. This project isn’t just about grades—it’s about proving to yourself (and maybe even colleges or future bosses) that you can use technology to make a difference. So, grab your laptop, phone, or whatever tech you love, and let’s build something incredible. Ready to be a high school tech hero? Let’s get started!
