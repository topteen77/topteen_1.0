Chapter 4: Ideation – Brainstorming Creative Solutions

Introduction: Unleashing Your Inner Innovator

Welcome, high schoolers, to Chapter 4 of your Design Thinking journey! After defining a clear and specific problem in Chapter 3, you’re now ready to dive into one of the most exciting stages: Ideation. This is where creativity takes center stage, and you get to dream up all kinds of solutions to the problem you’ve identified. Think of this chapter as your playground—there are no wrong answers, no limits to what you can imagine, and no idea too wild to explore. Whether you’re solving a school issue, a community challenge, or a personal goal, ideation is your chance to think big and let your imagination run free.

Ideation is the third stage of Design Thinking, and it’s all about generating as many ideas as possible to address the problem statement you crafted. In this chapter, we’ll explore what ideation really means and how to unleash your creativity without holding back. You’ll learn powerful brainstorming techniques like Mind Mapping, SCAMPER, and Crazy 8s to spark inspiration. We’ll encourage you to focus on quantity over quality by embracing wild, out-of-the-box ideas, and show you how collaborating with a team can bring diverse perspectives to the table. Finally, we’ll guide you through selecting the best ideas using voting and prioritization methods, so you’re ready to move into prototyping. Get ready to have fun, think differently, and discover that you’re full of amazing ideas just waiting to come to life. Let’s jump into the world of ideation and start creating solutions that can change the game!
