MCQs

Question 1: What does "scaling up" mean in the context of Design Thinking for community and global issues?

A) Focusing only on personal problems like study stress.

B) Applying Design Thinking to larger issues that affect more people beyond your immediate circle.

C) Ignoring community problems to focus on global challenges.

D) Solving problems without using the Design Thinking stages.Answer: B

 

Question 2: What is a key step in identifying local community needs using Design Thinking?

A) Avoiding conversations with community members to save time.

B) Observing your surroundings and empathizing with residents about issues like accessibility or the environment.

C) Focusing only on global issues instead of local ones.

D) Waiting for someone else to point out community problems.Answer: B

 

Question 3: How can high school students connect global issues like sustainability to their local area using Design Thinking?

A) By ignoring local problems and focusing only on worldwide challenges.

B) By identifying how a global issue, like plastic waste, shows up locally and taking small actions to address it.

C) By assuming global issues don’t affect their community.

D) By waiting for adults to solve global problems.Answer: B

 

Question 4: Why is partnering with community members important when using Design Thinking for community issues?

A) They can provide valuable insights, resources, and support to strengthen your solutions.

B) They will solve the problem for you without any input.

C) They are not affected by the issues you’re trying to address.

D) They always disagree with student ideas.Answer: A

 

Question 5: What is a strategy for designing solutions with limited resources using Design Thinking?

A) Waiting for a large budget before starting any project.

B) Focusing on low-cost ideas and leveraging people power or available resources to create impact.

C) Avoiding testing small versions of your idea to save time.

D) Ignoring partnerships to work alone on solutions.Answer: B

 

Question 6: How does applying Design Thinking to community and global issues benefit high school students?

A) It limits their focus to only personal challenges.

B) It builds skills like problem-solving, teamwork, and creativity for college, careers, and life.

C) It makes community issues more complicated to solve.

D) It discourages students from thinking about global challenges.Answer: B
