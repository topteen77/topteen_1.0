Chapter 9: Design Thinking for Community and Global Issues

Introduction: Be a Change-Maker Beyond School Walls!

Hey, high schoolers! Welcome to Chapter 9, where we’re taking Design Thinking to the next level—beyond your personal and school challenges to make a real difference in your community and even the world. You’ve already mastered using Design Thinking to improve study habits, classroom vibes, and club activities. Now, it’s time to think bigger. Imagine tackling issues like making your neighborhood more accessible for everyone, cleaning up local parks, or even contributing to global causes like sustainability or education for all. Sounds ambitious? It is, but you’ve got the skills to make it happen!

Design Thinking is your superpower for solving problems in a creative, people-focused way. In this chapter, we’ll explore how to scale up your thinking to address larger issues, identify needs in your local community, connect with global challenges, team up with community members, and design impactful solutions even when resources are tight. As high schoolers, you’re in a unique spot—you’re full of fresh ideas, energy, and a desire to make the world better. Whether it’s organizing a community cleanup or brainstorming ways to help kids access education worldwide, you can be a change-maker starting right now. So, let’s dive in, think big, and use Design Thinking to create a brighter future for everyone. Are you ready to change the world? Let’s go!
