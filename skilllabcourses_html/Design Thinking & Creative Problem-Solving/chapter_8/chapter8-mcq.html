MCQs

Question 1: What is the first step in using Design Thinking to address school challenges like study habits or bullying?

A) Brainstorming solutions without understanding the issue.

B) Observing your day and talking to others to spot problems.

C) Ignoring small issues to focus only on big problems.

D) Waiting for teachers to point out challenges.Answer: B

 

Question 2: Which stage of Design Thinking involves creating a small, testable version of your solution for a classroom problem?

A) Empathize

B) Define

C) Ideate

D) PrototypeAnswer: D

 

Question 3: What is a key reason to apply Design Thinking to extracurricular challenges, such as low attendance at club events?

A) To make clubs more competitive with other schools.

B) To make activities more enjoyable and engaging for members.

C) To reduce the number of clubs offered at school.

D) To focus only on sports teams instead of other activities.Answer: B

 

Question 4: Why is it important to involve teachers and peers as stakeholders in your Design Thinking process for school challenges?

A) They can provide support, input, and help make your ideas happen.

B) They will always agree with every idea you have.

C) They are not affected by the problems you’re trying to solve.

D) They should solve the problems instead of students.Answer: A

 

Question 5: What is an effective way to start a presentation of your Design Thinking solution to classmates or teachers?

A) Talk only about your personal opinion on the solution.

B) Explain the problem clearly to show why a solution is needed.

C) Avoid mentioning any challenges to keep the audience happy.

D) Focus only on the feedback you expect to receive.Answer: B

 

Question 6: How does using Design Thinking to solve school challenges benefit high school students beyond the classroom?

A) It limits creativity by focusing only on school problems.

B) It teaches problem-solving and teamwork skills useful for college and jobs.

C) It makes school more stressful by adding extra work.

D) It only helps teachers and not students.Answer: B
