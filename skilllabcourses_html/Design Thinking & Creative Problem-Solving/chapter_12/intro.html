Chapter 12: Reflecting and Growing as a Creative Problem-Solver

Introduction: Looking Back to Move Forward

Hey, high school trailblazers! Welcome to Chapter 12, the final stop in our journey of solving problems with tech and creativity. You’ve worked through identifying challenges, designing solutions, and pitching your ideas like pros with your Capstone Project and beyond. But before we close this chapter of your learning, it’s time to pause and reflect on how far you’ve come—and to plan how you’ll keep growing. Reflecting isn’t just about patting yourself on the back (though you deserve it!); it’s about understanding your strengths, learning from mistakes, and setting your sights on bigger goals. In this chapter, we’ll guide you through assessing your Design Thinking skills, reflecting on what worked and what didn’t, building a portfolio to showcase your awesome projects, setting long-term goals for creativity, and even exploring future careers in innovative fields. Think of this as your personal roadmap to becoming a lifelong creative problem-solver. Let’s dive in and see how much you’ve grown—and where you’re headed next!
