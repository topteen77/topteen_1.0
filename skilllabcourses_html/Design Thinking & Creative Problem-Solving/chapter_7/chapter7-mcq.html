MCQs

Question 1: What is one key benefit of teamwork for high school students in solving complex problems through Design Thinking?

A) It increases individual workload.

B) It limits the number of ideas generated.

C) It allows for diverse perspectives to create solutions for more people.

D) It makes projects more stressful by involving more people.Answer: C

 

Question 2: What is the primary responsibility of the Timekeeper role in a Design Thinking team?

A) To write down key ideas and feedback during meetings.

B) To guide the team by setting goals and solving disagreements.

C) To manage time during tasks and ensure the team sticks to schedules.

D) To brainstorm creative solutions for the project.Answer: C

 

Question 3: What does active listening involve in a Design Thinking team?

A) Interrupting teammates to share your own ideas quickly.

B) Paying full attention without interrupting and showing understanding.

C) Ignoring distractions by focusing on your own thoughts.

D) Only listening to ideas that agree with your own.Answer: B

 

Question 4: What is the first step recommended for resolving conflicts in a Design Thinking team?

A) Blame the person responsible for the disagreement.

B) Stay calm and take a break if emotions are high.

C) Ignore the conflict and move on to the next task.

D) Immediately vote on a solution without discussion.Answer: B

 

Question 5: Why is it important to learn from challenges in a Design Thinking project?

A) To assign blame to team members for mistakes.

B) To avoid repeating mistakes and improve for future projects.

C) To focus only on the negative aspects of the project.

D) To ignore the successes and only critique the team.Answer: B

 

Question 6: How does teamwork contribute to the different stages of Design Thinking, such as Empathize and Ideation?

A) It slows down the process by involving too many opinions.

B) It allows for broader insights through interviews and generates more ideas through group brainstorming.

C) It limits the team to only one stage at a time.

D) It prevents iteration by focusing on a single solution.Answer: B
