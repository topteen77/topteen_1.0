Chapter 2: Building Empathy – Understanding Others’ Needs

Introduction: The Heart of Design Thinking

Hey there, high schoolers! Welcome to Chapter 2 of your Design Thinking journey, where we’re diving into the very first and arguably the most important stage of the process: Empathy. If Chapter 1 was about getting a big-picture view of Design Thinking and creative problem-solving, this chapter is all about zooming in on the people at the heart of every problem. Empathy is like the secret sauce that makes your solutions truly meaningful—it’s about understanding others so deeply that you can design ideas that really work for them.

In a world full of quick fixes and one-size-fits-all answers, empathy stands out as a way to connect with people on a real level. Whether you’re trying to improve something at school, help out a friend, or tackle a bigger issue in your community, starting with empathy ensures you’re solving the right problem for the right reasons. In this chapter, we’ll explore what empathy means, why it’s a game-changer, and how you can build it using practical tools like interviews, surveys, and observation. You’ll also get hands-on with activities like role-playing, learn to spot “pain points” around you, and organize your discoveries using empathy maps. So, get ready to listen, observe, and step into someone else’s world—let’s build solutions that come from the heart!
