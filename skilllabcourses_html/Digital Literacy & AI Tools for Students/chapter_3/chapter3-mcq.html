Question 1: What is one effective way to narrow down Google search results for a school project, as described in the chapter?

A) Use broad terms like “history” to get more results

B) Put exact phrases in quotation marks (e.g., “Civil War causes”)

C) Only click on the first result for quick answers

Question 2: Which of the following is a common sign of fake news or an unreliable website according to the chapter?

B) A sensational headline with extreme claims in all caps

Question 4: What is a key step to avoid plagiarism when taking notes for a research project, based on the chapter’s advice?
