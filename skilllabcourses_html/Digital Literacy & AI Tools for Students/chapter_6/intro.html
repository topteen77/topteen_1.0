Chapter 6: Using AI Tools to Ace Schoolwork

Welcome to Chapter 6 of Digital Literacy &amp; AI Tools for High Schoolers (Grades 9-12)! Now that you’ve got the basics of AI down from Chapter 5, it’s time to level up and learn how to use AI tools to crush your schoolwork. High school can be intense with essays, research, exams, and presentations, but AI can be your secret weapon to work smarter, save time, and boost your grades. This chapter dives into practical, hands-on ways to apply AI to specific academic tasks while keeping your skills sharp and avoiding over-reliance. Whether you’re drafting an essay, studying for a test, or designing a project, we’ve got tips and tools tailored for teens like you.

In this chapter, we’ll walk through how AI can help you write stronger essays with tools like Grammarly, summarize long texts for research using apps like QuillBot, and study more effectively with AI-powered flashcards on Quizlet. You’ll also discover how to create eye-catching presentations with AI design features in Canva. Most importantly, we’ll guide you on when to lean on AI and when to trust your own brainpower to ensure you’re learning, not just copying. By the end, you’ll have a toolkit of AI strategies to ace your assignments while building confidence in your own abilities. Let’s get started and make schoolwork less stressful and more successful!
