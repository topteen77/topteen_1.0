Question 1: What is Digital Literacy?

A) The ability to play video games and use social media apps effectively

B) The set of skills to find, evaluate, create, and communicate information using digital technology

C) Knowing how to fix broken computers and other devices

D) Using technology only for entertainment and hobbiesCorrect Answer: B) The set of skills to find, evaluate, create, and communicate information using digital technology

 

Question 2: Why is digital literacy important for high schoolers?

A) It helps them spend more time on social media without getting caught

B) It empowers them to stay safe online, save time on schoolwork, and prepare for future opportunities

C) It ensures they can avoid all technology-related problems forever

D) It is only useful for students who want to work in tech fieldsCorrect Answer: B) It empowers them to stay safe online, save time on schoolwork, and prepare for future opportunities

 

Question 3: How does technology shape high school life in academics?

A) It makes learning harder by replacing textbooks with confusing apps

B) It offers tools like online platforms and tutorials to enhance learning, but can also be distracting

C) It is only used for playing games during study breaks
