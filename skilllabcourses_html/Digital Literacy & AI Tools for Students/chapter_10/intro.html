Chapter 10: Capstone Project: Solving a High School Challenge with Tech

As you’ve journeyed through high school, you’ve encountered numerous challenges—whether it’s juggling study time, managing stress, or staying connected with peers. Technology, with its vast array of digital tools and AI innovations, offers a powerful way to address these everyday struggles. Chapter 10, "Capstone Project: Solving a High School Challenge with Tech," is your opportunity to put everything you’ve learned about digital and AI skills into action. This chapter guides you through a hands-on project where you’ll identify a real high school issue, design a tech-based solution, evaluate ethical considerations, present your idea to others, and commit to ongoing skill development. Think of this as your chance to be a problem-solver, using technology not just to keep up, but to make a tangible difference in your life or the lives of your peers. By the end of this chapter, you’ll have created a practical project that showcases your creativity, critical thinking, and tech-savviness—skills that will serve you well beyond graduation. Let’s explore each step of this capstone journey in detail, empowering you to tackle high school challenges with innovation and confidence.
