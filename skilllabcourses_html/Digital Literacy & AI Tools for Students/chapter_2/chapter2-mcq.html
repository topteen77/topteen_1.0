MCQs

Question 1: What is online privacy, as defined in the chapter?

A) The ability to use social media without any restrictions

B) The control over who has access to your personal information on the internet

C) Ensuring no one can see your online activity, even companies

D) Only sharing information with trusted friends onlineCorrect Answer: B) The control over who has access to your personal information on the internet

 

Question 2: What is a key characteristic of a strong password according to the chapter?

A) It should be short and easy to type, like your name

B) It should be at least 12-16 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

C) It should be the same for all your accounts to avoid forgetting

D) It should include your favorite sports team for memorabilityCorrect Answer: B) It should be at least 12-16 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a sign ofcatfishing, as described in the chapter?

A) Someone sends you a funny meme on social media

B) Someone refuses to video chat and their photos look too perfect or inconsistent

C) Someone comments positively on all your posts

D) Someone asks to follow you on a public platformCorrect Answer: B) Someone refuses to video chat and their photos look too perfect or inconsistent

 

Question 4: What is one recommended step for safe social media use from the chapter?

A) Share your location in every post to connect with friends nearby

B) Set your profile to private so only approved followers can see your content

C) Post everything publicly to gain more followers quickly

D) Accept all friend requests to build a larger networkCorrect Answer: B) Set your profile to private so only approved followers can see your content

 

Question 5: Why is managing your digital footprint important for high schoolers?
