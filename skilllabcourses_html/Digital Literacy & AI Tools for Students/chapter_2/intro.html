Chapter 2: Staying Safe Online as a Teen

Welcome to Chapter 2 of Digital Literacy &amp; AI Tools for High Schoolers (Grades 9-12)! As a high school student, you’re likely online every day—whether you’re chatting with friends, posting on social media, doing research for school, or just browsing for fun. While the internet offers incredible opportunities, it also comes with risks that can affect your safety, privacy, and future. Staying safe online isn’t just about avoiding viruses or hackers; it’s about protecting your personal information, mental well-being, and reputation in a world where everything you do digitally can leave a trace. This chapter is your guide to navigating the online world confidently and securely as a teen.

In this chapter, we’ll dive into the essentials of online safety tailored for high schoolers. You’ll learn what online privacy really means and why it’s critical to guard it, how to create strong passwords and keep your accounts secure, and how to spot and steer clear of dangers like cyberbullying, scams, and catfishing. We’ll also cover how to use social media safely by mastering privacy settings and posting thoughtfully, and finally, you’ll discover how to manage your digital footprint to ensure it helps—rather than hurts—your chances for college and job opportunities. By the end of this chapter, you’ll have practical tools and strategies to protect yourself online and build a positive digital presence. Let’s dive in and make the internet a safer space for you!
