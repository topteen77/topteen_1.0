Chapter 7: Being Ethical with Digital and AI Tools

In today's tech-driven world, digital and AI tools are powerful allies for high school students, helping with everything from research to project creation. However, with great power comes great responsibility. This chapter dives into the ethical considerations of using these tools, ensuring you stay honest, fair, and respectful while navigating the digital landscape. We'll explore why cheating with AI is a risky path, how to distinguish between fair use and plagiarism, the importance of privacy, the potential biases in AI, and how to set personal guidelines for responsible tech use. Let’s break this down section by section to help you make smart, ethical choices.
