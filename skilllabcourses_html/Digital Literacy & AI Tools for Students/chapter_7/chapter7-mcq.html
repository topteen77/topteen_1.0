Question 1: According to Section 1, why is cheating with AI considered a bad idea in high school?

A) It makes assignments too easy and boring

B) It can lead to failing grades, suspension, and missed learning opportunities

C) It prevents teachers from using AI detection tools

D) It only affects college students, not high schoolersCorrect Answer: B) It can lead to failing grades, suspension, and missed learning opportunities

 

Question 2: What is the key difference between fair use and plagiarism, as described in Section 2?

A) Fair use requires permission for all content, while plagiarism does not

B) Fair use allows limited use of copyrighted material with conditions, while plagiarism is using others’ work without credit

C) Fair use applies only to images, while plagiarism applies to text

Question 3: Based on Section 3, what is one way high school students can respect privacy when using digital tools for group projects?
