MCQs

Question 1: Why is organizing a post-event cleanup important after a school event like a dance?

A) It’s only necessary if the venue charges a cleaning fee

B) It shows responsibility and ensures you can use the space again for future events

C) It’s a way to avoid returning borrowed items

D) It guarantees the next event will be bigger

Correct Answer: B) It shows responsibility and ensures you can use the space again for future events

 

Question 2: What is a key step in financial reconciliation after a school fundraiser?

A) Ignoring expenses to focus only on money earned

B) Calculating profit or loss by subtracting total expenses from total income

C) Guessing the final numbers without checking receipts

D) Spending any profit immediately without reporting to anyone

Correct Answer: B) Calculating profit or loss by subtracting total expenses from total income

 

Question 3: How can you effectively gather feedback after a school talent show?

A) Avoid asking attendees anything to keep things simple

B) Use quick surveys or casual chats to learn what people liked and what could be improved

C) Only ask your best friends for their opinions

D) Wait until the next event to see if anyone complains

Correct Answer: B) Use quick surveys or casual chats to learn what people liked and what could be improved

 

Question 4: What is a good way to celebrate success and learn from mistakes after a school movie night?

A) Focus only on what went wrong and skip any celebration

B) Host a small team hangout to thank everyone and reflect on wins and areas to improve

C) Ignore the team’s efforts and start planning the next event right away

D) Only celebrate if the event made a huge profit

Correct Answer: B) Host a small team hangout to thank everyone and reflect on wins and areas to improve
