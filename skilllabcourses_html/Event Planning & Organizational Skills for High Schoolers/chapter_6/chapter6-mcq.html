MCQs

Question 1: What is a key factor to consider when selecting a venue for a school event like a talent show?

A) Picking the most expensive location to impress attendees

B) Ensuring the space can accommodate the expected number of people

C) Choosing a venue far from school to make it exciting

D) Ignoring accessibility since everyone can figure out how to get there

Correct Answer: B) Ensuring the space can accommodate the expected number of people

 

Question 2: Which item would likely be included in an equipment checklist for a school sports day?

A) A personal diary for writing thoughts

B) A first-aid kit for handling minor injuries

C) A gaming console for entertainment

D) A stack of novels for reading during breaks

Correct Answer: B) A first-aid kit for handling minor injuries

 

Question 3: What is an effective strategy when negotiating with a vendor for event supplies like food or decor?

A) Accepting the first price quoted without discussion

B) Politely asking for a discount or free extras since it’s a school event

C) Demanding a lower price without explaining your budget

D) Avoiding contact with vendors and hoping they lower prices on their own

Correct Answer: B) Politely asking for a discount or free extras since it’s a school event

 

Question 4: Why is it important to secure permissions before hosting a school event?

A) To make the event look more professional on social media

B) To avoid last-minute cancellations and ensure compliance with school or local rules

C) To spend extra money on unnecessary paperwork

D) To complicate the planning process for no reason

Correct Answer: B) To avoid last-minute cancellations and ensure compliance with school or local rules
