MCQs

Question 1: Defining the Purpose of Your EventWhat is the primary reason for defining the purpose of an event?

A) To decide on the event’s decorations and theme

B) To guide every decision and keep the planning focused on a specific goal

C) To choose the best location for the event

D) To determine how much money to spend on food

Correct Answer: B) To guide every decision and keep the planning focused on a specific goal

 

Question 2: Identifying Your Target AudienceWhy is it important to understand your target audience when planning an event?

A) It helps you set a budget for the event

B) It ensures the event’s timing, activities, and promotion match the needs and interests of the attendees

C) It determines how many team members you need to plan the event

D) It decides the type of food you should serve

Correct Answer: B) It ensures the event’s timing, activities, and promotion match the needs and interests of the attendees

 

Question 3: Creating a Vision BoardWhat is the main purpose of creating a vision board for an event?

A) To create a detailed budget plan for the event

B) To visually capture the theme, vibe, and goals of the event to inspire and guide the planning team

C) To list all the tasks needed to complete before the event

D) To convince the school principal to approve the event

Correct Answer: B) To visually capture the theme, vibe, and goals of the event to inspire and guide the planning team
