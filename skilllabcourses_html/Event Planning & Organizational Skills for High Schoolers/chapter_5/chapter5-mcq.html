MCQs

Question 1: Understanding Budget BasicsWhat is the primary purpose of creating a budget for an event?

A) To spend as much money as possible on decorations

B) To track income and expenses to avoid overspending

C) To impress friends with expensive event elements

D) To ignore costs and focus only on ticket sales

Correct Answer: B) To track income and expenses to avoid overspending

 

Question 2: BudgetSpreadsheetComponentsWhich of the following is NOT a typical column in a budget spreadsheet for an event?

D) Notes

Correct Answer: C) Favorite Songs for Playlist

 

Question 3: Fundraising StrategiesWhich of the following is a creative fundraising idea suitable for high school students organizing an event?

A) Taking out a large bank loan

B) Setting up a themed car wash or mehndi stall at school

C) Ignoring the need for funds and hoping for donations

D) Spending personal savings without planning

Correct Answer: B) Setting up a themed car wash or mehndi stall at school

 

Question 4: Cost-Cutting TechniquesWhat is a cost-cutting tip mentioned for reducing event expenses without sacrificing quality?

A) Buying the most expensive decorations to impress guests

B) Borrowing equipment like speakers from friends or school instead of renting

C) Spending all the budget on food and ignoring other needs

D) Printing hundreds of flyers even if digital promotion is free

Correct Answer: B) Borrowing equipment like speakers from friends or school instead of renting
