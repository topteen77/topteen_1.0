MCQs

Question 1: Why is a day-before checklist important for a school event like a dance or talent show?

A) It helps you avoid doing any work on the event day itself

B) It ensures all details, like team roles and vendor arrivals, are confirmed to prevent last-minute chaos

C) It guarantees that nothing will go wrong during the event

D) It’s only necessary if you’re planning an outdoor event

Correct Answer: B) It ensures all details, like team roles and vendor arrivals, are confirmed to prevent last-minute chaos

 

Question 2: What is a good way to manage the event schedule during a school fundraiser if an activity runs late?

A) Ignore the delay and hope no one notices

B) Cancel the rest of the activities to save time

C) Use a backup plan like playing music or hosting a quick game to fill the gap while keeping the crowd engaged

D) Tell everyone to leave early since the schedule is off

Correct Answer: C) Use a backup plan like playing music or hosting a quick game to fill the gap while keeping the crowd engaged

 

Question 3: How can you handle a problem like low turnout at your school’s movie night event?

A) Focus on making the smaller crowd feel special by hyping up the energy and rearranging the space to feel full

B) Cancel the event since not enough people showed up

C) Ignore the attendees and focus only on cleaning up early

D) Refuse to let anyone else in to keep it exclusive

Correct Answer: A) Focus on making the smaller crowd feel special by hyping up the energy and rearranging the space to feel full

 

Question 4: What is an effective way to ensure guest satisfaction at a school talent show?

A) Avoid talking to attendees so they can focus on the performances

B) Greet guests warmly, plan interactive moments like contests, and check on their comfort with access to water and rest areas

C) Only focus on the performers and ignore the audience

D) Make the event as short as possible so guests don’t get bored

Correct Answer: B) Greet guests warmly, plan interactive moments like contests, and check on their comfort with access to water and rest areas
