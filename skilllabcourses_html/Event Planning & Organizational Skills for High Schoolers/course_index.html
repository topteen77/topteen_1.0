Course Outline: Event Planning &amp; Organizational Skills for High Schoolers

Course Objective: Develops abilities to plan, budget, and execute events such as school fairs, fundraisers, cultural programs, and community projects through practical skills and hands-on learning.

This course is designed to empower high school students with the tools to organize successful events while building critical life skills like time management, teamwork, budgeting, and problem-solving. Below is a detailed list of chapters and subtopics tailored for teens to master event planning and organizational prowess.

  • Key Skills of an Event Planner
  • Real-World Examples
