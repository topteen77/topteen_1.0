MCQs

Question 1: What is Event Planning?

A) The process of designing and organizing events to ensure they run smoothly and achieve their purpose.

B) The act of attending events to enjoy activities and socialize with others.

C) The skill of creating decorations for school and community gatherings.

D) The task of only budgeting for events without any other responsibilities.

Correct Answer: A) The process of designing and organizing events to ensure they run smoothly and achieve their purpose.

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an example of a teen-relevant event that high schoolers might organize?

A) A corporate business conference for local companies.

B) A school talent show featuring student performers.

C) A city council meeting for urban planning.

D) A professional sports league championship game.

Correct Answer: B) A school talent show featuring student performers.

 

Question 3: Which skill is emphasized as crucial for an event planner to handle unexpected issues during an event?

A) Communication

B) Creativity

C) Problem-Solving

D) Leadership

Correct Answer: C) Problem-Solving

 

Question 4: What lesson can be learned from the “MovieUnderthe Stars” case study in the chapter?

A) Events should always be held indoors to avoid weather issues.

B) Social media can be a powerful tool to promote events and attract attendees.

C) Only professional equipment should be used for successful events.

D) Fundraising events are impossible without a large budget.

Correct Answer: B) Social media can be a powerful tool to promote events and attract attendees.
