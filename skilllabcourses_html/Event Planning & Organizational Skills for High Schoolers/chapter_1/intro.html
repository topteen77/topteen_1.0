Chapter 1: Introduction to Event Planning

Welcome to the first step of your exciting journey into the world of event planning with Chapter 1: Introduction to Event Planning! Whether you're envisioning a vibrant school fair or a heartfelt charity drive, this chapter will ignite your passion for creating memorable experiences while equipping you with the fundamental knowledge to get started.
