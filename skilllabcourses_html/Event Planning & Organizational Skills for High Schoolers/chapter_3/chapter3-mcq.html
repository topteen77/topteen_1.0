MCQs

Question 1: Roles and ResponsibilitiesWhat is the main benefit of assigning specific roles to team members based on their strengths when planning an event?

A) It ensures everyone gets paid equally for their work

B) It allows each person to focus on tasks they’re good at or enjoy, making the planning process more efficient

C) It guarantees the event will have no problems or delays

D) It means the team leader doesn’t have to do any work

Correct Answer: B) It allows each person to focus on tasks they’re good at or enjoy, making the planning process more efficient

 

Question 2: Effective Communication StrategiesWhich of the following is the most effective way to keep a team updated and on the same page during event planning?

A) Only talking to team members when there’s a problem

B) Holding regular short meetings and using tools like group chats or shared documents for updates

C) Letting each person figure out their tasks without checking in

D) Sending long emails every day with every small detail

Correct Answer: B) Holding regular short meetings and using tools like group chats or shared documents for updates

 

Question 3: Conflict Resolution in TeamsWhat is a key step in resolving a disagreement between team members planning an event?

A) Ignoring the issue and hoping it goes away on its own

B) Listening to both sides calmly and finding common ground or a compromise

C) Telling the team leader to pick a side immediately

D) Removing the arguing members from the team

Correct Answer: B) Listening to both sides calmly and finding common ground or a compromise

 

Question 4: Motivating Your CrewHow can a team leader best keep morale high during the stressful parts of event planning?

A) By focusing only on the tasks and ignoring how the team feels

B) By showing appreciation, celebrating small wins, and adding fun elements to the process

C) By giving everyone extra work to keep them busy

D) By promising big rewards that might not be possible to deliver

Correct Answer: B) By showing appreciation, celebrating small wins, and adding fun elements to the process
