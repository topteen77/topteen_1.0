Chapter 10: Practical Projects and Assessments

Welcome to the hands-on finale of your event planning journey with Chapter 10: Practical Projects and Assessments! Designed for high school students in Classes 9-12, this chapter is all about putting your skills to the test through real-world challenges and creative projects. Whether you’ve been dreaming of organizing a class party or leading a major school event, this is your chance to apply everything you’ve learned. Here, you’ll tackle practical tasks, solve problems, and build a portfolio to showcase your growth as an event planner.

Jump into action as we guide you through planning a mini-event on a tight budget and timeline, collaborating on a larger school event like a fundraiser, and navigating tricky scenarios to sharpen your problem-solving skills. You’ll also create a portfolio to document your plans, budgets, and reflections, proving your readiness for bigger challenges. This chapter equips you with opportunities to practice, innovate, and demonstrate your event planning prowess. Let’s roll up our sleeves and turn your ideas into reality!

Chapter 10 Topics with Descriptions:

  • Mini-Event Planning Challenge - Plan a small-scale event (like a class party) with a limited budget and timeline.
  • Group Project: Organize a School Event - Collaborate to design and execute a larger event (e.g., a fundraiser or cultural day).
  • Scenario-Based Problem Solving - Respond to mock challenges (e.g., “Your main sponsor backs out—now what?”).
  • Portfolio of Skills - Create a record of your event plans, budgets, and reflections to showcase growth.
