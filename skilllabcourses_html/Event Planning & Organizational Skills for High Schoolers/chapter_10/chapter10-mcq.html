MCQs

Question 1: What is the main purpose of the Mini-Event Planning Challenge in Chapter 10?

A) To plan a massive school-wide event with an unlimited budget

B) To practice event planning skills on a small scale with a limited budget and timeline

C) To avoid working with a team and plan everything solo

D) To focus only on creating a portfolio without hosting an actual event

Correct Answer: B) To practice event planning skills on a small scale with a limited budget and timeline

 

Question 2: During the Group Project to organize a school event, what is an important step to ensure success?

A) Assigning all tasks to one person to keep things simple

B) Ignoring the budget since it’s a school event

C) Dividing roles among team members based on their strengths and collaborating on a detailed plan

D) Waiting until the last minute to start planning to build excitement

Correct Answer: C) Dividing roles among team members based on their strengths and collaborating on a detailed plan

 

Question 3: In the Scenario-Based Problem Solving section, what should you do if your main sponsor backs out at the last minute?

A) Cancel the event immediately without exploring other options

B) Brainstorm solutions like finding a new sponsor or cutting costs, then pick the best action plan

C) Ignore the problem and hope no one notices

D) Spend your own money to cover all the costs without telling anyone

Correct Answer: B) Brainstorm solutions like finding a new sponsor or cutting costs, then pick the best action plan

 

Question 4: What is a key component of creating a Portfolio of Skills for event planning?

A) Only including photos of events without any written explanations

B) Collecting budgets, plans, and reflections to showcase your growth and skills over time

C) Leaving out any mistakes or challenges to make it look perfect

D) Making it as short as possible with only one item to save time

Correct Answer: B) Collecting budgets, plans, and reflections to showcase your growth and skills over time
