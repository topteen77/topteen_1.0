Chapter 7: Marketing and Promotion

Welcome to an exciting stage of your event planning journey with Chapter 7: Marketing and Promotion! Tailored for high school students in Classes 9-12, this chapter is all about getting the word out and creating buzz for your events. Whether you’re organizing a school concert, a fundraiser, or a sports day, effective marketing is the key to drawing a crowd and making your event the talk of the town. Here, you’ll learn how to craft a standout brand, leverage social media, and use traditional methods to ensure everyone knows about your big day.

Dive into the world of promotion as we explore how to design a memorable event brand with a catchy name, logo, or theme. Master social media strategies tailored for teens, using platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat to create engaging content like posters, videos, or viral challenges. Combine these with classic promotion tactics such as flyers and school announcements, and learn how to build excitement early with teasers and countdowns. This chapter equips you with the tools to captivate your audience and pack your event with enthusiastic attendees. Let’s spread the word and make your event unforgettable!

Chapter 7 Topics with Descriptions:

  • Crafting an Event Brand - Designing a catchy name, logo, or theme to make the event memorable.
  • Social Media Strategies for Teens - Using Instagram, TikTok, or Snapchat to spread the word with posters, videos, or challenges.
  • Traditional Promotion Methods - Creating flyers, announcements, or word-of-mouth buzz in school.
  • Engaging Your Audience Early - Building excitement with teasers, countdowns, or early-bird ticket offers.
