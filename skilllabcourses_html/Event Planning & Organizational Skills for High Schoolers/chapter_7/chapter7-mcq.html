MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary purpose of crafting an event brand for a school event?

A) To make the event more expensive by adding logos and themes

B) To create a unique and memorable identity that helps people recognize and remember the event

C) To confuse attendees with complex designs and names

D) To avoid using social media for promotion

Correct Answer: B) To create a unique and memorable identity that helps people recognize and remember the event

 

Question 2: Which of the following is an effective social media strategy for promoting a school event on TikTok?

A) Posting long, detailed videos explaining every aspect of the event

B) Creating short, trendy videos with popular sounds or challenges to grab attention

C) Avoiding hashtags to keep the event exclusive

D) Posting only once a month to avoid annoying followers

Correct Answer: B) Creating short, trendy videos with popular sounds or challenges to grab attention

 

Question 3: How can traditional promotion methods like flyers be made more interactive for a high school audience?

A) By using small, hard-to-read text to save space

B) By placing them only in teachers’ lounges where students can’t see them

C) By adding a QR code that links to a signup form or sneak peek video

D) By making them black and white to save on printing costs

Correct Answer: C) By adding a QR code that links to a signup form or sneak peek video

 

Question 4: Why is engaging your audience early an important part of event promotion?

A) It ensures that tickets are more expensive closer to the event date

B) It gives people time to plan to attend and builds excitement well before the event

C) It prevents anyone from knowing about the event until the last minute

D) It makes the event planning process more complicated

Correct Answer: B) It gives people time to plan to attend and builds excitement well before the event
