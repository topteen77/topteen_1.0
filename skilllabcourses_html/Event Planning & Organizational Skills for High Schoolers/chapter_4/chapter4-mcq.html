MCQs

Question 1: Creating a Timeline for Your EventWhat is the first step in creating a timeline for an event, and why is it important?

A) Brainstorming all tasks to ensure nothing is forgotten.

B) Setting the event date to establish a clear finish line.

C) Assigning tasks to team members to divide the workload.

D) Building buffer time to avoid last-minute stress.

Correct Answer: B) Setting the event date to establish a clear finish line.

 

Question 2: Using Tools for OrganizationWhich of the following is a key benefit of using a digital tool like Google Calendar or Trello for event planning?

A) It allows you to decorate your plans with stickers and doodles.

B) It automatically completes tasks for you without manual input.

C) It enables easy sharing with your team and setting reminders for deadlines.

D) It works without an internet connection or phone access.

Correct Answer: C) It enables easy sharing with your team and setting reminders for deadlines.

 

Question 3: Prioritizing TasksUsing the Urgency-Importance Matrix, how should you handle a task that is high urgency but low importance, such as posting a last-minute social media update?

A) Do it first, as it requires immediate attention over all other tasks.

B) Plan for it soon, as it still contributes to the event’s success.

C) Delegate it if possible, since it’s time-sensitive but not critical.

D) Do it last, as it doesn’t impact the event significantly.

Correct Answer: C) Delegate it if possible, since it’s time-sensitive but not critical.

 

Question 4: Handling Delays and SetbacksWhat is a recommended strategy for handling a setback like a vendor cancelling at the last minute?

A) Panic and cancel the event immediately to avoid further issues.

B) Ignore the problem and hope it resolves itself before the event.

C) Stay calm, assess the impact, and activate a backup plan.

D) Blame the team for not anticipating the issue earlier.

Correct Answer: C) Stay calm, assess the impact, and activate a backup plan.
