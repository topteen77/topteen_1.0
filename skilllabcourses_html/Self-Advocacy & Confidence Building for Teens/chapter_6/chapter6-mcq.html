MCQs

Question 1: You’re struggling with history assignments because the readings are hard to follow. What’s the best way to advocate for yourself?

A) Keep quiet and hope it gets easier on its own.

B) Ask your teacher, “I’m having trouble with the readings—can we go over them or get extra help?”

C) Skip the assignments since they’re too difficult.

D) Complain to your friends but don’t tell anyone who can help.

Correct Answer: B) Ask your teacher, “I’m having trouble with the readings—can we go over them or get extra help?”

 

Question 2: At a hangout, some friends are pushing you to sneak out past curfew, but you’re not comfortable with it. How should you handle this negative influence?

A) Go along with it so you don’t look lame.

B) Say, “Nah, I’m good—gotta be home. Catch you tomorrow,” and change the subject.

C) Argue with them loudly to prove your point.

D) Pretend to agree but secretly stay behind without telling them.

Correct Answer: B) Say, “Nah, I’m good—gotta be home. Catch you tomorrow,” and change the subject.

 

Question 3: You want to surround yourself with positive friends who respect your choices. What’s the best way to start building this kind of circle?

A) Stick with people who pressure you because they’re popular.

B) Join a club or activity you enjoy, like art or sports, to meet people with similar interests.

C) Avoid making new friends since it’s too risky.

D) Only hang out with people who agree with everything you say.

Correct Answer: B) Join a club or activity you enjoy, like art or sports, to meet people with similar interests.

 

Question 4: You’re applying for a part-time job and have an interview. How can you practice self-advocacy during the process?

A) Stay silent and hope they guess what you need.

B) Say, “I’m excited to work here, but I have school until 3 PM. Can shifts fit my schedule?”

C) Don’t ask questions because it might look bad.

D) Demand they give you the job without explaining why you’re a good fit.

Correct Answer: B) Say, “I’m excited to work here, but I have school until 3 PM. Can shifts fit my schedule?”
