MCQs

Question 1: What type of boundary is being set when you tell a friend, “I can’t talk about heavy stuff every day—it’s stressing me out. Can we chat about something lighter?”

A) Physical Boundary

B) Emotional Boundary

C) Time Boundary

D) Social Boundary

Correct Answer: B) Emotional Boundary

 

Question 2: Your classmate asks to copy your homework right before class, but you’re not comfortable with it. What’s the best way to say “no” confidently and respectfully?

A) “No way, do your own work!”

B) “I’m not sure, maybe next time, I guess…”

C) “I can’t share my homework, but good luck with yours!”

D) “Why are you always asking me? Stop it!”

Correct Answer: C) “I can’t share my homework, but good luck with yours!”

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a sign that your time boundary has been crossed?

A) A friend hugs you without asking, even though you’re not okay with it.

B) A teammate keeps texting you during your designated “no phone” study hours after you’ve asked them not to.

C) A classmate teases you about your appearance despite you saying it bothers you.

D) A sibling takes your snack from your bag without permission.

Correct Answer: B) A teammate keeps texting you during your designated “no phone” study hours after you’ve asked them not to.

 

Question 4: Your younger sibling keeps barging into your room without knocking, even after you’ve asked them to stop. What’s the most effective strategy to reinforce your boundary?

A) Ignore it and hope they eventually stop on their own.

B) Yell at them every time it happens to scare them off.

C) Say, “I need my privacy, so if you come in without knocking again, I’ll lock the door for the day.”

D) Complain to a friend about it but don’t address it with your sibling.

Correct Answer: C) Say, “I need my privacy, so if you come in without knocking again, I’ll lock the door for the day.”
