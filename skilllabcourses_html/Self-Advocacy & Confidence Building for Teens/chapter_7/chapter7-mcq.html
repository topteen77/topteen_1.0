MCQs

Question 1: What is the main difference between a Growth Mindset and a Fixed Mindset?

A) A Growth Mindset means you’re naturally talented, while a Fixed Mindset means you’re not.

B) A Growth Mindset believes abilities can improve with effort, while a Fixed Mindset sees abilities as unchangeable.

C) A Growth Mindset avoids challenges, while a Fixed Mindset embraces them.

D) A Growth Mindset focuses on perfection, while a Fixed Mindset accepts flaws.Correct Answer:B) A Growth Mindset believes abilities can improve with effort, while a Fixed Mindset sees abilities as unchangeable.

 

Question 2: How can viewing challenges as opportunities to learn help you in high school?

A) It guarantees you’ll never fail at anything you try.

B) It makes you avoid difficult tasks to protect your confidence.

C) It builds confidence and skills by encouraging you to face tough situations and learn from them.

D) It ensures everyone will like you for trying hard.Correct Answer:C) It builds confidence and skills by encouraging you to face tough situations and learn from them.

 

Question 3: After messing up a presentation in class, how would someone with a Growth Mindset likely respond?

A) “I’m terrible at this and should never try again.”

B) “I’ll pretend it didn’t happen and avoid future presentations.”

C) “I struggled, but I can practice more and ask for feedback to improve next time.”

D) “Everyone thinks I’m a failure now, so I’ll quit.”Correct Answer:C) “I struggled, but I can practice more and ask for feedback to improve next time.”

 

Question 4: How can keeping a gratitude journal improve your mindset as a teen?

A) It forces you to ignore all your problems and pretend everything is perfect.

B) It trains your brain to notice positive things in your life, balancing out stress and negatives.

C) It makes you compare yourself to others who have more to be thankful for.

D) It guarantees you’ll never feel sad or stressed again.Correct Answer:B) It trains your brain to notice positive things in your life, balancing out stress and negatives.

 

Question 5: What should you do if you’ve been feeling overwhelmed and sad for weeks, and it’s not getting better?

A) Keep it to yourself because asking for help shows weakness.

B) Distract yourself by spending all your time on social media or gaming.

C) Use a quick breathing trick like 4-7-8 and hope it fixes everything.

D) Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, or counselor for support and consider professional help.Correct Answer:D) Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, or counselor for support and consider professional help.
