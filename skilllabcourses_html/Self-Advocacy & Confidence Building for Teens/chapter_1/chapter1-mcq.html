MCQs

Question 1: What is the best definition of self-advocacy?

A) Yelling at someone until you get what you want.

B) Speaking up for your needs and rights in a respectful way.

C) Letting others make decisions for you to avoid conflict.

D) Ignoring your feelings to keep the peace.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 2: How does self-advocacy differ from aggression?

A) Self-advocacy is loud and demanding, while aggression is quiet.

B) Self-advocacy is respectful and calm, while aggression disregards others’ feelings.

C) Self-advocacy avoids asking for help, while aggression seeks it forcefully.

D) There is no difference; they are the same thing.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 3: Why is self-advocacy important for teens in school settings?

A) It helps you avoid doing any hard work by making others do it for you.

B) It builds independence and helps you ask for support when needed.

C) It ensures you never have to follow rules or listen to teachers.

D) It stops you from ever needing to make decisions.Correct Answer: B

 

Question 4: What is an example of identifying a personal need and advocating for it?

A) Complaining to friends about a tough class but doing nothing about it.

B) Recognizing you need extra time on tests and asking a teacher for accommodations.

C) Ignoring a problem because you don’t want to seem weak.

D) Demanding a better grade without explaining why you think you deserve it.Correct Answer: B
