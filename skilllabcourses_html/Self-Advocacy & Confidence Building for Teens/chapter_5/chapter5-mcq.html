MCQs

Question 1: You’re struggling with a group project because one teammate isn’t doing their part. What’s the first step in solving this problem effectively?

A) Immediately tell the teacher to fix it for you.

B) Identify the specific issue, like “My teammate isn’t contributing,” and brainstorm solutions.

C) Ignore it and hope they start working eventually.

D) Confront the teammate angrily to demand they step up.

Correct Answer: B) Identify the specific issue, like “My teammate isn’t contributing,” and brainstorm solutions.

 

Question 2: You’re deciding whether to join a new club that meets after school, but you’re already busy. What’s the best way to make an informed decision?

A) Flip a coin and go with whatever it lands on.

B) Ask your best friend what they think and do whatever they say.

C) Weigh the pros (new friends, fun) and cons (less free time, stress) before choosing.

D) Join without thinking because it sounds cool right now.

Correct Answer: C) Weigh the pros (new friends, fun) and cons (less free time, stress) before choosing.

 

Question 3: During an argument with a friend about cancelling plans, you feel yourself getting angry. What’s the most constructive way to stay calm?

A) Yell louder to make your point and get it over with.

B) Walk away without saying anything and ignore them.

C) Take a deep breath, count to 5, and speak in a lower voice to discuss it.

D) Text someone else to complain about your friend mid-argument.

Correct Answer: C) Take a deep breath, count to 5, and speak in a lower voice to discuss it.

 

Question 4: You and a sibling are fighting over who gets to use the family laptop tonight. What’s the best way to negotiate a win-win solution?

A) Insist you get it all night because you asked first.

B) Suggest, “I feel frustrated when I can’t finish my homework. Can we split the time—1 hour each?”

C) Hide the laptop so neither of you can use it.

D) Let them have it but complain the whole time.

Correct Answer: B) Suggest, “I feel frustrated when I can’t finish my homework. Can we split the time—1 hour each?”
