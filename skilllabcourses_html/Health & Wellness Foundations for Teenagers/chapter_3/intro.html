Chapter 3: Physical Health Foundations – Exercise

Hey high schoolers, let’s get moving! In Chapter 3: Physical Health Foundations – Exercise, we’re diving into the awesome world of physical activity and how it can supercharge both your body and mind. This isn’t about becoming a gym rat or pushing yourself to the limit—it’s about finding fun ways to stay active that fit your life and make you feel amazing.

Here’s what we’ll cover together:

  • Benefits of Movement: Discover how exercise does more than just build muscle—it boosts your mood, sharpens your focus, and gives you endless energy.
  • Finding Your Fit: Explore all kinds of activities, from sports to dance to hiking, so you can find what gets you excited to move.
  • Creating a Realistic Workout Routine for Busy Teens: Learn how to build a plan that works with your packed schedule, so staying active feels easy, not like a chore.
  • Staying Safe: Get the lowdown on preventing injuries and avoiding overtraining, so you can keep moving without setbacks.

This chapter is your guide to making exercise a part of your life in a way that’s totally doable and super rewarding. Whether you’re a total newbie or already love breaking a sweat, you’ll find tips and tricks to level up your game. So, lace up your sneakers, bring your energy, and let’s get started—your stronger, happier self is just a step away!
