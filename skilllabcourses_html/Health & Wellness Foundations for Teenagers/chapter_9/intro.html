Chapter 9: Making Informed Health Decisions

Hey high schoolers, ready to take charge of your choices? In Chapter 9: Making Informed Health Decisions, we’re diving into the skills you need to make smart, confident decisions about your health and well-being. This isn’t about following the crowd—it’s about knowing what’s best for you and owning your path with clarity and strength.

Here’s what we’ll cover together:

  • Understanding Risks: Learn how to spot peer pressure and steer clear of unhealthy choices that don’t align with your goals.
  • Media and Health: Get savvy about separating real health info from trendy fads and diets that promise more than they deliver.
  • Advocacy for Yourself: Build the confidence to speak up and communicate your needs to adults, friends, or anyone else in your corner.
  • Planning for the Future: See how the habits you build today—good or bad—can shape your health and happiness down the road.

This chapter is your roadmap to making choices you can stand behind. Whether you’re facing pressure, confused by mixed messages, or just figuring out what’s right for you, we’ve got the tools to help you decide with confidence. So, trust your gut, bring an open mind, and let’s get started—your journey to smarter, healthier decisions begins right here!
