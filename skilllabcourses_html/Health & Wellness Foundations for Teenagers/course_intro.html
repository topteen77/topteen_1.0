Welcome to Health &amp; Wellness Foundations for Teenagers

Embark on a transformative journey with our Health &amp; Wellness Foundations for Teenagers course, an inspiring 8-week program crafted exclusively for high school students (Grades 9-12) eager to flourish in every aspect of life. With just 2-3 hours per week, you’ll unlock a treasure trove of practical tools, actionable insights, and lifelong skills to nurture healthy habits, master stress, and find harmony in the whirlwind of teenage life.

This isn’t just a course—it’s your personal guide to becoming the strongest, happiest version of yourself. Through captivating lessons, hands-on activities, and meaningful projects, you’ll discover how to prioritize your well-being, make empowered choices, and build unshakable resilience for any challenge ahead. Each week introduces a new dimension of wellness, culminating in a unique, personalized Wellness Action Plan that reflects your individual aspirations and lifestyle.

 

What You’ll Explore in This Course:

  • Introduction to Health &amp; Wellness (Week 1): Lay the foundation by understanding what true wellness means for your body, mind, and emotions.
  • Physical Health Foundations – Nutrition and Exercise (Week 2): Fuel your body with smart food choices and discover physical activities that excite you.
  • Physical Health Foundations – Sleep and Healthy Habits (Week 3): Unlock the secrets to restful sleep and sustainable routines for lasting energy.
  • Mental and Emotional Well-Being (Week 4): Dive into self-esteem, emotions, and the power of social connections for a positive mindset.
  • Stress Management for Teens (Week 5): Learn mindfulness and time management to stay calm and focused under pressure.
  • Building Resilience (Week 6): Develop a growth mindset and problem-solving skills to bounce back from setbacks.
  • Achieving Balance in Teen Life (Week 7): Master the art of balancing school, social life, and personal time while embracing digital wellness.
  • Capstone – Personal Wellness Action Plan (Week 8): Reflect on your journey and design a custom plan to carry your wellness goals into the future.
