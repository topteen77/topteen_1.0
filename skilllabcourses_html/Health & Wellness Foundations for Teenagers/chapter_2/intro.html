Chapter 2: Physical Health Foundations – Nutrition

Hey high schoolers, get ready to fuel up like never before! In Chapter 2: Physical Health Foundations – Nutrition, we’re diving deep into the world of food and how it powers your body, brain, and busy life. This isn’t about boring diets or strict rules—it’s about discovering how to eat in a way that makes you feel unstoppable, energized, and ready to take on anything.

Here’s what we’ll explore together:

  • Understanding Nutrition: Learn what your body really needs to thrive, from vitamins to proteins, and why food is your ultimate superpower.
  • Building a Balanced Plate: Figure out how to mix and match food groups and portion sizes to create meals that keep you full and focused.
  • Decoding Labels: Become a food detective by learning to read labels and make smart choices about what you’re eating—no more sneaky junk!
  • Healthy Eating on a Budget: Get awesome tips for you and your family to eat well without breaking the bank, because good food shouldn’t cost a fortune.

This chapter is all about giving you the tools to make nutrition work for YOU. Whether you’re grabbing a quick snack before practice or planning meals with your fam, you’ll know how to make choices that boost your energy and keep you going strong. So, let’s dig in—your journey to eating smarter and feeling better starts right here!
