Chapter 6: Stress Management for Teens

Hey high schoolers, feeling the pressure? In Chapter 6: Stress Management for Teens, we’re tackling the big “S” word—stress—and learning how to keep it from running your life. This isn’t about pretending everything’s fine—it’s about getting real with what stresses you out and finding practical ways to chill out and stay in control, even when life gets crazy.

Here’s what we’ll dive into together:

  • What Is Stress? Figure out what stress really is and pinpoint the triggers in school, home, and life that get under your skin.
  • Mindfulness and Relaxation: Try out easy techniques like breathing exercises and mindfulness to calm your mind when it’s racing a mile a minute.
  • Time Management: Learn how to organize your chaotic schedule to cut down on last-minute panic and make room for what matters.
  • Healthy Coping Strategies: Discover better ways to deal with stress instead of falling into unhealthy habits that only make things worse.

This chapter is your go-to guide for keeping stress in check. Whether it’s exams, drama, or just too much on your plate, we’ve got tips to help you breathe easier and handle it like a pro.
