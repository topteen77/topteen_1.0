Chapter 1: Introduction to Health &amp; Wellness

Hey there, high schoolers! Welcome to the start of something awesome—your journey to understanding and owning your health and wellness! In Chapter 1: Introduction to Health &amp; Wellness, we’re diving into the foundation of what it means to truly thrive. This isn’t just about eating right or working out—it’s about feeling great in your body, mind, and heart, and setting yourself up for success in every part of your life.

Here’s what we’ll unpack together:

  • What Is Wellness? We’ll break down the big picture of physical, mental, and emotional health, so you know exactly what “being healthy” really means for you.
  • Why Health Matters: Discover how taking care of yourself can supercharge your grades, strengthen your friendships, and even shape your future dreams.
  • Busting Myths: Let’s clear up those crazy misconceptions about teen health—like whether you really need to skip meals to “stay fit” (spoiler: you don’t!).
  • Setting Personal Wellness Goals: Get ready to kickstart your journey by creating goals that are all about YOU and what you want to achieve.

This chapter is your launchpad. It’s where you’ll start to see how small changes can make a huge difference in how you feel every day. So, grab your notebook, bring your curiosity, and let’s figure out what wellness means to you. Your healthier, happier life starts right here, right now!
