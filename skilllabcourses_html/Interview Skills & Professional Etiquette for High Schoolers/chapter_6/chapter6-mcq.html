MCQs

Question 1: What is the best way to show respect during a classroom discussion or when a guest speaker is presenting at a school event?

A) Checking your phone for updates to stay informed

B) Listening actively, keeping side conversations to a minimum, and raising your hand to ask questions

C) Talking to a friend quietly about unrelated topics

D) Leaving early if you’re not interested in the topic

Correct Answer: B) Listening actively, keeping side conversations to a minimum, and raising your hand to ask questions

 

Question 2: Why is punctuality important in a workplace setting like an internship or part-time job?

A) It shows you can manage your time and don’t need supervision

B) It proves you’re serious, reliable, and value the team’s schedule

C) It allows you to take longer breaks later in the day

D) It’s only important if your boss notices you’re late

Correct Answer: B) It proves you’re serious, reliable, and value the team’s schedule

 

Question 3: When writing a professional email to a teacher or potential employer, what should you include to make a good impression?

A) A vague subject line like “Hey” and lots of emojis for personality

B) A clear subject line, polite greeting, concise message, and a proper sign-off

C) Texting slang like “u” instead of “you” to keep it casual

D) A long message with every detail of your day to show effort

Correct Answer: B) A clear subject line, polite greeting, concise message, and a proper sign-off

 

Question 4: What is a key tip for maintaining digital etiquette during a virtual meeting or Zoom call for school or work?

A) Joining late to avoid small talk with others

B) Keeping your camera off and staying on mute the entire time, even when asked to speak

C) Testing your tech beforehand, joining a few minutes early, and dressing appropriately

D) Using a funny virtual background to entertain everyone

Correct Answer: C) Testing your tech beforehand, joining a few minutes early, and dressing appropriately
