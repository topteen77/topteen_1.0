MCQs

Question 1: SpeakingClearlyand ConfidentlyWhat is the primary benefit of avoiding filler words like “um” or “like” when speaking in a professional setting?

A) It makes you sound more casual and relatable.

B) It helps you speak faster and finish quicker.

C) It conveys confidence and clarity in your message.

D) It ensures you don’t need to pause while thinking.

Correct Answer: C) It conveys confidence and clarity in your message.

 

Question 2: Active Listening in ConversationsWhich of the following is an example of active listening during a conversation?

A) Checking your phone while someone is speaking to you.

B) Interrupting the speaker to share your own opinion.

C) Nodding and asking a follow-up question based on what was said.

D) Thinking about what you’ll say next while the other person talks.

Correct Answer: C) Nodding and asking a follow-up question based on what was said.

 

Question 3: Handling Awkward or Tough QuestionsWhen faced with a difficult question during an interview, such as “What is your biggest weakness?”, what is the most effective strategy to respond?

A) Avoid answering by changing the topic completely.

B) Admit the weakness honestly and explain how you’re working to improve it.

C) Say you don’t have any weaknesses to avoid seeming unprepared.

D) Give a long, detailed story about an unrelated success.

Correct Answer: B) Admit the weakness honestly and explain how you’re working to improve it.

 

Question 4: General Communication SkillsWhy is maintaining an appropriate pace while speaking important in a professional or academic setting?

A) It allows you to finish your thoughts as quickly as possible.

B) It ensures your audience can understand and follow your ideas clearly.

C) It makes the conversation more exciting and dynamic.

D) It prevents you from needing to think about your words.

Correct Answer: B) It ensures your audience can understand and follow your ideas clearly.
