MCQs

Question 1: What is the best way to receive constructive criticism gracefully during a school project review or mock interview?

A) Interrupt the person giving feedback to explain why they’re wrong

B) Listen without interrupting, ask clarifying questions if needed, and thank them for their input

C) Ignore the feedback and focus only on what you think you did well

D) Get upset and walk away to avoid hearing more criticism

Correct Answer: B) Listen without interrupting, ask clarifying questions if needed, and thank them for their input

 

Question 2: How can you turn rejection into motivation after not getting selected for a club or job?

A) Give up on trying for similar opportunities since you weren’t chosen

B) Feel disappointed for a short time, ask for feedback if possible, and use it as a learning opportunity to improve

C) Blame others for the decision and refuse to try again

D) Pretend the rejection didn’t happen and avoid thinking about it

Correct Answer: B) Feel disappointed for a short time, ask for feedback if possible, and use it as a learning opportunity to improve

 

Question 3: Why is self-reflection important after a mock interview or class presentation?

A) It helps you criticize yourself harshly so you never make mistakes again

B) It allows you to identify what went well and what to improve, setting goals for next time

C) It’s only useful if someone else tells you what you did wrong

D) It wastes time since you can’t change what already happened

Correct Answer: B) It allows you to identify what went well and what to improve, setting goals for next time

 

Question 4: What should you do if feedback from a teacher or coach feels unclear or confusing?

A) Assume they don’t know what they’re talking about and ignore it

B) Politely ask for clarification with a question like, “Can you explain what you mean by that?”

C) Argue with them to prove your point instead of listening

D) Nod and pretend you understand, even if you don’t

Correct Answer: B) Politely ask for clarification with a question like, “Can you explain what you mean by that?”
