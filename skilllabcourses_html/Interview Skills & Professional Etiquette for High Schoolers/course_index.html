Course Title: Interview Skills &amp; Professional Etiquette for High Schoolers

Chapter 1: Introduction to Interviews and Professional Etiquette

  • Why Interview Skills Matter for Teens (Understanding the importance of interviews for college admissions, internships, and part-time jobs)
  • What Is Professional Etiquette? (Defining workplace norms and why they’re relevant even in high school)
  • Setting Personal Goals for the Course (Identifying individual areas for improvement in communication or confidence)

Chapter 2: First Impressions – Making a Positive Impact

  • The Power of Body Language (Learning posture, eye contact, and handshakes for interviews and professional settings)
  • Dressing for Success (Understanding appropriate attire for different scenarios—interviews, events, and workplaces)
  • The 30-Second Rule: Introducing Yourself Confidently (Crafting a quick, memorable self-introduction or elevator pitch)

Chapter 3: Building Effective Communication Skills

  • Speaking Clearly and Confidently (Practicing tone, pace, and avoiding filler words like “um” or “like”)
  • Active Listening in Conversations (Learning to listen attentively and respond thoughtfully during interviews or discussions)
  • Handling Awkward or Tough Questions (Strategies to stay calm and answer unexpected or personal questions)

Chapter 4: Preparing for an Interview

  • Researching the Opportunity (How to learn about a school, company, or role before an interview)
  • Common Interview Questions for Teens (Preparing answers for questions like “Tell me about yourself” or “What are your strengths?”)
  • Creating a Personal Cheat Sheet (Organizing key points about skills, experiences, and goals to reference mentally)

Chapter 5: Mastering the Interview Process

  • Before the Interview: Planning and Punctuality (Tips for arriving on time, bringing necessary materials, and calming nerves)
  • During the Interview: Staying Poised (Techniques to stay focused, polite, and engaged while answering questions)
  • After the Interview: Follow-Up Etiquette (Writing thank-you notes or emails and reflecting on the experience for improvement)

Chapter 6: Professional Etiquette in Different Settings

  • Classroom and School Event Etiquette (How to interact respectfully with teachers, peers, and guest speakers)
  • Workplace Basics for Internships or Part-Time Jobs (Understanding punctuality, teamwork, and asking for help appropriately)
  • Digital Etiquette: Emails and Online Communication (Writing professional emails, using proper tone, and avoiding common mistakes)

Chapter 7: Handling Feedback and Building Resilience

  • Receiving Constructive Criticism Gracefully (Learning to accept feedback without taking it personally)
  • Turning Rejection into Motivation (Coping with not getting a role or job and using it as a learning opportunity)
  • Self-Reflection for Continuous Improvement (Assessing personal performance in mock interviews or real scenarios)

Chapter 8: Group Dynamics and Teamwork Skills

  • Collaborating in Group Interviews or Projects (How to stand out while being a team player in group settings)
  • Respecting Diverse Perspectives (Understanding cultural and personal differences in professional environments)
  • Resolving Conflicts Professionally (Strategies to handle disagreements politely with peers or colleagues)

Chapter 9: Presentation Skills for Interviews and Beyond

  • Preparing a Short Presentation About Yourself (Creating a brief talk or portfolio to showcase skills and achievements)
  • Using Visuals and Props Effectively (Tips for using resumes, certificates, or digital tools during presentations)
  • Overcoming Stage Fright (Techniques like deep breathing and practice to build confidence in speaking)

Chapter 10: Capstone Project – Mock Interview and Professional Portfolio

  • Compiling a Professional Portfolio (Organizing resumes, certificates, and personal statements for interviews)
  • Participating in a Mock Interview (Practicing a full interview with peers or mentors for real-time feedback)
  • Reflecting on Your Growth and Setting Future Goals (Evaluating progress in skills and planning next steps for professional development)
