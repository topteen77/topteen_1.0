MCQs

Question 1: During a group interview for a summer job at a local store, your team is tasked with creating a quick marketing plan. You have a great idea for a social media campaign, but you notice others are hesitant to speak up. What is the best way to contribute while being a team player?

A) Explain your idea in detail and insist the group uses it since it’s the best option.

B) Share your idea briefly and invite input by saying, “I think a social media campaign could work—what do you all think, or do you have other ideas?”

C) Stay quiet and let others take the lead, even if it means not sharing your idea at all.

D) Tell the group you’ll handle the marketing plan alone since no one else is contributing.

Correct Answer: B) Share your idea briefly and invite input by saying, “I think a social media campaign could work—what do you all think, or do you have other ideas?”

 

Question 2: In a group project for history class, a teammate suggests focusing on a holiday from their culture that you’ve never heard of. Some group members seem unsure about including it. What’s the most respectful and inclusive way to respond?

A) Say, “That’s interesting, but let’s stick to something more common that everyone knows.”

B) Ask, “That sounds unique! Can you tell us more about it so we can see how to include it in our project?”

C) Ignore the suggestion and move on to another topic to avoid any awkwardness.

D) Tell them, “We don’t have time to learn about that—let’s pick something easier.”

Correct Answer: B) Ask, “That sounds unique! Can you tell us more about it so we can see how to include it in our project?”

 

Question 3: During a group project for science class, you and a teammate disagree on how to split the workload. They want to do less because they’re busy, but you feel it’s unfair. How should you handle this conflict professionally?

A) Say, “That’s not fair, you’re just being lazy,” and demand they do more.

B) Use an “I” statement like, “I feel worried about the balance of work—can we discuss a way that feels fair to everyone?”

C) Ignore the issue and do their share of the work yourself to avoid a fight.

D) Tell the teacher immediately without talking to your teammate first.

Correct Answer: B) Use an “I” statement like, “I feel worried about the balance of work—can we discuss a way that feels fair to everyone?”

 

Question 4: In a club meeting, your team is planning a fundraiser, and ideas are all over the place with no clear direction. What’s the best way to show teamwork and leadership while helping the group focus?

A) Take over the meeting and decide on a plan yourself since no one else is stepping up.

B) Suggest, “Let’s take turns sharing one idea each and then vote on the top two to focus on—does that work?”

C) Stay silent and wait for someone else to organize the group, even if it takes longer.

D) Criticize the group for being disorganized and tell them to get it together.

Correct Answer: B) Suggest, “Let’s take turns sharing one idea each and then vote on the top two to focus on—does that work?”
