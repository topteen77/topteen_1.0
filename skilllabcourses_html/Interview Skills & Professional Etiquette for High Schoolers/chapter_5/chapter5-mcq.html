MCQs

Question 1: What is the recommended time to arrive before an interview to show punctuality and preparedness?

A) Right on time, to avoid waiting around

B) 5 minutes early, to keep things casual

C) 10-15 minutes early, to account for unexpected delays

D) 30 minutes early, to make a strong impression

Correct Answer: C) 10-15 minutes early, to account for unexpected delays

 

Question 2: Which of the following is a key element of making a strong first impression during an interview?

A) Checking your phone to stay updated

B) Smiling, making eye contact, and offering a firm handshake (if in-person)

C) Talking about your personal hobbies right away

D) Sitting with crossed arms to appear relaxed

Correct Answer: B) Smiling, making eye contact, and offering a firm handshake (if in-person)

 

Question 3: What is the purpose of sending a thank-you note or email after an interview?

A) To ask immediately if you got the position

B) To show gratitude, keep yourself memorable, and reinforce your interest

C) To explain any mistakes you made during the interview

D) To request feedback on how you performed

Correct Answer: B) To show gratitude, keep yourself memorable, and reinforce your interest

 

Question 4: When reflecting on your interview performance, what is a helpful strategy to improve for future interviews?

A) Focus only on what went wrong to avoid repeating mistakes

B) Ignore the experience if you don’t get the opportunity

C) Jot down what went well, what was tricky, and how you can improve

D) Wait for the interviewer to give you feedback before reflecting

Correct Answer: C) Jot down what went well, what was tricky, and how you can improve
