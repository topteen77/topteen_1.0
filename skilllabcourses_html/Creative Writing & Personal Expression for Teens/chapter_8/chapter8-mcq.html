MCQs

Question 1: The Importance of Revision in Creative WritingWhy is revision an important step in writing, according to Chapter 8?

A) It’s only about fixing typos and grammar mistakes to avoid embarrassment.

B) It helps make your writing clearer, stronger, and more true to your voice.

C) It’s a quick way to finish your work without much effort.

D) It’s just for teachers to grade your work better.Correct Answer:B) It helps make your writing clearer, stronger, and more true to your voice.

 

Question 2: Grammar, Punctuation, and Style BasicsWhich of these is the correct way to fix a common grammar mistake in a sentence like “Your gonna love this party!”?

A) Change “Your” to “You’re” because it means “you are.”

B) Change “Your” to “Yours” to show possession.

C) Leave it as “Your” because it’s about the party belonging to someone.

D) Add a comma after “Your” to make it clearer.Correct Answer:A) Change “Your” to “You’re” because it means “you are.”

 

Question 3: Peer Review and Collaborative EditingWhat’s a good way to give constructive feedback during a peer review, based on Chapter 8?

A) Just say “This is bad” so they know to rewrite everything.

B) Start with something positive, then suggest a specific improvement.

C) Only point out what’s wrong without mentioning anything good.

D) Tell them to change their whole style to match yours.Correct Answer:B) Start with something positive, then suggest a specific improvement.

 

Question 4: Preparing Your Work for Sharing or PublicationWhat’s an important step when prepping your writing for publication in a school magazine, as per Chapter 8?

A) Post it on social media first to get likes before submitting.

B) Ignore the magazine’s rules since your writing is already good.

C) Format it properly, like using the right font and following word count limits.

D) Only share it with friends instead of submitting it officially.Correct Answer:C) Format it properly, like using the right font and following word count limits.
