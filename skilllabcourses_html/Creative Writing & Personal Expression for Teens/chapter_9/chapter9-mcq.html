MCQs

Question 1: Which of the following is a benefit of sharing your writing on a platform like Wattpad, according to Chapter 9?

A) It guarantees you’ll become a famous author overnight.

B) It allows you to connect with other teen writers and get feedback from a global community.

C) It’s the only way to get your work published in a school magazine.

D) It automatically enters your work into writing contests.Correct Answer:B) It allows you to connect with other teen writers and get feedback from a global community.

 

Question 2: What is one key advantage of joining a writing workshop as a teen, based on Chapter 9?

A) You are guaranteed to win a cash prize for every session you attend.

B) It provides a safe space to learn new skills, share work, and meet other writers your age.

C) It’s the only way to get your writing published online.

D) It ensures colleges will accept you without looking at your grades.Correct Answer:B) It provides a safe space to learn new skills, share work, and meet other writers your age.

 

Question 3: Why is creating an online portfolio important for a teen writer, as discussed in Chapter 9?

A) It’s a way to show off your best work and stand out to colleges or contest judges.

B) It’s required to join any writing contest or workshop.

C) It’s the only way to share your writing with friends.

D) It automatically makes your writing go viral on social media.Correct Answer:A) It’s a way to show off your best work and stand out to colleges or contest judges.

 

Question 4: What is a good strategy for handling criticism of your writing, according to Chapter 9?

A) Ignore all feedback because it’s always meant to hurt you.

B) Argue with the person giving feedback to prove they’re wrong.

C) Listen without getting defensive and use specific, helpful suggestions to improve.

D) Stop writing altogether if someone doesn’t like your work.Correct Answer:C) Listen without getting defensive and use specific, helpful suggestions to improve.
