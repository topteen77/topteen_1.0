MCQs

Question 1: What is a key factor to consider when selecting pieces for your creative portfolio, according to Chapter 10?

A) Including every single piece of writing you’ve ever done to show quantity.

B) Choosing a variety of pieces that reflect different sides of your personality and skills.

C) Only picking pieces that got perfect grades from teachers.

D) Selecting pieces based solely on what your friends liked the most.Correct Answer:B) Choosing a variety of pieces that reflect different sides of your personality and skills.

 

Question 2: Why is writing a reflection piece important for your portfolio, as discussed in Chapter 10?

A) It’s a way to fill up space if you don’t have enough writing pieces.

B) It helps you recognize how you’ve improved and what you’ve learned as a writer over time.

C) It’s required to get feedback from peers or mentors.

D) It guarantees your portfolio will be accepted for publication.Correct Answer:B) It helps you recognize how you’ve improved and what you’ve learned as a writer over time.

 

Question 3: What is a good strategy for presenting your portfolio to others, based on Chapter 10?

A) Avoid explaining your work and let the audience figure it out on their own.

B) Prepare a short intro about your portfolio and why you chose the pieces, to give context.

C) Only share it with people who you know will give positive feedback.

D) Read every single piece aloud, even if it takes hours.Correct Answer:B) Prepare a short intro about your portfolio and why you chose the pieces, to give context.

 

Question 4: What is one practical step for planning your future as a writer, according to Chapter 10?

A) Setting only big, long-term goals like becoming a bestselling author overnight.

B) Breaking down your dreams into small, achievable goals for the next year, like joining a writing club.

C) Waiting until after high school to think about writing goals.

D) Focusing only on resources that cost money, like expensive writing courses.Correct Answer:B) Breaking down your dreams into small, achievable goals for the next year, like joining a writing club.
