MCQs

Question 1: What is one effective method for brainstorming short story ideas, as discussed in Chapter 4?

A) Writing a detailed outline before coming up with any ideas

B) Asking “What if” questions to spark imaginative scenarios

C) Copying plots from popular movies without any changes

D) Focusing only on ideas that are completely realisticCorrect Answer:B) Asking “What if” questions to spark imaginative scenarios

 

Question 2: According to Chapter 4, what is a key element in creating relatable characters for a short story?

A) Making them perfect with no flaws or struggles

B) Giving them a clear goal or want, along with flaws and fears

C) Describing their appearance in extreme detail in the first paragraph

D) Ensuring they are based entirely on fictional, unrealistic traitsCorrect Answer:B) Giving them a clear goal or want, along with flaws and fears

 

Question 3: How does Chapter 4 suggest making dialogue sound natural and authentic for teen characters?

A) Writing long, formal speeches that explain every detail

B) Using slang and short, casual exchanges like real teen conversations

C) Avoiding any conflict or emotion in what characters say

D) Having characters speak in complete, grammatically perfect sentencesCorrect Answer:B) Using slang and short, casual exchanges like real teen conversations

 

Question 4: What is the purpose of conflict in a short story, based on Chapter 4?

A) To confuse readers with unnecessary side plots

B) To create tension and keep readers engaged by presenting a struggle

C) To avoid showing any character growth or change

D) To focus only on describing the setting in detailCorrect Answer:B) To create tension and keep readers engaged by presenting a struggle

 

Question 5: What is an important step in revising a short story, as outlined in Chapter 4?

A) Leaving the first draft unchanged because it’s already perfect

B) Cutting parts that don’t support the main conflict and polishing language for impact

C) Adding as many new characters as possible to make the story longer

D) Ignoring feedback and focusing only on grammar correctionsCorrect Answer:B) Cutting parts that don’t support the main conflict and polishing language for impact
