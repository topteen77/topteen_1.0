MCQs

Question 1: Why is self-expression through writing especially important for teens, according to the chapter?

A) It helps teens get better grades in school subjects like math and science

B) It provides a way to process emotions and explore identity during a time of rapid change

C) It ensures teens will become famous authors or poets in the future

D) It replaces the need for talking to friends or family about feelingsCorrect Answer:B) It provides a way to process emotions and explore identity during a time of rapid change

 

Question 2: What is one method suggested in the chapter for identifying emotions and thoughts through writing?

A) Writing only about happy moments to avoid negative feelings

B) Using a stream of consciousness technique to write without filtering thoughts

C) Copying poems from famous writers to understand emotions better

D) Avoiding writing about personal experiences to stay objectiveCorrect Answer:B) Using a stream of consciousness technique to write without filtering thoughts

 

Question 3: How can writing contribute to mental wellness for teens, as described in the chapter?

A) It guarantees that teens will never feel stressed or anxious again

B) It offers a way to release stress and process tough experiences at their own pace

C) It requires teens to share all their writing with others for validation

D) It focuses only on solving problems rather than exploring feelingsCorrect Answer:B) It offers a way to release stress and process tough experiences at their own pace

 

Question 4: What is a key consideration when balancing vulnerability and privacy in sharing personal writing?

A) Always sharing everything online to get the most feedback possible

B) Starting small by sharing with trusted people before a wider audience

C) Never sharing any writing to avoid any risk of judgment

D) Only sharing writing that has been approved by a teacher or parentCorrect Answer:B) Starting small by sharing with trusted people before a wider audience

 

Question 5: What is one benefit of sharing your writing with others, as highlighted in the chapter?

A) It ensures everyone will always agree with and like your work

B) It can create connections with others who relate to your experiences

C) It is the only way to improve as a writer

D) It makes writing less personal and easier to doCorrect Answer:B) It can create connections with others who relate to your experiences
