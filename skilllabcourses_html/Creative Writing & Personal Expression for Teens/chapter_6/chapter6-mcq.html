MCQs

Question 1: What is one of the main benefits of daily journaling for teens, as discussed in Chapter 6?

A) It helps improve handwriting skills for school assignments

B) It provides a stress release and a way to process emotions without judgment

C) It guarantees better grades in creative writing classes

D) It replaces the need for talking to friends or family about problemsCorrect Answer:B) It provides a stress release and a way to process emotions without judgment

 

Question 2: Which of the following best describes a Gratitude Journal, according to Chapter 6?

A) A journal where you write down your dreams immediately after waking up

B) A journal focused on writing about things you’re thankful for, big or small

C) A journal for reflecting deeply on why certain events or feelings happened

D) A journal used to plan future goals and career aspirationsCorrect Answer:B) A journal focused on writing about things you’re thankful for, big or small

 

Question 3: How can writing prompts help teens with journaling, as explained in Chapter 6?

A) They provide strict rules to ensure journal entries are written correctly

B) They act as starting points to overcome a blank page and spark personal reflection

C) They are only useful for turning journal entries into creative stories

D) They limit the topics teens can write about to avoid emotional topicsCorrect Answer:B) They act as starting points to overcome a blank page and spark personal reflection

 

Question 4: What is a key step in turning a journal entry into a creative piece, as outlined in Chapter 6?

A) Deleting the original entry to start fresh with a new idea

B) Identifying the core feeling or idea in the entry to build a poem, story, or song around it

C) Copying the entry word-for-word into a new format without changes

D) Sharing the entry online before deciding on a creative formCorrect Answer:B) Identifying the core feeling or idea in the entry to build a poem, story, or song around it
