MCQs

Question 1: What is personal branding?

A) The way others talk about you online without your control

B) A unique mix of your personality, skills, and values that you present to the world

C) Only the posts you share on social media platforms

D) A fake image you create to impress others

Correct Answer: B) A unique mix of your personality, skills, and values that you present to the world

 

Question 2: Why does personal branding matter for teens?

A) It helps them get more followers on social media

B) It shapes how colleges, employers, and others perceive them, even at a young age

C) It guarantees a job or college admission

D) It’s only important for adults, not teens

Correct Answer: B) It shapes how colleges, employers, and others perceive them, even at a young age

 

Question 3: How can a strong personal brand impact future opportunities for teens?

D) It only helps with getting more likes on posts

Correct Answer: B) It can help stand out in college applications or job interviews by showcasing skills and passions

 

Question 4: What is the main difference between personal branding and online reputation?

A) Personal branding is what others say about you, while online reputation is what you control

B) Personal branding is the image you create and control, while online reputation is how others perceive you online

C) Personal branding and online reputation are exactly the same thing

D) Personal branding only matters offline, while online reputation is for social media

Correct Answer: B) Personal branding is the image you create and control, while online reputation is how others perceive you online
