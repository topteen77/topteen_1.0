MCQs

Question 1: What is the main purpose of developing a Personal Branding Plan as a teen?

A) To post as much content as possible on every social media platform.

B) To create a roadmap that defines your online identity, sets goals, and ensures consistency while staying true to yourself.

C) To copy the branding style of popular influencers to gain followers quickly.

D) To avoid using social media and focus only on offline activities.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a key step in implementing and reviewing your online presence?

A) Posting randomly without a schedule to keep things spontaneous.

B) Launching content based on your plan, engaging with followers, tracking progress, and adjusting based on what works after a few weeks.

C) Ignoring feedback from analytics or followers to maintain your original idea.

D) Changing your entire brand every week to keep it fresh.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: Why are peer feedback and group discussions important for teens building their personal brand?

A) They allow you to compete with friends to see who has the most followers.

B) They provide fresh perspectives, new ideas, and support from peers to improve your brand and build confidence.

C) They are only useful if everyone agrees with everything you do.

D) They focus on criticizing others’ work to make your brand look better.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is one benefit of reflecting on your growth and planning future steps after completing a personal branding course?

A) It helps you forget past mistakes and start over with a completely new identity.

B) It allows you to celebrate wins, learn from challenges, and set new goals to keep your brand evolving with purpose.

C) It ensures you never have to change or update your brand again.

D) It focuses only on comparing your brand to others’ to see who did better.Correct Answer:B
