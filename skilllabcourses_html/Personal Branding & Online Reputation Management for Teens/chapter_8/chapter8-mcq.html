MCQs

Question 1: What is a key step in identifying fake news or misinformation online?

A) Believing every viral post because it has a lot of likes.

B) Checking the source of the information to see if it’s from a trusted or verified outlet.

C) Sharing the post immediately to warn others without verifying it.

D) Only reading the headline without looking at the full story.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: Why is fact-checking important before sharing content on social media?

A) It helps you get more followers by posting quickly.

B) It prevents spreading false information and protects your online reputation.

C) It ensures you always agree with everyone in your group chat.

D) It’s unnecessary since most online content is true.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: How do algorithms shape your online experience as a teen?

A) They randomly show content with no connection to your activity.

B) They track your likes, searches, and views to customize your feed and keep you engaged.

C) They prevent you from seeing ads or targeted content.

D) They only show content from people you follow.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is an ethical way to use technology and respect others’ privacy online?

A) Post group photos or videos without asking anyone in them for permission.

B) Share personal information like addresses or schedules to seem friendly.

C) Ask for consent before posting content involving others and protect your own privacy with strong settings.

D) Use someone else’s account or content without giving credit.Correct Answer:C
