MCQs

Question 1: Why is networking important for teens?

A) It helps in avoiding social media altogether to focus on studies.

B) It builds relationships that can offer friendships, advice, and future opportunities like college or jobs.

C) It’s only useful for adults and not relevant to high school students.

D) It guarantees immediate job offers without any effort.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a safe and effective way to connect with someone online as a teen?

A) Share your home address and phone number right away to build trust.

B) Send a short, friendly message about a shared interest and avoid sharing personal information.

C) Spam multiple messages until they reply to show your enthusiasm.

D) Only connect with strangers in private chats without telling anyone.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: How can you engage in a meaningful conversation on social platforms?

A) Ask open-ended questions and share a bit about yourself to keep the chat flowing.

B) Send one-word replies like “cool” to keep things short and simple.

C) Argue with others to show you have strong opinions.

D) Avoid responding to messages to seem mysterious.Correct Answer:A

 

Question 4: What is a key tip for building a professional profile as a teen on platforms like LinkedIn?

A) Use a silly profile picture with filters to stand out.

B) Post personal drama and memes to show your personality.

C) List achievements like school projects or volunteer work and keep posts positive and relevant.

D) Connect with as many random people as possible without personalizing messages.Correct Answer:C
