Questions to Ask Before Posting:

Question 1: Why are privacy settings important for teens on social media?

A) They make your posts look cooler and get more likes

B) They control who can see your content and personal info, protecting you from unwanted attention

C) They automatically delete old posts to keep your profile clean

D) They are only useful for professional platforms like LinkedIn

Correct Answer: B) They control who can see your content and personal info, protecting you from unwanted attention

 

Question 2: What is a key reason to think before posting content online?

A) Posts can be permanent and impact your future, like college or job opportunities

B) Posting quickly gets you more followers instantly

C) Only public accounts need to worry about what they share

D) Deleted posts are gone forever and can’t be seen by anyone

Correct Answer: A) Posts can be permanent and impact your future, like college or job opportunities

 

Question 3: What is a recommended step to handle cyberbullying or negative comments on social media?

A) Reply angrily to show you’re not affected by the comments

B) Ignore it completely and hope it goes away on its own

C) Save evidence, block/report the user, and talk to a trusted person for support

D) Delete your account to avoid any further issues

Correct Answer: C) Save evidence, block/report the user, and talk to a trusted person for support

 

Question 4: How can teens protect themselves from online scams and predators?

A) Share personal info only with people who seem nice online

B) Keep personal info private, verify strangers, and trust their gut if something feels off

C) Accept all friend requests to build a bigger online network

D) Click on links promising free prizes to test if they’re real

Correct Answer: B) Keep personal info private, verify strangers, and trust their gut if something feels off
