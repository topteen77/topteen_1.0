Chapter 4: Social Media Etiquette and Safety

 

Section 1: Understanding Privacy Settings and Their Importance

Why Privacy Settings Matter: When you’re on social media, you’re sharing pieces of your life with the world—or at least with whoever can see your profile. Privacy settings are like the locks on your digital “house.” They control who can see your posts, photos, and personal info, helping protect you from strangers, oversharing, or even future consequences (like a college or job seeing something you didn’t mean to share). For teens, getting a grip on these settings is a must to keep your online presence safe while still having fun.

Breaking It Down—What Privacy Settings Do:

  • Who Sees Your Stuff: You can set your account to “private” (only approved followers see your content) or “public” (anyone can see it). Most platforms like Instagram and TikTok let you choose.
  • Who Contacts You: Settings can limit who can message you or comment on your posts—friends only, or no one at all.
  • What Info Is Visible: You can hide things like your location, email, or birthday from people you don’t know.
  • Tagging and Mentions: Some settings stop others from tagging you in posts or photos without your OK, which prevents unwanted attention.

Relatable Example for Teens: Imagine Sophia, a high school freshman, posts a funny video on Instagram with her account set to public. A random stranger comments something creepy, and she realizes anyone can see her profile—including her school location in her bio. She switches to private mode, limits comments to friends, and removes personal info. Now, she feels safer sharing. Have you checked your settings lately?

Fun Fact: Did you know that 60% of teens don’t even look at their privacy settings when they join a platform? That’s like leaving your front door wide open! Taking 5 minutes to adjust them can save you a lot of stress.

How to Check Your Settings (Quick Tips):

  • Instagram: Go to Settings &gt; Privacy. Set your account to “Private,” turn off location sharing, and choose who can comment.
  • TikTok: Head to Privacy &gt; Manage Account. Make your account private and disable “Suggest your account to others.”
  • Snapchat: Tap Settings &gt; Who Can… and pick “My Friends” for who can see your story or location.
  • General Rule: If you’re unsure, set everything to “Friends Only” and only accept people you know IRL.

Activity to Try: Grab your phone and open your most-used social media app. Check your privacy settings right now. Answer these questions:

  • Is my account private or public? _________________________
  • Who can see my posts or message me? _________________________
  • Is my location or personal info visible? _________________________ If anything feels risky, change it! Write one change you made: _________________________

Key Takeaway for Teens: Privacy settings are your first line of defense online. Take control of who sees your content and info—it’s like locking your digital door to keep the creeps out while still enjoying social media.

 

Section 2: Think Before You Post: The Long-Term Impact of Content

Why Think Twice? Every post, comment, or photo you share online can stick around forever—even if you delete it. Screenshots, archives, and search engines can keep your content alive, and it might impact your future, like college admissions, job applications, or relationships. For teens, it’s easy to post in the heat of the moment, but taking a second to think can save you from regret.
