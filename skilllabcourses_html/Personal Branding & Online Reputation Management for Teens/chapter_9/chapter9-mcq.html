MCQs

Question 1: Why do colleges and employers often check your online presence?

A) To see how many followers you have on social media.

B) To get a first impression of your personality, skills, and potential red flags.

C) To find out if you post funny memes or trending content.

D) To make sure you’re using the latest social media apps.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a good way to showcase your achievements and skills online as a teen?

A) Post about every small thing you do every day to flood your feed.

B) Share specific wins with a thoughtful caption and balance it with personal posts.

C) Exaggerate your accomplishments to seem more impressive.

D) Only post about achievements on private accounts where no one can see them.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: What is one benefit of creating a digital portfolio or blog for a high school student?

A) It guarantees you’ll get into any college or job you apply for.

B) It provides a controlled space to showcase your best work and impress colleges or employers.

C) It’s a place to share personal secrets and daily rants.

D) It’s only useful if you’re already a professional in your field.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: How can you align your personal brand with your career or academic aspirations?

A) Post random content without any connection to your goals to seem unpredictable.

B) Update your bio and share content related to your dream field while engaging with relevant communities.

C) Avoid mentioning your goals online to keep them a surprise.

D) Only follow friends and ignore professionals or pages in your field of interest.Correct Answer:B
