MCQs

Question 1: Why is engaging content important for personal branding as a teen?

A) It helps you get more followers without any effort.

B) It builds relationships, grows your audience, and strengthens your online presence.

C) It ensures you always use the latest social media apps.

D) It guarantees you will never make mistakes online.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a key tip for creating a strong visual aesthetic on your social media profile?

A) Use a different style for every post to keep things exciting.

B) Stick to a consistent theme or color scheme for a cohesive look.

C) Avoid posting pictures to focus only on text updates.

D) Change your profile picture every day to stay fresh.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: What is one effective strategy for writing captions that increase engagement?

A) Write long paragraphs to explain every detail of your post.

B) Include a question or call to action to encourage comments.

C) Use only hashtags without any text.

D) Keep captions unrelated to the post to surprise followers.Correct Answer:B
