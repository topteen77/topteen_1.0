MCQs

Question 1: What is a digital footprint?

A) A physical mark left by your device on the internet

B) The collection of data and information about you that exists online due to your internet activities

C) Only the social media accounts you create

D) A temporary record of your online searches that disappears after a day

Correct Answer: B) The collection of data and information about you that exists online due to your internet activities

 

Question 2: What is the difference between an active and a passive digital footprint?

A) Active is what you delete online, while passive is what you post

B) Active is what you choose to share, like posts, while passive is data collected without your direct input, like search history

C) Active is only on social media, while passive is only on websites

D) Active is private, while passive is public

Correct Answer: B) Active is what you choose to share, like posts, while passive is data collected without your direct input, like search history

 

Question 3: How can your online actions leave a trace in your digital footprint?

A) Only through posts you share on social media

B) Through likes, comments, searches, location data, and tagged content, even if you delete them later

C) Only if you use public Wi-Fi to access the internet

D) Only if you give permission for websites to track you

Correct Answer: B) Through likes, comments, searches, location data, and tagged content, even if you delete them later

 

Question 4: Why is assessing your current digital footprint important for teens?

A) It helps you get more followers on social media

B) It lets you see what’s online about you and if it matches the personal brand you want to build

C) It guarantees that nothing bad will ever show up online

D) It’s only necessary if you’re applying for a job right now

Correct Answer: B) It lets you see what’s online about you and if it matches the personal brand you want to build
