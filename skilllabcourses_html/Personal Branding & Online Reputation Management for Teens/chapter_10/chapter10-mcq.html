Question 1: What is copyright, and why is it important for teens building their personal brand online?

A) A type of social media filter that makes content look professional.

B) A legal protection for original creations like songs or art, important to avoid legal trouble and respect creators’ rights.

C) A rule that prevents teens from posting any content online without a license.

D) A way to claim ownership of anything you find on the internet.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a potential consequence of plagiarism or unauthorized sharing for a high school student?

B) Receiving a failing grade, facing school penalties, or damaging your reputation with colleges and employers.

C) Being rewarded by teachers for finding good content online.

D) Having your online accounts automatically upgraded to premium status.Correct Answer:B
