MCQs

Question 1: Why is balancing online and offline life important for teens building their personal brand?

A) It ensures you get more followers by posting constantly.

B) It helps recharge your mental health, boosts real-world connections, and keeps your content fresh and authentic.

C) It means you should avoid social media completely to focus on school.

D) It guarantees you’ll never miss out on online trends or events.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: How can social media pressure and comparison affect a teen’s mental health, and what’s one way to handle it?

A) It always makes teens more confident by showing them how to improve.

B) It can lower self-esteem and cause stress by comparing to others’ highlight reels; unfollowing negative accounts can help.

C) It encourages teens to post more often to keep up with friends.

D) It has no impact on mental health since social media is just for fun.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 3: Which of the following is a sign of digital overload or addiction that teens should watch for?

A) Feeling energized and focused after spending 5 hours straight on social media.

B) Spending hours online without noticing, feeling anxious without your phone, or skipping IRL activities.

C) Using your phone only for schoolwork and nothing else.

D) Checking social media once a day for 10 minutes.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 4: What is one strategy for healthy social media use that can help teens maintain mental well-being?

A) Spend as much time as possible on social media to build a bigger following.

B) Set time boundaries, like limiting social media to 1-2 hours a day, to stay in control and reduce stress.

C) Post every single moment of your day to stay relevant online.

D) Keep notifications on all the time to never miss a like or comment.Correct Answer:B
