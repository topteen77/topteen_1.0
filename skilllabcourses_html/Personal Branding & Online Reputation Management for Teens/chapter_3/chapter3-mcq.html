MCQs

Question 1: Why is identifying your values, strengths, and passions important when building an online presence?

A) It helps you copy popular trends online

B) It forms the foundation of your personal brand and guides what you share

C) It guarantees you’ll get more followers instantly

D) It’s only necessary for professional platforms like LinkedIn

Correct Answer: B) It forms the foundation of your personal brand and guides what you share

 

Question 2: What is an online persona, and how should it relate to who you are?

A) It’s a completely made-up character you pretend to be online

B) It’s the version of yourself you show online, and it should be authentic to your real personality

C) It’s only about looking perfect and professional for everyone to like you

D) It’s something you change every day based on trends

Correct Answer: B) It’s the version of yourself you show online, and it should be authentic to your real personality

 

Question 3: How should teens choose the right platforms for self-expression?

A) By joining every platform to reach the most people

B) By picking platforms that match their goals, persona, and the type of content they want to share

C) By only using the most popular platform among their friends

D) By avoiding platforms like LinkedIn since they’re only for adults

Correct Answer: B) By picking platforms that match their goals, persona, and the type of content they want to share

 

Question 4: What does it mean to create consistent and authentic content for your online presence?

A) Posting random things every day to stay active

B) Sharing content that follows a similar style or theme and reflects your true self, not a fake version

C) Only posting polished, perfect photos to impress others

D) Copying what popular influencers post to gain followers

Correct Answer: B) Sharing content that follows a similar style or theme and reflects your true self, not a fake version
