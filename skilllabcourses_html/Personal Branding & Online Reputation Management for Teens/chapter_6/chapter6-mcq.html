MCQs

Question 1: What is the primary goal of Online Reputation Management (ORM)?

A) To delete all negative content about yourself from the internet.

B) To control and influence how people perceive you online through your digital presence.

C) To avoid using social media altogether to prevent mistakes.

D) To create fake profiles to confuse people about your identity.Correct Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is a recommended step for monitoring your online presence?

A) Never Google yourself to avoid seeing negative content.

B) Only check your social media once a year to save time.

C) Set up Google Alerts to get notifications when your name appears online.

D) Ignore tags and mentions since they don’t affect your reputation.Correct Answer:C

 

Question 3: How should you handle negative feedback or a mistake on social media?

A) Immediately argue with the person who criticized you to defend yourself.

B) Ignore the feedback completely and hope it goes away.

C) Stay calm, own up to mistakes if necessary, and respond politely or apologize.

D) Delete your entire account to avoid further conflict.Correct Answer:C

 

Question 4: What is an effective strategy to repair a damaged online reputation?

A) Post as little as possible online to avoid further issues.

B) Share positive content regularly to outweigh past negativity in search results.

C) Create a new account and pretend the old one never existed.

D) Blame others for the damage to shift attention away from yourself.Correct Answer:B
