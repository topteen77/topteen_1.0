MCQs

Question 1: What is the first step in planning a Capstone Project, as discussed in the chapter?

A) Creating a colourful presentation for your class

B) Choosing a topic of personal interest, like sports stats or environmental data

C) Collecting data without a clear goal

D) Sharing your findings on social mediaCorrect Answer:B

 

Question 2: What is the purpose of defining a problem statement and objectives for your project?

A) To make the project look more professional with big words

B) To keep your project focused by clearly stating the issue and specific goals

C) To confuse your classmates with complicated ideas

D) To avoid collecting any data for the projectCorrect Answer:B

 

Question 3: Which of the following is an example of collecting and organizing data for a Capstone Project?

A) Guessing the results without any information

B) Tracking your screen time for a week and entering it into a table in Google Sheets

C) Drawing random pictures instead of using data

D) Ignoring data and writing a fictional storyCorrect Answer:B

 

Question 4: Why is creating visualizations, like charts or graphs, important for your project?

A) They make your project look pretty but don’t add any value

B) They help others understand your data findings quickly and clearly

C) They are only useful for art class, not data projects

D) They replace the need to explain your project in wordsCorrect Answer:B

 

Question 5: What should you include when presenting your Capstone Project to peers?

A) Only the parts that didn’t work, to get sympathy

B) Your topic, key findings, visualizations, and a recommendation based on your data

C) A long speech with no visuals or data insights

D) Only the data collection process, without any conclusionsCorrect Answer:B
