Introduction to Data Literacy &amp; Basic Analytics for High Schoolers

Welcome to Data Literacy &amp; Basic Analytics for High Schoolers, an innovative and empowering course designed specifically for students in classes 9-12! In today’s fast-paced, data-driven world, understanding data is no longer just a skill—it’s a superpower. From decoding trends on social media to making informed decisions in academics and beyond, data literacy is the key to navigating the future with confidence.

This course is your gateway to mastering the fundamentals of data and analytics in a fun, engaging, and practical way. You’ll learn how to collect, organize, and interpret data, create impactful visualizations, and apply critical thinking to solve real-world problems. Through hands-on projects, interactive activities, and inspiring case studies, you’ll discover how data shapes everything—from sports and entertainment to education and social change.

Course Chapters Overview

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to Data Literacy - Understand the basics of data and its importance in everyday life.
  • Chapter 2: Sources and Collection of Data - Learn where data comes from and how to gather it effectively.
  • Chapter 3: Organizing and Representing Data - Master the art of structuring and visualizing data with simple tools.
  • Chapter 4: Basics of Data Analysis - Discover how to identify patterns and draw meaningful conclusions from data.
  • Chapter 5: Introduction to Basic Analytics - Explore the world of analytics and its real-life applications.
  • Chapter 6: Critical Thinking with Data - Develop skills to spot misleading data and think logically about information.
  • Chapter 7: Data in Action – Real-World Applications - See how data solves problems in sports, health, and social issues.
  • Chapter 8: Future of Data and Career Opportunities - Get inspired by emerging trends and career paths in data and analytics.
  • Chapter 9: Capstone Project - Apply your skills to a real project and showcase your data-driven insights.
