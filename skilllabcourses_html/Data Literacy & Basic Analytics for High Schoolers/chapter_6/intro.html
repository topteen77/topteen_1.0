Introduction to Chapter 6: Critical Thinking with Data

Welcome to Chapter 6: Critical Thinking with Data, a vital chapter in your journey through Data Literacy &amp; Basic Analytics for High Schoolers! Designed for students in classes 9-12, this chapter focuses on sharpening your ability to think critically about data in a world full of information. If you’ve ever questioned whether a statistic or graph is trustworthy or wondered how to separate fact from fiction, this chapter will equip you with the tools to navigate data with confidence and skepticism.

In this chapter, we’ll uncover how data can be misused, learn strategies to verify information, and build a mindset that questions and evaluates data logically. Through thought-provoking examples and practical tips, you’ll develop the critical thinking skills necessary to make sense of data in media, news, and everyday life.

Chapter Sections Overview

  • Section 1: Spotting Misleading Data - Understand how graphs and statistics can be misleading, with examples of data manipulation in media.
  • Section 2: Fact-Checking with Data - Learn how to verify claims using data (e.g., checking stats in news articles) and recognize the importance of credible sources.
  • Section 3: Developing a Data Mindset - Build skills to question assumptions and biases while thinking logically about data-driven conclusions.

Get ready to become a savvy data detective with this essential chapter. By the end, you’ll be able to spot questionable data, verify facts, and approach information with a critical eye. Dive in now and take your data literacy to a deeper level—let’s think critically about data together!
