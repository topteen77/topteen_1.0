MCQs

Question 1: What is Debt?(Section 1: What is Debt? Understanding Loans in Family Contexts) Debt is best defined as:

A) Money you save for future goals.

B) Money you borrow with a promise to repay, often with interest.

C) Money you earn from a job or allowance.

D) Money you spend on daily needs like food.Correct Answer: b) Money you borrow with a promise to repay, often with interest.

 

Question 2: Common Reasons for FamilyLoans(Section 1: What is Debt? Understanding Loans in Family Contexts) Which of the following is a common reason families might take on debt?

A) Buying a new video game console for entertainment.

B) Taking a luxury vacation abroad.

C) Paying for a home or car through a loan.

D) Purchasing daily groceries.Correct Answer: c) Paying for a home or car through a loan.

 

Question 3: Understanding InterestRates(Section 2: Interest Rates and Borrowing: Education Loans, Personal Loans, and More) What is an interest rate?

A) The total amount of money borrowed.

B) A fee or percentage charged on borrowed money as the cost of borrowing.

C) The monthly payment made to repay a loan.

D) The time period over which a loan is repaid.Correct Answer: b) A fee or percentage charged on borrowed money as the cost of borrowing.

 

Question 4: Types ofLoans(Section 2: Interest Rates and Borrowing: Education Loans, Personal Loans, and More) Which type of loan typically has a higher interest rate due to being unsecured and riskier for lenders?

A) Education Loan

B) Home Loan

C) Personal Loan

D) Car LoanCorrect Answer: c) Personal Loan

 

Question 5: Cultural Views onDebt(Section 3: Cultural Perspectives on Debt: Why Avoiding Unnecessary Borrowing Matters) In many cultural contexts, such as in Indian traditions, debt is often:

A) Encouraged for all types of purchases to build wealth.

B) Seen as a last resort and avoided for non-essential items.

C) Considered irrelevant to financial planning.

D) Always viewed as a positive way to achieve goals.Correct Answer: b) Seen as a last resort and avoided for non-essential items.

 

Question 6: Avoiding UnnecessaryDebt(Section 3: Cultural Perspectives on Debt: Why Avoiding Unnecessary Borrowing Matters) What is one key reason to avoid unnecessary debt, as discussed in the chapter?

A) It helps you spend more on fun activities.

B) It prevents financial stress and preserves future freedom.

C) It guarantees a higher credit score instantly.

D) It ensures you can borrow more in the future.Correct Answer: b) It prevents financial stress and preserves future freedom.
