Personality Assessment

Personality assessment is the systematic evaluation of individual differences in characteristic patterns of thinking, feeling, and behaving. The HEXACO model is a comprehensive personality assessment framework that identifies six major dimensions: Honesty-Humility (H), Emotionality (E), Extraversion (X), Agreeableness (A), Conscientiousness (C), and Openness to Experience (O).

Personality Analysis - RIASEC Model

Our assessment is based on John Holland's renowned RIASEC model, which categorizes personalities into six distinct types:

Realistic (Doers)

Practical, hands-on individuals who enjoy working with tools, machines, and physical tasks. Ideal for engineering, construction, and technical fields.

Engineering Construction Mechanics

Investigative (Thinkers)

Analytical, curious problem-solvers who enjoy research and exploring complex ideas. Perfect for science, technology, and research careers.

Research Science Technology

Artistic (Creators)

Creative, expressive individuals who thrive in unstructured environments. Suited for arts, media, writing, and design careers.

Design Media Fine Arts

Social (Helpers)

Empathetic, supportive people who excel at helping others. Ideal for healthcare, counseling, teaching, and social services.

Teaching Healthcare Counseling

Enterprising (Persuaders)

Outgoing, ambitious leaders who thrive in business environments. Perfect for sales, management, and entrepreneurship.

Business Sales Leadership

Conventional (Organizers)

Detail-oriented, organized individuals who prefer structured environments. Suited for accounting, administration, and data management.

Finance Administration Data Analysis

Career Interest Assessment

In addition to personality analysis, the test includes a comprehensive Interest Model. Students are presented with a curated list of 200+ professions across diverse industries and sectors. From this list, they are asked to select the professions they:

  • Feel most interested in
  • Are inspired or influenced by
  • Or aspire to pursue in the future

This exercise helps uncover not only immediate preferences but also deeper motivations, passions, and potential career directions. The selections are then mapped against career clusters and future opportunities to give meaningful insights into long-term career alignment.
