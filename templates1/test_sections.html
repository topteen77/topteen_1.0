Aptitude Test

Welcome to the Aptitude Assessment!
Purpose

This timed assessment measures seven core ability areas: Abstract, Numerical, Logical, Verbal, Clerical, Mechanical, and Spatial Reasoning. Your performance will highlight natural strengths and help you choose roles that fit your skill set.

General Rules
  1. Environment -Sit in a quiet, interruption-free space.
  2. Timing - The entire test lasts 65 minutes; each section has its own timer shown on-screen.
  3. Completion - Attempt every question for the most accurate profile.
  4. Resources - No calculators, internet searches, or outside help. Keep one blank sheet for notes.
  5. Focus - Read carefully; answer honestly. The goal is insight, not perfection.
  6. Section Guidance - Review the brief on-screen instructions before each part.
    7. Section Time What to Expect Quick Tips
    Abstract Reasoning 10 min Patterns, sequences, mirror images, odd-one-out Eliminate options quickly; trust visual instincts.
    Numerical Reasoning 10 min Arithmetic, percentages, ratios, algebra, speed-distance, probability Work fast; move on if stuck. Mental math or rough paper only.
    Logical Reasoning 10 min Analogies, direction sense, syllogisms, number puzzles Base answers strictly on given facts; spot underlying rules.
    Verbal Reasoning 10 min Synonyms & antonyms, sentence correction, idioms, grammar Identify tone and context before choosing.
    Clerical Accuracy 5 min Pattern matching, character sequences, visual discrimination Scan for subtle differences in letters, numbers, symbols.
    Mechanical Reasoning 10 min Force, motion, energy, levers, gears, magnetism Apply basic physics and common-sense cause-and-effect.
    Spatial Reasoning 10 min 2D/3D rotation, folding, shape matching, mirror views Visualize rotations; sketch quickly if needed.
    Final Advice
  7. Pace yourself - Section clocks run continuously—keep one eye on the timer.
  8. Stay calm - Breathe, think clearly, and trust your first instinct if unsure.
  9. Review - If time allows, re-check flagged items before the section closes.

Good luck! Approach each question with confidence and clarity—you've got this.
