Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

Explore our FAQ section for quick and reliable answers to common queries. Whether you're a student or a parent, find the guidance you need to make informed decisions about education and career choices.

Parents are a child's first mentors and play a crucial role in guiding career decisions, especially when children are too young to decide independently. While parental involvement is important, it's essential to encourage children to explore their own interests. We recommend parents accompany their child to counselling sessions for a broader understanding of career options.

The earlier, the better! Ideally, career counselling should start in Class 9 or 10 to help your child choose the right stream. For Class 11-12 students, it helps in making informed career decisions, selecting college courses, and planning for entrance exams. Early planning allows time for in-depth research, skill development, and reduces stress.

Early career planning:

  • Allows time for in-depth research and preparation
  • Helps plan for necessary skills or entrance exams in advance
  • Encourages exploration and informed decision-making
  • Builds focus and ambition through goal-setting
  • Reduces stress and increases confidence in career choices
  • Provides direction for academic subject selection

Parents play a key role in shaping their child's career choices. You can support by:

  • Encouraging open discussions about interests and aspirations
  • Supporting exploration of different career options
  • Guiding them towards expert advice and professional counselling
  • Being patient and understanding during the decision-making process
  • Providing emotional support and encouragement
  • Staying informed about current career trends and opportunities

This is common and completely normal. A career counsellor can help you and your child understand the prospects, risks, and growth potential of different career paths, making decision-making easier. Professional guidance helps bridge differences by providing objective insights and facilitating productive discussions.

It's common for students to have diverse interests. We suggest taking our Stream Selector Test to assess aptitude, interests, and strengths. A counselling session can further clarify options and help make an informed decision. Multiple interests can actually be an advantage in today's interdisciplinary career landscape.

A good career choice should align with your child's:

  • Natural interests and passions
  • Inherent skills and abilities
  • Personality traits and work preferences
  • Long-term goals and values
  • Market opportunities and growth potential
  • Academic strengths and learning style

Yes, parents have a significant influence on career choices. We encourage their participation in counselling sessions to provide insights and support their child's decision-making. Your involvement helps create a comprehensive understanding of your child's potential and family expectations.

Parents are not required during testing but are encouraged to attend counselling sessions, especially for students in grades 9-10. For students 18 and above, parental presence is optional. The testing environment should be comfortable for the student to ensure honest responses.

Yes, you can book a counselling session if the test was taken within the last six months. If it was conducted earlier, we recommend retesting for updated insights, as interests and abilities can evolve over time.

The results will provide insights into:

  • Your child's personality type and learning preferences
  • Natural abilities and cognitive strengths
  • Career interests and motivational patterns
  • Recommended academic streams or career paths
  • Areas for development and skill-building
  • Specific action steps for career preparation

Career assessments provide valuable insights and suggest the best-fit options based on scientific analysis. However, success depends on your child's efforts, skill development, and adaptability. The tests are tools for guidance, not absolute predictors of success.

Test results are based on your child's responses and scientific analysis. If there's a disconnect, it's worth exploring why. Sometimes parents' expectations may not align with the child's natural inclinations. A counselling session can help reconcile these differences and find common ground.

Academic scores alone don't define future success. It's important to assess your child's interests and aptitude alongside their academic performance. Our Stream Selector Test and counselling sessions can help identify the best-fit stream based on strengths, preferences, and career goals rather than just marks.
