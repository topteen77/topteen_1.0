Career planning is the process of identifying your interests, strengths, skills, and career goals to make informed decisions about your future. It helps you choose the right subjects, courses, and career path based on your abilities, ensuring you're prepared for success in your chosen field.

The earlier, the better! Class 9-10 is the ideal time to explore different career options and understand your interests, while Class 11-12 is crucial for making final decisions about higher education and career choices. Starting early gives you more time to prepare and make informed decisions.

Parents are a child's first mentors and play a crucial role in guiding career decisions, especially when children are too young to decide independently. While parental involvement is important, it's essential to encourage children to explore their own interests. We recommend parents accompany their child to counselling sessions for a broader understanding of career options.

Early career planning:
  • Allows time for in-depth research and preparation
  • Helps plan for necessary skills or entrance exams in advance
  • Encourages exploration and informed decision-making
  • Builds focus and ambition through goal-setting
  • Reduces stress and increases confidence in career choices
  • Provides direction for academic subject selection

Parents play a key role in shaping their child's career choices. You can support by:
  • Encouraging open discussions about interests and aspirations
  • Supporting exploration of different career options
  • Guiding them towards expert advice and professional counselling
  • Being patient and understanding during the decision-making process
  • Providing emotional support and encouragement
  • Staying informed about current career trends and opportunities

This is common and completely normal. A career counsellor can help you and your child understand the prospects, risks, and growth potential of different career paths, making decision-making easier.
