Confused about What is a Psychometric Test?

A Psychometric test is a scientific assessment designed to measure an individual's mental capabilities, personality traits, and interests. These tests are commonly used to evaluate how a person thinks, behaves, and interacts with the world around them. By using a combination of questions and tasks, psychometric tests provide objective, reliable insights into a person's cognitive abilities and personal preferences.
