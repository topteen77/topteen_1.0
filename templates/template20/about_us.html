Stream Selector Test (Class 10)
  • Scientific assessment designed specifically for Class 10 students

  • Provides detailed insights into strengths and career inclinations for informed stream selection
Career Direction Test (Class 12)
  • Advanced assessment for Class 12 students ready for higher education and career planning
  • Matches personality, skills, and interests with specific career paths and college courses
  • Offers detailed career recommendations with industry insights and growth prospects
Know Your Learning Style
  • Identifies your unique learning style based on Howard Gardner's Multiple Intelligence Theory
  • Discovers whether you're a visual, auditory, kinaesthetic, or combination learner
  • Helps optimize study methods and career choices based on your natural intelligence patterns
EQ (Emotional Intelligence) Test
  • Measures emotional intelligence quotient and interpersonal skills
  • Assesses self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, and social skills
  • Provides insights for personal development and career suitability
  • Helps identify careers that align with your emotional strengths and communication style
