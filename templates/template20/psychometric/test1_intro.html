Test Instructions!

Part 1 - Personality Assessment

Welcome to the Personality Assessment Test! This test is designed to help you discover your interests and identify potential career paths that align with those interests. Please read the following instructions carefully before you begin.

  1. Ensure you are in a quiet and comfortable environment where you can focus without interruptions.
  2. The test consists of a series of statements related to different activities and preferences. Read each statement carefully before responding.
  3. Respond based on your genuine interests and preferences, not what you think is expected or what others might prefer.
  4. The time limit for this test is 20 minutes.
  5. Complete the test independently to ensure that the results accurately reflect your personal interests. Avoid External Help.

For each statement, indicate how much you agree or disagree using the following scale:

  • Not at all like me
  • A little like me
  • Somewhat like me
  • A lot like me
  • Exactly like me

Stay Focused. Try to avoid distractions while taking the test.
