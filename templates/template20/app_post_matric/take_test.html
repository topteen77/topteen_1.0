Introduction

Extrinsic & Intrinsic Motivation Model

Purpose

This test explores what truly drives you—inner satisfaction (intrinsic motivation) or external rewards and recognition (extrinsic motivation). Understanding your dominant drivers can help you choose study paths and careers that keep you energized.

How to respond
  1. Read each of the 13 items carefully. Each item presents four options labelled a – d.
  2. Select the single option that best reflects your genuine preference or excitement.
    • Think of what you naturally enjoy or value, not what you feel you should choose.
  3. Mark only one letter per question and do not leave any item blank.
  4. Answer spontaneously and honestly. Your first instinct usually captures your true motivation.
  5. Work steadily—about 5-6 minutes is enough for most people.
  6. Keep your answers confidential. Results are used solely for personal guidance.

Introduction

Interest Inventory (RIASEC Model)

Purpose

This inventory identifies the types of activities you naturally enjoy, helping you explore career fields aligned with your interests across the six RIASEC domains (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional).

How to respond
  1. Read each of the 30 statements carefully. Think about how much you would genuinely enjoy or prefer the activity described—not whether you can do it, but whether you like the idea of doing it.
  2. Choose one response number for every statement, using the 5-point scale below:
    Response Meaning
    1Highly Disagree — "I would really dislike doing this."
    2Disagree — "I would probably not enjoy this."
    3Neither Disagree nor Agree — "I'm neutral or unsure."
    4Agree — "I would probably enjoy this."
    5Strongly Agree — "I would really enjoy doing this."
  3. Mark only one number per statement and do not leave any items blank.
  4. Answer quickly and honestly. Your first impression usually reflects your true interest.
  5. Work straight through. The inventory typically takes 9-10 minutes to finish.
  6. Do not overthink skills or experience. Focus on interest, not ability.
  7. Keep your responses private. They will be used solely for guidance and will remain confidential.

When you are ready, click "Begin" to start the test.
