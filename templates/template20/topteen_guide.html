Tour Stop 1

Overview

TopTeen is an AI-powered career readiness platform built for Indian school students in Classes 9 to 12. It brings together psychometric assessment, structured career exploration, stream selection guidance, college and scholarship research, and one-to-one AI counselling into a single dashboard — so that instead of guessing your way through “What should I become?”, you follow a structured, evidence-based path.

TopTeen is built and operated by TopTeen Educational Services Private Limited (part of the Canam Group), headquartered in Chandigarh, and is designed specifically around the Indian education system — CBSE, ICSE, and State Boards — and aligned with the goals of NEP 2020.

1.1

What TopTeen Helps You Do

Self-Discovery

Scientifically designed assessments (Stream Sorter, Career Interest Profiler, and others) that map your interests, aptitude, and personality to suitable streams and careers.

Stream Selection

Class 9–10 students get structured help choosing between Science, Commerce, and Humanities — backed by data, not guesswork.

Career Exploration

Browse career clusters and individual career profiles across fields such as Engineering, Design, Commerce, Computer Applications, and more.

Four-Year Planning

A class-wise roadmap (Class 9 through Class 12) that tells you what to focus on at each stage — foundation building, board prep, competitive exams, and higher-education readiness.

College & Course Research

Explore undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, diplomas, and certifications to understand which path fits your goal.

AI Career Counsellor

A conversational assistant you can message any time for guidance on streams, careers, exams, or next steps.

Parent Involvement

Resources and guidance that help your parents understand and support your choices.

1.2

Who Should Use This Guide

This guide is written for students — not parents, schools, or counsellors. If you are in Class 9, 10, 11, or 12 and have been given access to TopTeen by your school or have signed up independently at www.topteen.in, this guide will walk you through everything from your first login to building your full career roadmap.

NoteSome features described here (for example, certain assessments or the AI Counsellor) may be switched on or off depending on what your school has enabled for your batch. If something in this guide is not visible on your screen, check with your school's TopTeen coordinator or write to info@topteen.in.
Tour Stop 2

Accessing TopTeen

TopTeen is a mobile-responsive web platform. You do not need to install anything — it works directly in your phone, tablet, or computer's internet browser.

2.1

Platform Address

Open your browser and go to: www.topteen.in

If your school has its own TopTeen sign-in page or QR code, use the link or code your school coordinator shares with you instead — it will take you to the same platform with your school already linked to your profile.

TipBookmark the TopTeen sign-in page on your phone and laptop so you can return to it quickly without searching for it each time.
2.2

Creating Your Account (First-Time Students)

TopTeen uses a simple email-or-mobile sign-up with OTP (one-time password) verification — there is no registration code to remember.

  1. Go to www.topteen.in and click Sign Up.
  2. Enter your email address or mobile number.
  3. Click Continue. TopTeen sends a one-time OTP to the email or number you entered.
  4. Enter the OTP in the Verify OTP screen. If it does not arrive within a minute, use Resend Code.
  5. Once verified, choose a password for your account and click Create Account.
  6. Complete your profile: full name, date of birth, class/grade, school name or code, and your city.
  7. Select your current academic stream (if you are in Class 11 or 12) or skip this if you are in Class 9 or 10 and have not chosen a stream yet.
  8. Click Continue to land on your TopTeen Dashboard for the first time.
What happens on your very first loginA short welcome tour highlights the main sections of your dashboard. You may be prompted to start the Stream Sorter or Career Interest Profiler assessment — see Section 5. If your school has enrolled you in advance, some profile fields (school, class) may already be filled in for you.
2.3

Logging In (Returning Students)

  1. Go to www.topteen.in and click Login.
  2. Choose Email Login or Mobile OTP.
  3. Email Login: enter your registered email and password, then click Continue.
  4. Mobile OTP: enter your registered mobile number, click Continue, then enter the OTP sent to your phone.
  5. You will land directly on your Dashboard (Home).
2.4

Forgotten Password or OTP Issues

Forgotten password: on the Email Login screen, select Forgot Password, enter your registered email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

OTP not received: check that you entered the correct mobile number or email, wait 60 seconds, then use Resend Code. If it still does not arrive, check your spam folder (for email) or signal strength (for SMS).

Account locked or unrecognised: contact your school's TopTeen coordinator first, since your account may be linked to your school's batch. If you signed up independently, write to info@topteen.in.

Menus and Drill-Down Navigation

Most sections follow the same two-level pattern: a top-level menu item (for example, Colleges & Courses) expands into a short list of sub-sections (for example, Search Colleges, Shortlisted Colleges, Scholarships). 3.3

Inside each section, information is organised into rectangular content cards, each with a clear title. Depending on the card, you may see a Read More, See All, or an action button (such as Add to Shortlist or Start Assessment) at the bottom. Once you recognise this pattern, every new page on TopTeen will feel familiar.

TipIf a page looks empty or a card says “No data yet,” it usually means you haven't completed the related step — for example, your Career Match card stays empty until you finish the Stream Sorter assessment.

Tour Stop 5

Assessments

TopTeen's assessments are the foundation of everything else on the platform. Your results here directly shape the career and college recommendations you see throughout the rest of your dashboard, so it is worth taking them seriously and answering honestly rather than guessing what sounds impressive.

5.1

Available Assessments

Assessment
Best For & What It Measures
Stream Sorter
Class 9–10. Interests, aptitude, and learning style, mapped to a recommended academic stream (Science, Commerce, or Humanities).
Career Interest Profiler
Class 9–12. A Holland Code (RIASEC) style interest assessment that surfaces career clusters matching your personality and interests.
Personality & Strengths Assessment
Class 9–12. Broader personality traits that influence the work environments and roles likely to suit you.
Aptitude Check
Class 9–12. Quick checks on logical, verbal, and numerical reasoning to round out your profile.
NoteNot every assessment is enabled for every student or school. If you don't see one listed here, it has not been switched on for your batch yet.
5.2

Taking an Assessment

  1. From the top navigation bar, go to Assessments.
  2. Choose an assessment from My Assessments to Take.
  3. Read the short instructions screen — most assessments take 10–20 minutes and cannot be paused once started, so set aside uninterrupted time.
  4. Answer every question as it genuinely applies to you, not how you think you “should” answer.
  5. Submit at the end. Your results usually appear immediately on the My Results screen.
If you're stuck partway throughMost assessments auto-save your progress every few questions — check the status bar at the top of the screen. If you do get interrupted, returning to Assessments will usually show your test under Assessments in Progress so you can resume. Avoid refreshing the page or pressing the back button mid-assessment, as this can occasionally reset unsaved answers.
5.3

Understanding Your Results

Each completed assessment produces a results page with three parts:

  • A summary score or profile type (for example, your top two Holland Code letters, or your recommended stream).
  • A plain-language explanation of what that result means — written for students, not psychologists.
  • A list of suggested career clusters or streams, each linking directly into the Careers section so you can explore further.

You can retake most assessments, but TopTeen will generally show you your most recent result as your “active” profile. If your interests or circumstances change significantly (for example, after Class 10 board results), it is worth retaking the relevant assessment.

Tour Stop 6

Exploring Careers

Once you have a sense of your interests — whether from an assessment or simply from curiosity — the Careers section is where you go to actually explore what different paths look like in practice.

6.1

Career Clusters

Careers on TopTeen are grouped into broad clusters such as Engineering & Technology, Commerce, Economics & Finance, Design, Fine Arts & Performing Arts, Computer Applications, Arts, Humanities, Education & Training, Business Management & Marketing, and Agriculture & Allied Sciences, among others. Each cluster contains dozens of individual career profiles.

  1. Go to Careers in the top navigation bar.
  2. Browse all clusters, or click Recommended for Me to see clusters matched to your assessment results.
  3. Click into a cluster to see the full list of careers inside it.
6.2

Reading a Career Profile

Each individual career page is built to answer the questions students actually ask, structured consistently so you always know where to look:

6.3

Shortlisting Careers

  1. On any career profile, click the heart icon or Add to “Careers I'm Exploring.”
  2. View your full shortlist any time from My Favourites on your Dashboard, or from Careers I'm Exploring inside the Careers section.
  3. Remove a career from your shortlist by clicking the same heart icon again.
TipShortlisting a career does not commit you to anything — think of it as a personal “maybe” list you can revisit and refine as you learn more.
Tour Stop 7

Colleges, Courses & Scholarships

This section helps you move from “What career do I want?” to “Where and how do I actually get there?” — covering specific colleges, courses, and the scholarships that can make them affordable.

7.1

Searching for Colleges and Courses

  1. Go to Colleges & Courses in the top navigation bar.
  2. Use the search bar to look up a specific college by name, or use Browse by Course to start from a subject or degree instead.
  3. Apply filters such as location, course type (Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Diploma, Certification), and fees range to narrow your results.
  4. Click any result to open its full profile — typically including admission criteria, fee ranges, course duration, and application windows.
7.2

Shortlisting and Tracking

Add any college or course to Colleges I'm Interested In using the heart icon, exactly as you would shortlist a career.

Your shortlist appears on your Dashboard under My Shortlists and inside My Roadmap as upcoming application tasks once deadlines are known.

7.3

Scholarships

The Scholarships tab inside Colleges & Courses lists scholarships you may be eligible for, based on your profile (class, stream, location, and category where disclosed). Each listing shows eligibility criteria, the award amount, and the application deadline.

ImportantAlways verify eligibility criteria and deadlines directly on the scholarship provider's official page before applying — TopTeen aggregates publicly available scholarship information, but final terms are set by the provider.
Tour Stop 8

My Roadmap

My Roadmap is your personal four-year plan, broken down by class. It turns long-term goals (“get into a good engineering college”) into specific, dated actions you can actually check off.

8.1

The Four-Year Framework

Class 9

Foundation building and broad exploration — trying out interests before committing to a stream.

Class 10

Stream selection and board exam preparation — supported directly by your Stream Sorter results.

Class 11

Deepening subject expertise and beginning competitive exam readiness for your chosen path.

Class 12

Higher-education preparation — applications, entrance exams, and life-skills development.

8.2

Roadmap Views

Tab
What You'll Find There
Overview
A combined view of overdue items, what's coming up, and what you've recently completed.
Goals
Longer-term personal goals you set for yourself — for example, “Score 90%+ in Class 10 boards.”
To-Do List
Short-term actions you create — for example, “Register for the entrance exam.”
Tasks Assigned to Me
Items your school or counsellor has specifically assigned to you.
8.3

Managing Tasks

  1. Go to My Roadmap from the top navigation bar.
  2. Switch between Overview, Goals, To-Do List, and Tasks Assigned to Me using the tabs at the top of the page.
  3. To add your own task or goal, click + Add Task (or + Add Goal) and fill in a title, optional notes, and a due date.
  4. Mark any task complete using the checkbox or Mark as Done button — completed items move to Recently Completed, where you can undo the change if needed.
  5. Use the calendar (Calendar View) or simple list (List View) to see what's coming up.
NoteTasks Assigned to Me are set by your school's counsellor and usually cannot be deleted — only marked complete. If you believe one is incorrect or no longer relevant, contact your school coordinator rather than deleting it yourself.
Tour Stop 9

AI Career Counsellor

The AI Career Counsellor is a conversational assistant available from a chat icon on every page of TopTeen. It is designed to answer the kinds of questions you might otherwise have to wait for a human counsellor's free slot to ask.

9.1

What You Can Ask

  • Based on my Stream Sorter result, what careers should I look at?
  • What is the difference between a B.Tech and a B.E.?
  • What entrance exams do I need for a career in [field]?
  • Help me compare two colleges I've shortlisted.
  • What should I be doing right now, this term, given my class?
9.2

Starting a Conversation

  1. Click the AI Counsellor icon (visible on every page, usually in the bottom-right corner or top navigation bar).
  2. Type your question in plain language — no special format required.
  3. Use the language selector inside the chat panel if you would prefer to communicate in a regional language.
  4. Review the response, and ask follow-up questions in the same conversation for more specific guidance.
9.3

Limitations to Keep in Mind

ImportantThe AI Career Counsellor gives general, evidence-informed guidance — it does not replace a one-to-one session with a human counsellor, especially for sensitive personal, family, or mental health concerns. If a conversation touches on something serious or personal, the assistant will typically suggest connecting you with a human counsellor or a trusted adult, and you should follow that suggestion.
Tour Stop 10

My Profile & Account Settings

Your profile is more than a settings page — it's the information TopTeen uses to personalise every recommendation you see, so it's worth keeping accurate and up to date.

10.1

Viewing and Editing Your Profile

  1. Click your Profile Icon (top-right corner) and select My Profile.
  2. Review your personal details, academic details, interests, and goals.
  3. Click Edit on any section to update it — for example, after choosing your stream at the end of Class 10.
  4. Click Save to confirm changes.
10.2

Account Security

  1. From your Profile Icon, select Account Settings > Security.
  2. Click Change Password, enter your current password, then your new password twice to confirm.
  3. Click Save. You may be asked to log in again on other devices after changing your password.
ImportantUse a password that is at least 10 characters long and not something easy to guess (avoid your name, birthdate, or “password123”). Never share your TopTeen password with classmates, even to “show them something.”
10.3

Notifications and Language

Notification Preferences: choose whether you receive deadline reminders and counsellor messages by email, SMS, or in-app only.

Language: select your preferred display language for platform menus, where supported by your school's configuration.

10.4

Logging Out

Click your Profile Icon and select Log Out. On a shared or public computer, always log out when you're finished, especially before closing the browser.

Tour Stop 11

Troubleshooting & Quick Reference

11.1

Common Issues

Issue
What To Try
Can't log in
Confirm you're using the correct email or mobile number. Try Forgot Password. If your school manages your account, confirm with your coordinator that it is active.
OTP not arriving
Wait 60 seconds, then Resend Code. Check spam folder for email OTPs; check signal strength for SMS.
Assessment won't load or freezes
Refresh the page once. Avoid using the browser's back button mid-assessment. Try a different browser (Chrome or Safari) if the issue continues.
My results don't reflect reality
Assessment results are based only on the answers you gave — consider retaking the assessment if your circumstances or interests have changed.
A card on my Dashboard says “No data yet”
This is expected until you complete the related action — for example, complete an assessment or shortlist a career.
I can't see a feature mentioned in this guide
It may not be enabled for your school or batch yet. Check with your TopTeen coordinator.
Page looks broken or oddly formatted
Try refreshing, clearing your browser cache, or switching between mobile and desktop view.
