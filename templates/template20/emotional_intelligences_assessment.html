Emotional Intelligence Assessment

Test Instructions
How to Take This Emotional Intelligence Test

This assessment is divided into six levels, each focusing on a different part of emotional intelligence. Answer honestly for the most accurate picture of your current strengths and growth areas.

  • Read each statement carefully: Think about how true it feels for you in real life (at school, home, and with friends).
  • Use the 1–5 rating scale: For every statement, choose one option: 1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly Agree.
  • One answer per statement: Select exactly one option for each statement before moving to the next level.
  • Six levels of EQ: You will complete six levels covering Self-Awareness, Self-Control, Empathy, Conflict Resolution, Self-Motivation, and Assertiveness.
  • No right or wrong answers: Answer honestly rather than how you think you "should" respond.

Some items are reverse scored, which simply means that higher agreement lowers the score for that area. The system handles this automatically when calculating your results.

When you are ready, click Start Test. You will move through the six levels one screen at a time, and at the end you'll see a full scoring breakdown and can download your scoring report.

How the Scoring System Works

Your emotional intelligence score is built from six subscales. Each subscale contains six statements rated from 1–5. Some items are reverse scored (higher agreement reduces the score). The system converts your answers to subscale scores between 6 and 30.

After calculating subscale scores, weights are applied to create a composite EQ score: SA × 1.0, SC × 1.3, EM × 1.2, CR × 1.1, SM × 1.2, AC × 1.0. From there we also compute a Profile Balance Index (PBI), Intrapersonal EQ, Interpersonal EQ, and Adaptive EQ.

Interpretation bands for the composite EQ score: ≥ 160 = Exceptional (Top 10%), 140–159 = High (65–89th %ile), 115–139 = Average (35–64th %ile), 95–114 = Low (15–34th %ile), ≤ 94 = Very Low (Bottom 14%).
