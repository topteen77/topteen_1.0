Personality Assessment Report

This report is based on the RIASEC model, developed by John Holland, which categorizes career interests into six distinct types: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional. By identifying your dominant interest types, you can better understand your career preferences and suggest suitable career paths that align with your strengths and passions.

Holland’s Code description
Holland Code Description
Realistic Individuals with Realistic interests prefer hands-on activities and practical tasks. They enjoy working with tools, machines, or animals & often excel in environments that require physical activity & problem-solving. Careers that suit Realistic types include engineering, construction, agriculture, and skilled trades.
Investigative Investigative individuals are curious and analytical, often drawn to research and intellectual challenges. They enjoy exploring ideas, conducting experiments, and solving complex problems. Suitable careers include those in science, medicine, technology, and academia, where critical thinking and data analysis are paramount.
Artistic Artistic individuals thrive in creative and expressive environments. They enjoy activities that involve art, music, writing, and design, often seeking opportunities to innovate and express themselves. Careers in the arts, entertainment, fashion, and media are ideal for Artistic types, as they allow for personal expression and creativity.
Social Social individuals are empathetic and enjoy helping others. They excel in roles that involve teaching, counseling, and providing support. Careers in education, healthcare, social work, and human resources are well-suited for Social types, where they can make a positive impact on people's lives.
Enterprising Enterprising individuals are natural leaders and persuaders. They enjoy activities that involve leading teams, making decisions, and taking risks. They often thrive in dynamic environments where they can influence others and achieve goals. Careers in business, management, sales, and entrepreneurship are ideal for Enterprising types, who are driven by ambition and leadership opportunities.
Conventional Conventional individuals prefer structured and orderly tasks. They excel in roles that involve organization, data management, and attention to detail. Careers in accounting, administration, finance, and logistics suit Conventional types, where precision and reliability is the key.
