Career planning is the process of identifying your interests, strengths, skills, and career goals to make informed decisions about your future. It helps you choose the right subjects, courses, and career path based on your abilities.

The earlier, the better! Class 9–10 is the ideal time to explore different career options, while Class 11–12 is crucial for making final decisions about higher education and career choices.

Your stream should match your interests, aptitude, and career goals. Taking a Stream Selector Test and seeking career counselling can help you make the right decision.

If you feel your choice is not right, don't worry! Career transitions are possible. Career counselling can help you explore alternative paths or career shifts.

A career should align with your interests, skills, personality, and long-term goals. Career assessment tests and expert guidance can help you identify the best-fit options.

  1. Self-assessment – Identify your interests, skills, and values.
  2. Explore career options – Research different fields and job opportunities.
  3. Set career goals – Define short-term and long-term goals.
  4. Choose the right education path – Select subjects, courses, and training.
  5. Gain experience – Internships, extracurricular activities, and skill-building.
  6. Make informed decisions – Get expert guidance through career counselling.

Follow the SMART goal method:

  • T – Time-bound (Set deadlines)
