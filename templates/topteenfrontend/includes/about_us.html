About Us

TopTeen, a venture of Canam Consultants Ltd, is an Ed-Tech career platform. Our goal is to establish India's most influential and dependable career counselling ecosystem, providing students, professionals, and career counsellors with opportunities to change their lives.

One of the most crucial choices in a student's academic and professional life is their career choice. These choices must be made with the highest care and knowledge. We combine artificial intelligence and machine learning with thoughtful human engagement to assist students in enabling career success. We strive to reduce human bias in the choice of a vocation. We use technology and research to assist parents, teachers, career counsellors, and students in making informed career decisions. Together, our platform and consultants streamline this procedure.

With over 25 years of domain knowledge and expertise, Canam has become one of the most trustworthy brands in the higher education and study abroad sectors, one of the most rapidly expanding Ed-tech enterprises, and has already had a significant impact on thousands of careers.

We’ve counselled students seeking an international education with course/ country/ college or university selection based on their academic abilities, skills, interests, and learning style. Also, we provide aspirants with turnkey solutions, from admissions to enrollment, visa guidance, and value-added services related to accommodation/ loans/ scholarships etc. We strive to be able to do it for more than a million children throughout India.

Having assisted thousands of students in the past and being aware of the immense shortage of career counsellors, we believe that a well-structured career guidance platform will add value to students seeking to explore their true potential.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to design and develop India’s most efficient and reliable career guidance ecosystem that will assist students in navigating their future. Through this result-oriented platform with a built-in mentoring process, students will be empowered to make the most critical decision for their future careers and life.

THE PLATFORM

Our objective is to launch a technology-driven career guidance platform for the benefit of students, across India, in a counselling-deficit education sector. While there are a few competitors, the space is still largely unexplored, and the market potential is enormous. As such, we believe that our innovative platform is best positioned to disrupt and grow this domain by algorithmically matching students’ characteristics with occupational demands to assist them in shortlisting their best-fit career choice.

After completing an assessment test, the student is assigned personalized recommendations to achieve their personal, academic and career goals. With increasing competition for admissions to

leading universities and colleges in India, we aim to strengthen students’ competencies and build their confidence to make informed decisions through self-discovery.

The platform will provide all key decision-making stakeholders, i.e. parents/ counsellors/ schools, with the requisite career tools to view long-term career opportunities and better prepare their students for the changing workplace. Through active engagement and development of relationships with schools and institutions across India, we will facilitate training and certification programs to upskill their teachers and counsellors vide our flagship programs and scientific career guidance tools. Further, this interactive platform will facilitate better communication among students, parents and schools and provide access to all stakeholders to monitor the career search process.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our AI-driven, feature-rich, intuitive platform seeks to actively engage students and provide them with the requisite college and career readiness tools, access world-class post-secondary education, and empower them to make the right career choices. The platform will offer independent dashboards for Students, Parents and Schools/ Counsellors to manage the career search process; a few of the platform features are listed below:

  • Parent Involvement We understand that parents significantly influence most career decisions in India, and hence, we seek to involve parents at multiple levels 
  • Platform: Provide interactive tools and outlined deliverables for students to achieve their future career goals and career counsellors to scale their practice
  • Training and Development: Facilitating training for budding career counsellors through a certified Career Counsellor Training Program
  • Skill Building: Digitally interactive content and personalized learning courses to upskill and reskill.
  • Turnkey Solution: One-stop destination for students across India to get end-to-end career guidance
  • Mentoring: Technology-driven, supported by online one-to-one sessions with career counsellors
  • Value Added Services: Future college and career-related offerings 
  • Psychometric Test: RIASEC personality assessment to help students identify their best- fit career path.
  • Curated Resources: Updated and researched Career Library, careers-related blogs, videos and events 
  • Discover and Plan: Equip students with the requisite career-centric knowledge, research and shadow real-world skills, and get recommendations based on data-driven insights and predictive analytics software to navigate their future. 
  • Gamification: To motivate, engage and make the career search process fun and more effective 
  • Gap year solutions and Virtual Internships

PLATFORM’S POTENTIAL

Our platform has been conceived to encourage students to coherently and cohesively formulate their dreams and aspirations while complementing them with their personality, interests, aptitude and educational understanding. With a McKinsey report stating that most graduates are not employable, our platform focuses on providing students with the best-fit career options supported by a college and future career readiness program. We are solving a dire shortage of over 1.5 million career counsellors across India, wherein only 5% of the over 30 million eligible students get access to career and college consulting advisors. With our unique and scalable model, which will address the needs of the unorganized career guidance sector, we aim to work on both the demand and supply sides of career guidance. Through this multi-lingual career platform and a holistic career development process, our objective is to provide equal opportunity to students from diverse socio-economic diverse backgrounds.
