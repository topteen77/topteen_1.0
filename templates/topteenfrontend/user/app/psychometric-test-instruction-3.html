Part 3 - Aptitude Assessment

Welcome to the Aptitude Assessment Test! This comprehensive test evaluates your abilities in various areas, including numerical, spatial, logical, mechanical, language and spellings, verbal, and critical reasoning. The results will help you understand your strengths and identify potential career paths that match your skills. Please read the following instructions carefully before you begin.

General Instructions

  1. Ensure you are in a quiet and comfortable environment where you can focus without interruptions.
  2. Each section has a time limit. Manage your time wisely and try to answer as many questions as possible within the given time.
  3. The time allotted for this specific test is 70 minutes (10 minutes for each and every section).
  4. Make sure to read each question and all the answer choices thoroughly before selecting your answer.
  5. Make sure to read each question and all the answer choices thoroughly before selecting your answer.
  6. Choose the answer that you believe is correct based on your knowledge and understanding. There are no penalties for wrong answers, so it’s better to attempt all questions.
  7. You may use scratch paper for calculations or note-taking. Make sure to have a calculator if permitted for certain sections.
  8. Pay attention to specific instructions given for each section. Different sections may have different types of questions and answer formats.
  9. For logical section if the images appears too small, students can zoom in by pressing ctrl + or they can click on the image to get popped up.
  10. Stay Focused. Try to avoid distractions while taking the test.
  11. Be Honest. The accuracy of your results depends on your honest responses.

Section-Specific Instructions

  1. Numerical Reasoning:
    • You will encounter problems involving arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and data interpretation.
    • Use logical reasoning and calculation skills to find the correct answers.
  2. Spatial Reasoning:
    • You will be given tasks that involve visualizing and manipulating objects.
    • Identify patterns, shapes, and their relationships in two and three dimensions.
  3. Logical Reasoning:
    • You will face questions that test your ability to recognize patterns, sequences, and logical relationships.
    • Use your reasoning skills to solve puzzles and problems.
  4. Mechanical Reasoning:
    • You will be asked about principles of mechanics, such as force, motion, and energy.
    • Apply basic physics concepts to solve the problems.
  5. Language Usage and Spelling:
    • You will be tested on your understanding of language, grammar, and spelling.
    • Choose the correct spellings and grammatical structures.
  6. Verbal Reasoning:
    • You will be given passages to read, followed by questions about the content, structure, and meaning.
    • Answer the questions based on the information provided in the passages.
  7. Critical Reasoning:
    • You will encounter scenarios that assess your ability to think clearly and critically with decision-making ability.
    • Choose responses that reflect objectivity, analytical thinking, problem-solving and basic reasoning abilities.

By understanding your aptitudes, you can make informed decisions about your education and future career. Good luck, and do your best!
