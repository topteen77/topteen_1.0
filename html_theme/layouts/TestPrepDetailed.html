Competitive Exams For School Students (Class 9 and 10)

Entrance exams are an important aspect of a student's life in India! An Indian student must pass necessary entrance exams in order to get admission to institutions, obtain a government job, or benefit from a scholarship, among other things.
Students who have completed the 10th grade are entitled to take a few competitive tests. The majority of them have to do with scholarships and Olympiads. If you wish to join the Armed Forces after 10th grade, you'll have to pass a written test as well!
Competitive tests after 10th grade have the power to shape your future! For example, if you pass a test and are awarded a science education scholarship, your education will define your professional route!

National Talent Search Examination or NTSE

About

NTSE is a national level examination cum scholarship program, to identify students who have high academic talent, exceptional intellectual ability and analytical reasoning. Qualifying students for the two-tier exam are eligible to receive cash scholarship for the full academic year. NTSE exam consists of two stages.

Conducted by

National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT)

Level of Exam

Stage 1 test is carried out at State or UT level. Stage 2 test is carried out at National Level.

Frequency

Once a year

Subjects

Science, Mathematics, Social Science, English, Mental Ability and General Awareness

Eligibility

The Stage 1 examination is open to all students in Class 10 who are enrolled in a recognized school. There are no restrictions on domicile status or where you live.
